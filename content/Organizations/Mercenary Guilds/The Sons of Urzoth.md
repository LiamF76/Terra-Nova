An army of Orcs, Bugbears, and assorted peoples. Led by Urzoth Tuskbreaker. They were once the strongest military group in Arkonia, but were weakened when Zyrathia imprisoned Urzoth and her honor guard, exiling the rest.

**Members**:
- Urzoth Tuskbreaker, Leader
- [[Ulgan]] Bloodbraid, Lieutenant
- Shel Longjaw, Lieutenant
