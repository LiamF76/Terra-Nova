There are three great Fey houses dating back to the ancient Aelarian era:

- **House Lirienveil** – Known for their connection to moonlight and dreams, House Lirienveil is steeped in mysticism and ancient wisdom. They are protectors of the fey’s ethereal secrets and often serve as mediators between the mortal and fey realms.
	- *Leader*: **Mistress Lyrielle Lunastra Lirienveil**: Born over a thousand years ago Mistress Lunastra is a beloved leader of her house. She encourages research and magical pursuits through all her followers, and those born into the nobility of her cousins and her own immediate family are always offered study in her arcane observatories.
	- *Notable Members*:
		- **Vaelith Lirienveil**: Known as the Dreamstrider, Vaelith is Lyrielle's younger brother, and while he is loyal and honest amongst his allies, he is a known trickster. He spies on opposing houses and house enemies through their dreams, walking through them using the power of moonlight as if her was strolling down the path effortlessly. His meme presence is a whispered threat to other houses.
		- **Elowyn Lirienveil**: Elowyn is the house’s most charming diplomat, often sent to courts across the Feywild to gain favor or gather intelligence. Her beauty is otherworldly, but beneath her delicate exterior lies a sharp and calculating mind. She wears a veil enchanted to enhance her presence, making her an irresistible force in negotiations. 
		- **Cyradel Lirienveil**: Cyradel oversees the protection and preservation of House Lirienveil's sacred artifacts, including the legendary _Celestial Bloom_—a flower said to bloom only under the rarest phases of Thalun. She is wise, patient, and fiercely protective of the house’s legacy.
    
- **House Thornshade** – A proud and fiercely independent house tied to the wild and untamed aspects of the Feywild. They command the power of the deep woods and bramble thickets, embodying both the beauty and danger of fey nature.
	- *Leader*: **Oberyn the Feylord**: 
    
- **House Aurelith** – This noble house is associated with sunlight and the golden glades of the Feywild. Known for their splendor and generosity, they often act as guardians of fey harmony and the delicate balance of light within their domain.

The Fey governing structures is based on monarchs from the three great houses. There is the Dynasty of Lirienveil, which was founded by the Dusklight family of Ancient Aelaria, the descendants of the sun worshippers House Aurelith, and Feylord Oberyn, the overall ruler of the Feywild and King of the elves therein.
