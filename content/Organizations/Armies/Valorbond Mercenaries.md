The Valorbond Mercenaries are a monster hunting and mercenary group made up by the characters Valeria, Owlvin, and Gimble. They are soldiers of fortune, willing to fight for coin and glory, they are not evil but they are motivated by coin. They won't kill an innocent, but they are always willing to take a higher offer even if it means breaking their word. They also are willing to give up on allies if it means getting themselves out alive. Owlvin is the least sympathetic to this and is much more morally good.
