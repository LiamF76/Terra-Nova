
This is a breakdown of the Rulers of Iramo.

| Ruler                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Reference Image                             |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| **The Queen**: The Queen is more akin to a Goddess than a ruler. Her followers are branded with arcane tattoos that wash their minds in her 'love', imparting her will onto them. It does not entirely control them, but changes their greatest motivation to getting her approval. This allows her to dictate her slaves actions. The Queen's exact origins are a tightly held secret by the members of her Cabal. Her powers are eldritch in nature, and it is said she rose from the depths of Konduell's Grasp to scour Iramo for its hidden power.                     | ![[Pasted image 20260223100236.png \| 200]] |
| **The Master**: A powerful ancient mutant dragon who rules over powerful warriors and servants. The master curses magical items that he gives to his followers, which force them to do his bidding. He drives his slaves as hard as they can be driven, allowing his greatest warriors to live in splendor while his lowest slaves are nothing. He encourages infighting and testing his subjects against one another and the maze.                                                                                                                                         | ![[Pasted image 20260223100455.png \| 200]] |
| **The Conjurer**: A nigh all-powerful wizard, the Mage is an enigmatic ruler. He does not bind the minds of his slaves, he believes it useless. He simply rules because none can rival him. With his staff and orb his eyes pierce many walls of the Maze, and with knowledge over his enemies and subjects he controls them. He does not brainwash, because it is a waste of his power. His courts are lined with warlocks who siphon his power from their pacts. His followers gather the strongest monsters from the Maze, and bind them to the service of The Conjurer. | ![[Pasted image 20260223100933.png \| 200]] |
