Name: **Hypatia Laethire**, The Voice of Venom

Voice: Voice Description
- "I only learned one thing from Melantha. Half-Gods are worshiped in prayer, real Gods require blood."

Description: Appearance & vibe description

Important Info: 
- Emissary and trusted advisor of [[Vesponous]]
- Hypatia puts on a facade of frivolity, alcoholism, and not caring for the larger world. She appears to be a drunken merrymaker, someone who takes her position and power largely for granted.
- Hypatia in reality is a frighteningly intelligent and shrewd negotiator, who uses her ruse to lure her enemies into a poor position.
- Was married off to an Elf Nobleman who forced her to move to the Triumvirate, he died and she became an advisor to [[Vesponous]] in his place
