Jerome and Agamemnon were born out of wedlock, twin brothers with an older sister. Their Mother was a chromatic dragonborn slave woman to the noble bloodline of Steel. Their father was Wilton Steel, a noble silver dragonborn. The Steel family was silver, yet they were honored with the name steel as being steel colored and proving their valor in battle.

Their mother raised them as best she could but they too were slaves. When she passed and Wilton left the city of Dragonswatch they were thrown to the streets, left to fend for themselves at the age of just 7 years. They were half chromatic half metallic, taking the name Darksteel for themselves along with their sister. 
She made a name for herself as a thief but was eventually caught and arrested, separated from her siblings. Jerome and Agamemnon survived by doing underground pit fights, where Agamemnon was very successful. He was powerful, big and strong, standing taller than many adults before he was 10. Jerome was little, weaker than his brother, and he had a kind heart unlike his large twin. Jerome didn't care much for fighting.

After 2 years of surviving on their own, Dragonswatch came under new rulership. Ivar conquered what is now known as Arkonia, Jerome and Agamemnon were caught during an illegal pit fight. The army brought them before the interim ruler of the city, Zyrathia, a cruel witch known to do worse than imprison or execute criminals. Her associates, who was unknown to the brothers, said they were perfect test subjects. A human man with white hair and rat like features, and a grey skinned elf with strange demonic eyes and mutations led them to a labratory, where the brothers were imprisoned.

After months of torture, strange potions, experiments, and augmentations, the brothers were unrecognizable. Over a year passed as the boys were augmented, trained into killing machines, drilled into being the prefect soldiers. The man, whom they knew as Teacher Damion, said they were going to be made into the perfect weapons for their King Ivar. Their final trail came when the elf, Sylvar, returned. He said the timeline had to be moved up, the Young Wolves had sealed Kairos and had thrown their plan into chaos.

They administered a demonic ritual on the brothers, fusing their augmented bodies and souls with the souls of lesser demons, The Ritual of Abyssal Rebirth. Agamemnon was given Vezgoth, a bloodthirsty rage demon, and Jerome was given Jagathun, the 3 Eyed Demon.

However big and strong Agamemnon was before the ritual, he became even more imposing. Becoming 12 feet tall, muscular beyond measure, and possessing the strength to topple buildings with one swing of his axe. He was a perfect soldier, and through his conditioning, training, and now the demonic soul he possessed, he was more than willing to follow Arkonil for the chance at blood and battle. 
\
Jerome was different. He became a super soldier the same as his brother, unnaturally strong and fast, but not nearly as big or as angry. He was still kind at heart, but with a soldier's resolve and focus. It was as if he could feel everything around him, his reactions were enhanced and his skill with weapons became unmatched. He was stealthy and quick in equal measures. 

The brothers worked together on many missions, taking down valued targets such as knights and generals of Adrithyr's army, and some Redsong mages. When they had become masters of their new abilities they were brought before Ivar, and assigned the task of eliminating the village of Millsworth, a small farming town in the White Plains. The goal was to destroy Adrithyr's infrastructure and food supply to make Ivar's impending war against his father much easier.

Agamemnon was pleased to do any killing he could, not caring for the target or the effect he had. Jerome was not. Enemy soldiers were soldiers, they had known the consequence of facing him, but innocents, non combatants, women and children? He couldn't bring himself to that level. He pleaded with his brother to leave with him, but Agamemnon would hear non of his brothers supposed weakness. Jerome fled, wounded badly by his brother, a bloody battle between brothers in the streets of Arkonia.
Jerome barely escaped, and fled into the Dreadlands, eventually falling unconscious from his wounds. He would have died there, but [[The Old Man]] nursed his wounds, helping Jerome recover the strength he needed to escape from Arkonia. The old man wouldn't give his name to the young dragonborn, but he said that if Jerome wanted to use his poer for something better, a plague was coming. A plague upon the world itself, and the people of the west needed a hero. Men can't defeat what is coming, they need a monster. Jerome agreed to be that monster, and to fight for those who couldn't.
