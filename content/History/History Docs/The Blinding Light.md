
Long ago in the early days of Agamarth, and the Elven Kings first sat in their thrones of the enchanted city Thylor Aethil, there was a king who believed himself to be so great that he was divine. King Erevan, a well studied king and mage, believed himself so powerful and so gracious to his people, that they should worship him, and they did. He looked upon himself in the mirror each day believing he shined so brightly that he outdid the sun, and Illuman. He did not.

He declared to all in his city that he was their god, and they must worship him. Most of the city lived in his graces, and lived well from King Erevan's generosity, so they accepted his decree with joy. Illuman learned of this development, and sent his champion at the time, Orphis the Grail, to the city, to bless all those who chose to abandon Illuman for their mortal king. Orphis gave to all who would accept it, a single sip from the Black Grail, a holy artifact Illuman crafted specially for this blessing. Orphis bid all the non faithful farewell, saying that if they chose to live without the light, they would never see the truth.

Orphis departed, and Erevan was crowned the Sun King of Agamarth. As the golden crown touched his head, a disaster struck the city. The child of every elf who drank from the Black Grail, was permanently blinded. The parents wept, and begged their Sun King to heal their children, but the pretender to the light could not, and they revolted. Erevan lost everything for his hubris, and Orphis carried his Black Grail into history, where both were lost to the ages.
