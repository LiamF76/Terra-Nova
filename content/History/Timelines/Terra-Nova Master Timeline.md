## The Dawn Age Age -*The Age of Making*

*Year 0 - Year 100* The gods shape the world through creation and war, and the first mortal races emerge.

- **Year 0** - The Elder God [[Shao Daraan]] gives shape to the energies of the universe, creating the planet Terra-Nova and everything on it including the races of Dwarves and Aelarian Elves. He invites the Precursors, a race of raw sentient astral energy, to join his creation and become the [[Gods of Terra-Nova]]. Most refuse, but those that agree become the deities we know today. The world is young and the [[Gods of Terra-Nova]] are very close to their children. After completing creation Shao Daraan enters a deep sleep within Nova Magnus.
- **Year 2** - The first sentient race of Elves known as the Aelarians are born into the world, released from the heavens and given the gift of Terra-Nova by [[Shao Daraan]] and [[Gods of Terra-Nova]].
- **Year 20** - The Night God [[Balthazaar]] attempts to devour the sun showing his dominance over [[Illuman]]. A coalition of gods band together to stop him and save the world from the Night Serpent's grasp. Illuman battles him with the power of light, Fulgrim smites him down from the heavens and sky into the sea, Mira creates the great riptide that pulls him down to the depths of the ocean, Iros forges the celestial chains that bind him, Tena gives her life to power and strengthen the chains, [[Aton]] grabs the chains and uses his speed to pull Balthazaar to the very deepest part of the Abyss at the edge of non-existence. 
- **Year 30** - The Aelarians explore their continent of Atlas, learning the ways of the world both arcane and mundane. They reach a low level of civilization before splitting across the world. Scholars agree they became Elves, and the Half-Elves, and some argue they even evolved into the races of Orcs, Leonin, and Halflings/Gnomes.
- **Year 40** - The Aelari discover the power of magic, and combined with their immense lifespan (estimated to be many thousands of years), they use its power to catapult their civilization ahead by centuries, reaching a golden age of magic and technology within one generation.
- **Year 45** - Through ancient methods, no longer possible in today's world, the Aelarians harnessed the power of the still celestial world and created creatures called Titans from [[Shao Daraan]]’s power. This could have been intentional or accidental. These gigantic elemental creatures were as large as mountains and controlled forces comparable to the divine.
- **Year 50** - The continent of Atlas, home of the Aelarians, suffers from tectonic plate movement, and is doomed to fall into the sea. The Aelarians ask one of the Titans to stand beneath the city and uphold it on the ocean, the Titan Genesar agrees, and uses his strength ho hold up the continent for many years.
- **Year 50** - Using the still active celestial gates a large chunk of Aelari civilization undergoes a migration into the celestial gates and begins to populate new realms such as the Fey and the Shadowfell. Scholars embark on journeys of great importance and discovery.
##### The Titan Wars - Year 55 - Year 65
- **Year 55 - Year 65** - The Titans use their immense power to wage war upon the [[Gods of Terra-Nova]]. This war devastates the land, creating the hills, valleys, and oceans as we know them today. The Titans are defeated but it leads to the destruction of Atlas and causes it to sink into the depths of the ocean. Aelarian society collapses and the dark age begins. The celestial gates were destroyed or lost their power during this war. Eventually the Titans are defeated by an alliance between Illuman and the Ancient Dragons.
- **Year 60** - The City of Atlas falls into the sea. Genesar fails in his quest to uphold the continent due to the overwhelming force of the war, causing the land to sink into the Alistair Sea. There is a great scattering of the Aelarians across the world, but the largest groups migrated across the sea to Ikon, where they became the Elves.
- **Year 85** - In a battle between gods, A long forgotten god now known as [[Arkonil]] and his allies waged war against the other gods. He along with the Gods Voracia and Korvus, believed that [[Illuman]] was unfit to lead the Heavens in [[Shao Daraan]]'s absence, and Arkonil wanted to rule himself. Arkonil is known to have slain Oteus (God of Mercy) and many other lesser gods, this is why there are few gods remaining today. They became known as the [[The Betrayer Gods]] and were banished to the Abyss.

## The First Age -*The Age of Beginnings*

*Year 100 - Year 10,000* The gods shape the world through creation and war, and the first mortal races emerge.

- **Year 150** - In a stunning show of comradery, Dwarves and Elves make contact and establish a trade system for knowledge, sharing the secrets of technology and magic with one another and beginning a massive leap in society. This leads to a time often called a renaissance or “Second Golden Age”. Elves learn from the mistakes of their forefathers and become the stewards of the world, seeking to maintain order and balance in society and nature against evil.
- **Year 200** - After the cataclysms of the Titan war and the Reckoning, the remaining Aelari migrated from their sinking city of Atlas to the land of Ikon to the east. They intermingled with the other younger species there and formed a new race/civilization that became known as Elves. This began the formation of the oldest remaining nation in Terra-Nova, Agamarth.
- **Year 1000** - In a legendarily rare celestial event every heavenly body in the solar system aligns with the sun and causes a disturbance in the magical balance of the world. During a harmonic convergence magic is much more potent and mages can cast spells almost effortlessly but the world is very volatile and portals to other worlds open across Terra-Nova (not celestial gates). Elven mages used this power to enact a second Dimensional Expedition (on a smaller scale).
##### The Time of Elders - Year 1000 - Year 4999
- **Year 1000 - Year 4999** - The Elves, and Dwarves slowly grow to dominate the planet, and as Aelarian populations dwindle, the others rise, inheriting their knowledge, magic, and wisdom in different lands. They become the new stewards of the world.

- **Year 5000** - Humans enter the world, said to have been born of the heart of [[Shao Daraan]] himself and destined to inherit the world, they came down from the blade peaks and established villages on the north and south of the mountain range. They are a primitive society for a number of years but despite their short lifespans they advance to the medieval level within a few hundred years, indicating their aptitude for learning and ability to adapt.
- **Year 6620** - Humans enter the pact of Elves and Dwarves called the Inheritor’s Pact, and share the secrets of stories and emotion with the elder races of Elves and Dwarves. Humans are recognized for their ability to lead and learn, and become the de-facto masters of the pact, replacing the elves as the world's protectors and loosening the Elven moral code on the world.
- **Year 6730** - The Elven Oracle Hanaria has a vision of a golden haired King who comes from the West, leading all who will follow him to paradise and defeating the evil [[Balthazaar]] once and for all. It is said that the King will be a Demi-God, a son of [[Illuman]] and carry the light of the sun with him as he walks the world. This becomes known as The Lightbearer Prophecy.
- **Year 6850** - A large group of Elves make their way south across Ikon, and begin to settle the harsh deserts of the continent. They call themselves the "Dark-Elves", as their skin darkens to better protect them in the harsh desert heat and sun. This founds the desert nation of Drok'Shah.
- **Year 6950** - A Dark-Elf named Hara with strength and wisdom beyond his years campaigns across the deserts of Drok Shah, uniting his people through politics and war. He unites the people of his nation and they name him "Al Ibn- Al Mawt", the Son of Death. He founds a great city on a cluster of Oasis, calling it "Akmu", The Emerald Oasis. He becomes the first King of Drok Shah and takes the name Hara Khan.
- **Year 7500** - In a show of unity a tower known as “The Rose Spire” is constructed on an island between [[z Old/Old Geography/Old Escarth/Escarth]] and Ikon, serving as neutral territory and named as a vault for dangerous and powerful magical artifacts to be kept. Watched over by “The Enchanter” which is a title passed down from one vault keeper to the next.
- **Year 9020** - Powerful dragons known as the 4 Dragons of the Apocalypse form the first Tiamat and begin a multi front war against the mortals of Terra-Nova using their own power and armies of Dragonborn and Kobolds. 4 Heroes from different parts of the world unite and become known as the Wyrm-Wardens. They work together to defeat the dragons and save civilization by defeating the 4 dragons. The Dragonborn are freed from their dark influence and join the sentient races of Terra-Nova.

## The Second Age -*The Human Age*

*Year 10,000 - Year 20,000* The gods shape the world through creation and war, and the first mortal races emerge.

- **Year 10,200** - The Humans build the first massive city on Escarth, Praetoria. It is the center of their civilization and marks the beginning of the magic era of humanity. It is the largest mortal city since Atlas and is known for its legendary heroes and center of the adventuring trade. Founded and led by the first elected king Lazarus. He was known as the King of Kings, said to have ruled for a thousand years and been one of the greatest mortals to have ever lived.
- **Year 10,800** - The Starshaper Wizards found their arcane university and order in Praetoria. The Druids of Tena make themselves known and emerge from the shadows of the Eldermourne Forest. The Magusflame Wizards also begin their arcane order in another city of Solstice.
- **Year 11,000** - The Elven Oracle Hanaria has a vision of a war across time and sees the sun rise and set a hundred times in a single day. This is the battle between [[Balthazaar]] and [[Illuman]], where a hero emerges. It is said they will wield a weapon born in the Astral plane, and their fingers of starlight hand will determine the fate of the world. “A body of Iron, a wild heart, and fingers of starlight”.
- **Year 11,100** - The prophecy of the chosen begins a movement of selflessness and a search for glory among the people of Terra-Nova. This begins a long period of people becoming adventurers and seeking glory. These heroes shape the course of history and accomplish legendary deeds. Examples: Valerius Stormbringer, Aria Frostwind, Roderick Elroy, etc...
- **Year 11,600** - The gods took their power and created items to give to those they named their champions. This gives the people who wield them part of their divine power and a deep connection to that god beyond any cleric or paladins. To be a god's champion is the greatest honor in the minds of the devout and you become similar to a living god. The first champion named was Lazarus the Reborn, Champion of [[Vulcan]].
##### The Abyss Wars - Year 11,625 - Year 11,810
- **Year 11,625 - Year 11,810** - Using the powerful blood magics gathered by [[Voracia]] the Dreaded, [[Arkonil]] the Wicked and his army of demons made war against the mortal plane through Abyssal gates. A conflict spanning multiple planes that lasts 30 years. They were held back and eventually stopped by the warriors and mages of the Inheritor pact and through the binding magics of an unknown wizard Arkonil along with his generals were sealed within different items. This is considered to be the greatest alliance of moral powers in history. The 3 Betrayer Gods were sealed within powerful artifacts, and distributed for safekeeping among Men, Elves, and Dwarves.

- **Year 11,810** - A power struggle within Inheritor pact due brews over which race should watch over the 3 artifacts of the Abyss Wars. [[The Betrayer Gods]] [[Arkonil]] , [[Voracia]], and Korvus. In the end the Humans took Arkonil (crown), the Elves took Voracia (amulet), and the Dwarves took Korvus (ring).
- **Year 11,825** - The Human kingdom of Praetoria collapses into civil war across Escarth over the crown of Arkonil. Many rulers and warlords rise up and attempt to seize the crown and its power for themselves. This fractures human society and destroys their prosperous civilization. This is partly due to the collapse of the Inheritor Pact. The wizard orders fracture and fall to the conflict. The artifacts are lost to the ages.
##### The Dark Age of Men - Year 11,820 - Year 20,400
- **Year 11,820 - Year 20,400** - Humanity falls into lower magic medieval society and out of kinship with the Dwarves and Elves. This resulted in a far more primitive society and lacking in military power they were forced back into Escarth from their other territories. This resulted in the formation of new nations in the aftermath, Stormfjord (a kingdom of Viking like raider warriors practicing tribal government), Veredian, and Artoria.
- **Year 11,900** - In an act of fear and prejudice the people of Escarth enact a purge against the Druids of Tena, (wrongly) believing them to be responsible for the fall of the great kingdoms and the start of the dark age. The Hyperion Church led them to believe their practice of nature magic brought about the collapse. Few druids survive and enter into hiding or assume false identities to keep their order alive.
- **Year 13,650** - In less than a week all dragons on Terra-Nova disappear. It is documented that they were all witnessed to be returning to the Wyrmlands but the reason for that and for their disappearance is unknown. Since this event there are no dragons on Terra-Nova unless later stated.
- **Year 19,036** - Aethos the Enchanter and steward of the Rose Spire is killed and all the artifacts within are stolen or claimed. Many legendary and dangerous artifacts were stolen and scattered across the world.

## The Third Age -*The Age of Return*

*Year 20,000 - Year 30,000* Humanity returns to the glory they once knew, and the world is forever altered.

- **Year 20,212** - Aria, an elven mage takes residence in and began to rebuild the Rose Spire. She was known for her enchanting and foreign abilities and has begun to create a host of new magical items while gathering many that were lost to restock the vault within the spire.
- **Year 20,375** - King Uudahaq of the Drocuthu Isle attempts to become a living god by ritualistically sacrificing every being in his kingdom. The casualties are in the hundreds of thousands and the result is an island of dangerous undead cursed to roam the island for eternity.
- **Year 20,400** - Adventurers of Human and elvish along with other races set out to map out and discover the secrets of the world. They are responsible for the maps and knowledge we have today. They discover the power of Konduell that keeps anyone from leaving the known world by sea. The human explorers bring knowledge home to [[z Old/Old Geography/Old Escarth/Escarth]].
- **Year 20,575** - The Artorian kingdom of Humans rises in power and begins a new wizard order: The Redsong Wizards, using the teachings and remaining books of the old Starshaper and Magusflame orders. This in addition to a treaty with the Dwarves of Bourn Dorhul begins a somewhat prosperous and decent era of Humanity leading up to the current day.
- **Year 20,650** - A series of  bloody and brutal wars between kingdoms small and large in Escarth leads to the unification of the continent south of the Blade Mountains. This new empire becomes known as Estor and is ruled by the ancient bloodline of Realmcrest leading to Adrithyr Realmcrest in the present day.
- **Year 20,875** - Arkeron the Machine's mother is built and born in the mines of Bourn Dorhul. She becomes conscious and gains free will. Through unknown methods she ascends into her own plane and becomes the god of order and of all Warforged.
- **Year 20910** - Kagerra, Queen of Agamarth (the elves), becomes the owner of a dragon hatched from an egg through unknown means. Known as Kezurdur the Pale, this dragon is the only known living dragon in all of Terra-Nova.

## The Fourth Age -*The Age of Divines*

*Year 20,000 - Year 30,000* Demons are exiled, and Gods are born.

##### The War of Miracles - Year 30,283 - Year 30,285
- **Year 30,285 - Year 30,290** - The King of Drok Shah, Amir Khan, is executed by Queen Kagerra of Agamarth for refusing to raise taxes on his subjects in her vassal state. His son Igan, takes the title of Khan, and openly calls for Kagerra's head, beginning a war between the Elven nations. The war lasts 2 years and ends in Drok Shah becoming independent. It is called the War of Miracles because it is said to be a miracle that Drok Shah won, considering they were outnumbered in the many hundreds of thousands of soldiers.
##### The Shattered Crown Rebellion - Year 30,295 - Year 30,312
- **Year 30,295** - A long and bloody war started by the royal bastard [[Ivar]] the Uncrowned. It began as a rebellion against his father [[Adrithyr]] Realmcrest on the basis of the murder of his mother and his right to the throne as the eldest son. Adrithyr recognizes his legitimate son Lukas Realmcrest (The Thunderlord) as the heir. Ivar represents the downtrodden and forgotten, acting as a “champion of the people”.
- **Year 30,298** - Ivar Uncrowned and Lukas Realmcrest, Prince and Son of King Adrithyr Realmcrest, battle at the dark and legendary city of Oligoth. In a fit of range and violence, Ivar fully assumes the form of the Reckoner, Arkonil, wielding the blade of the Betrayer God. He battles Lukas and pears his half-brother in two, assumedly killing him. 2 years later he is discovered alive in the capital city of Terraven. Ivar declares Lukas' death as repayment for the death of one of his siblings at Adrithyr's hands, and demands Adrithyr's head ass repayment for the many others publicly. Oligoth is totally destroyed, leaving a cursed ruin known as 'The Shattered Crown.'
- **Year 30,298** - After becoming Lord of the Obelask region in Western Estor, Tyran Obliteras, Champion of Vulcan, fell to the dark forces of the Abyss, corrupting him and his connection to the War God. He was hunted and defeated by Vulcan's new champion Melantha Celestia-Shield, and her allies known as the Young Wolves.
- **Year 30,300** - When the rebellion of Ivar the Uncrowned reaches its pinnacle in a devastating siege on Estor's capital city, Terraven, the kingdom suffers a great political blow as many nations secede. Estor maintains its holdings on the north and east sides of the continent, but Escarth becomes home to many new nations. Calixos, a monarchy southeast of Estor, New Praetoria, a republic based in the south, Arkonia, a monarchy along the west side of the Warming River, and the Freehold, an empire on the west coast. Political ties between Estor and its neighbors are tense, and war between Estor and Arkonia continues in a stalemate for the long winter.
- **Year 30,302** - United by the efforts of War Champion Melantha, the divine champions of Terra-Nova unite at Castle Ikaris, holding the second champions convergence. They make a pact to join in the war of Two Crowns, and to work together towards the goal of defeating Ivar and his wicked patron, Arkonil the God Killer. Alongside Augustus Auranthor, suspected hero of prophecy and Lightbearer, they lead their armies across the continent to begin the war effort.
- **Year 30,302** - In a vicious attack on the King's Palace in Terraven, Adrithyr Realmcrest is captured by the forces of Ivar and taken prisoner, believed to be held in Arkenstone (The capital of Arkonia) awaiting trial under the Arkonian government. In a simultaneous and dark effort, half of the living Divine Champions are killed by agents of the Abyss.
- **Year 30,302** - Aldred Blacksun, now known as Aldred God of the Black Sun, Lord of Penance and Punishment, ascends for mortality to Godhood in a magical ritual of unknown and untold power. The heavenly city on the face of Nova-Magnus goes dark, and thousands of souls are lost for eternity. Aldred assumes his place amongst the [[Gods of Terra-Nova]].
- **Year 30,312** - Ivar Uncrowned faces the Young Wolves in battle. He is defeated by their combined efforts. Arkonil the Reckoner is sealed in stone alongside the former Rogue Timekeeper, Kairos. The world celebrates the end of a great evil, and an era of peace falls over Escarth. 

- **Year 30,314** - Melantha Celestia-Shield becomes known as the Mother of Gods, in a stunning divine event. Tens of gods are freed from the clutches of Khal Daraan. Once thought to be dead, these Gods are restored to the Terra-Novan pantheon and their worship reignited. The Fourth Age becomes known as the Age of Divines for her great achievement.
- **Year 30,315** - Melantha Celestia-Shield and Alabaster Ironhawk are officially married in a ceremony celebrated on both Heaven and Earth. 
- **Year 30,330** - The Fade begins. Magic begins to retract from the world due to the expanding and corrupting Scar that has been laid across Escarth. In a collaborative effort Ivys Arthur and mage allies from across the world work together to reduce its impact, containing the Scar to localized areas around the original Obelisks that formed it. The damage however, could not be fully stopped, and there is significantly less magic available to mortals through leylines.
- **Year 30,350** - Estor falls to ruin. A kingdom that has stood as a bastion of strength against chaos falls to disarray after the premature death of Lady Elena Syrell, who had assumed the position of Steward of Estor. Her death causes widespread collapse, and Estor's lands fall into a dark age of danger. Warlords, petty kings, and bandits rule the largest portion of South Escarth, leaving Calixos and the Freehold as the only safe lands in the South.
- **Year 30,360** - Rolf Duradin, the richest man in Calixos, is overcome by a vision from the heavens. He is shown a land of pure splendor in which the law and society are determined by riches, and those who are richest rule with unrelenting power. Using his vast wealth he hires a dragon of the far and wild land of Wahellia to assist him in conquering Calixos. He succeeds, and renames the nation Xerathos, instituting a Plutocracy. The Dragon leaves Xerathos, but not Escarth.
- **Year 30,361** - The Dragon hired by Rolf Duradin builds a lair within the northern shores of the Goldgrove, and in an event known as the small massacre, obliterates the innocent and peaceful populations of Halflings and Gnomes living in the Underhill and surrounding areas. This Dragon is known as Garthrax the Living Storm.
- **Year 30,370** - The free states of the Freehold unite under one banner, forming the first modern democratic republic known as Sovrentia. Sovrentia stands as a bastion of safety for those fleeing the Dragon of the north and the chaos of Estor's fall.
- **Year 30,400** - The power and wealth amassed by Garthrax attracts other dragons to the area, and starts a cataclysmic war across the Northwest corner of Escarth, making it uninhabitable to mortals for years. This is known as the First Scale War, and after a year only 3 of the tens of dragons that fought remain. They agree on a tentative peace, and divide the land amongst themselves, forming the first nation in history ruled by Dragons. The Triumvirate Scales, ruled by the 3 dragon Monarchs, Garthrax, Gravebinder, and Vesponous.
