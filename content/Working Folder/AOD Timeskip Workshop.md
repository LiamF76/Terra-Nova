#### Where are we now? What nations still exist?

- Sovrentia: Born from the Freehold, Sovrentia is a nation of independent states. Each state of Sovrentia has its own government system. Some are managed by companies, others have monarchs such as lords or nobles, abut most have elected Governors. Sovrentia is the longest standing nation in Escarth, having been around for almost 250 years. The population is highly diverse.
- New Estorias: Formed from the unification of some Estor territory and New Praetoria, New Estorias is a theocratic empire ruled by an eternal emperor. There is a Council of High Priests who guide the Emperor and advise them, but the emperor is one person. An eternal ruler whose soul is stored within a magic amulet known as the Phoenix Soul. This Emperor is [[Horus Hixs]], the Phoenix Emperor. The religion of New Estorias, [[The Paragon of Rebirth]], believes that Emperor Hixs is a living God, sent by the heavens to guide them and mandated by heaven to rule. The population of New Estorias is largely Fire Genasi, Tieflings, Beastfolk, and Orcs / Goblins.
- Xerathos: The evolution of Calixos, a Plutocracy where the rich owners of the massive mithril, silver, and gold mines rule the nation. Largely they are dwarven kings who hold great hordes beneath mountains. Easily the richest kingdom due to the powerful and ancient Mintherals, monsters that create and populate the mines and ground of Xerathi mountains with gold and other ores.
- Triumvirate Scales: Nation ruled by 3 dragons. Ghost dragon ruling with undead and death knights, skeleton dragon simulacra. Blue dragon worshipped by storm cultists and pirates. Green dragon with 3 heads followed by tribal and shamanic underdeveloped tribes. The 3 dragons have a mutual understanding to keep their territory, but are constantly trying to kill or rob one another.

## What happened to magic / mages?

In Frieren, after the defeat of the Demon King, there are far less mages and magic users throughout the world. This is also true in Terra-Nova. After the defeat of Arkonil, magic changes in the world.

The leylines of Terra-Nova have been poisoned, like constricted arteries in a body, and no longer push the same amount of magic through the world. This poison came from the Scar. This means there are literally just less wizards in the world, because there is less magic to go around. Combined with the new chaos and danger associated with the world post Age of Divines, people have less time to practice and learn magic.

The world may be less magical, but magic still exists, just in smaller scale, and more concentrated in specific areas like academies or mage cities. You are lucky to find a magic user in a village, possible in cities, but still they are few and far between outside of population centers.

Divine magic is far more common, such as mediocre healers and priests, paladins and clerics. Though this does not make everything better, as resurrection magic becomes more difficult with resource scarcity. The world's supply of diamonds has been almost completely used up, and nations are hoarding them more than ever.
