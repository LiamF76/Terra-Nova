
## Overview

Normally, 5e assumes 6–8 encounters per adventuring day between Long Rests.
In travel-based adventures, however, each “day” of travel counts as only a Short Rest,
and the entire journey equals one "adventuring day" leading up to a single Long Rest when the destination is reached.

✅ This keeps resource pressure real
✅ It makes travel meaningful and dangerous
✅ It allows time to pass realistically while keeping 5e's intended challenge level


## What is the 6-8 Encounter Day?

| Encounter Difficulty | % of Encounters | Number (out of 6–8) | Notes                                                                                 |
| -------------------- | --------------- | ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Easy                 | 10–20%          | 1                   | Should drain minor resources or provide minor pressure.                               |
| Medium               | 50–60%          | 3–5                 | Main body of the adventuring day; expected to cost meaningful resources.              |
| Hard                 | 20–30%          | 1–2                 | Should be threatening; may force use of major abilities, healing, or tactics.         |
| Deadly               | 0–10%           | 0–1                 | Optional; meant to seriously threaten party survival if engaged poorly or unprepared. |


## Travel and Encounter Flow

Basic Cycle During Travel:

| Step | What Happens                                                                    |
| ---- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | Party travels for a day (or part of a day).                                     |
| 2    | 1 meaningful encounter (combat, hazard, social) happens during or after travel. |
| 3    | After the encounter, party camps for the night (**Short Rest only**).           |
| 4    | Next day, repeat: travel ➔ encounter ➔ Short Rest.                              |
| 5    | After completing the full journey, the party gains a Long Rest.                 |


## Encounter Distribution

Plan 6–8 total encounters over the entire journey (whether the journey is 6 days or 16 days). Not one encounter per day — some days may have no encounters, some days may have two.
Suggested pacing:
<mark style="background: #FFB86CA6;">65–75%</mark> of days should have an encounter. Some days might be entirely travel/roleplay with no dangers. Critical encounters (boss fights, major hazards) should be later in the journey when resources are low.


## Rest Rules During Travel

| Rest Type                         | What Happens                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Short Rest (camping at night)     | - Spend Hit Dice to recover HP.  <br>- Regain short-rest abilities (e.g., Warlock spell slots, Fighter Second Wind).  <br>- **NO long-rest recovery**: no regaining spell slots, no full HP heal. |
| Long Rest (only at journey's end) | - Full recovery of hit points, spell slots, and class features.                                                                                                                                   |


## Simple Example Timeline

**Journey:** 12-day trek across a deadly marsh.

|Day|Event|
|---|---|
|1|Uneventful (roleplay)|
|2|Bandit attack (combat)|
|3|Heavy rain — hazard (Constitution saves or HP loss)|
|4|Rest quietly (no encounter)|
|5|Swarm of undead rise from the bog (combat)|
|6|Negotiation with swamp druids (social)|
|7|Uneventful (travel montage)|
|8|Landslide (hazard)|
|9|Giant crocodile attack (combat)|
|10|Party camps and spots cursed ruins (exploration choice)|
|11|Final boss encounter: Marsh Hag and her minions (combat)|
|12|Arrive at destination — full Long Rest earned.|


# Summary

During travel, days are Short Rests; nights are Short Rests. The party _only_ gets a Long Rest when the journey concludes.

Spread the normal 6–8 encounter "adventuring day" across the entire trip, keeping travel dangerous and resource-taxing but realistic for how much time passes.
