

**First Steps**

- Roll initiative if you haven't already.
- Determine starting distance(s) between quarry and pursuers, track these throughout the chase.
- Designate the pursuer closest to the quarry as the lead (this can change from round to round).
- Start the chase, going round-by-round the same as combat.
	- Each participant can take one action and one move on its turn.


**Dashing and Exhaustion**

- A participant can use the Dash action 3+CON modifier times.
- For each additional use of Dash after that, the creature must pass a DC 16 Constitution saving throw at the end of its turn or gain one level of exhaustion.
- Exhaustion goes from 0-5, when a creature gets to level 5 it drops out of the chase (its speed becomes 0).


**Chase Complications**

- Each participant rolls a d20 at the end of its turn, consult the appropriate environment table (Urban/Wilderness) in the DMG (or create your own) to determine if a complication occurs.
- Complications affect the next participant in the initiative order, not the one who just rolled.


**Ending a Chase**

- A chase ends when one side stops, the quarry escapes, or when the pursuers are close enough to catch their quarry.
- At the end of each round, if neither side gives up, the quarry makes a Stealth check.
    - Compare the result of the Stealth check to the Passive Perception scores of the pursuers.
    - If the quarry's Stealth check is higher than the highest Passive Perception score, they escape. If not, the chase continues.
    - If the quarry is not out of the lead pursuer's sight, the Stealth check fails automatically.
    - The quarry gains advantage or disadvantage on the Stealth check based on certain factors (see "Escape Factors" table in DMG p.253).


**A few additional notes:**

- You can run the chase using theater of the mind or you can map it.
- Feel free to place specific obstacles along the chase route like an overturned cart, these can require an ability check or saving throw to navigate.
- Chase participants can create their own obstacles/complications using spells or improvising an action.
- A quarry's escape attempt can be helped or hindered by factors other than what's listed in the table, like certain spells.
