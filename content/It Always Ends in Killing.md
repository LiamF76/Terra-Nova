## It Always Ends in Killing

"What did you want to be when you were little? I know you didn't want to be this..."

"I don't understand the question."

"What did you dream about? When you pictured yourself, tomorrow, months, years later, what did you see?"

"I guess... nothing. I didn't dream until I carried the shard... and by then..."

"By then it was already too late."

"Yes."

"You never had a chance to be anything else did you?"

"Does it matter?"

"I am asking."

"Wizards ask many questions, most do not matter."

"I am a special kind of wizard."

"So it seems."

"Do you know why Horus sent us here?"

"To kill the daughter."

"Your sacred duty is to evaluate, and dispense appropriate force, with my help."

"It always ends in killing."

"Perhaps not this time. I thin she may be different."

"She poisons the world with self righteous nonsense, desperate to grow only for the sake of growth."

"Like a tumor."

"Exactly."

"Then why not get it over with now?"

"I would like the tumor to grow larger. Perhaps this one will kill me."

"You want to die?"

"Desperately. I have been doing this for... how long?"

"You took the Shard about... 317 years ago."

"Far too long."

"How do you know you will die one day?"

"It always ends in killing."

"Her mother will be upset. She will surely kill you."

"Such is the way."

"Would you like me to clear your memory again?"

"Quickly, I am beginning to remember... Lorn? Where is he?"

"Hush now, forget, and be free. Follow me Shardbound, there is killing to be done."
