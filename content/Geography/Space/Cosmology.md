
#### Stars:
- <mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">Nova Magnus</mark> (The Sun): The Heart of the Heavens, The central star of Terra-Nova’s solar system, Nova Magnus is more than just a life-giving sun—it is a divine entity. Its surface is said to house **Elysium**, the radiant city of the gods, where celestial beings dwell and watch over creation. Legends say that only the purest souls ascend to Elysium upon death. Nova Magnus shines with a golden light, symbolizing justice, creation, and divine authority. Some cults revere it as the ultimate arbiter of mortal deeds.
- <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">Rovan</mark> (The North Star): Known as the North Star, Rovan is the brightest star in the night sky, second only to the glow of **Astreth, the Moon of Mani**. A beacon of navigation and hope, Rovan is known as the "traveler’s light," anchoring the celestial compass of the **Ark** constellation. Sailors, explorers, and nomads believe Rovan holds the wisdom of ancient gods, pointing the way to destiny or safety in the darkest hours. It is also called "The First Star," as it is said to have been the first light lit by the gods to illuminate the night sky.
- <mark style="background: #D2B3FFA6;">Aria</mark> (Black Hole): The Black Star, Unlike any other celestial body, Aria is not a star in the traditional sense but a **black hole**, an arcane and physical phenomenon of immense power and terror. It is a remnant of an ancient star consumed by its pride, cursed by the gods to devour everything near it. First recorded during the **Aelarian Age of Discovery**, Aria is both feared and studied. Sorcerers and scholars believe its existence bends the fabric of reality, making it a source of forbidden power. Some myths claim Aria whispers to mortals who gaze upon it for too long, tempting them with promises of unbridled strength.
- <mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">Zaldeth</mark> (Star): The Wandering Star, Zaldeth is a rogue celestial body that does not follow a fixed path. Unlike other stars, which are bound to their constellations, Zaldeth drifts across the sky, appearing in different regions depending on the season or year. Known as a symbol of freedom and unpredictability, Zaldeth is said to be the soul of a god who rebelled against the cosmic order, eternally searching for something lost. Astronomers and astrologers alike consider Zaldeth an omen, its position marking times of change, upheaval, or discovery.

#### Constellations: 
- **Valyris**: The Mage, A constellation forming the image of a robed figure with one hand outstretched, holding a starry staff. Valyris is a symbol of wisdom, arcane mastery, and curiosity. Mages and scholars often look to Valyris for inspiration and insight during times of study or magical experimentation.
- **Lazarus**: The Warrior, Depicted as a towering figure with a raised sword, Lazarus embodies courage, strength, and honor in battle. The stars of Lazarus are said to burn brighter during times of war or great conflict. Many soldiers offer prayers to this constellation before marching to the battlefield.
- **Ozmythos**: The Thief, A sly figure seemingly crouched in shadow, Ozmythos represents cunning, agility, and survival. Its stars flicker faintly, disappearing in cloudy skies as if hiding from view. Rogues and adventurers often leave small offerings beneath its stars for luck in their heists or stealthy endeavors.
- **Sorvella**: The Dancer, Stars forming a flowing silhouette of a figure mid-spin, Sorvella represents joy, freedom, and the ephemeral beauty of life. Her constellation is believed to grant grace and charm to those born under its sign. Fey creatures often celebrate Sorvella during their seasonal revelries.
- **Khulidius**: A mysterious oval of stars with a bright "pupil" in the center, Khulidius is a symbol of vigilance, foresight, and hidden truths. It’s said to watch over travelers at night, protecting them from unseen dangers. Many seers and diviners claim their visions are strongest when Khulidius is at its peak.
- **Ark**: A grand constellation that stretches across the sky like an arrow pointing north, Ark represents guidance, discovery, and purpose. At its tip is **Rovan, the Brightest Star**, which serves as the North Star. Sailors and explorers rely on Ark to navigate the unknown, believing it to be a gift from the gods to lead the lost.
- **Drakthar**: The Wyrm, A sprawling constellation that looks like a coiled dragon, Drakthar is a symbol of power, greed, and chaos. Its shimmering tail is said to point toward hidden hoards or treasures. Adventurers often interpret its movements as signs of fortune—or doom.
- **Eryssia**: The Harp, The celestial harp’s delicate string-like stars shimmer faintly, said to resonate with the music of the heavens. Bards and dreamers are drawn to Eryssia’s beauty, often believing its light inspires creativity and heals emotional wounds. Stories claim its melody can calm even the fiercest storms or temper angered gods.
- **Kaelith**: The Bridge, A unique constellation resembling an arched bridge of stars, Kaelith is the passage between worlds and ideas. Scholars and philosophers revere it as a symbol of enlightenment and unity. It’s believed to glow brightest when great discoveries or alliances are forged.
- **Serenith**: The Silver Tear, A soft silver light emanates from Serenith, giving it a gentle and melancholic presence in the night sky. Myths say Serenith formed from the first tear shed by **Mani, God of the Moon**, as he mourned the mortals who perished in the gods' wars. Serenith is revered by healers, mourners, and those who grieve, who believe it offers solace and hope. The star is said to glow brightest on nights when the Feywild draws closest to the Material Plane, hinting at its connection to the ethereal and the divine.

#### Planets:
- **Terra-Nova**: The Earth-equivalent planet, home to a variety of ecosystems and civilizations. It is the baseline of life as known to most beings in the universe.
- **Sylaxos**: A planet where the laws of time and space are chaotic. Landscapes constantly shift, creating an unpredictable and ever-changing world. Inhabitants are often displaced across time, existing in parallel realities.
- **Nocthor**: A planet with a vast, obsidian ocean, inhabited by ethereal and gaseous beings. The ocean is nearly impossible to navigate due to its strange, semi-solid nature, and the planet’s gravity and atmosphere are incredibly weak.
- **Reth'Kaar**: A scorched and desolate wasteland world where survival is the highest form of worship. Once a cradle of divine experimentation, it is now sealed from the stars, its three forgotten gods guarding secrets that could shatter the cosmos.

##### Moons: 
- **Astreth**: The Shadow’s Keeper, a darkened moon visible only during eclipses, linked to transitions and endings.
- **Nysria**: The Veiled One, an enigmatic moon shrouded in eternal mist, associated with secrets and fey magic.
- **Thalun**: The Blue Moon, a celestial body whose glow influences tides and dreams, said to guide lost souls.
- **Selene**: Mani's Pride, a radiant moon revered for its protective light, a symbol of purity and vigilance. The symbol of the Moon God Mani.
- **Arrak**: The Yellow Stone, a moon of muted golden light, tied to harvests, wealth, and grounded wisdom.
- **Lumina**: The Bright Daughter, a young star-like moon that appears only during solstices, a harbinger of hope and renewal.
