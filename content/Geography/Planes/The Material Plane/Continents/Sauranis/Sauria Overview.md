Sauria is a strange nearly alien land, a vast and unique continent to the Southeast of Ikon. It is inhabited by strange creatures native to only Sauria, known as Dinosaurs. Sauria is a violent and dangerous land, so far it has been so wild that it has been divided since the beginning of recorded history. Government structure is rare due to the chaos of weather and wildlife, meaning the land is made up of vaguely bordered territories not nations.
