
## **1. Core Identity**

- **Nation Name:** Agamarth
- **Nicknames / Epithets:** Land of the Witch-Queen
- **One‑Sentence Summary:** Magical land of ancient wizards, magical spirits, and enchanted landscapes where the eternal Queen Kagerra rules.
- **What Makes It Unique:** Inherently magical landscapes such as floating islands, inverted waterfalls, and powerful magics. Magical prowess determines standing and strength, every knight is a wizard too. Ruled by Queen Kagerra, a witch who lives forever and watches over all of Agamarth from her tower.

## **2. Geography & Environment**

- **Location on the Continent:** Agamarth makes up everything North of the Agamar Mountains.
- **Climate & Biomes:** The climate of Agamarth is varied, but generally tropical. There are wetlands, jungle, and dry forests. In the very north of Agamarth there are stretches of temperate woodlands. Most of the continent is warm year round, with slightly colder winters, but the seasons are controlled and curated by Queen Kagerra, it has been recorded as summer for the past 17 years.
