
Kheagon is a province of the nation Sovrentia. Kheagon is a monarchy. It is one of the largest provinces in Sovrentia, and its ruling family is descended from Maple Celestia, a hero and warlord who helped found what became Sovrentia. The ruling family in Kheagon are the Ferrans.

The region has a history of untamed wilds and small villages. There is only one large city in the region centered around a castle. Most of the province is dangerous country, forests, and hills. There are a healthy amount of monsters. This is offset by the strong warrior culture. Many people make livings as wandering warriors and mercenaries. Warrior is not so much a title as it is an occupation, though many will denote themselves as "A warrior of Kheagon", to show their ability and status.

Kheagon's capital is [[Highstride]], a small city on its western border. There is another small town called [[Silversong]]. Another town is Tarnkeep, which is a fortress in some mountains.
