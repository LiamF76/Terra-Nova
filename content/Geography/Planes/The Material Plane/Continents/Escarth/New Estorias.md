# **New Estorias**

## **1. Core Identity**

- **Nation Name:** New Estorias
- **Nicknames / Epithets:** The Rising Sun Empire, The Land of the Eternal Emperor, Land of the Phoenix
- **One‑Sentence Summary:** A roman inspired theocratic empire (like under Caesar), ruled by an immortal Emperor who is the head of state and figurehead of the religion, they are generally a bastion for order, and strength.
- **What Makes It Unique:** Theocratic Empire ruled by an eternal Emperor, [[Horus Hixs]]. They have a state religion that worships their Emperor Horus, and the spirit of the Phoenix.
