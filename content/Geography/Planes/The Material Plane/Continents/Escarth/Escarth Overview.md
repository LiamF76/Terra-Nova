Escarth is known as 'The Land of Legends', and despite not being the fist land populated by mortals, it holds what is considered to be both the richest and most significant histories in Terra-Nova.


# Nations of Southern Escarth

### [[The Triumvirate Scales]]

Nation ruled by 3 dragons. Ghost dragon ruling with undead and death knights, skeleton dragon simulacra. Blue dragon worshipped by storm cultists and pirates. Green dragon with 3 heads followed by tribal and shamanic underdeveloped tribes. The 3 dragons have a mutual understanding to keep their territory, but are constantly trying to kill or rob one another.

---

### [[Sovrentia]]

Born from the Freehold, Sovrentia is a nation of independent states. Each state of Sovrentia has its own government system. Some are managed by companies, others have monarchs such as lords or nobles, but most have elected Governors. Sovrentia is the longest standing nation in Escarth, having been around for almost 250 years. The population is highly diverse

---

### [[New Estorias]] 

Formed from the unification of some Estor territory and New Praetoria, New Estorias is a theocratic empire ruled by an eternal emperor. There is a Council of High Priests who guide the Emperor and advise them, but the emperor is one person. An eternal ruler whose soul is stored within a magic amulet known as the Phoenix Soul. This Emperor is [[Horus Hixs]], the Phoenix Emperor. The religion of New Estorias, [[The Paragon of Rebirth]], believes that Emperor Hixs is a living God, sent by the heavens to guide them and mandated by heaven to rule. The population of New Estorias is largely Humans, Fire Genasi, Tieflings, Beastfolk, and Orcs / Goblins.

---

### [[Xerathos]]

The evolution of Calixos, a Plutocracy where the rich owners of the massive mithril, silver, and gold mines rule the nation. Largely they are dwarven kings who hold great hordes beneath mountains. Easily the richest kingdom due to the powerful and ancient Mintherals, monsters that create and populate the mines and ground of Xerathi mountains with gold and other ores.
