# Pokemon Campaign Ideas

## Brainstorm

- Party are new trainers in a program to earn a scholarship to study aboard in other regions, given a starter and pokedex
- 2 requirements of the program are to compete in the gym challenge, and work on the pokedex
- There are no strict requirements, but the program is judged by Professor juniper, meaning if she feels you did not succeed at the end, you will not get the scholarship, so work hard at both
- Main bad guys, Team Helix. Led by Colress, team helix is researching genetic enhancement of Pokemon, creating wild and rampaging alpha-like Pokemon, which are dangerous.
	- Colress does this enhancement using a modified gene splicer from black and white 2. His ultimate goal is to use the genetic enhancer on Kyurem to recreate the Original Dragon Pokemon. he is searching for the final piece of DNA needed to mutate Kyurem, and realizes it needs genetic material from beyond earth, from Deoxys.
	- Colress is researching Pokemon enhancement through mega evolution, gigantimax, and alpha. This results in his discovery of a 3rd ancient stone, the Ice Stone. This stone contains Kyurem's essence they way light and dark stones contained Reshiram and Zekrom. Colress wants to use the stone combined with his genetic enhancer to create Primal Kyurem.
- Team Helix:
	- *Strand*: Splicer Expert. 
	- *Locus*: Team Enforcer. 
	- *Colress*: Team Leader / Scientist. 
- Rivals:
	- *Axio*: Confident to the point of arrogance, naturally gifted, well connected, and always a step ahead of the party in the early game. Professor Juniper's young cousin.
	- *Jun*: Trainer who was raised in the original Plasma, was around for the Plasma Frigate incident, and now runs rescue and rehabilitation work for abandoned and mistreated Pokémon. Wren isn’t in the scholarship, but keeps crossing paths with the party, often one step ahead of them at a crisis site. Member of Plasma Remnant, a group of reformed former Team Plasma members.
- The Perfect Enhancement: One Pokemon that was enhanced with the splicer is not dangerous, and has great power while retaining its nature. The party finds it and has to decide whether to turn it in or keep it.
