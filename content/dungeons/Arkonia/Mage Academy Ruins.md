## Entrance Puzzle: The Mages Two

TBD

*The Door's Riddle.* The Door reads in ancient text: "All who seek to enter hear, the secrets within are held dear, tarry not to take nor steal, but to learn with righteous zeal. Alone magic cannot be done, for in towers like ours, two are stronger than one. Stand atop your perches high, and raise your staves blazing with light. Those who take these words as true, secrets of the mage open to you."

*Puzzle Answer.* A mage must stand at the top of each tower beside the bridge with a light, and hold it up high. This will open the door to the ancient magic academy.
