
An underground club operated by the Blue Dragon Eythora and her Red Dragonborn ally Calliope Windfang.

## Entrance and Guardian

The entrance to the club is an underground temple. Dark red light emanates from the steps that descend into the thumping music. As you approach you see heavily armored Hobgoblins in tinted lens glasses, they quickly surround you, the largest among them standing eye level with Elysia. 
The little ones grunt and stare angrily, while the leader lowers his shades and stares you down. 

"Unfortunately for you, you don't have wristbands, and you aren't on the list. Gonna have to ask you to... taste my sword bitch!" The Hobgoblin makes a longsword attack, and you all need to roll initiative!

**Enemies:** 1x [[Hobgoblin Warlord 2024.png]], 2x [[Hobgoblin Captain 2024.png]], 5x [[Hobgoblin Warrior 2024.png]]

## Puzzle/Roleplaying Challenge: The Dance Floor

As you enter the club you see the entire room is a moving living dance floor. There are strange glowing runes on the floor as magical lights strobe across the ceilings. Women hang and dance from silk tied above in the darkness. The entire room is stuffed with people, strange winding dances that are uniform almost as if they are in a trance. 
Along the wall to your side is a triple decker bar where people happily engage in drugs and drinks. Not a care in the world as they partake in Traxgate's splendor.
A large door at the back wall is sealed shut with magic, and it beckons to you, as if that is the path forward. A sign nailed to the door says, "*THE REAL ARENA: VIP*"
There is a faint pattern in the dancing, and when studied you see a brief opening, long enough for those of you whom can move across the entire dance floor (70ft) to cross before the path is closed again.

*Dance Floor.* The dance floor can be crossed with 70ft a movement speed, flown over, or made checks to get through. Those who cross by dancing or movement must roll a luck check, if they fail the luck check they hit a rune on the dance floor causing an explosion of lights and disorienting themselves. (3d6 psychic damage and disadvantage on ranged attacks for 1 hour.)
- DC 16 Performance Check: To dance through with the crowd.

*The Bar.* Talking to the bartender reveals that everyone here is under the spell of Windfang, the Singing Dragon. Her magic is so powerful it removes all worry and fight from the souls of her opponents. The drinks help patrons accept the magic, which is desired by many warriors who cannot escape their nature.
