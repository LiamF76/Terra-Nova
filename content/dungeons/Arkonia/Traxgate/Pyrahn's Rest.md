## Entrance: The Wax Watchers

The halls of this place are ancient yet beautiful, the stones are crawling with moss and thick globs of wax, time having taken control of this place. It looks as if there has not been any mortal in this place for nearly a century. You can see faint paint beneath the vines, as if there are images covered being the overgrowth.

**Enemies:** 1x [[elemental evoker golem.png]], 1x TBD, 6x candle soldier ([[skeleton.png]])

*Murals.* Beneath the vines you can see ornate murals painted on flat slabs of stone, depicting an beautiful Aelarian woman carrying a winged boy's body into the catacombs walking within a thin beam of light. She lays him low in his grave, plucking his wings and affixing them to her ears as earrings. The mural continues along the walls, as she places a kiss upon the boys forehead, and wings grow on her back of golden feathers, carrying her across the sky, always within the sun's light.

## Puzzle/Roleplaying Challenge: Walk in the Light

The room is coated in a thick blanket of darkness. A single beam of light shines from the dais in the entrance, piercing through the doorway and hitting the back wall of the room. before you is a tall pillar with a hovering glass mirror about 4ft off the ground. The light creates a path through the room that is barely 5ft wide.

*Magic Darkness.* The darkness coves every inch of the room not touched by the beam of light. Anyone who enters it or starts their turn there takes 2d12 necrotic damage.

*Glass Mirrors.* The glass mirrors can be used to direct the light to create a path through the darkness, point one around the room until you see another mirror and hit that one. (DC 16 Investigation check) Eventually create a walkable path to cross the room.

## Trick or Setback: The Watcher Prism

In a vaulted ceiling room with ornate carved pillars bearing the images of a stunning Aelarian woman there hovers a shining blue prism. It is an octahedron, its edges are glowing yellow with a shining blue interior. It spins slowly, and you can feel it watching you as you enter.

The Prism asks them questions.
- Why do you deserve to wield Illuman's light?
- What sin hides in your shadow?
- If you stood in full truth before your allies, what would they condemn you for?
- What would you sacrifice to complete your quest?
- Do you seek power in places like this for others... or yourself?

If they answer dishonestly it will shock them for 3d6 lightning damage. It will remind them that only it can open the door, so if they attack it they will never move forwards. 
"Violence is not the answer to every problem. I am one of those problems."

*Golden Wings.* Above the prism, hanging from the ceiling 50ft above, is a tiny pair of golden wings. They are hanging from a single golden thread. They are made of soldi gold and shine with a wax finish in the golden light of the prism.

*Wax Trap.* When you grab the wings you can feel they are coated with a thin layer of wax, it glistens but has an undertone of shadow, a powerful magic is connected to the coating. "Flew too close to the sun did you?" As you hold them in your hand you hear a faint sizzle and pop, pale wax begins to pour from beneath the cracks in the ceiling. Everyone make a DC 17 DEX saving throw, taking 3d6 fire damage on a failure and half as much on a success. On a failure you are also restrained.

*Purify the Wings.* Cast Remove Curse or melt the wax coating on the golden wings to stop the trap.

*Return the Wings.* Placing the wings again on the golden thread stops the trap. DC 14 Slight of hand check to tie them to the thread.

## Climax, Big Battle, or Conflict: The Wax Lord

The final room is a beautiful grand cathedral. The walls are lined with flowing paintings of red orange waves like fire pouring over shores of wax. The entire room is decorated in ornate depictions of flying angels and crying elves trapped on the ground. The entire floor of the room is solid maroon wax.
As you enter, a candle person clad in red robes raises a flaming staff high above his head, and pools of wax on the ground congeal into soldiers and a large golem before you. He slams his staff down and they lock eyes with you, ready to attack, roll initiative!

**Enemies:** 1x [[aelon, last light of pyranth.png]], 1x [[candle colossus.png]], 2x [[Elemental Cultist.png]]

*Wax Floor.* Aelon lights his staff and fires burning balls of fire across the room. The floor begins to melt and bubble. Everyone who starts their turn or enters the non dais floor takes 3d6 fire damage, or half as much on a successful DC 16 DEX save. Anyone who fails the save is also restrained. The floor becomes difficult terrain, but not for candle constructs.
