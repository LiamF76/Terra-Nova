## Puzzle/ Roleplaying Challenge: The Ruin Forge

TBD

**Enemies:** 

*The Forge Hammer.* A massive iron block that is lifted and slammed down with metal portcullis on either side, separating the party into two sides of the battlefield. South side statue with controls can move the portcullis' upwards allowing seeing into the other side. Using anvil on north side can lift the iron block.

*The Hammer and Anvil.* The beautiful adamantine anvil is untouched by time. On it sits a small hammer, its handle covered in moss, though it is not from age, this tool was made from the fusion of nature, magic, and science. It begs to be used, to be slammed down on the anvil as it was many centuries ago. Holding it in your hand imparts a deep sense of magical power, but it is fading. This tool has only one use left in it.
Using the hammer on the anvil lifts the iron block permanently.

*The Statue.* A statue of an Aelarian woman dressed in ornate robes paired with strange magical augments. Her arm has been replaced by a magical stone one, connected with flowing tendrils of roots and mana, similar to the golems that now inhabit these ruins. At her feet are a set of controls, buttons levers and dials that you don't quite understand. The one thing that is clear is the massive button with a gate symbol beside it. 
This button causes the portcullis' to lift up and slam down at the start of the activator's next turn.

## Trap: Moss Floor Garden

The air is thick with the scent of damp earth and wildflowers as you step into the ruined courtyard. Before you lies a wide, circular space, its cracked stone tiles buried beneath a thick carpet of emerald moss. In the center is a 20ft wide circular fountain, still flowing with water from the top. The basin is filled with flower petals and branches.
A bird hops along the moss and stops on the ledge, sipping from the fountain as a pair of squirrels dart across the space, each grabbing a stick before skittering back into the brush around the fountain and out a small hole in the bottom of the walls around you.

The hall to continue through is on the opposite side of the room.

*Moss Pit Traps.* The moss floor is a pit trap, which covers the entire area. It is soft moss which is sturdy enough for animals, but collapses under the weight of people. Everyone who falls in takes 3d6 bludgeoning damage and falls 30ft into the pit.

*Ivy Walls.* The walls of the pit are covered in ivy and vines, tugging on them indicates they are very strong, easily able to support you climbing out.
The walls are actually sentient Ivy, and when climbed they attack for 2d4 piercing damage, causing you to either drop or take damage for every 10ft you climb up.
