
OVERVIEW: This dungeon is Evander's Chronovault where he researched ways to survive replicant decay after betraying Kairos, believing himself to be perfect and their play to destroy the world foolish. Why destroy when they can rule as a perfect being?
He survived by becoming a Reforged, a process he invented. The problem was his defense system, AEGIS, which is an AI based on a beholder he captured. Aegis harbored anger, and when Evander became a Reforged Aegis used what remained of its power to plant a similar system it was under, making Evander beholden to the defense system, and vice versa. They were both trapped there, until the party shows up and activates the defense system, which in turn awakens Evander, without freeing him.
