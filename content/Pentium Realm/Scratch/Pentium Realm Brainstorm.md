## Ideas Dump

- Pentium realm is a high tech / sci-fi future in which humanity has transitioned to a digital existence through uploading 
- Setting inspirations: Pantheon, Cyberpunk 2077, real life
- There are vastly less people due to historical events, climate collapse and war and such, so a smaller population of a few million people in the digital world
- Players are first generation of humans born digitally, through a process of combining the code of 2 uploaded people to create a new being.
- Players created by a company called Aeneas Industries the project was known as 'Gemini Forge'
- **Aeneas Industries**: A hardware company, they use robots and physical infrastructure to distribute processing powers and abilities for a price to people. Founded by *Marcus Aeneas*. Aeneas Industries blazed the frontier of computer intelligence in the 2050s, and was at the forefront of the Uploading campaigns to save humanity. They built the digital world uploaded minds now inhabit. 
- *Abel Kestrel*: The last human. Abel was a violent environmentalist and extremist who believed the uploading and computerization to be anti human. He survived the attempt of Marcus to wipe out humanity, and as the last human he went to the central data center is the former United States and uploaded a deadly virus, this virus created the Daemons (the monsters of this world) and shattered the algorithms Madison Kim created, corrupting the world. This is the central conflict of this tabletop world
	- **The Virus**: 
- *Madison Kim*: Wife of Marcus Aeneas, she developed the procedural generation algorithms and designed the digital world of Pentium Realm. She build Aion, and all of the digital world alongside Marcus at Aeneas Industries.
- *[[Marcus Aeneas]]*: Born named Marcus Telos, he changed his name to Aeneas. Inspired Virgil's Aeneid. He  has hidden himself away in a private partition to work on and lead Project: Gemini Forge, as his next step in humanity's evolution. 
	- *Flight from Troy*: He led humanity from the earth into the digital world. He destroyed everything that remained of non-uploaded humans in secret, and sees himself as the shepherd of humanity.
	- *Queen Dido*: Marcus was consumed by the idea of entirely digital minds, the next evolution of humanity, creating humans inside the Pentium Realm. His wife opposed this, and he forced her out of Project: Gemini Forge, abandoning her. Losing her husband and life's work to Marcus's madness caused her to commit suicide.
	- *Founding of Lavinium*: Marcus speaks with an artifact of his father's mind, the first uploaded mind. It was not successful, but his father guides him towards cleansing dissenters. Marcus initiates a launch of many nations nuclear weapons in secret, ending the last of humanity on earth without any of the digital minds knowing. The cover story is climate disaster. Marcus builds Aion, the great city of the Pentium Realm.
- **Endymion**: Home of Project: Gemini Forge, and where Marcus Aeneas hides. Located on a Lunar data center built by Aeneas Industries. 
- Marcus is obsessed with Greek mythology, believing himself to be Prometheus. Stealing fire from the Gods and creating digital souls. He wants to be the father of a new race of purely digital minds.
- **An Empty World**: much of the the world was built and networked for a gigantic population of humans, with so many dead before they could upload, there are vast wastelands and empty digital spaces to explore
- **The Digital Frontier**: most of the digital world wasn't hand crafted but generated through procedural systems, simulation engines, and abandoned software left running without oversight. Over decades the processes kept evolving, so there are strange alien regions and corrupted areas
- Crypto Miners that actually mine crypto coins in digital mineshafts, backbreaking manual labor, bottom of the food chain work
- **NULLs**: Uploaded minds who have lost their personality, just code mind slaves. Usually done by companies when people cannot afford to pay off debt or commit cybercrime. Their personality is removed or deleted leaving them company property, a husk of a person.
- **Sanguine Code**: old uploaded minds who were uploaded before the process was perfected, they feed on other people to steal their code and processes to survive, this usually kills those fed upon.
- **Aion**: The city of eternal life. Built by Aeneas Industries to house the minds of the future in the digital world, Aion is the only city in Pentium Realm. There are strict social classes based around who has good processing power and hardware. Ranges from sprawling slums of filth to beautiful gardens of plenty.
- **Daemons**: Malicious programs / AIs. "Monsters" are daemons, they come in many forms but are generally unintelligent
- Sheriffs / law enforcement who are just antivirus / security program AIs.
	- The Defender: Windows Defender, not very effective, often mistakes trusted programs for daemons, and vice versa.
	- McAfee: snake oil salesman, corrupt, makes deals with adware and malware daemons
- *Madison's Margins*: 
	- **Unpopulated territory.** Kim designed the world for billions, and only a few million remain, so the generators keep building into empty space.
	- **Corrupted Kim terrain.** The last human’s virus shattered her algorithms, so the Margins are where generation went wrong: landscapes that are beautiful but broken, structures that don’t obey their own logic, regions that drift or reconnect.
	- **Daemon country.** Since the virus created the Daemons, the Margins are where they are densest and where the corruption is still spreading.
	- **Still occasionally hers.** Some regions may be untouched or partly fixed, places where Kim’s original intent survives through the damage. Explorers call these "signed ground."
- *Companies*:
	- **Aeneas Industries**: The megacorp that develops and sells hardware and software upgrades to uploaded minds, and runs the digital birth program that creates new people. Maintains the physical servers on the frozen Earth that keep everyone alive.
	- **Wayfinder Company**: Independent frontier outfit that escorts explorers, surveyors, and caravans through unstable and corrupted regions. Sells maps of the shifting *Madison's Margins*. Small, underfunded, and well liked, with a reputation for bringing clients home.
	- **Kernel Defense**: PMC, high budget private military that does security and bounty hunting work. Largest PMC in Pentium Realm.
	- **Latency Zero**: Data transport company, does shipment of packets and information cargo across the Pentium Realm.
	- **Checksum Foundation**: Smaller PMC that does working hunting corrupted minds, Daemons, and Sanguine Code. Think digital monster hunters.
