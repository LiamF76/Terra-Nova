### What is Pentium Realm?

==Pentium Realm is a fractured digital world born from video games, where college players are periodically pulled into games made real. Each of you enters as yourself, but wielding the powers, abilities, and personas of your in‑game characters. The realm is overseen by HELIX, an AI obsessed with optimizing performance at any cost.==

---

### Who are you?

This part is easy. You are yourself! You play as you in real life, a college student at Concord University, attending classes, going to esports practice, and making late night sheetz runs.

There is a sense of Dual-Identity, because while you are yourself in real life, within the Pentium Realm you play as your in-game avatar. Example: *Liam* vs. **maiL**. These identities may have vastly different levels of ego, skill, and abilities, depending on the characters you draw power from in your player development journey!

Outside of game choices such as social interactions, classes, and personal hobbies will affect the game, and vice-versa!

`Note: For in-game abilities and powers within the Pentium Realm, flavor and homebrew are strongly encouraged. You will select 1 character from a video game title you wish to draw your power from, such as Tracer from Overwatch or Sage from Valorant.` 
`Please feel free to find created content online that aligns with your vision for the character you draw power from, or create you own homebrew and clear it with me for balance.`

### What do we actually do?

- Compete inside corrupted game-realities
- Explore the digital world, discovering interesting characters and mind-bending locations
- Navigate college life and esports pressure IRL
- Compete in matches and tournaments, and build your collegiate esports legend
- Shape the future of Pentium Realm through choices, not just wins
