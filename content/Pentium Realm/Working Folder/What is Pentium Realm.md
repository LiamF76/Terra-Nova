# Pentium Realm

## The World

I have an idea for a tabletop setting / campaign based around computers and video games. Specifically my friends being pulled into their computers and gaining powers based on their chosen video game characters. In a near future where advanced augmented / virtual reality video gaming has become deeply ingrained in daily life. 
People are paid to do jobs, play as college / semi-pro players, and spend much of their time within the Pentium Realm.
