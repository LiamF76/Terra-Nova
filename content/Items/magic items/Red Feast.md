Name: Red Feast, Blade of the Blood Princess

Rarity/Attunement: Rare (Scimitar), Yes

"The queen needed an executioner, a loyal follower who would slay her enemies, tear them limb from limb, and I needed dinner." - Scarlett Rex

Description: A blood red Scimitar that when drawn hisses softly like a vampire. Its hilt is cold to the touch and its blade is jagged on the back edge, giving it an imposing look. It is permanently stained each time it draws blood from an enemy.

Effects: Red Feast is a +1 Scimitar, while attuned to it you gain the following effects:
- This weapon deals an additional 1d6 necrotic damage.
- You can inflict 1d4 necrotic damage, cutting yourself with the blade, to make it a +2 weapon for 1 minute.
- Once per long rest, you can force an enemy hit with this weapon to make a DC 16 CON saving throw, on a failure all healing they receive is halved for 1 hour.
