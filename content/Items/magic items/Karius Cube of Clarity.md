Name: *Karius Cube of Clarity*

Rarity/Attunement: Rare, Yes

"If you want something done right, do it yourselves." - Karius Bontell

Description: A simple white and gold mechanical cube, it bears a glass circle button in the center of one side, which can be depressed inwards to activate the cube.

Effects: While Attuned to this magic item you can cast the spell identify as a ritual. When doing so through the cube the identify spell operates using the knowledge and personality of Karius Bontell. The spell functions normally in addition to adding Karius' knowledge and expertise to the analysis. You can cast the spell on more than magical objects including pieces of history, locations, and mundane items.
When the spell is cat, press the glass button and hold the cube, an image of Karius will appear above the glass as a specter, and dictate his understanding of the target to the user.
