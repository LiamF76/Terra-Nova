Name: The Celestial Astrolabe, The Key to Beyond

Rarity/Attunement: Legendary, No

"I do not dream of worlds beyond, I see them, I walk in them, and soon you shall to, all you need do is close your eyes and walk."

Description: A flowing glowing sphere of glass containing within a strange entity of the cosmos, as f the whole universe was shrunk and shoved inside. It is perfectly smooth and always warm to the touch, there is not a single imperfection on its hard glass exterior. 

Effects: While holding this Astrolabe:
- The Astrolabe glows brightly when celestial beings are near, and darkens for Abyssal creatures. (This includes artifacts of sentient nature from either origin.)
- Over the course of 1 minute you can discern the location of the nearest Infinite Corridor on your current plane. While doing so you must sit and meditate with the artifact, focusing entirely on it and unable to perceive through your other senses while doing so.
- While at an entrance to the Infinite Corridor, you can activate the orb while holding it over the course of 2 actions, causing the doorway to open. Once entering the doorway the Corridor takes the form of a hallway you are familiar with, each doorway is labeled with he name of a plane that you are aware of and its nature. Only planes that you are aware of have present doorways.
- Entering a doorway allows you to travel to the chosen plane, where you roll a d100 upon entry:
	- 1-50: Nothing happens, you enter at a random Corridor gateway or a position of your choosing if you have been there previously.
	- 51-75: You appear at a random location no matter if you have been there before or not.
	- 76-99: Same as the previous effect but you also take two levels of exhaustion.
	- 100: You are transported to a random other plane.
