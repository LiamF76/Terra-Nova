Name: *Varkul*, The Worborn Heart

Rarity/Attunement: 

"Kneel or be crushed beneath my heel." - Varkul the Kneeling Tyrant

Description: This petrified, still-beating heart was torn from **Varkul the Kneeling Tyrant** when he pledged his soul to Joriel, the Abyssal God of Conquest. The heart pulses with raw fury, beating faster in times of bloodshed.

Effects: To attune to this item you must ingest it in its entirety, every drop of burning hot blood, and every piece of beating red muscle and flesh. Upon doing so you gain the following effects:
- **Warlord's Vitality**. Your hit point maximum increases by 25.
- **Feast upon their Despair**. Once per long rest you gain temporary HP equal to half the damage dealt with a melee attack.
- **Fearless Carnage**. You have advantage on saving throws against being frightened or charmed.
- **Endless Advance**. You cannot move in any direction that does not lead you to an enemy during combat.
- **Warlord of Joriel**. If you die while attuned to the Warborn Heart, your body **rises after 24 hours** as a **Frenzied Abyssal Champion** under Joriel’s control. Your mind is lost, but your soul fights forever in his name.
