Name: Frostbound Gauntlet

Rarity/Attunement: Very Rare (*Wondrous Item*), Yes

"If the world shall be cold, then let it be cold, and I shall turn it all to ice and watch as it shatters." - Rosalind Frostshadow

Description: This chilling gauntlet is crafted from enchanted ice and replaces the user’s left arm upon attunement. When worn, the Frostbound Gauntlet fuses seamlessly to the wielder, empowering them with icy might while requiring the sacrifice of their arm. While wearing the gauntlet you are always cold to the touch.

Effects: In order to attune to this item you must fulfill the following requirements:
- Place the gauntlet on your left arm and allow the ice to seep into and destroy the arm beneath. You become attuned to the item after the course of 3 days as your natural arm is removed.
While attuned to this item you gain the following effects:
- **Frozen Focus**: This item is a +3 spellcasting focus. 
- **Icy Strike**: When you hit with a melee weapon attack, you deal an additional 1d8 cold damage.
- **Frozen Shield**: You can cast the _shield_ spell once per long rest without using a spell slot. When cast, a shimmering shield of ice forms around you, granting the usual benefits of the spell and dealing 1d6 cold damage to the attacker if this spell is cast in reaction to a melee attack. This effect only applies once.
- **Cursed Bond**: The Frostbound Gauntlet binds itself to your body. Once attuned, removing the gauntlet requires a _remove curse_ spell or similar magic, and you will need to undergo magical healing or prosthesis to regain functionality in your left arm.
