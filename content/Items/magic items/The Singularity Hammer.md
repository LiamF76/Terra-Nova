Name: Singularity Hammer

Rarity/Attunement: Very Rare (*Warhammer*), Yes 

"In the event horizon I did not see the end of all things, but the beginning. An infinite possibility of everything all at once. Every person, destiny, and soul, except her." - Unknown

Description: 

Effects: While attuned to the hammer you gain the following effects:
- **Aelarian Silver**. The hammer is a +2 weapon.
- **Gravity Well**. When you hit a creature with the Singularity Hammer, you can use a bonus action to unleash a gravitational pull. The target and all creatures within 15 feet of it must succeed on a Strength saving throw (DC = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Strength modifier) or be pulled 10 feet closer to the target. If the target would collide with you, they instead take damage equal to your strength modifier.
- **Event Horizon**. As a reaction to being hit by an attack, you can activate the hammer's event horizon effect. Until the start of your next turn, ranged attacks against you have disadvantage, and any creature that ends its turn within 10 feet of you must succeed on a Strength saving throw (DC = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Strength modifier) or take 1d8 force damage from the gravitational forces around you. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Strength modifier (minimum of 1), regaining all uses after a long rest.
