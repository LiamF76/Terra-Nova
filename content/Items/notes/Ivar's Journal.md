
27th October, Year 30301, Third Age
Training progresses well, Zyrathia tells me the scar will soon open, and my augmentations can begin. Catherine is disloyal at best, and my reliance on her to survive must be rectified if Arkonil is to survive, should Catherine need to be removed. My skill with the blade has progressed father than I ever believed I was capable. As much as I despise its necessity, I would like to test myself against a worthy opponent soon. 
The witch is powerful, she alters my perception daily, I know it. I pretend I don't notice, I believe it keeps me sane, things are changing, her surgeries are painful, but necessary. She plans to remake my body, enhance its strength to get me as close to the level I had attained before my injury at the siege of Terraven. That fucking child Augustus, how dare he lay his blade in my shoulder, but I cannot lie that it hurt. He may just be their Lightbearer. I have fought many men, and many things worse than men, but nothing like him. He was not my equal, but it was like facing ten of his in a single moment, attacks from every direction, the sting of his blade in my skin, I remember it well. I will not forget.
Kor Goroth is ever the loyal hound. It is a strange thing to think that I, a boy from a farm, is now the leader of the armies of the Abyss, but such is the winding and volatile nature of the Fates. I should like to speak to them, perhaps there is still a chance for me. If there is a way to heal, to fix what has been done, they will know.
Liana and the child will arrive soon, they are being sent as discreetly as possible. She would not submit to a portal by Zyrathia, she says she fears the witch. I do not blame her. I would never force her, so I sent my best men to guard her. It would take a large force of Adrithyr's men to stop them, and they do not know she is coming. I just pray that my afflictions, and her travel, do not harm the child. I say I pray, but I don't really pray to anyone in particular anymore. Nobody answered before, I suppose I should pray to myself, Ivar the Reckoner!

I gave Catherine a child. My second child. I had so recently lost my first but it was our deal, and as much as I despise myself for it I had to keep it. How terrible I must be, that the best thing I could do for an evil vile woman is give her a child of my blood. Is more of me really so bad? What happened to me... what have I let myself become? Am I still me, or anyone at all? Where is the boy that dreamed of a family? Where is the family I began? What have I done...


4th December, Year 30302, Third Age
I feel like I am dragging myself, my own life, along by the thinnest thread. Deals with devils, vampirism, child rearing pacts with blood sorcerers, its nothing worth what Ferran did for me. He kept me alive made me a man. I've betrayed his memory. Was I worth it? 
It does not matter how slowly you go as long as you keep going. That's what he always told me. I don't think this is what he meant. I'm sorry old man. I'm sorry. I's so sorry.
I had hoped he would forgive me, but Zyrathia has promised me an eternal reward, that when I die I will fade into Arkonil, into nothing. There is no afterlife for men like me. I will never see Ferran, I will never get to apologize for letting him down. I will never see my wife and child again, and I don't deserve to.
I snapped at a servant today. The shame I felt was deep. I was a servant once, who am I to demand things of them, as if I am their better? Ferran used to say a great man is hard on himself; a small man is hard on others. I was a small man today. She is a kind girl, spilling tea should not leave you fearing for your life. I've asked my steward to console her, and bring her a moderate sum of coins. The very least I can do for her. I can imagine the fear, a man in a crown, his terrible power emanating across the room, making your hands shake and knees wobble. I remember it well from when I met my father. I hope she at least can forgive me, before the end. To be forgiven by even on soul for my actions, what a blessing that would be.


10th December, Year 30302, Third Age
My astrologers and mages tell me the declaration of a new age is coming. The age of the Broken World they call it. A scar across the face of Terra-Nova, a scar I brought them. I have ripped from the very flesh of the world, and it felt good. It felt good to exact a toll on the mortals who betrayed me... or is that Arkonil speaking? Are we separate anymore? The lines blur so deeply in these recent days. I have grown I know it. Zyrathia lies, she hides her true face from me and shows it to her dark god while I sleep. They clothe me in larger garb, but I know there have been changes, my muscles, my very being swells with the Reckoner's power. She has pulled my veins from my flesh, sharpened my very bones. I am more weapon than man. 
I fear that if I could see my children I could not hold them, for my skin would burn and corrupt them. I hope they are nothing like me. They should have the capacity to forgive, to let go like I never could, or maybe they will hate me as I hate my father, and so turns the wheel. I should like to see the Fourth Age, the Third was the shortest in history, I hope my age of terror is short too. Let Arkonil raze the land, and the gods fall at his blade.


19th December, Year 30302, Third Age
I had always wanted a father. I wanted someone who loved me, someone who could teach me to be strong, to be a hero. I had been desperate, and when I met Adrithyr I knew that he would never be what I needed. I wallowed in my rage, how could he have done that to my mother, didn't he love me, love us? But then I realized I had a father. Ferran. He loved me. He put a sword in my hand and taught me to stand for what was right. I killed him for it. I had a father, and I killed him. I don't even know what's left of me anymore. Is there still a soul in this body? Am I still me? I was something to my wife, to my child, to Ferran, now I'm nothing but a husk for Arkonil. A tool for Zyrathia's modifications. I died a long time ago. Only Arkonil remains. We are the Reckoner.


27th December, Year 30302, Third Age
Now that I look upon his face, the anger, the hate, it's almost unbearable. He sits in my dungeons, the object of my rage. I could reach out and touch him, tear out his heart, Arkonil thirsts, we thirst for his blood. Our pact was signed in the deep rage of men beyond reason, but now I wish for some semblance of justice. I want him to deserve what I will give him, in the eyes of the people that I swore to protect, at least until I fall to the Godkiller completely. 
Adrithyr, my father, the Red King. His blood is that of the dragon, the same as mine. The blood I so despise that flows through me. I cannot comprehend the vile soul he harbors, the willingness to kill his children. Me, the little twins, the tens of babies, innocent of his sins, and he killed every last one. How much greed can one have? To take my siblings, my mother, my wife and child? When does it end? There can be no more kings like him, because as long as there are kings like him, the world will need men like me.


30th December, Year 3032, Third Age
I often find myself dreaming, yet not sleeping. Its as if my eyes close and Arkonil's open, he wakes and I drift from my form. I see my child, my beloved, I see a life I tore myself from with my desire for revenge. I dream of that night on the balcony, when I asked Celestia-Shield to join me, to finally break the cycle that we are all trapped in, I dream she said yes, I dream this was all over. I see him in my dreams, he pokes his eyes into my mind though he does not know it, perhaps he is here now, perhaps I am a dream. A dream of a son who hates his father, a dream of a son who loved him without knowing him. The Timekeeper, his eyes are everywhere, open in shadow, seeing those truths that we cannot even admit to ourselves. The little White Wizard thinks he is free, he is a tool. Even Arkonil knows he plans something. The Reckoner's blade remains sheathed while in the presence of the God in Glass. He sees right through you they say, I have felt it. I feel you White Wizard, what were you searching for? My weaknesses? None to speak of, for I am the rebel, the Uncrowned, I was better than most as a man, I am more terrible than any other as the Reckoner. If you are listening: I am waiting for you, come and face me.
Yet another one of my ramblings. My mind sees things that are not there. Zyrathia will replace them too soon enough. A means to an end.

Catherine is dead. My body is crippled, tearing itself against the weight of staying alive. Arkonil stays my breath for a moment longer. All I need is a moment. I will dispense justice on my father, and I will face the Wolves of Death. Erebos sends his hounds, they call my name, I can feel the cold hand of the reaper. I pray for them, they will need it. I will not die easily. I am the World Ender, I am the God Killer, I am the Reckoner. I cast this journal to the winds, let their mortal hands catch and hold it tightly. Come mortals, face me, I will carve your limbs, I will shatter your souls, and your blood will the staircase that I climb to heaven as I sunder all that is and will be. I do not believe I will write again. There is nothing left to say but this, the ancient chant of the dark cults that Lazarus fought against, my last prayer:
Hark, cometh the Reckoner, his is the Blade of Blood and Shadow, look upon him and despair.
