The gods took their power and created items to give to those they named their champions. This gives the people who wield them part of their divine power and a deep connection to that god beyond any cleric or paladins. To be a god's champion is the greatest honor in the minds of the devout and you become similar to a living god. The first champion named was Lazarus the Reborn, Champion of [[Vulcan]].
