Humanity falls into lower magic medieval society and out of kinship with the Dwarves and Elves. This resulted in a far more primitive society and lacking in military power they were forced back into Escarth from their other territories. This resulted in the formation of new nations in the aftermath, Stormfjord (a kingdom of viking like raider warriors practicing tribal government), Veredian, and Artoria.
