The Elven Oracle Hanaria has a vision of a war across time and sees the sun rise and set a hundred times in a single day. This is the battle between [[Balthazaar]] and [[Illuman]], where a hero emerges. It is said they will wield a weapon born in the Astral plane, and their fingers of starlight hand will determine the fate of the world. “A body of Iron, a wild heart, and fingers of starlight”.
