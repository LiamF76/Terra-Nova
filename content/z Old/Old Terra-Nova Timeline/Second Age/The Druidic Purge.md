In an act of fear and prejudice the people of Escarth enact a purge against the Druids of Tena, (wrongly) believing them to be responsible for the fall of the great kingdoms and the start of the dark age. The Hyperion Church led them to believe their practice of nature magic brought about the collapse. Few druids survive and enter into hiding or assume false identities to keep their order alive.
