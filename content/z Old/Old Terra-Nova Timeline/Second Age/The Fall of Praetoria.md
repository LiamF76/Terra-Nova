The Human kingdom of Praetoria collapses into civil war across Escarth over the crown of Arkonil. Many rulers and warlords rise up and attempt to seize the crown and its power for themselves. This fractures human society and destroys their prosperous civilization. This is partly due to the collapse of the Inheritor Pact. The wizard orders fracture and fall to the conflict. The artifacts are lost to the ages.
