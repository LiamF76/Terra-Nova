In a battle between gods, A long forgotten god now known as [[Arkonil]] and his allies waged war against the other gods. He along with the Gods Voracia and Korvus, believed that [[Illuman]] was unfit to lead the Heavens in [[Shao Daraan]]'s absence, and Arkonil wanted to rule himself. Arkonil is known to have slain Oteus (God of Mercy) and many other lesser gods, this is why there are few gods remaining today. They became known as the [[The Betrayer Gods]] and were banished to the Abyss.
