The Elder God [[Shao Daraan]] gives shape to the energies of the universe, creating the planet Terra-Nova and everything on it including the races of Dwarves and Aelarian Elves. He invites the Precursors, a race of raw sentient astral energy, to join his creation and become the [[Gods of Terra-Nova]]. Most refuse, but those that agree become the deities we know today. The world is young and the [[Gods of Terra-Nova]] are very close to their children. After completing creation Shao Daraan enters a deep sleep within Nova Magnus.
