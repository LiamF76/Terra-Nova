The continent of Atlas, home of the Aelarians, suffers from tectonic plate movement, and is doomed to fall into the sea. The Aelarians ask one of the Titans to stand beneath the city and uphold it on the ocean, the Titan Genesar agrees, and uses his strength ho hold up the continent for many years.
