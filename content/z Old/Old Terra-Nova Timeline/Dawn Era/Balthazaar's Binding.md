The Night God [[Balthazaar]] attempts to devour the sun showing his dominance over [[Illuman]]. A coalition of gods band together to stop him and save the world from the Night Serpent's grasp. Illuman battles him with the power of light, Fulgrim smites him down from the heavens and sky into the sea, Mira creates the great riptide that pulls him down to the depths of the ocean, Iros forges the celestial chains that bind him, Tena gives her life to power and strengthen the chains, [[Aton]] grabs the chains and uses his speed to pull [[Balthazaar]] to the very deepest part of the Abyss at the edge of non-existence.
