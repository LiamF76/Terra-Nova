The Titans use their immense power to wage war upon the [[Gods of Terra-Nova]]. This war devastates the land, creating the hills, valleys, and oceans as we know them today. The Titans are defeated but it leads to the destruction of Atlas and causes it to sink into the depths of the ocean. Aelarian society collapses and the dark age begins. The celestial gates were destroyed or lost their power during this war. Eventually the Titans are defeated by an alliance between Illuman and the Ancient Dragons.
