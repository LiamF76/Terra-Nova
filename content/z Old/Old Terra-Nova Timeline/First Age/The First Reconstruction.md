After the cataclysms of the Titan war and the Reckoning, the remaining Aelari migrated from their sinking city of Atlas to the land of Ikon to the east. They intermingled with the other younger species there and formed a new race/civilization that became known as Elves. This began the formation of the oldest remaining nation in Terra-Nova, Agamarth.
