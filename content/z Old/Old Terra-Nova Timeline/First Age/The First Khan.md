A Dark-Elf named Hara with strength and wisdom beyond his years campaigns across the deserts of Drok Shah, uniting his people through politics and war. He unites the people of his nation and they name him "Al Ibn- Al Mawt", the Son of Death. He founds a great city on a cluster of Oasis, calling it "Akmu", The Emerald Oasis. He becomes the first King of Drok Shah and takes the name Hara Khan.
