The Elven Oracle Hanaria has a vision of a golden haired King who comes from the West, leading all who will follow him to paradise and defeating the evil [[Balthazaar]] once and for all. It is said that the King will be a Demi-God, a son of [[Illuman]] and carry the light of the sun with him as he walks the world.
