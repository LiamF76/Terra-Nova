A series of  bloody and brutal wars between kingdoms small and large in Escarth leads to the unification of the continent south of the Blade Mountains. This new empire becomes known as Estor and is ruled by the ancient bloodline of Realmcrest leading to Adrithyr Realmcrest in the present day.
