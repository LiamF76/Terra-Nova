Ivar the Uncrowned as he came to be called, made a proclamation in front of the people of Terraven, making the alleged crimes of Adrithyr known to the people. Ivar stated that Adrithyr kidnapped his mother, forced her to be a concubine, raped her, and kept her in chains until he was born, and on that same day Adrithyr had her murdered by his Knight Rigar Blackblood. Ivar fled the city after, escaping through the sewers while pursued by the city watch.
