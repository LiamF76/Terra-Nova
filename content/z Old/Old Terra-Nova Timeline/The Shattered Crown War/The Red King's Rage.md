Adrithyr, so far having refused to recognize Ivar as a rival or anything more than an outlaw troublemaker, finally recognizes his bastard as a threat, and declares war against him. Adrithyr calls his first war council, calling upon the Dwarves of Bourn Dorhul and the lords of the west. Adrithyr amasses an army of 2 Legions, 12,000 Men of the Royal army. Notable people at his war council were Lady Syrell, Consular Nerva, and the respected Knights of Iron, Lords Oldac and Trojan. He dispatches his army to Annevar challenged by Ivar to fight him there.
