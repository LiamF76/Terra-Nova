The first battle of the civil war, and the most well known. Taking place just outside Oligoth, Ivar led a small militia of his first allies and soldiers to the city and opened their ranks to anyone from the city who wanted to join him and remove what he called a "corrupt monster" of a King (referring to Adrithyr). Known as the Battle of the Burning Bastard because Ivar was said to have fought so fiercely it was like he was on fire, spreading across the grasslands outside the walls as if he was a blazing inferno. Ivar did not win the battle, unable to take the fortified city, but he made a statement that people were sympathetic to his cause, and that he was a foe to be feared by Adrithyr. Ivar escaped into the night once again.
