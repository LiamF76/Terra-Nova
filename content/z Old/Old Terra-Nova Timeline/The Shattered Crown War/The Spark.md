After failing to take Oligoth Ivar set his sights on growing his army, beginning an almost political campaign through the cities of the west, posters of his image with captions evoking a sense of rebellion. He wanted the people to know he was a bastard, abandoned by his father and raised with the common folk, he would fight for them if they would fight with him. Ivar used guerilla tactics, but this work bolstered his number by three times its pervious size, and quickly turned the west towards his side.
