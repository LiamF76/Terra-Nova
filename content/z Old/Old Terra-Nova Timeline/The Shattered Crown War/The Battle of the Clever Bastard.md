Adrithyr's Legions crossed the nation aided by a pair of Redsong mages, using their arcane power to quicken their pace. They reached Annevar, having been challenged directly by Ivar in a letter to meet him there and fight him, but they found nothing, The people had emptied the city, no soldiers, not a single soul within the walls. As the Legions searched the began to fall one by one silently. Ivar and his men had hidden outside the city and snuck back in behind the army, using the city terrain and cover to slowly thin the Legions numbers. Eventually a full scale battle broke out, and the Generals of Adrithyr's army fled as their men were slaughtered.
