
## The Entrance

As you come to the entrance of the temple you are greeted by a wondrous sight. Ancient stonework dating back to the First Aelarians, beautiful fey plants, vines, and blossoms cover the outer structure. Ornate stained glass artworks of heroes, gods, and dragons adorn the windows.

Before you is a bridge of stone over the outdoor water feature, with patches of moss, and eventually a 25ft long gap. The water around you is clear blue, shining with lily pads and not even a ripple on its surface.

A massive maple tree with reaching old roots sits in the center of the bridge before it divides into 3 separate roads leading to the end of the pool, where three tall doors made of pure gold sit, and in front of them three glowing purple portals of swirling energy. The eastern bridge is blocked by some rubble, while the other two are unobstructed.

As you stand on the central platform, admiring the magnificent sights and the tree at the center, a single ripple is seen on the crystalline water, and three giant heads explode out from the water, ROLL INITIATILVE! [MUSIC](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ud7e2Qvm5Mg)

**Enemies**: [[hydra.png]] (each head has 100HP and spans two new ones at the top of the round.)

**Loot**: Should the party dive into the water and retrieve the treasure they will find... an ancient magically locked treasure chest containing:
- A small golden seed (Eternal Seed, grows overnight into a small tree that grows golden apples)
- A long gilded black cloak(Sylvan Cloak of Shadows, cloak that allows you to cast pass without trace once per day at no cost)
- A golden scroll (a fey contract between Oberyn and the Hydra, binding it to guard something called "The Greenstride", as long as Oberyn provides it food and protection rom the other Fey)

*The Portals*. There are three glowing purple portals. The one in the center is in front of the doorway as apposed to inside it like the other two. The doorway behind the center portal has three slots, on in the shape of a rose, one in the shape of a maple leaf, and one in the shape of a diamond.


## Portal A

As you step through **Portal A**, you are enveloped in a swirling mist of vibrant pinks and greens, the air perfumed with the sweet scent of cherry blossoms. The mist parts to reveal a serene and surreal landscape. The sun always hangs low on the horizon, casting a golden-pink hue across the grove. A gentle breeze carries petals from the endless bloom of cherry blossoms that cascade like snow across the soft, mossy ground. At the center of the grove stands a massive **Cherry Blossom Treant**, its ancient bark etched with glowing, floral runes. This guardian exudes a calming yet imposing presence, its eyes shimmering with a soft pink light. Its branches stretch wide, draped in cascading blossoms, some of which flutter down to the ground as the Treant moves. As it stands and raises its arms at you to attack, roll initiative!

**Enemies**: 1x [[bloom sentinel.png]], 4x [[pixie.png]]

**Loot**: A single pink rose flutters down from the highest branch on the Treant's back.


## Portal B

Stepping through **Portal B**, you enter a realm steeped in eternal autumn. The air is crisp and fragrant with the scent of fallen leaves, bonfires, and ripe fruit. The golden-red canopy above glows with a perpetual twilight, as if the sun lingers on the horizon, basking the land in hues of amber and crimson.

Before you materializes a massive spectral form of a white elk with ornate horns, and you hear the skitters of insects through the brambles and trees as swarms of spiders appear, roll initiative!

**Enemies**: 1x [[eltheer guardian sprirt.png]], 6x [[swarm of spiders.png]]

**Loot**: A spectral maple leaf is left behind in the fading body of the Eltheer guardian.


## Portal C

**Portal C** opens into a frigid, icy expanse where silence reigns and the air bites with an unnatural chill. This fey pocket dimension is a harsh and desolate tundra, dominated by jagged cliffs, frozen rivers, and snowfields that stretch into a pale, glimmering horizon. 
In the heart of this winter wasteland stands a terrifying Frost Giant! He raises his club and roars as he charges forward, beside you forming from ice and snow are ice mephits, roll initiative!

**Enemies**: 1x [[eltheer guardian sprirt.png]], 6x [[swarm of spiders.png]]

**Loot**: Upon death a diamond shaped crystal of white snow and ice falls from the weapon of the Frost Giant.


## The Inner Sanctum

(requires all three keys from Portals A, B, and C to open.)
As you place the final piece of the key into the doorway it begins to creak open, dust and moss formed over ages tumble down before you as the ancient Temple of Three Roads opens to you. A massive chamber with bridges spanning over crystal blue water, at the back of the room is a small dirt platform bearing a massive emerald leafed tree, it is pulsing lightly. In the center of the tree hangs a humanoid, covered in mosses and glowing verdant vines that bind his body to the tree with jagged thorns. He stirs slightly as you enter, shaking slightly, as blood pours from the wounds inflicted by the thorns.

The door slams shut behind you as a long white scaled and feathered dragon curls down and along the tree, its mystical eyes watching you closely. On the bridges you see shimmering pixies and two large wolves made of purple crystal almost instantly appear, taking defensive stances.

(The dragon is there to guard [[Kerro]], and the Veins, if the party shows any hostility or mentions taking either it will attack them.)

*The Water*. In the center of the Temple sits a swirling gold and blue portal, almost like magic has fused with the water creating some kind of gateway to the Infinite Corridor .

**Enemies**: 1x [[fey dragon.png]], 2x [[crystal wolf.png]], 4x [[pixie.png]]

**Loot**: 
- Upon killing the dragon, the vines and Kerro are both dropped from the tree, leaving a small bracelet and the strange horned and green skinned man on the dirt platform. ([[Kerro]] and [Thornebinder](https://homebrewery.naturalcrit.com/share/cC7AByikx50k))
- Additionally the dragon drops 3 Fey Dragon Feathers, worth 100GP each (with the right buyer)
