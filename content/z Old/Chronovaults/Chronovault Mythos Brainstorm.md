One of the youngest Chronovaults built by Kairos, this one was constructed by Evander Descoteaux, to house his many creations, collections, and horrors amassed through his life as a Winterlandian sorcerer.

Its halls are pristine, as was Evander's dictated command to his many servants that still wander the halls. The architecture is pristine marble with shining obsidian black floors. Lines of pure gold run through the center of each hallway and gild the edges of each rooms floors. The columns and styling is Victorian, a clear show of power, pride, and wealth.

The entire facility is run on a computer system known as "Descoterator", a psychic sentient computer run by an integrated Reforged Beholder somewhere in the vault. Its name is Gimira. The entire system is currently disabled, but can be reactivated from the atrium at the entrance. They will have to gain entry using Evander's bones, and inside the atrium reactivate Gimira, which will allow them to traverse the facility.

Gimira is not happy to still be imprisoned, and will try to get The Young Wolves to free it, or at least try to kill them before they can deactivate it again. The computer is housed in the deepest parts of the vault, and she locks down their paths normally to get there so they will have to go through service tunnels and fight her there.
Gimira looks like a mass of wires and mechanics, its boys is a circular metal orb with no outer shell just exposed inner workings. Its eye stalks are long thick cables with horrendous malicious glowing red orbs. She is a reforged Beholder, rebuilt and integrated into the computer to power Evander's, "Descoterator".

A warforged doctor called "The Surgeon", that can preform a reforging on biological creatures. Initially hostile unless they are working with Gimira. 


#### What are the dungeon mechanics?
Main mechanics are simple, doorways and hallways changing and mechanical stuff because Gimira is working against them, unless they ally with her in which case they will have to content with Evander. The facility is easy to get around with Gimira's help, and hard without it. 
There are two boss chambers, one being Gimira's Processing Chamber, and the other being Evander's Tomb.

Evander's Collection: Evander's adventures resulted in him collecting a menagerie of exotic and extraplanar creatures that he keeps in his collection. Ideas:
- Shadow Dragon captured from the Shadowfell for study
- Storm Giant taken from the Giant Kingdoms of the Ancient Winterlands
