The continent of Akash is a set of  3 major landmasses and a few smaller ones, all together known as, "The Golden Land". The name comes from its history of being protected by Gold Dragons, and for its iconic Marigold flowers. It is highly varied in climates from tundra to plains and all the way to small deserts. The continent is largely rivers and grasslands, making it rife with fertile soil and healthy land for settlement.
##### The Nations:
- **The Aravalli Accord**: Republic, power is held by democratically elected officials who form the Avalonian Senate and create law and policy.
- **The Wahellian Wilds**: The largest part of the continent, Wahellia is known as The Wilds for its unique properties and lack of rulership. It is a vast and dangerous land that is full to the brim with opportunity for profit due to its rare creatures and many mysterious remains of ancient civilizations. Many adventurers make a living on expeditions to Wahellia. Original home to most beast races such as Leonin, Loxodon, Tortle, Owlin, and many many more.
- **Lazarus**: Empire, Monarchy, power is passed through a bloodline. There are Emperors and their Princes who inherit upon the Emperor's death or forfeit of power. They have a council made up of advisors who preside different state organizations, including regional governors. Founded by Lazarus, the King of Kings, a wanderer from the Forbidden Lands who united the world under his banner.
- **Talasva**: Theocracy, central state religion and the government is the church. There are religious ceremonies to determine the next leader who is a known as "The Prophet". 


Akash is a land of plenty, and a land of mystery. Much of history is unknown due to the indigenous peoples lack of written language, making it difficult to understand the nations past. That being said there is one surefire way to learn of Akash's history, explore and experience. The knowledge of the ages is bountifully available to those who would seek it in ruins and ancient treasures.

<mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">The Wahellian Curse</mark>: The Pheyzoth ravine is the birthplace of the Wahellian curse. The curse holds the land hostage, and any who would seek to rule or be called king in Wahellia dies along with their claims and their lands. Long ago King Ren Omrotva took the beast races of Wahellia into slavery, forcing them to toil in his name. He choked the land and its people for wealth and power, and ruled as a violent and cruel tyrant. He became so powerful that through his mages rituals, when is time to die came he bound the spirit of death sent to kill him. He lived for hundreds of years in undeath. Eventually he was overthrown by the Beastfolk, and they released death, killing him. The angel of Erebos lay a curse on the land, preventing another from calling it king forever. It is said that any who attempt to do so will face an army of the dead called upon by the spirits of death, and Wahellia is littered with the ruins of civilizations and rulers to prove it.
