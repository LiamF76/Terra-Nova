Chronovault Sanctum: 

LAYOUT: The first level of this dungeon is pristine, gleaming white and silver steel walls, lined with glowing white lights, illuminating the entire floor clearly. 

- 1a: 
    
- 1b: A small room with a table and chest, the table has a small notebook on it that outlines 3 layers of security in the vault, there are 3 guardians, one for each layer. First layer is “The Scythemind”, second layer is “The Brothers”, and the third is called Temporial the Wyrm.
    
- 2: The door at the back wall is sealed shut magically, it has a small indent where it appears some kind of blade can be placed. Behind the door you can hear faint running water. On either side of the door are statues of some machine warriors. One is single eyed and has an arm with 3 barrels and a scope, the other is tall and heavily armored with a massive shield and mace. At the foot of the armored statue are a few small torn pieces of paper.
    

- LOOT: Torn pages 1 and 7
    

- 3: Large pillared room/ hallway, nothing special.
    

- 4: The doorway to this room is fitted with a set of moveable emitters of holographic screens. Each one of these screens lists a different historical event: The Reckoning, The Drocuthu Massacre, The Dragon Break, The Shattered Crown Rebellion, Birth of the Last Dragon, The Birth of Lumiithiil
    

The Correct order is: Reckoning, Dragon Break, Birth of Last Dragon, The Drocuthu Massacre, Birth of Lumiithiil, Shattered Crown Rebellion

- LOOT: Hourglass Pendant, Torn page 3, 50,000 GP, 1 White ruby gem (10,000 GP)
    

- 5: This room was once a bedroom but has been ransacked and turned over, the bed is thrown from the corner and there is a broken upside down table beside the door, in the corner is an empty chest with a shattered lock.
    
- 6a: This large room is lined by pillars, in the center is a 15 by 15 ft pit covered by a metallic grate, beneath is a room of greenery and flowing water. There are 6 hooded figures kneeling before the raised altar at the back of the room. 
    

- 6b: Upon this raised altar there are two statues, on shattered and broken (DC 30 check: It is Kronos), the female statue is of a woman kneeling with silver feathered wings, she wears a 3 pointed crown and carries a large sword on her back with a gemstone in the crossguard, and you see faint engravings of stars on the underside of her wings.
    
- 6c: This small library off the temple is mostly bare, only containing a small table and a few bookshelves. There is an empty armor stand in the corner as well.
    

- LOOT: Kronos the Timeborn, War in Ages Past, Secrets of Celestial Gold
    

- 7a: Large circular room, there are hundreds of engravings covering one another and languages written in blood across it, it is jumbled and very confusing DC 20 investigation: A lot about the Usurper Vaior, and how the tower is a prison not a protector, a weapon for the vengeful, DC 25: The blood and carvings make out a little bit about how the Demi-God will be born of the fallen not of the risen. He will carry not light, but blinding darkness.
    
- 7b: This room is a magical forge, it is an anvil and pool of burning hot liquid metal, inside a floating crystalline ball is a glowing and burning star, frozen in time and appears to be used to power the forge within this place. 
    

- LOOT: The Temporal Star Forge, Horus Hammer , Horus’ book of creations: List of things he made including the Clock-Hand staff, the Clock-Hand blade, the Hourglass Pendant, the Cube of Confessions, Mintaka, The Chronal Chains, the book also details that the hammer can be used to destroy the chains binding Kronos that he was asked to forge by Vaior
    

- 7c: The Scythemind’s lair, this church-like room is home to the almost alien and lifelike construct. 
    

- LOOT: Torn page 2 and Scythe Key on the altar
    

- 7d: This room contains 5 coffins and a pedestal in the center of the room with a handprint in glowing light on it. If you touch the pedestal with your hand a voice will speak to you, “Welcome to Horus’ Crypt. Here lies Horus’ Temporal Replicants” One of the coffins is clearly missing. The coffins are solid silver and sealed just like the one you found in the Rollinghouse. The missing coffin has a plaque above it labeled “Eisenhower”
    

- Each coffin has a name (going clockwise): Noah, Velias, Emandyr, Samuel, Binvyes
    

  

SECOND FLOOR: The second layer is totally overgrown filled with vines and a couple inches of water in every room. 

- 8a: Large totally overgrown hallway, there is a channel of flowing water through the center of the room, it is murky and the bottom cannot be seen.
    
- 8b: Another overgrown room, there is about 3 inches of standing water along with leaves, vines and moss hanging from the ceiling. There are 3 doorways, one on either side and one at the end of the hall. 
    

- LOOT: On the floor to the right of the door is Torn Page 4
    

- 8c: This room is behind a locked door that can only be opened with the twin keys. The back wall is covered with a gigantic magical mirror (matter mirror).
    

- LOOT: On the ground beside the mirror are Torn Pages 12 and 5
    

- 9: A storage room filled with food that seems to not ever expire in barrels, standing in the center of the room is a tall mirror. Upon looking at the mirror you see yourself, but the longer the look the more menacing it becomes, you see your fears, your own death, and the deaths of those you love. 
    

- LOOT: Torn pages 6 and 11
    

- 10: 
    
- 11: Mostly empty except for what appears to be piles of machine parts covered in growth, upon further inspection these look to be the parts of some automatons resembling the description of the twins from the security overview.
    
- 12: This large room is lined with some kind of magical emitters, and at the back of the room is a large pool of water inset to the ground. DC: 20 check: This is a gigantic stationary temporal experimentation chamber that has been inactive for centuries. There is a control pedestal beside the door. 
    

- 13: This room is lined with gray metal, it is an exact copy of the room you found Kronos in. There is a large black stone platform in the center with stairs leading up to it. 
    

- DC 25 Check: This is a temporal binding platform, lacking only the dimensional chains to bind someone there. This is a torturous prison that keeps the chained one alive, awake, and aware but unable to move or speak. 
    

  

THIRD FLOOR:

- 14: In the hallway before the steps down across the walls you read (in draconic) “He who defies creation defies the Elder Dragon, Shao Daraan, behold his son, who holds time within his maw.”
    

- ENEMIES: Temporal, the Chronoscale
    

- LOOT: Hanging on the back wall behind the alert is a black shield with evil curved horns and many red eyes, with a large one in its center. (Shield of Daraan)
    

- 15: A small bedroom adorned with children's drawings in red and blue ink across the walls. There are drawings of gigantic eldritch beasts, but they do not look evil, one is holding a planet in its hand, while another has a sun for an eye. 
    

- LOOT: Inside the chest there is a journal with only one entry. A single white dragon scale (100,000GP), Torn Pages 9 and 10  
    First of days, Journal of Temporial.  
    I write here once, only to document what truly matters, my friend Horus and I have been beaten. He discovered the lies of Vaior and in a fit of rage the false god struck him down, I took him as quickly as I could, to the Sanctum, home. We hid here as long as we could, but eventually we realized the Usurper was not coming, Horus lost his will after many years trapped here. He asked me to end his suffering, and for my friend it was a small favor to ask. The replicants returned slowly as well, laid to rest as they knew to do. I may remember nothing but beautiful skies I once ruled here, and the love for my friend. I will stand by his side, Horus and Temporial, until my end and beyond.  
    Horus, if one of your replicants is reading this and I have passed, know that I would lose my mind before I would forget you.
