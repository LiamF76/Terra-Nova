Castle Ikaris is one of the oldest structures in [[z Old/Old Geography/Old Escarth/Escarth]]. It was built on top of Mount Daedalus by some of the first Owlin and Aarakocra. It was named after the legendary Prince Ikaris, who led his people to the top of the mountain to be close to their God Aethor, who they believe is not a dragon but a great elder bird.
Castle Ikaris has a long standing tradition of being a meeting place for legendary heroes who gather together to defeat great threats to the world. It is known to be where the Wyrm-Wardens before their quest to defeat the first Tiamat, led by Thermavox the Red Tyrant. 

The Castle is, by all accounts MASSIVE. There are more than 50 bedrooms, not including servants quarters. There is a full kitchen and large pantry, with a massive dining hall, a head table and 12 more around the room. Attached to the dining hall through a large archway and heavy wooden door is the ballroom. The ballroom features a gigantic chandelier made of exquisite gems like diamonds. There is a gigantic courtyard at the center of the castle. There is a full 100ft archery range, a wizards dueling platform, and a sand area for sword training and combat. There are covered and uncovered spectator areas, as well as lost of room on balconies from the castle walls and places to watch the courtyard.
