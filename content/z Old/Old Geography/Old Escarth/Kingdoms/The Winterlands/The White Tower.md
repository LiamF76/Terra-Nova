Formerly the home of [[Clovis]], now the tower belongs to Ivys Arthur, and it responds to his commands. As he stands before the door it flies open, giving its treasures and mysteries to its new master.
Most of the tower is unoccupied or unused, filled with spoiled food in the kitchens, and long since burned our piles of ash in the fireplaces.

- LABRATORY: 
	- A Warforged named [[Ramshackle]] resides inside, who was once Clovis' assistant. He can provide a martial weapon once per day.
	- The laboratory is filled with magical research and spell components. There are jars of different substances like sulfur and iron filings. There are small pieces of petrified creatures like bat fur and mandrake roots.
	- SMALL DESK: On the desk are a bunch of journals. There are a few dedicated to spell research and creation, which have such confusing and broken documentation that its impossible to decipher them. There are notes on comets, documenting where and when they have landed and things that were recovered at the site including a book on Sozin. There are flyers for dragon bounties, and writing about sightings of dragons across the southern seas. There are more notes on dragons and the Wyrmlands dating back hundreds of years, the earliest set during the first Realmcrest king's rule. 
		There is an interesting and well documented spell research paper on something called "Mortis Memoria". It is interesting and easier to understand because it isn't spell creation, but appears to be spell reverse engineering by something Clovis believes was created by Karius. There isn't much actual information other than (meta knowledge) it is an insanely powerful spell, essentially 10th level, unusable by mortals, and that it is an enchantment spell. 
	- LARGE DESK: The largest desk is covered in books and random papers. There are translations of The Words of Light (The Illuman bible, book of [[The Sunlight Road]]), in all major languages. There are a few other religious texts from [[The Sunlight Road]] too. 
	- CHALKBOARD: The chalkboard is covered in writing in both Clovis' and another person's writing that you don't immediately recognize (DC 15 Invest, it's Aldred). The writing is clearly scratch work, scattered and confusing being done over long periods of time, it appears to be a collaborative effort of the two people. The notes pertain to a concept labeled, "The Lysandrian Heresy." Lysander was a monk and religious scholar over 20 years ago. He had proposed the theory that gods have counter domains to one another. If there is a light god, there is a dark god. Many people subscribed to this theory and it wasn't inherently evil or heretical, but Lysander pushed this further. There are holes in the theory, but most can be accounted for by fallen gods, such as Tena or Arkonil. Lysander became ladled as a heretic and his writings were outlawed in the church after this final implication, if there is a Creator, there must be a destroyer. What happened to Lysander after this is unknown.
		There are diagrams drawn on the board depicting the gods and connecting them to their antithesis gods ([[Gods Drawing]]). It appears that Aldred continued the work for a short time without Clovis as Aldred's writing makes up the last of the work, but it is incomplete, and they never discovered anything objective about the "Destroyer". The last thing on the board reads "The Great Devourer..."
		
		The Great Devourer in these notes, seems to be connected to a historical figure, a warlord and dark sorcerer by the name of Thaldrax, the Nightbringer.
