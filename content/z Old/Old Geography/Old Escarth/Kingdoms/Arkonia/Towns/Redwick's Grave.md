### Overview

Named for it's ancient legend, the grave of a forgotten hero known only as Redwick. This place is considered a sacred tomb, and bore tales of blessed guardian angels and holy knights who guarded the town long ago, but now they are just legend. The castle had crumbled long ago, but the town was built in the grasslands near the marsh that has taken the ruins. From time to time, villagers claim to hear strange whispers in the night, and there have been desecrations at local gravesites over the last year. 

### Places and People of Interest

The official Alderman of the village is Daniel Thistledown, a human whose great-great-grandfather founded the town many years ago. He inherited his power, and believes that handshakes and firm deals are the only way to stay in charge. Thistledown has a talent for public speaking and charisma, contrary to the other leader of the village, the young druid [[Cerwan]].
[[Cerwan]] was sent to Redwick's Grave as a guardian and representative of the Eldermourne Druids, supposedly a safe and easy posting guarding the grave of the ancient hero.
