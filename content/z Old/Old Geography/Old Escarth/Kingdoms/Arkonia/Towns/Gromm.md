
Gromm is a small village on the edge of The Bloody Lake. It was founded by an orc chieftain named Gromm during the unification wars. It served as a bastion for both logging and fishing, trading down the river with the fortress city of Traxgate for many years, but has fallen on hard times with the birth of The Scar.

- ##### People and Politics:
	- Demographics: The population is predominantly orc with a splash of different races including Humans, Half-Elves, and the occasional Dragonborn or Beastfolk.
	- Factions: [[The Sons of Urzoth]], are the strongest and only remaining faction, after the Logging Guild and the Fisherman's Union left town when the economy went belly up.
	- Politics: The city belongs to the nation of [[Arkonia]], but is not loyal or protected by them at the time. The town is in open but uncontested rebellion against their action lord, [[Zyrathia]], in [[Krag Irath]]. 
- ##### Locations and Architecture:
	- The town is small and quaint. There are three major areas, the Town, the Harbor, and the Temple.
- ##### Specific Locations:
