The city of Ironpetal is an entirely underground city, based around the 3 giant spires of stone littered with powerful glowing white crystal flowers that light the city. The city is massive, one of the larger cities in Escarth and has a population of over a million at least. The city is riddled with crime, due to its dark and dangerous nature. It is a trade hub for metals, and known for mining and smelting massive quantities of iron and steel. It is known as the second greatest smithing center after Bourn Dorhul. 
The city sprawls across the base of the cavern and along the massive pillars at least a thousand feet tall. The floor of the city is poorer district called "The Underspire", and the three spires above being the nicer district known as "The Sunspire" and "The Moonspire" respectively. 
The city is known for the unique creatures that inhabit the cavern known as "Rockrunners", they are large lizards that are used to pull carts, carriages, and people through the city. Rockrunners can be rented for personal use, or carriages can be used to get around, these special carriages are built on the back of Rockrunners with rooms inside that rotate, so that when Rockrunners go up walls you stay upright in your seat. 


- ##### People and Politics:
	- Demographics: The city is highly diverse, but there are less elves than other places, they don't generally enjoy the underground nature of the city. There is a large population of dwarves, humans, orcs, and dragonborn. 
	- Factions: There are many powerful factions in the city, some local to the area and some being larger organizations that have bases or outposts here. The **Ivory Spire Institute** is an academic and magic university that is heavily ingrained in the city's workings. They are led by a council of 3 educated and learned people: Lorekeeper [[Talinor]], Weavekeeper [[Centus]], and Songkeeper [[Surge]]. There is **Court Rex**, a court of royal vampires led by [[Catherine Rex]] that resides in the Veinspire Castle, they control the city by King Ivar's royal decree. The **Goldengloves** are a group of criminal mercenaries that run the Underspire, and they control the Iron Maw fighting pit. They are run by a well known mastermind called, "[[Mykoz Tythos]]". The local law enforcement is known as **The Calcite Guard**, they bear the symbol of a shining white crystal on their armor, and are known to ride Rockrunners.
	- Politics: The power in this city is divided between **Court Rex** and **The Goldengloves**. They are both evil aligned people, the vampires are working for Ivar and Catherine, making commanders into vampires to enhance their strength, and Catherine is researching the Catacombs of Cain. They have a tentative peace with the criminals predicated on that the vampires can feed off the pit fighters, and the criminals are allowed to operate without interference by the court. They are in a sort of cold war, vying for strength and always looking for an edge over one another. The criminals have numbers and control over most of the city, while the vampires are individually stronger and control the castle.

	- The climate is warm on the Spires, but in the Underspire and notably on the Whispering Walk, it can get very cold.

It is underground and technically illegal but is the most famous arena in Arkonia. Many mercenaries come to test their strength against others in fights for massive payouts. 
	- The Iron Horizon: mining/ merchant guild hall
	- The Shifting Prism: store
	- The Whispering Walk: bridge
	- The Catacombs of Cain: underground tunnel network
- ##### Religion and Temples:
	- The Church of the Hammer makes its home in the Cathedral of the Eternal Anvil. The followers of Iros are strong willed and dedicated people. The church has the tenets of achieving mastery in whatever craftsman's discipline you choose, and in trade getting the best price you can for your hard worked wares. 
	- The Order of Armul resides in the Hall of Good Fortune. The Order of Armul was actually founded in Ironpetal. They believe that all are welcome in Hermod's sacred house, and have offered many deserters sanctuary from the war.
