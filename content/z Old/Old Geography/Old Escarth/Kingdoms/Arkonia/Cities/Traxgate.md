Know as the "Bastion of Order" or, "The City of Water and Wind", Traxgate is ruled by a rigid military government and strict legal system that defines personal and all other aspects of life. It is a military society, where citizens are those who have fulfilled their mandatory 3 year military service, and those are the only ones with express rights. There are systems for travelers, but it is known to be a generally accepting society to adventurers. Traxgate is a fortress city, the entire thing was built as one impenetrable castle by the ancient General, **[[Ortrax]] Stonecrown**, a dwarf warlord. The city is known for its canals and large water systems using locks and bridges, the most common mode of travel is Serpents and Wyverns, by either air or water. River Serpents are fitted with large platforms functioning as public transport through the canals. Wyverns are used for aerial travel between the many key watchtowers in the city. Traxgate operates on the social structure instituted by King [[Ivar]] and the Government of [[Arkonia]], race, sex, and anything else do not matter more than strength. There is no discrimination for faith or any other factor so long as you use your power to further Traxgate and Arkonia.

Traxgate is known for its ancient protector, the spirit of a Silver Dragon, who gave the first King an amulet known as "Valor Made Shape". It grants its wielder the power to summon the protector, the spirit of the Silver Dragon Liora. It is a fierce dragon who defends the wielder with its life. When Ortrax descended into Tyranny, Liora and the Wizard Council united with heroes of the realm to banish him to the Shadowfell.

- ### People and Politics:
	- Demographics: [[Traxgate]] is a highly diverse city, with almost equal distributions of humans, Dragonborn, Dwarves, Half-Elves, and Halflings. Races intermingle regularly and are not divided in living spaces. People are generally united in the Traxian way, work together, strength is everything.
	- Factions: The city has a few major factions but most are part of the city's massive standing military.
		- **The Drake Wardens**: The city's police/law enforcement. They are known to ride Wyverns in the skies above [[Traxgate]], gliding from tower to tower through the high reaching guard stations, and patrolling the rivers on their massive River Serpents. They often perform stop searches and checks for valid Traxian ID (which are steel plates)
		- **The Ivory Fists**: The loyal followers of Hannibal, they are not native to Traxgate, and follow their Loxodon master. Their members are Hannibal's brother, [[Vashmira]], and his [[Blade Binders]].

- ### Specific Locations:
- ##### The Shield District:
	- **The Scriptorium**: Operated by the *Constructors Accord*, this is where the bureaucratic operations take place. Citizens can do business such as apply for licenses or get identification. Visitors and adventurers are required to receive temporary identification here when they enter the city. Valid ID are small steel plates containing names, titles, identifying information, and a unique ID number. Some rich citizens will spend extra to have theirs made in silver, gold, or other precious metals.
	- **The Ironscript Archive**: The massive forges of Traxgate, this is where their rams, catapults, and other instruments of war are designed, tested, and built by members of the *Constructors Accord*. You can have instruments of war built on commission, but they do not take coin by itself, it must be accompanied by deeds in the name of Traxgate.
	- **The Water Treatment Plant**: One of the most important pieces of infrastructure in Traxgate. Ina  city of water canals, water treatment to keep it safe for serpents to travel in is of the utmost importance. The Treatment plant is a marvel of magical technology, designed to clean and filter water, keeping its safe to circulate through the canals of the city constantly. Currently it is overloaded and overworked due to the pollution from the Scar in the Sea of Smoke. Hannibal had Aurathia captured in order to maintain the city during the time of increased stress.
	- **[[Antiques and Uniques]]**: Boutique shop for unique items, rare curiosities, and all sorts or crap nobody should really want, or maybe they should! Owned and operated by Rikkit the red Kobold.

The walls are adorned with nameless blades, pieces of armor, and depictions of battle. This hall is dedicated to the thousands of warriors that have lived and died in the way of Traxgate.

- ### Religion and Temples:
	- [[The Warrior's Way]]: The Church of Vulcan, they do not so much worship Vulcan in Traxgate as much as they worship battle and war. All things dedicated to Vulcan are dedicated to the city, to its people, and to furthering the progress of the collective. Vulcan has a modest temple called the *House of Duty*. Followers in the city often bear a sword and shield necklace, symbolizing both Vulcan and Traxgate as their masters. Vulcan is happy to oblige the worship of the city, as he believes Traxgate and their tenants align with his own.
	- [[The Cult of the Black Grail]]: The worshippers of Aldred, followers of the great Lord of Penance and Punishment. In Traxgate punishment and making good on your failures is critical, and this has recently allowed the Black grail to advance their position in the city. Their church is a repurposed armory, made into the hall of worship for Aldred. It is known as *The Forgotten Armory*. Aldred is not quick to forgive, and often dispatches offenders on dangerous missions in order to rejoin the city, the worse the crime the more dangerous your mission. If they refuse the missions, they are sent to the Arena, *The Crucible*. 
- ### Economy
	- Traxgate is a military-industrial complex. They exist in a constant state of production, creating war machines, weapons, and armor, all in the name of the Traxian destiny. Producing catapults for Arkenstone, armor for Dragonswatch, and other things for all facets of the Arkonian military. People trade services and achievements for goods between one another, more often that coin. 
- ### Law and Military:
- ##### Traxian Law: 
	- The law of Traxgate is strict and unbending. People follow it to the absolute maximum. Due to the mandatory military service the law is highly enforced and respected by people who are brought up under it. There are a strict set of rules, all combat is illegal except for sanctioned duels and battles. All crimes are settled through a court, often resulting in trial by combat or mandated military service for a varying number of years. The law is enforced by the Drake Wardens, the police of the city who are known for their Wyverns and River Serpents that patrol the skies and canals of the city.
		- **Treason**: Any action deemed by the Traxian Council as acting against Traxgate, is punishable by execution or forced slavery into the lowest ranks of the Steel Wardens.
		- **Defense Sabotage/ Refusal of Duty**: Refusal to do your duty or obey any lawful command by the Council is seen as a failure to defend the city and its rights, and those who do so can be punished with entombment within the walls of the city.
		- **Combat Trials**: Trial by combat is performed by groups, called Trial by Battle, where 7 individuals face off against 7 other individuals. 
- ##### Military Factions: 
	- **The Burning Legion**: Led by [[Marza]] Anguish. The martial legion of Traxgate, they are the frontlines, the swords and shields of the city, and one of the deadliest forces in Terra-Nova. Though they are not the strongest, they are very well equipped by *The Constructors Accord*, and they are disciplined and strategic beyond any other army in Terra-Nova.
	- **The Weave Sentinels**: Led by [[Vazzrick]]. The warrior mages of Traxgate, they manage arcane defenses, magical artillery, and binding of arcane power to Traxgate's purpose. In addition to being a military branch, they run the magic academy, The Crucible. They produce military mages with vast destructive capabilities, often encouraging pacts with demons or other forces to increase a mage's power.
	- **The Constructors Accord**: Led by [[Helm]].
