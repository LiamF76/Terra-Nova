
Nestled within the freezing Berserker Peaks, this place is ageneral store, trading post, and trapper's center. This store is run by Wendell Ursine, older brother of Barnabus.

They specialize in equipment for adventurers and travel in the Berserker Peaks. Wendell also has a softness for strange creatures that he collects and will only sell to those who he thinks will treat them well. he also carries a small stock of magic items that Barnabus keeps there.

## Cute Creatures

 - A strange blue racoon with feathered wings leaps between ropes and the tops of crates above you. This is a **Blue Racujay**. His name is Rubbish.
- Slowly crawling across the counter is an orange and red frog with small draconic wings, it burps and a small puff of fire is spat from its large mouth. It is a **Red Dragon Frog**. Her name is Ruby.
- As you feel a faint tap on your shoulder Audrey, you look down and see what appears to be a large red leaf like that of an oak tree in fall. It shudders slightly and stretches, revealing it is a **Baby Woodland Dragon**. It is a small body of wood like a twig and wings like that of the leaf. His name is Autumn.
- As you bargain, you feel a slick wet tongue run across your knee, looking down you see the large form of a St. Bernard, wearing a saddle and some saddle bags like a horse. This is **Hugo**, Barnabus' dog.
- In a shimmering glass jar with a sturdy cork in its top there is a shining golden being. A small spirit of pure light that dances and sways gracefully in its container. This is **Solith** the Light Spirit.
