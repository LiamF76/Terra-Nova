Name: *Irontooth*, The Nails of Famine

""

Description: A set of **six rusted iron spikes**, each meant to be driven into the flesh. They are long, almost 4 inches in length, corrupted and rusty, unable to be cleaned no matter how much water and soap runs over them.

Effects: To attune to the nails you must drive one into your flesh with a hammer as deep as it can go. You can only be attuned to one nail at a time, and you gain the following effects:
- **Power in Need:** Embedding a nail into oneself allows the user to go without food or drink indefinitely. You never need to eat or drink.
- **Strength in Famine**: Your strength score becomes 22 and cannot be increased by any means beyond that.
- **The Wretched Curse:** Each day, you lose 1 current and maximum HP permanently. This effect can only be removed by death, and follows you when revived afterwards.
