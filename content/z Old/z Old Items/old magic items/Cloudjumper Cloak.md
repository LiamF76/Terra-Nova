Name: Cloudjumper Cloak

Rarity/Attunement: Very Rare, Yes

"Fly you fools!"

Description: A cloak of beautiful white wool, its patterns are adorned with blue designs of clouds and golden threads laced throughout.

Effects: While attuned to this cloak you gain the following effects:
- You gain resistance to thunder damage.
- You can use a bonus action to grant yourself a flying speed of 30ft for 1 minute, twice per long rest.
- While you have a flying speed granted by this item, you do not take fall damage.
