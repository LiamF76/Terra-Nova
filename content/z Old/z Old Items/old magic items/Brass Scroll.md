Name: Brass Scroll

Rarity/Attunement: Uncommon, No

"See? Just wait a minute next time. Scroll would've caught those spikes and you would still have a left leg."

Description: A small scroll made entirely of bronze, it has a golden sheen and is much heavier than you would expect.

Effects: 
- You can place the scroll inside a room and activate it by pressing a button on both ends, if there is a magical or mechanical trap in the room it will vibrate slightly.
- You may cast Identify once per long rest through this item with no material cost.
- If you store a spell scroll inside, the scroll is protected from all damage except a _Disintegrate_ spell.
