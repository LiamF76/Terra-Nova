Emrys is in a difficult place, the man he wronged and got excommunicated has ascended to Godhood and has become the lord of pain and punishment.
Where does Emrys go from here? He is on a quest to defeat Arkonil, the Reckoner, and the god of Rebellion, its doom driven but he remains driven. Emrys is tragic in a hopeful sense. He fights even when he might lose because he wants to help, redeem those who fall and save those who have been lost. He is faith, maybe not in a god, but in the people of this world's capacity to be helped.
His relationship with Illuman wavers, but it could remain, the important part is hope. He remains positive, even if its subtle at times, that he and the Young Wolves can win. He also believes that he needs to talk to Aldred. Conflicting feelings here, but he feels shame and regret over what happened to Aldred in the Hyperion Church because he caused it, and he feels a desperate need to reach out to a once friend and help them, mantling is a vile and heretical process, and he might need help or saving, Emrys just really wants to be there for him, but he doesn't know how.
To learn how, he should work towards learning more about how Aldred became a god, and his current domain, maybe search for some of his followers, those paths lead to the Black Sun Punisher himself!

## Plot points

- **Emrys Prays to Aldred**. When Emrys reaches out to Aldred he is overcome with a sickening anger, anger towards the church, towards Illuman, and towards himself. Aldred is enraged by Emrys' attempt to reach out, and refuses to answer. As Emrys probes toward Aldred searching for any kind of connection, he finds a single name, "Brother Tarot" and has a faint vision of a pair of balancing scales. (This is his spur to action to find Brother Tarot, who is the start of rebuilding their connection.)
	- You reach out, trying to form a connection, to send a message, to let him know you are open to speaking, and you receive anger. Primordial, divine, celestial anger. "You do not deserve penance, you must be punished, for one with such a soft heart you must become the punisher. Seek out the vile witch Catherine Rex, lay waste to her form, and destroy her vile creations. Punish the vampire queen for her sins. Sunder her very soul from her flesh and send her to my fields of retribution. Report your success to Brother Tarot. You may yet be worth saving."
- **Search for Brother Tarot**. Tarot is not hard to find, but the name is new. He was once a priest of Mani, and a follower of Elena Syrell. Now he preaches in the Akronian city of Traxgate, giving the words of Aldred to the people. In order to gain this information Emrys could talk to travelers, adventurers, or merchants who have traveled through Arkonia. He has put up a small temple in the city known as Hall of Repentance. He is known for allowing anyone who has unbalanced scales inside, but those who are balanced are forbidden, as they do not need healing from the Black Sun.
