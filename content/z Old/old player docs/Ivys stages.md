Ivys has made a deal with Kairos, allowing Ivys to tap into Kairos' power in exchange for some freedom from his prison in the Endless Void. 

#### STAGES OF POWER:
- Kairos inhabits Ivys' familiar with his own (a small night serpent), which he can somewhat control but still obeys Ivys and he can see through.
	- Ivys begins to experience dark visions, blurred between what is real and what is not, even resulting in dream combat against Kairos.
- Kairos becomes able to talk through Ivys, and see through his eyes when Ivys allows it, he is not keen to do so unless he gets something out of it. This could be something of value or something he finds interesting.
	- Ivys begins to experience subtle personality changes, becoming more brutal and deceptive. His shadow appears to lengthen, and his skin begins to turn subtly grey.
- Kairos possesses Ivys entirely for combat, still being willed and controlled by Ivys, but fighting him at every turn.
	- Ivys becomes able to use some of Kairos' abilities at the cost of some MAX hp and maybe levels of exhaustion.

#### NOTES: 

These effects are an interesting and detailed way to show the dynamic between Kairos and Ivys as they fight for control of Ivys' replicant body. They can be slowed, and sometimes outright prevented by a ring of mind shielding (write a way for them to get one). 


### Bodily Decay

Stages for Ivys' bodily decay during the final Zyrathia session. 

- **Brain Decay**: Pulsing headaches, blood drips from your nose and a bit of clear pinkish fluid, you have disadvantage on saves with INT and WIS
- **Body Decay**: You watch small chunks of skin peel away revealing the necrotic bulbous growths. Your movement speed is reduced by 10ft, and your current and max HP are reduced by 10 points.
