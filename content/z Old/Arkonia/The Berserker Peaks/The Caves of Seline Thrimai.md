
Seline Thrimai is a 5 room dungeon style. The room structure is as follows:

- Entrance: Guardians, horde of skeletons and a ice elemental guarding the door, Fortress Entrance battle map
- Puzzle: Dance puzzle, ballroom battle map, dance puzzle to follow the footprints in the show and mimic the traditional Fey court dance, [Banshee](https://www.aidedd.org/dnd/monstres.php?vo=banshee) try to distract, 
- Setback: library, reveal they are in a trap set by the mage, doors ice over and lots of enemies attack. Also books reveal there is a Moondial portal to the Fey through the infinite corridor in the caves
- Climax: Moondial boss battle, frost mage and her ice wyvern, more skeletons. Dial mechanic that opens the portal
- Resolution: Go through the Moondial portal and talk to Sylvaris, reward them with Rootsunder and Thornebinder.


## Room 1: The Fortress Entrance

As you walk through the icy mist, small floating ice crystals drift through the air around you as you cross through the frozen gale. As you emerge from the frozen misty wall, you see yourself before the gates of the underground fortress, and a large horde of monsters.
There are icy skeletons carrying scimitars, a massive ice elemental standing in the center of the bridge, and a much more official armored thrall who caries a bow made of pure blue ice, he turns to face you, his icy blue eyes piercing through you as he screeches with undead fury, Roll initiative!

**Enemies**: 10x [[ice thrall.png]], 1x [[ice thrall captain.png]], 1x [[ice elemental.png]]

*The Frozen Gateway Puzzle*. The massive wooden doorway is frozen shut with an enchanted ice seal, covering the entire doorway from head to toe.
Removal methods:
- Place a biological being on each of the glowing rune platforms
- Sozin and Solith use their elemental powers together to melt the ice off the doorway, allowing the lazily swing open.

**North Door**. The massive frozen wood gateway leads into the Fey Ballroom.

## Room 2: The Ancient Ballroom

Upon crossing the threshold of the fortress you are met with a glorious sight. Encased in a magical winter you are within a massive royal ballroom. A throne sits atop a grand platform, beautiful inscriptions of traditional Fey dances adorn the ornate tiled floors, and a massive pine tree sits in the center, decorated with twinkling magic lights.

*The Dance Paintings*. Across the floor there are tiles depicting a beautiful fey dance. Through all of the dances a figure is depicted watching over the performer from a high seat. 
(Each one of these is an acrobatics check, DC 10, 12, 14, 16, after the first tile banshee appear and dance alongside you, second they attempt to distract you, giving disadvantage, and third they attack.)
- **Tile of the Glimmering Spin**
    - The tile shows a fey spinning in place, arms outstretched with tiny lights swirling around them like fireflies. Mimicking this requires a pirouette, accompanied by a twirl of the hands as if throwing imaginary stardust into the air.
- **Tile of the Leaping Sylph**
    - A sylph is caught mid-leap, one leg bent back and arms reaching forward as if soaring through the air. To mimic it, a player must leap forward with a graceful arc, landing lightly on their toes.
- **Tile of the Clover Step**
    - A sprite dances atop a patch of glowing clovers, their feet moving in a quick, intricate pattern. This step demands fast footwork, a bouncing rhythm, and lighthearted hops as though one were skipping through a meadow.
- **Tile of the Moonlit Bow**
    - A slender figure bows low under a glowing crescent moon, their arms sweeping to the side in an elegant motion. To replicate this, the player must perform a dramatic, sweeping bow with arms tracing arcs like the curve of the moon.

*The Throne*. A large throne made of branches and natural material, it has become grey and icy blue from the dark freezing magics that have infected the hall.

*The Dance Puzzle*. One player must sit in the throne. One player must dance. When the dance is complete with no failed checks, the tiles glow blue and white as the platform where the tree sits lifts up and floats in the center of the room, revealing a staircase spiraling deeper into the earth.

**The Secret Passage**. This staircase leads down to the Library.

## Room 3: The Frozen Library

As you make your way down the stairs, you feel ice along the steps, everyone make a Dexterity saving throw (DC 18).
On a failure you being to slip, sending you and your partners tumbling and sliding down what becomes an icy ramp. You slide directly into a table in the center of the library.

The doorway behind you gives off a crackle as it is encased in ice. (The ice cannot be melted except by Sozin and Solith.)
The sides of the library are also blocked by walls of enchanted ice.

At the back of the room is a glowing astrolabe, Ivys, you would recognize this as a telepathic lock arcane machine, which is likely generating the magical ice. It requires a password to unlock.

*Book Riddles*. 
In the center of the room are two books. Their covers read: 
(If they answer something close have it work and the cover reveals the real answer.)
- Book 1: "Only the spark of creation can ignite what is frozen." Solution: Fire
- Book 2: "To brighten the way, all paths must converge." Solution: Light

*Ice Wall Compactor*. The walls close in by 5ft at the top of every round. They can be deactivated by speaking the passcode to the Astrolabe: "Fire and Light"
After speaking the codeword the astrolabe's glow fades, and the ice through the room melts, Revealing a hidden doorway behind the bookshelves int he Northwest corner of the library.

**Loot**: A stack of scrolls sits beside the doorway, along with a small parchment note folded into a triangle.
- 1x scroll of [Draconic Transformation](https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/spell:draconic-transformation)
- 2x scroll of [Moonbeam](https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/spell:moonbeam)
- 1x Healing Potion
- Note from Sylvaris: "Lady Ovosath, I leave the caves in your care. My children are fleeing through the gateway to the Feywild. I shall follow, If you read this please protect the gateway. Should it be destroyed I fear I shall never return to your plane. Do not attempt this at your own peril. May your days shine like your scales."

**Northwest Door**. The secret doorway leads to the Moondial Chamber. It is covered with the same strange mist, but it is warmer, almost hot to the touch and filled with strange particles of pink leaves and the smell of Jasmine Tea.

## The Moondial Chamber

As you fall through the mist your visions blurs, you mind clouded by it, and you open your eyes in the center of a Sundial, with phases of the moon designed on its edges. You are in an underground garden, a haven of nature, still warm like a spring day despite the encroaching cold visible in the grasses and plants. Its as if you are within what the caves were meant to be, a Fey magical forested paradise for magical creatures and unique plants.
Around you there are more Ice thralls, a captain, and a gigantic swirling blizzard in a perfect globe shape 50ft above you.

#### Mechanics:

- *Moondial*. The Moondial controls the gateway to the Feywild. It has 100 HP and an AC of 17.
- *Void Crystals*. They have an AC of 12 and 10HP. They fire crackling black eldritch as a lair action (initiative count 20), each crystal deals 10 damage on each attack. 
- *The Blizzard Orb*. Above the battlefield is a gigantic ball of raging ice snow and hail. The storm rages around a nearly opaque ball of enchanted blue ice. While Rosalind remains in the orb 4x [[ice thrall.png]] spawn or 1x [[ice elemental.png]] at the top of each round. Attacks against the orb are meaningless until the Void Crystals are destroyed.


**Enemies**: (Helyndor) 1x [[adult white dragon.png]], 1x [[rosalind shadowfrost.png]], 4x [[ice thrall.png]], 1x [[ice thrall captain.png]]

*ROUND 1 FLAVOR*. (to help emphasize mechanics): The crystals thrum with angry and cruel energy as 4 blasts of black eldritch energy lash out, striking the centerpiece of the dial. The stone cracks slightly under the pressure of the blasts, small beams of moonlight escaping from the crevice's. 

*SUMMON FLAVOR*. You see a burst of white light from the blizzard above as beams of light point downwards, and 
- 4 Ice thralls spawn for the individual beams.
- The beams converge 

**MOONDIAL HP TRACKER**: 100 initial

Boss fight Tactics:
- Rosalind has cast mage armor, stone skin, and flight on herself before the fight
- uses AOE spells, Void Chains and Ice wall to disorient and distract while Helyndor unleashes damage and tanks
- If the Crystals die Rosalind will use her thralls to attack the Moondial, if that fails she will focus on killing the party
