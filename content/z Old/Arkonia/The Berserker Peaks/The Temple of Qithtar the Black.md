
Once a place of abyssal magic study and research, Qithtar is long dead, and so too are his assistants. Now how ancient place of study has been overtaken by the minions of Zyrathia, seeking to prevent the Young Wolves from learning of the Binding Ritual that could seal Arkonil in stone forever.

## Entrance:

The heavy stone doors groan open, revealing the oppressive atmosphere of the temple. The air is thick, almost choking, with the acrid scent of burning incense and something fouler, rotting decay mixed with the tang of magic. The chamber is vast, the light from your torches swallowed by shadows clinging to the high vaulted ceilings. Pillars carved with writhing serpents and eerie, hollow-eyed faces line the room like silent sentinels.

At the far end of the temple, your eyes are drawn to the massive carved stone face dominating the back wall. The face is grotesque yet captivating, with an expression frozen in a malevolent sneer. Its hollow eyes seem to bore into your very souls, and its gaping mouth seems to draw the ambient sound into itself, leaving the room disturbingly quiet apart from the echo of your footsteps.

Before the face kneels a figure clad in tattered black robes, its skeletal form unmistakable. The skeletal mage's bony fingers weave through the air in deliberate, jagged movements, as if clawing at reality itself. Its voice rasps out in dark incantations, the words in a guttural, ancient tongue that sends shivers down your spine. Shadows ripple unnaturally along the floor, swirling around the mage in a hypnotic dance.

The walls seem to pulse in response to the mage's chanting, as if the temple itself is alive, feeding on the forbidden ritual. Faint whispers echo from the stone face, impossible to discern but brimming with malice. You feel the weight of unseen eyes upon you, and the air grows colder with every step you take deeper into the room.

## Room 1:

In the giant square room with rounded corners are stone pillars holding the high vaulted ceiling, the pillars are carved into the shape of giants, holding the stone pillars on their shoulders like great feats of strength. In the center of the room is a large circular dais, with a strange 6ft tall headstone in the center.

You see a hulking tower of shadow and flame turn to face you, its abyssal wings and whip lashing out in fury as it slashes across the stone floors. There are winged devils, small pig like creatures, and bouncing little Quasits. A small horde of Manes, rotting zombie-like creatures shamble towards you, roll initiative!

**Enemies**: 1x [[Terra-Nova/Resources/Statblocks/Old Statblocks/Balor.png]], 1x [[Vrock.png]], 8x [[Manes.png]], 2x [[Dretch.png]], 3x [[Quasit.png]]

*The Headstone*. In the center of the keystone rests a keyhole (Inserting the key from the dragons horde opens the portcullis to Room 3). The rest of the headstone is covered in abyssal writing, describing something called, "The Path of Blood". It says the path is a way to seal a being in stone by destroying its essence through blood. The details are in [[Qithtar's Ritual of Binding]]

**West Doorway**. A gigantic stone doorway, a faint sickly green mist can be seen flowing from beneath it. It radiates a sickly sweet smell like that of rotting flesh and death.
**South Portcullis**. Behind a 50ft tall wrought iron portcullis sits before a triangular room, you see a stone altar holding a skull with green crystal eyes and two heavy stone coffins with runes on them. (Attempts to do anything to the portcullis reveal it is cursed, and interacting deals 1d6 necrotic damage.)


## Room 2:

As you travel down the stairs you see an open stone doorway and a gaping black pit of abyssal fog, an arcane force shoves you forward, forcing you onto the tiny shelf beyond the door as it slams shut. The room is a large cavern, small rocks and pillars stand above the fog, and a large undead dragon rotting flesh and wings, sits atop a horde.

The dragon stirs above its horde, a small pile of riches on a stone pillar, many kobolds, some winged and some clad in armor or robes, stir with undead fury, and a black dragonborn in shining silver armor rises and points a black bladed greataxe towards you menacingly, roll initiative!
