
This is a modified 5 room dungeon with 3 bosses, Genies. This dungeon is part of the Traxian Vault. There are 3 Genie Bosses: <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Ice Axe Warrior Genie</mark>, <mark style="background: #FFB86CA6;">Fire Mad Mage Genie</mark>, <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">Shadow Trickster Genie</mark>

The Ice has a big axe and fights melee with ice crystal summoning abilities for ranged. ice/ slashing damage
The Fire fights like a mage with big AOE spells and burning effects. fire/lightning damage
The Shadow fights using illusions and summoning minions like cloud elementals. radiant/necrotic damage

The entrance Guardian is a massive golem. 


## Entrance: The Guardian Golem

In the entrance of these ornately carved stone halls stands a massive statue of a silver dragon, his hands and tail spun forward and palms up, there are slots as if something must be inserted into each hand and the tail. On the base of the statue reads the words: "Be you worthy of the treasures held below, show your power over wishmakers, and bring me these lamps three."

**Enemies:** 1x [[elemental evoker golem.png]], 2x [[Elemental Cultist.png]]

*Loot.* Wish Resonance Gem (Vibrates slightly when you talk to someone who has a wish granted by a Genie)
