## Entrance: 

Li leads you to his boats, two small rowboats. He directs you along the coast. Stay low and follow the rocks, you'll see some kind of metal cave, that's your entrance. 

As you land you see the entrance, sealed shut by a thick brass sliding door. It is reminiscent of the Chronovault in the Temporal Plane where you met those mechanical dragons, but somehow more primitive. There are many pillars bearing grates, they look like exhaust pipes for releasing excess heat from machinery below. On the largest and center pillar is a large mechanical lever.

**Enemies:** 1x [[clockwork myrmidon.png]], 2x [[Clockwork Oaken Bolter.png]]

*Lever.* The center raised device holds a massive lever fixed to a golden gear. It takes more than one person to push upwards and activate (DC 24 Strength check), opening the door. When the level is pulled up, Clockwork enemies erupt from the machines, roll initiative!

## Puzzle/Roleplaying Challenge: The Gear Gauntlet

A bridge made of multi tiered moving gears, they spin and twist making it dangerous but necessary to cross. Other gears and devices spin above and through the air, making it impossible to simply fly past the bridge. The walls are vertical scaffolding that is slick with oil, impossible to hold on to. The entire crossing is 120ft. Beside you are a set of gears with spokes, almost like handles. You can grab them but you can only hold the gears still for so long.

In order to cross they need to run across the gears with an Athletics check (DC 18) and then a DEX saving throw (DC 17) to not be thrown off the gears as they move. Anyone who simply stands at the start of the bridge and lets the gears carry them will reach the other side unharmed, because that's funny (the gears just rotate and move them along the intended path easily).

Slow the gears with magic (time stuff, maybe suggest a chronal shift?) or stop them moving (strength for a time).

## Trick or Setback: The Brass Oracle

This room is a brass cathedral, vaulted ceilings like a church, but instead of a platform for a priest to speak on, there is a massive bronze face built on the back wall. Its eyes are spinning gears and it has a strange mechanically emotive face. The eyes watch you intently as you enter, and the door slams behind you.

*3 Riddles.* "You must answer 3 riddles to pass. Only those of sound mind may face our Guardian Hugo. ARE YOU PREPARED?"
- Riddle 1: I speak without a mouth, I judge without a face, I am feared by kings yet never leave my place. What am I? (Time)
- Riddle 2: I am always hungry, I must always be fed. The finger I touch will soon turn red. What am I? (Fire)
- Riddle 3: I have cities but no people, forests but no trees, rivers but no water. What am I? (Map)

## Trap: The Wild Gear

A long hallway stretches before you, the sound of a ticking pendulum echoes through the halls, the source seeming to be around the corner in the next room. The walls of this room are strange. The hallway is 100ft long and 5ft wide and 6ft tall, some of you will have to crouch to walk here.

The floors are lined with gears, some smaller (1ft diameter) and some larger (2ft diameter). The smaller gears are the pressure plates. In order to avoid stepping on 1ft diameter gears they have to go slowly and carefully, makes the hallway difficult terrain.
One gear beside the Bolt Gun is the single point of failure for the trap. Someone who reaches the end can

*Pressure Plate.* Smaller gears are pressure plates. Stepping on one triggers the Bolt Gun at the end of the hallway. If the player triggers the trap, notify them they step on one of the 1ft diameter gears, and it moves downward, causing the entire hallway to whir to life.

*Bolt Gun.* The turning gears of the hallway cause a loud thunk at the end of the hall. A massive brass bolt attached to a coiled metal cable fires down the hallway. DC 20 DEX saving throw. 8d10 piercing damage and knocked prone, half on a success and not prone.

*Bolt Reset.* After firing the bolt resets, the winch mechanism pulls the bolt back. Everyone in the hallway makes a DC 18 DEX saving throw or get clotheslined for 4d6 bludgeoning damage, half as much on a save.

## Climax, Big Battle, or Conflict: 

The final room is a massive clockwork hall built behind the face of a clock. A pendulum swings along the inside of the clock face. perched atop the pendulum unmoving is a Knight of similar fashion. He wears armor forged in brass gears, and a hammer made of machinery that hums with faint electricity. As you enter he raises the hammer, slamming the pendulum frozen in place, and leaps into the air carried by brass wings. Roll initiative!

**Enemies:** 1x [[hugo, the last clockwork knight.png]], 4x [[Clockwork Oaken Bolter.png]], 3x clockwork bird
