## Room 2: Rune Pillars (Puzzle)

*3 Pillars*. In the room stand 3 large circular pillars, each bearing a different rune. One reads fire in primordial, one reads life in celestial, and one reads death in abyssal. Each pillar needs a specific kind of leveled spell cast on it (Cantrips fizzle out, seeming to ignite something in the rune but not enough to maintain its activation)
- Fire: A spell that deals fire damage
- Life: A spell that heals hit points
- Death: A spell that deals necrotic damage
