Ancient and mysterious, these tunnels and catacombs were constructed over many years by the ancient first Lord of Ironpetal, the known arch mage and vampire, Cain. Cain was known to dabble in blood magics, and Ironpetal has stood for many hundreds of years, what mysteries could this hold?

The mechanisms in the catacombs are powered by great arcane batteries, which were charged by an ancient Aelarian arcane engine. The party has information on this kind of engine from Catherine's office in Ironpetal. This particular engine is powered by internal steam systems, which run on a piece of Thrynax's soul. Thrynax's Soul has been stolen by Catherine Rex, and thus has been replaced with Bloodwing: Bloodwing now powers the Colossus, the Guardian of the catacombs.

What they don't know is that the machine Catherine is working on is actually a retrofit of the original design of a Celestial Malefactor (a god killing weapon) that the Aelarians were building in secret. Catherine has built another and is planning to use it instead of killing a god, to try and make a Pseudo-God.

**So what's what**?
- Catacombs are protected by the Colossus, who has Bloodwing in its chest.
- The engine is powered by Thrynax's flame, which will be sealed into baby Dante when Catherine's machine ritual is completed.

**Colossus Boss Ideas**:
- Big metal warrior construct
- Has Bloodwing in its chest in like a glass energy core
- While Bloodwing in, it has blood dragon abilities
- When enough damage done, Bloodwing chamber breaks and it falls out, has its own statblock and can be killed, Colossus enters phase 2
- Phase 2 the boss will activate a teleporter and will say a voice line similar to "High Threat level, systems critical, hyper lethal protocol authorized, first law, PROTECT THE WEAPON"

## Room 1: Entryway

Like the entrance to a bank or magic vault, a large circular door made of bronze and glass pipes blocks the way forward. Machines and engines can be heard echoing from the room beyond, and if you look through the glass pipes you can barely make out some kind of large rectangular crystal in the next room.
*The Table*: On the west side of the room there is a large metal and mechanical console of some kind. It has a single large lever (DC 30 Athletics Check to pull), and a square shaped hole indented into the table by about 6 inches. (When a control cube is inserted into the console it opens the North Door. When the cube is removed the door closes.)

**North Door**. Leads to the Control Room.


## Room 2: Control Room

With walls of solid greenish Adamantine and beautiful obsidian floors, this massive chamber has ceilings painted with beautiful scenes of Aelarian make. There are pillars of solid celestial gold, and in the center of the room another console with a large screen.
On the large screen is a display with three machines pictured, a large system of pipes that appears to gather steam from heat chambers, and a pair of engines labeled "Celestial Malefactor". One is labeled as decommissioned and the other is active, and seems to be on.
The northern door is sealed shut, and hundreds of small bronze pipes run up the back wall from the doorframe onto the ceiling and towards the east door.

*The Central Console*: In the center of the long room is a large central console with two massive levers and another control cube slot in the center. The right lever glows red, the left glows blue.
- **Using the Red Cube**: The Red Cube allows you to open the East Door, it does not grant administrator permissions.
- **Using the Blue Cube**: The Blue Cube allows you to open the West Door, it grants administrator permissions to the system database. See information below.
*The Catacomb Database*: The central database contains the information from lead developer Tolith Cain, an Aelarian magic researcher. His notes are on a device he led construction of known as, "The Celestial Malefactor". They were developing a powerful arcane disruptor that can target celestial energy. Testing suggest it can easily destroy lesser angels and possibly minor gods, but may need additional charging time or a secondary power source in addition to Thrynax's Soul Shard to kill a full Divine. 
He states that the Colossus will protect the machine, and due to their depth and pact with Khal, the gods will not detect their work. He talks about how he regrets his involvement with the God in Glass and his, "Project Last Sun". (Have them roll intelligence to determine if it is Sun or Son, DC 25), but he needed his help to get celestials to test on. 
He warns about the removal of Thrynax's soul, and that it could easily destroy a large part of the continent, or perhaps rebirth the dragon if freed.
There is also a 756 page long set of blueprints for the machine, though it is impossible to discern its function and the nature of many components.

**West Door**. Leads to the Colossus Chamber.
**East Door**. Leads to the Steam Harvester. 


## Room 3: The Steam Harvester

A large mechanical chamber, the entire room is hot like the inside of a machine. There are multiple buttons on the floor, a stairway to a raised platform, and two large side rooms with massive glass windows. Inside the glass rooms are two large black and red scaled beasts (Volcanic Drakes). At the top of the stairs is a console depicting that all harvested energy besides crucial life support has been diverted the experimentation chamber, where the second active machine was said to be in the Control Room. In the center of the console is a red glowing Control Cube.
*Button Puzzle*: **(ABDFCE)** There are labeled buttons, they have to enter them in the correct order. If they get it wrong two times, the chambers for the drakes will open. Roll initiative! The correct combo opens the Northeast Door.
*Enemies*: 2x Volcanic Drake
*Loot*: Red Control Cube.

**West Door**. Leads back to the Control Room.
**Northeast Door**. Leads to the Energy Storage Room.


## Room 4: The Colossus Chamber

The source of most of the loud mechanical sounds, inside can be seen many large pillars, and a pair of moving mechanical beings. They operate machines across the walls of the room, sweep the floors, and maintain the structure. Upon your entrance they cease all other function, the door slams shut behind you, "Intruders detected, engage the Colossus." They are like raptors with large back legs, a slim body with little arms, and a large mechanical mouth and eyeball int heir heads.
As they echo their chant in Aelarian, a massive mechanical guardian with a slick green Adamantine body rises from the floor on the south end of the room, Roll Initiative!
*Drone Manufacturing Chamber*:  The north and south alcoves hold two diases with many mechanical arms and other machinery. They animate violently, immediately pulling material from converyo belts on the back wall, beginning to assemble something. (Produces warforged drones during the boss combat. Can be destroyed using 2 melee attacks AC 15, or an AoE spell.)
*Steam Vents*: The metal vents on the ground are for steam ventilation, they activate at the top of the round, dealing 1d8 fire damage to anyone (not the colossus) standing within 5ft of them as burning hot steam shoots out of them.
*Enemies*: 1x Colossus, 4x [[warforged beast.png]], (If not deactivated, 2x [[warforged drone.png]] at the top of each round, initiative count 20.)
When the Colossus dies, it will teleport away to the Celestial Malefactor, emitting the message in Aelarian, "System integrity critical, revert to backup power. Defend the Weapon."

**East Door**. Leads back to the Control Room.
**South Door**. Leads to the Celestial Malefactor.


## Room 5: The Celestial Malefactor

A massive silent engine chamber, the gigantic machine with many pyramid  shaped coils of brass wire protrude from the central glass tube, a small hatch in the center lies open, and empty. You notice that the many glass tubes and brass wires running from the top of the machine has been destroyed. (DC 18 invest, they were melted, destroying them).

*The Engine*: Putting a control cube in the engine allows you to activate it, although it no longer functions, it will draw power, which lengthens the amount of time Catherine's ritual takes.
*Enemies*: 1x Phase Two Colossus 

**South Door**. Leads to the Lexicon


## Room 6: The Energy Storage Room

This large room has a low 10ft high ceiling, there are six octagonal tanks of tempered glass filled with a white hot steam. Placing your hand on the glass deals 1d4 fire damage, singing your flesh. 
*Steam Tanks*: Each of the tanks las a large metal control screen, with a slot for a control cube. Placing a red or blue cube in the tanks causes a message to appear on the screen, the rough translation is, "Expunge?" If so, the steam is released from the tanks into the caves above, and the Colossus will not enter its second phase.

## Room 8: Catherine's Engine

The largest chamber in the catacombs, two large marble staircases rise on the north and south sides. There is a 15ft raised platform, with a small black stone altar, and a second massive machine, similar to the last, but larger, more pipes, and currently active. Gigantic mechanical arms spin across the ceiling as the room roars with the sound of arcane engineering. Behind the altar stands a woman clad in red, a long thin curved blade on her back, her hand on the shoulder of a smaller masked figure, facing you. Antigone and Catherine Rex. The altar glows red as [[Catherine Rex]] turns to face you, a dome of blood red glass appears around the unmoving mass of red cloth that sits on the table.

**Dialogue**. 
- So... for the uncountable numbers of times I have been hunted, Kings, Knights, Hunters, mercenaries, the list goes on, finally, I am caught by the wolves.
- I am pleased, Antigone did so want to see you again before you died. I am sorry as well, you had only the best on intentions, but here is where you die. 
- Your blood will fuel the birth of the Lightbearer, be proud your filthy veins to satiate the hunger of the new demi-god. Behold, my beautiful Dante. (She unveils the child from its cloth, it immediately begins to wail and cry as the machine fires bolts of red energy into it.)
- You are the DIRT BENEATH MY BOOTS! LUCKY TO BE WALKED UPON, and now it is MY TURN to RULE!
- Roll initiative!

*The Vents*: The vents emit steam on their tiles, dealing 1d6 fire damage to people on the at the top of the round, they are not active if the steam storage is disabled.
*The Altar*: On the altar is Dante, the baby. The dome around it has an AC of 18 and 50HP. If broken the engine will go haywire, roll on the wild magic table to see the effects of the uncontrolled engine.
*The Machine*: In the center of the machine, behind layers of pipes and other workings, lies a small chamber inside of which is a glowing red flame. 
- The Machine has 16 AC and 200 HP.
- Every 50 HP lost causes an explosion, DC 16 DEX save, 1d8 lightning damage to everyone on the second floor. 
- Removing Thrynax's Soul is a DC 16 Slight of Hand check, on a success you become EMPOWERED, gaining 50 Temporary HP, and sprouting draconic wings for 1 minute, gaining a flight speed of 60ft. If you hold Thrynax's soul for more than 1 hour, you gain 2 levels of exhaustion. The flame can be removed by your own will to do so.
- Inserting a control cube with the flame will allow you to disable the machine, stopping the ritual.
*The Ritual*: The ritual requires all parts to be intact, there is a counter for that goes up to 10 (15 if they activate the old engine), the counter increases in the following ways:
- +1 at the start of each round
- +1 for each creature that dies in the room (Flavor: As the creatures falls to the ground dead, the soul flies from its body in wicked tendrils and into the dome, passing into the skin of the now screaming baby.)
- +5 for a PC death
- -2 for every 50HP the machine loses

*Enemies*: [[catherine (1).png]], [[antigone rex.png]], 2x [[giant bat.png]], 6x [[skeleton.png]]
- Strategy: Catherine wants to isolate someone within her blood dome, maximum two people, while Antigone focuses down the rest buffing the bats and making them weaker or make saves. Catherine above ALL ELSE wants to protect Dante, the baby, even if it means ending the ritual.
- If Catherine is losing, going to die, or they are close to stopping the ritual, she will immediately get Dante and cast Teleport, trying to leave. 
- Should Catherine die, Zyrathia will arrive and steal the child.
- If the ritual finishes, the machine will go into overload, exploding at the start of the next round, describe it going haywire and make it obvious that it's about to blow up. Catherine will immediately try to escape with Dante.
