A large throne room with high vaulted ceilings and ornate tapestries across the walls, bearing a raised platform with stairs leading to where the throne would be, but instead there is a grand piano. Sitting at the piano is Scarlett Rex, daughter of Catherine and sister of Antigone. Upon entry the door slams shut behind you, a thick coating of blood flowing down from the top of the doorway covering the handles and blocking your exit as it solidifies.
Scarlett continues playing the piano as she takes note of your entry. 
- Welcome to Veinspire Castle dogs. We don't have to fight just yet, I do enjoy a talk before dinner. Dead men tell no lies after all. DO you like the throne room? Mother had prepared it for her battle with you but... now she is preoccupied with her eldest and youngest, leaving you with the middle child, don't be too disappointed.
- My mother always hated kings and lords. They were dumb, they think with their swords and their cocks. No idea of legacy, of the future, they lived too well and died too quickly to understand generational power. Vampires though, we live long enough to measure our success in centuries.
- Did you know the first king who ruled here was 75 years old when he ascended to the throne? My mother tore that throne out and had it destroyed. He and it represented the old way. The days of bitter and violent old men ruling over all of us are over. 
- The rest of the thrones are on the chopping block, Ivar, Adrithyr, Kagerra, and Illuman. No more dynasty, no more inheritance, only rules chosen to rule because they are capable. The rules my mother will bring to this world with the help of the Lightbearer.
- House Rex will destroy Arkonil and bring a new age of darkness and prosperity for vampire kind we will allow the Betrayers to restore their glory and retake the heavens, and you cannot stop us. 
- Your friend Antigone will sing the song of the end, the catastrophe, everything you love will burn. In those ashes a new age will begin. So many traitors in your midst...
- I have just one question Young Wolves. Where on these walls would you like your blood painted, like your poor dead elf bitch Melantha?"

A long dark hallway that has 3 doors on its northern side. It turns north at its end.

**Door D**. A storage room of extra weapons and armor. There is a large hole in the ground that appears to have a ladder leading down many hundreds of feet. This is the entrance to the catacombs, following it leads to [[Cain's Catacombs]]. 

**Door E**. A closet of cooking supplies and excess furniture. In a corner is a faintly glowing light beneath some chairs.
If they investigate, they find the decrepit body of a warforged ([[Centus]]).

There is a large dark wood desk along the back wall, and in the corner beside it a small chest on a side table.
