
As you approach what should be a glorious Cloud Giant society, the high ceiling pantheons and beautiful marble architecture, you are instead greeted with ruins. The road ahead, leading to the massive stone arched entrance to the lower level of the city is shattered.

## Entrance

Before you are shoddy wooden bridges, seemingly built by smaller humanoids as they clearly do not have enough room for giants to traverse. The shattered bridge ahead consists of a few remaining standing stone platforms connected by rickety wooden bridges. There are unlit circular braziers on two of the platforms. It is cold, an almost deathly chill hangs in the air, as if the warmth of this place had been forcefully ripped out.
