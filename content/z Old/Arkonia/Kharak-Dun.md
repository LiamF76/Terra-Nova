
Kharak-Dun, the ancient ancestral forge temple of the orcs in Escarth. This is a sacred site for their people, being the place where Urzoth herself forged the first weapons of dragon and giant slaying that the orcs used to take the Berserker Peaks as their homelands.

## The Bridge

As you walk to the end of the road you catch sight of Kharak-Dun, a small stone castle with a massive jagged and precarious bridge leading from small island to small island, peppered with tall stone watchtowers. 
Upon reaching the forest, you can clearly see a small group of orcs dotting the islands, standing guard with their shiny new steel weapons. Additionally there are some strange creatures atop the towers, a weird mutation of flesh and eldritch tentacles along their arms.

**Enemies**: 4x [[blightcannon.png]], 6x [[orc soldier.png]], 1x [chimera](https://www.aidedd.org/dnd/monstres.php?vo=chimera) (arrives after 2 rounds)
Flavor! As the mutant raises its arms the tentacles reach out and wrap its tow arms together forming a large almost cannon, and crackling black energy bursts outwards towards you!

*Towers*. Can enter towers, takes 15ft of movement to reach the top.

# The Underground

Descending the stairs into the temple below, you find no signs or ruin or decay. The walls are of fine carved and hewn stone that is still warm to the touch, the forge within this place must still burn with the fire of the first Orc Queen.

### Room 1:

A narrow ledge runs along the upper edges of the room, with a small trench of flowing molten liquid in its gulley.

**West Doorway**. A heavy iron door, it is stuck shut. DC 25 to break open, 60HP.
**East Doorway**. An open hall leading to a steep set of stone stairs.
