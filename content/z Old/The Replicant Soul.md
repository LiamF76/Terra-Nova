Ivys, as your vision starts to fade and you fall unconscious, you feel them open again, a killer headache quickly fades as you awaken lying down in a gigantic empty space. There is a smooth stone tile floor, a completely white sky, and a man floating in front of you, meditating. He wears the clean black and gold robes with white trimmings, you recognize this to be Kairos, as he turns slowly, hovering about a foot off the ground.

He looks you up and down, a look of disdain covers his face. His hair and face have changed slightly, affected by the time spent in the Infinite Void. He has white hair shaved on the sides and longer going backwards. His skin has turned slightly gray and he looks less human than he did before. 

Kairos: 
- "I gave you my intellect and you still aren't smart enough to stay alive, or is this just another manifestation of your childish need to rebel against the inevitable?"
- "I've been meditating, this place is a limit I have not surpassed, but I can still project myself and research, I am searching inward now."
- "You need me. I may be trapped here, but you are a replicant soul. This barrier may stop me from leaving, but not the power already within you."
- "If you want me to save you and that metal moron of yours, you'll agree to my terms."
- "I will grant you my power, but I want your familiar, I will inhabit it with mine. It will obey you totally, but it will be my serpent, and I will see through it as well. A welcome and earned distraction from this prison within a prison."
- "Every time you harness my power it will take a great toll upon you. You will slowly return to me the longer you access it, and you can only do so when I allow it."
- "Haha, yes, I will grant you my power, and my insights. You will understand the price, and suffer for your desperation." He floats down in front of you, his feet just barely not touching the ground, and holds out his hand. "It's a deal."
