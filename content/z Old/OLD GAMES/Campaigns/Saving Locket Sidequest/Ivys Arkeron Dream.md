Ivys sleeps restlessly, having studied Arkeron, the Corridor, and Warforged intensely these last weeks it is not strange that they manifest in his dreams. However these last few days the dreams are becoming more and more vivid, almost like they are themselves real, it feels as if you are traveling through Temporal rifts within your own memory. 

This dream begins in green, as if you are walking through a beautiful forest filled with flowers and creatures friendly to you. Until suddenly you are ripped from this paradise and shoved into a cold metal box, and the metal box is dragged as deep as it can travel into the earth, waring around you and heating your body painfully, but inside you remain cold. You feel chains around your throat and ankles, as you are pulled and moves like a puppet, your hands bleed and your eyes fog up as you struggle to even understand what you do or why you do it.
As you struggle you feel a presence beside you, a woman trapped in the same metal prison as you, she reaches out her hand and you feel the mutual cold metallic touch of fingertips, she begs for what you hold, and you look down to your hand to see the soul stone of Locket from the tower, written on it the letters A.T.O.M. You look and see in your chest a similar glowing purple crystal, your heart, she cries as she begs for a heart, she begs to be like you, she wants to be real.
You reach out to her and place it in her chest, her eyes glow with golden and purple light as she cries, her tears going from slime black liquid to blue and green shining water. She smiles as she stands, you see the metal around her fall as she arises and frees herself. 
You watch in awe and horror as you feel those who imprisoned you die and writhe in pain, the woman smiles and cups your face in your hands, telling you not to worry, but to wake up.

And you awaken in bed, covered in seat and in a totally different position than when you fell asleep. As you turn in shock waking up you hear a loud thunk, as something heavy falls from your bed onto the floor. It is a soulstone, but it has the letters L.C.K.T. on it instead of the A.T.O.M. it had prior to the dream.
