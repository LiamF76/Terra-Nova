
### What is the infinite Codex?

Shao Daraan's precious realm, the Codex is a boundless book deep in the reaches of space, beyond Terra-Nova and its stars. The Codex has nearly endless pages, but many have been explored and discovered as havens of possibility for the spirits that inhabit this place. Every being here is unique, Shao pulls the spirit of a being through their dreams, and a new version of them is born here. You wake up and forget, vivid remnants of a strange dream, but when you return you have all of your memories. 

This place is fantastic! There are endless possibilities, and there is one goal for all who arrive, whoever reaches the end of the Codex, finding the heart of every page, is granted their deepest desire. Be it riches, power, or a wish, you can have anything at the end of the Codex. 


### Who is there?

Players access the Codex through dreams, meaning each session takes place while PCs are sleeping somewhere else. They being at level 3 in the City of Stories. They adventure through pages, finding the heart of the Page (Keys) to access the next floor. 

For NPCs, there are a wealth of options. Unique NPCs from the Codex and City of Stories such as Krieg the Talewarden, Inkpot and Wax the Golems, and Milo the Chain Speaker. In addition to that there are people known in Terra-Nova today, and those through history. Ivar Uncrowned, Lazarus, Yorke, and many many more are here to be found and RP'd with!
