## The Second Page

Hello and welcome to another exciting installment of the Infinite Codex! I am your humble dungeon master and you are my daring dreamers, say hi daring dreamers! In our last session you all entered the Infinite Codex for the first time, and we defeated a rogue narrative in the form of a vengeful angry Augustus who attempted to take his revenge out on Shao Daraan! As you were victorious you felt yourselves awaken, and your memory of this place faded, but it returns as if no time had passed.

*You awaken... though your bodies never truly moved.*

The stone streets of the City of Stories are gone like a finished chapter. In their place is **a canvas of parchment**, stretching into a horizon of ink and vellum hills. The sky is a muted cream, and overhead, the clouds swirl like spilled ink in water—constantly reshaping into fleeting symbols and forgotten words.

The **air smells of musty tomes and candle wax**, and with every breath you take, you feel a faint static, like your thoughts themselves are being cataloged.

Around you, the world begins to clarify, as if a story is just now being written around your presence.

A massive stone gate rises in the distance—towering, monolithic, and half-sunken into the pale hills. Ivy grows from the cracks of its arch, but the runes etched into its face pulse with a soft violet glow. This is not a ruin. This is a place that remembers.

Nearby, at the edge of a gently sloping hill, a weathered signpost leans forward as though eager to speak. Its ink is faded, but legible:

**"Page 2 — The Breathing Library: Repository of Forgotten Truths."**

The earth is soft beneath your feet—fibrous like paper. Each footstep leaves a faint trail of ink that fades moments after you move on.

The gate to the Library looms ahead, sealed… but waiting.

Beside you there is a belabored cough, [[Krieg]] stands leaning on his staff, coughing and tapping his chest.
- "Apologies friends, this page wreaks havoc upon my airways. Ever since I shared a battle with the Red Prince, my lungs have been quite sensitive!"
- "Welcome to the second page, and your second test, you've begun an exciting and rewarding journey."
- He swirls his staff, conjuring a magical image for the Infinite Codex, the astral book that Shao Daraan claims as his realm. "These are the pages of the Codex, and you are bid to travel them. Each page has gateways, from one page to the next, but to open them your group must obtain a key. These keys can be items, magic, anything, but you cannot proceed without it."

## Enter the Breathing Library

The entrance is a tall arch of obsidian inkstone, etched with glowing script. At its base is a three-sided pedestal. Floating above each side is a **quill**: one **silver**, one **gold**, one **inky black**.

The arch bears a magical riddle inscribed in glowing letters, which fade unless read aloud.

#### The Door Puzzle

<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Step 1: Quill Selection</mark>
Players must insert **one of the three floating quills** into the pedestal socket.
- 1. **Silver Quill:** When held you see a vision of a library.
- 2. **Gold Quill:** When held you see a vision of a battlefield.
- 3. **Inky Black Quill:** When held you see a vision of someone telling a story.

<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Step 2: Glyph Alignment</mark>
The ink forms three 10ft wide glyphs on the ground:
- 1. **Glyph of Knowledge** (a scroll)
- 2. **Glyph of Power** (a sword)
- 3, **Glyph of Memory** (an open eye)

**Correct Solution:**
The players must **step on one glyph each** while holding the corresponding numbered quill. If more than one player steps on the same glyph, it glows red and resets. The party must **spread their choices** and align with the "true voice of time."

<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Step 3: Spoken Truth</mark>
A final phrase appears in glowing ink:

*"Speak an unwritten truth."*

Each player must **speak aloud a truth about their character** they’ve not shared before (roleplay opportunity!). A PC who hesitates or lies (DM discretion) is briefly silenced by a band of magical ink for 1 minute.

Once all players have spoken their truth, the ink swirls upward, forming a keyhole. The inky quill, now transformed, serves as a temporary **Key of Passage**.
You feel spurred forward as you hold the key, you know how to enter, you must write your greatest wish, whatever you hope to achieve in the Infinite Codex, on the door, and the way will open.

As the final glyph hums and fades, the massive stone doors creak open with a sound like a thousand pages turning in unison. A soft wind pushes outward—warm, dry, and heavy with the scent of old parchment, leather bindings, and a faint trace of ink.

## The Halls of the Archive

Inside lies a vast chamber of darkness and golden light. Rows upon rows of bookshelves stretch to impossible heights, disappearing into the swirling, ink-stained clouds overhead. The architecture warps with subtle surrealism—shelves twist in spirals, staircases curve sideways and upward, and the very shadows seem to twitch when you’re not looking.

Lanterns float like fireflies, casting gentle pools of amber light. Books flutter from shelf to shelf like birds, reshelving themselves. In the silence, you hear faint whispers—not malevolent, but as if the books themselves are sharing secrets with the air.

Somewhere within this ancient place, guarded by long-forgotten enchantments, lies the **key**—a literal or symbolic token—that will unlock the **gateway to the next page** of the Infinite Codex.

Let them take in the beauty for a moment ask them:
- Has your character ever seen anything like this?
- What's the first thing they would think of to look for here?
- Are they excited to be here or not, and why?

As you take in the beauty of this place for a moment, you hear a faint flitting of paper in the air. Near the entryway is a floating scrap of parchment, which zips away as the players approach, drawing them deeper into the library.
You approach and it darts away from you, spinning at the end of a hall of bookshelves and dashing around a corner.

#### Chase the Parchment

As the parchment starts to escape, do you chase after it? If so how?

Have them make athletics checks to run, acrobatics checks to jump over tables and bookshelves, and perception to keep track of the parchment.
- **Athletics (DC 14):** Chase the parchment until it reaches the next area.
- **Acrobatics (DC 13):** Leap over the bookshelves and tables to catch the parchment.
- **Perception (DC 12):** Keep track of the parchment and follow its path.

As the parchment reaches a strange place, a spiral of moving floating crystal orbs shining with light and thousands of floating stacks of books, it begins to fly upwards and away from you. 
- **Arcana (DC 14):** Identify the magical pattern of the floating objects and use them for momentum.
- **Dexterity (Sleight of Hand, DC 13):** Grab onto a levitating book to launch yourself forward.

The parchment finally darts through a pair of large wooden doors and stops, hovering above a landing and some stairs.

## The Bookwyrm Nest

As you follow the parchment through the doors you begin to hear a soft hissing, like little serpents or dragons... The doors slam shut as 4 small paper dragons erupt from the bookshelves around you, the parchment led you directly to their nest! Roll Initiative!

**Enemies.** 4x [[bookwyrm.webp]] <- 2x more spawn every round, 1x Magic Tome

*Magic Tome*. AC 14 30HP. The magic tome is the controller of the Bookwyrms. It is on a bookshelf hidden. Seeking it out reveals it.
The first party member to get close to the bookshelves hears a faint ringing, the sound of rustling pages. Its as if one of the bookshelves on the wall is moving.

*Loot.* 
- [[Brass Scroll]]

After defeating the Bookwyrms, the doors open once again, though the exit looks different. You get a strange feeling, the bookshelves acquire a fresh sheen, you feel something watching you and can sense a presence move through the shelves before disappearing out the door.

## The Innocent Intern

As you exit the nest you find yourselves in another hall, leading to a room with many tables and lamps, shelves lining the walls, it appears to be a study room of some kind. You hear someone singing, a ballad, albeit the voice is awful, but they are reciting tales of heroes and warriors.

As you enter you see a young boy, human male, he smiles as you enter, removing his reading glasses as he looks up from a tome, *"Welcome patrons! Thank you for visiting the Breathing Library! I'm Maxim, and if you need help finding anything do not hesitate to ask!"*

- *"Hm? I work here! Krieg offered me the position after I finished my schooling at the Quillspire Academy. I'm enjoying it a lot!"*
- *"I've been reading about moons made of black water, jellyfish magicians, machine soldiers that wage wars against people made of slime, everything here is so strange and wonderful!"*
- *"I always wanted more time to read, now I have it!"*
- *"Pontius correct? Thank you for visiting, this is for you."* He hands you a small tome titled **"The Chronicle of the Nameless Legionary"**. He smiles, *"I heard it's about you! I hope you enjoy it, very heroic. Oh, and there is a gift inside. Oh and don't forget, let Kaelith guide you!"*
- *"Well you had best get going, the guardian is waiting for you down that hall, best of luck travelers! See you in the pages!"*

(Kaelith is the constellation of the Bridge, a guide between worlds and realms.)
Pontius, when you open the book you find a small key with a string inside, it is warm to the touch.

## The Gateway and the Guardian

The room beyond is vast and rectangular, a grand, domed observatory hidden within the library’s deepest folds. Walls of towering bookshelves spiral upward like the rings of a planet, and at the back wall stands a massive **astral device**: the **gateway to the next page**, its brass rings slowly rotating in impossible directions, casting glimmers of star-like light across the polished floor. The ceiling overhead is painted with constellations, many of which begin to subtly shift as you watch — as though reacting to your presence.

But the calm does not last.

From the shadows between the shelves, a thick splash of black ink seeps across the floor, spreading like a spilled secret. It lifts and twists, taking the form of a lithe, vulpine creature with a two long brush-tipped tail that drips endless blackness — a **fox of ink and parchment colored fur**.

Its eyes shimmer with silver curiosity and mischief. It speaks without sound, its words blooming in your minds like poetry on parchment:

*"You’ve done well page-turners. But this story is not yet ready for its end. Not until you’ve bled a little more ink."*

With a flick of its tail, globs of shadow peel from the air and solidify — **Inklings**, writhing and fluttering like corrupted words come alive.

The Astrolabe pulses, the exit beckoning — but it is **not yet open**. You will have to **fight**, and you will have to **earn** your escape. Roll initiative!

**Enemies.** 1x [[inktail fox.webp]], summons 2x [[inkling.png]]

*The Gateway.* 
- The Astrolabe pulses at initiative count 20, causing books to fall and stuff, players make saves to dodge or take damage.
- Ink begins to crawl up the walls, the Fox and Astrolabe pulse in unison, as if the Fox is drawing power from the device.
- DC 14 check to interact with the Astrolabe as an action, can manipulate the settings to point to Kaelith the Bridge Constellation.
- When successfully set to Kaelith, the fox will be weakened, halving its current HP.

*Loot.* Enspelled Armor ([Resistance](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/spell:resistance))
