
_“Laughter rings eternal in a land that forgot how to stop.”_

A page in the Infinite Codex that loops like a song stuck on the final, jubilant note — **The Endless Carnival** is a demi-plane of revelry, illusion, and quietly unraveling minds.

The moment one steps onto this page, reality begins to shimmer under stage lights. The sky is a velvet curtain of stars, never changing, never dawning. The air is thick with the scent of caramel, burning oil, and something… sour beneath it. Pipe organs and whistling calliopes play endlessly from nowhere and everywhere.

Color is more vibrant here, as though the plane itself refuses to fade. Ferris wheels spin without riders. Firework bursts of confetti erupt from invisible points above. Parade floats glide down cobbled paths pulled by beasts made of ribbon and bone. At a glance, it’s festive, even magical.

But look closer, and you’ll see:
- Carnival barkers with masks they never remove.
- Performers who repeat the same show, word-for-word, until their mouths bleed.
- Patrons who laugh too hard. Cry while smiling. Dance when no music plays.
- A funhouse mirror that doesn’t reflect you — but the version of you that never left.

The Carnival is ruled by a capricious and theatrical tyrant: the **Carnival King**, a being of pure performative madness who sees everything as a stage, and everyone as players in **his eternal show**. He alone holds the **Heart of the Page**, the key to progressing to the next leaf of the Codex.

Time doesn’t pass in the Endless Carnival — or if it does, it loops, resets, or rewrites itself when you’re not looking. For many who arrive, the challenge isn’t escaping… it’s _remembering that they wanted to._

By day the Carnival is a sight to behold, a vibrant festival with games to play and delicious foods to be eaten. Performers do tricks and beast tamers showcase their magical creatures. But by night, things get dangerous. The carnival workers masks twist into evil grins with sharpened teeth. Be ready for a fight, for every person killed by the carnival means another party goer is bound for eternity to the page, much to the delight of the Carnival King.



## Carnival Games

#### 🎈 1. **Balloon of Secrets**

A stall run by a silent clown with a stitched mouth. Rows of floating balloons hover above a table of sharp, silver darts.

- **Mechanic:** Each player throws a dart (Dex check DC 13). Popping a balloon reveals a **secret**, whispered only to that player — a truth about another character, an NPC, or something bigger...
- **Twist:** One balloon contains a secret **about the Carnival King** — or about the player’s own past they forgot.
- **Optional Curse:** One balloon is rigged and forces a Wisdom save or be haunted by laughter (Disadvantage on Insight checks for an hour).

---

#### 🐤 2. **Duckpond of Doom**

An enchanted pool filled with rubber ducks. Each duck has a rune on the bottom.

- **Mechanic:** Pull a duck, roll a d6:
    1. Cursed: Take 2d6 psychic damage.
    2. Spooked: Disadvantage on next saving throw.
    3. Clue: Gain a cryptic hint about the page.
    4. Lucky Duck: Recover a spell slot or HP.
    5. Soul Duck: It speaks a name the player doesn’t know but feels important.
    6. Prize Token: Can be exchanged for a consumable Codex item.

---

#### 🎯 3. **Ring the Jester’s Bell**

A classic high striker — you swing a giant candy-colored hammer to ring the bell.

- **Mechanic:** Strength (Athletics) check DC 18.
- **Prize:** Ring it, and you get a **boon** — temporary HP, advantage on a saving throw, or a strange blessing from a watching entity.
- **Twist:** Fail badly (natural 1), and a jester’s spirit leaps into your shadow and mocks your actions for the rest of the day (DM provides unhelpful or creepy commentary).

---

#### 👁️ 4. **Guess the Illusion**

A series of three spinning illusions — faces, landscapes, monsters. Guess which one is the real image.

- **Mechanic:** Insight or Investigation (DC 15).
- **Consequence:** Guess wrong, and the illusion grabs you and pulls you in (minor combat or puzzle sequence).
- **Reward:** Guess right three times in a row and you get a rare trinket or info from the illusions.

---

#### 🪞 5. **Mirror Maze Duel**

A solo challenge: Enter the Hall of Mirrors and face yourself.

- **Mechanic:** Inside the maze, each PC is confronted by a twisted version of themselves (mirror clone — same stats but taunting personality). Defeating it may require clever use of personal knowledge or weaknesses.
- **Reward:** Gain inspiration, or +1 to a single ability score for the duration of the page.

---

#### 🍬 6. **Taffy Tug-of-War**

Giant sticky ropes made of sentient magical taffy. You compete against enchanted homunculi or another party member.

- **Mechanic:** Opposed Strength or Acrobatics checks.
- **Twist:** The taffy becomes sentient halfway through and tries to _eat the winner_.

---

#### 🎡 7. **The Wheel of Woe and Wonder**

A huge spinning wheel operated by a blindfolded oracle.

- **Mechanic:** Spin the wheel. Roll a d20:
    
    - 1–5: Woe (Debuff, minor curse, lost item)
    - 6–15: Wonder (Healing, buffs, gold)
    - 16–19: Surprise (weird but harmless effects)
    - 20: Wishlight — a one-time magical effect of the player’s choosing, within reason.

#### NIGHT FALLS

As night falls on the carnival people begin to make their way to the big top, the only safe place at night, where you can watch and clap instead of be subjected to the Carnival King's games.



## The Performance

The party is ushered into the Big Top after playing carnival games and exploring. Golden confetti and fireworks erupt as you are all shoved into the tent and shoved by workers onto the wooden platform in the center. A crowd of people, some masked workers, some faceless spirits, and others seemingly normal mortals, surrounds you cheering with a little excitement for the penultimate act.

"WELCOME ONE AND ALL TO THE GRAND BIG TOP!!!! OUR FAIR HEROES TODAY WILL FACE OUR BELOVED CONSTRUCT, THE EMBODIMENT OF MERRYMAKING, THE CULMINATION OF JOY, THE PAINTED... MAWWWWWWWWWWW!"

From beneath the stage, the drums thunder — and out slithers the beast. Its body is a tapestry of smeared face paint, stitched carnival costumes, and gnashing teeth. It screams in five voices: laughter, weeping, growling, singing... and yours.

This monstrous snake of masks and carnival tapestries enters the battlefield with you, roll initiative!
