
The party is summoned by [[Krieg]], a Talewarden of the Codex. Talewardens are wizards bound to the Codex, whose mission is to protect the codex and act as protectors of the citizens of the book. They summon chosen individuals to the Codex and guide them to traverse and search the pages of the Codex.

There is a rampant issue, stories that were never written and stories that were are being twisted by the presence of someone who should not exist. An evil remnant of an unwritten story, the spirit is someone from history, but this version of them has a different ending than what should be, and it is causing problems in the city. 

Krieg gives them some weapons and gear to enter the city, says their best place to start is at the Shrine of Shao Daraan in the Footnote district. That shrine mysteriously collapsed and looked almost desecrated, was the first indication of the problem.

### Act 1: A City in Revision

_As you drift in the fading warmth of dreams, something tugs at the corners of your mind — not harshly, but with curiosity, as if an unseen hand turns a page that wasn’t meant to end._

Suddenly, **weight returns to your limbs**, the sensation of wind brushing against your skin. You feel stone beneath your feet — smooth, cold, and ancient. Then your eyes open…

You stand atop a **massive tower**, so tall the air is thin and laced with starlight. The floor beneath you is carved of **silver-veined obsidian**, etched with swirling runes that slowly rearrange themselves, forming unfamiliar letters that vanish when you try to read them.

Above you? **Nothing.** Or perhaps **everything** — a swirling cosmic expanse of **ink-dark space**, dotted with constellations that shift and pulse like living things. A **giant book**, the size of a mountain range, hangs open across the stars behind you — its pages stretching like wings across the void.

And below?

A city. But not just any city.

An impossible city, built within and between the **pages of the book itself**. Towers rise like quills, streets spiral like sentences, and bridges arc between pages that ripple as though alive. Entire neighborhoods float on floating leaves of parchment. Glowing script scrolls across the buildings like living graffiti — **a city made entirely of stories**, alive with light, color, and quiet wonder.

Before you stands a wizard clad in green robes and armor, similar yet unique to those of the Green Wizards you have seen before, as if they are both ancient and futuristic at once. He bows kindly, waving his arm around the room before you and speaks, "Welcome! I am [[Krieg]], Talewarden of the City of Stories. You have been summoned at the behest of our God of Stories and Creation."

- "Alrighty, I'm not sure of your names, but you were in my logs from the big guy, so you must be important! Take some time to get to know one another after this briefing." He smiles and opens a large business ledger. "Names please?" He takes down your names quickly and slams the book closed with a bang.
- "Well everything is in order there. Officially welcome to the city of stories!" He claps his hands and the chairs disappear out from under you, a massive scroll flies to his side and unfurls, rolling down to the floor and across the room. "We have conscripted you into the City of Stories for two reasons: 1. to seek out and eliminate a remnant of a rogue narrative, and to undergo the challenge of the Infinite Codex."

You need to investigate and solve this issue, then you are free to enjoy the splendor of our Infinite Codex! I recommend you start at the Shrine of Shao Daraan."
He slams his staff into the ground and you feel yourself lifted from the ground, your vision blurs as you reappear on a streetcorner, a road paved with stones in the shapes of letters and words, and the city stretches out before you. You don't know where this shrine is, but you feel your feet urging you forward, as if the book is trying to guide you.

### Act 2: The Shrine of Shao Daraan

As you make your way through the spiraling alleys of the City of Stories, the streets ripple subtly beneath your feet — cobblestones shaped like punctuation marks, glowing faintly with the rhythm of some unseen author’s quill.

In a narrow square hemmed in by bookshelves taller than towers, you spot an old man — or perhaps a story written in the shape of one. His beard is made of unraveling letters, and his robe flutters like open parchment in a breeze you can’t feel. He’s hunched over a cracked inkpot, trying to coax a sentence to life with a stylus made of starlight.

He notices you, eyes twinkling like commas catching moonlight. He chuckles.

> “Off to the Shrine, are you? Dangerous place now. Ungrateful to revision, that one...”  
> “Still—best you ride, not walk. The sentences are restless today.”

He lifts a hand, and with a deft flick of his stylus, **writes a single glowing sentence into the air**:

> _“Let these words take flight, and carry the bearers of this tale.”_

The letters twist, shimmer, and **unfurl into a living sheet of parchment**, hovering a few feet off the ground. Quills snap into shape as rails, and script trails along the sides, whispering ancient blessings of safe passage.

The skiff gleams with gold-inked sigils and feels weightless, though you sense immense power coiled inside it — a vehicle **made from story itself**.

As you step aboard, the old man bows.

> “Speak your destination with purpose — the skiff only obeys clarity.”
> “Say it now: ‘**To the Shrine of Shao Daraan.**’"

The moment you do, the parchment tightens, the quills spark, and the skiff **lurches skyward** — slicing through the city’s winding paragraphs like a blade of meaning through noise. Below, the city fades into a haze of turning pages and spell-lit streets.


#### Reaching the Shrine

As you drift down upon an enchanted parchment skiff, the once-lustrous district of **Inner Sanctum** greets you with eerie silence. Floating quill-lamps flicker unnaturally, dimmed not by time but by **deliberate interference**.

Ahead stands the ruined shell of the **Shrine of Shao Daraan**, the Patron of Creation, Stories, and Legends — a deity revered in the Infinite Codex as the protector of the one true narrative.

Once a temple of spiraling script and translucent crystal walls, now it lies **split**, cracked like brittle pages. The very walls shudder faintly — as though **unwritten** mid-sentence. Whole lines of sacred inscription have been **scratched out**, their glowing letters dripping like ink tears into the void below.

The air smells of _burned parchment and regret_.

As you enter you see the broken altar, a broken quill of the Talewardens hovers ominously in the ruins, and the collapsed walls of the temple, some having fallen inward.

#### **Clue 1: Memory Residue** _(DC 15 Arcana)_

Near the broken altar is a silvery smear — **a fragment of stolen narrative**, vibrating faintly.

- Touching it (WIS save DC 15 or willingly) gives a momentary vision:
    
    > A young boy stands on the beach alone, eyes hollow, holding a quill like a blade, wearing armor made of stories. He speaks a name that was never meant to be written. Then—screaming. Ink explodes across reality like a wound.
    
- The boy is the rogue narrative, but **his original story was one of redemption**. Something changed.

#### **Clue 2: Broken Quill Sigil** _(DC 13 Investigation or Religion)_

One of the shrine's sacred quills has been twisted into a jagged, unnatural shape — ink and blood fused.

- It bears a warped rune that doesn’t belong to any known story. An aberrant mark.
    
- **A clue for future deciphering** — the rune matches one glowing on the outer cracks of the Unscripted Sanctum.
    

#### **Clue 3: Survivor – Scribe Nyrellen** _(Automatic if players call out or spend 10 minutes searching)_

A wounded shrine guardian lies hidden under a collapsed wall, protected by a magical stasis.

- Nyrellen is a **glass-ink construct**, a sentient being of script and mirrored eyes. Her voice flickers like a broken phonograph.
    

> “He came… the boy with no end. Not unfinished — _unwritten_. He did not belong… so he wrote _himself in_. He knew the sanctuary’s true name. _He unspoke it._”

She can answer 2–3 questions before fading or needing magical healing. Possible questions:

- “Who was he?”
    
    > “He said he was a footnote once… then a fable… then a **lie given a mouth**. He was chosen by our lord, and then forgotten...”
    
- “What did he want?”
    
    > “To erase all second chances. He said ‘every mercy is a chain. Daraan is a liar.’”
    
- “Where did he go?”
    
    > “To the tower that even the Author feared. The place where **stories break apart**... The Unscripted Sanctum!”

### The Unscripted Sanctum

The skiff glides across a gap between realities — a flicker of white void where the sky becomes parchment and gravity forgets which way is down.

Ahead, a **tower caught mid-unraveling** looms into view: the **Unscripted Sanctum**.

Once a revered **library-cathedral**, its architecture now ripples like paper soaked in chaos. Massive bookshelves tilt at impossible angles, their tomes spilling words that hang suspended in the air, twisting into **unfinished sentences**, arguments never resolved, prayers never answered. Every sound echoes strangely — as if the world itself is waiting for the next line.

The skiff slows as it draws near the Sanctum’s open breach, where a page of reality has been **torn** wide. Beyond it lies a fractured interior of shattered altars, hovering ink-chains, and **memories that walk like ghosts**.

Floating within the nave, between drifting script and broken stained glass windows of former champions, is a man — or what remains of one.

#### Enter: Augustus Auranthor

[[Augustus Statblock]]

Clad in armor made of shattered oaths and ink-bound pages, **Augustus Auranthor** hovers above the ruined dais once reserved for the high scribes. His once-golden eyes are now void-filled parentheses, and his voice arrives before his lips move — like a forgotten narrator speaking aloud.

> “They sent you,” he says. “The heroes. The _rewriters_. The faithful of divine editors.”

His expression is one of bitter amusement, but there’s sorrow underneath — a scar older than this place.

> “Do you even know who I was? I was his hand. His _voice_. I fought for mercy, for stories allowed to grow beyond what they were written to be.”

He gestures wide — and around you, **ghosts of his memories** flicker: Augustus as a young man offering peace to a bloodthirsty king; Augustus shielding a village meant to burn. Then: Augustus on his knees before a fading image of **Shao Daraan**, begging for aid. Silence. Abandonment.

> “When I fell… she said nothing. No edits. No reply. Just a blank space where I had once mattered.”

The floating debris twists suddenly, as the Sanctum’s walls pulse with the strain of contradiction.

> “So I took the pen myself. I rewrote my role. I _became_ the ending they refused to acknowledge.”

#### The Choice

He reaches into the air and **grips a glowing, shifting glyph — the Last Page** — the final command of this story’s reality.

> “Stand with me. Cast off your divine shackles, and help me forge a new narrative. One where stories belong to those who live them, not those who watch from on high.”

Then, quieter:

> “You were written to stop me. But maybe you’re more than what they wrote.”

### 🗡️ Duel or Surrender

You feel it in the weave of the air: a moment of **paracanonical choice**.

- Accept Augustus’s offer to **reshape the world**, and side with the rogue narrative.
    
- Attempt to persuade him to **stop rewriting**, at great emotional and narrative risk.
    
- Challenge him — and **duel for the Page** — the soul of the Sanctum.
    

If the party chooses combat, Augustus smiles.

> “Very well. Let us write the ending together — in blood and ink.”
