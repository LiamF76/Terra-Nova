## 🖋️ Augustus Auranthor – *The Rogue Narrative*
*Medium humanoid (echo of a story), chaotic neutral*

---

**Armor Class**: 15  
**Hit Points**: 75 (10d8 + 20)  
**Speed**: 30 ft., hover 10 ft.

**STR**: 12 (+1)  
**DEX**: 14 (+2)  
**CON**: 14 (+2)  
**INT**: 16 (+3)  
**WIS**: 12 (+1)  
**CHA**: 18 (+4)

**Saving Throws**: Cha +6, Int +5  
**Skills**: Insight +3, Arcana +5, Deception +6  
**Damage Resistances**: psychic; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks  
**Condition Immunities**: charmed, frightened  
**Senses**: passive Perception 11  
**Languages**: Common, Celestial, Storyscript  
**Challenge**: 2 (450 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus**: +2

---

### 📝 Narrative Shell (Mythic Trait)
When Augustus drops to **0 HP**, he shatters into script fragments and reforms in a **mythic second phase** at 30 HP. The battlefield subtly shifts, unfinished sentences hang in the air, and he becomes a chaotic, spell-less narrative echo with new abilities.

---

### 📚 Spellcasting (Phase 1 – Echo of a Hero)
Augustus is a 3rd-level spellcaster. His spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 14, +6 to hit).

- **Cantrips (at will)**: *Vicious Mockery, Minor Illusion*  
- **1st level (2 slots)**: *Dissonant Whispers, Command*

---

### 🗡️ Actions (Phase 1)

**Inkblade Slash.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.  
**Hit:** 7 (1d8 + 3) slashing damage + 2 psychic damage.

**Inkstep (Recharge 5–6).** Augustus teleports up to 30 feet, leaving glowing script behind. Creatures adjacent to the space he left must succeed on a DC 12 Wisdom saving throw or be **distracted** (disadvantage on their next attack roll).

---

### ✨ Bonus Action (Phase 1)

**Disrupting Word (Recharge 4–6).** One creature within 30 ft. must succeed on a DC 14 Charisma save or take 4 psychic damage and lose its reaction.

---

## 💀 Mythic Phase: Unwritten Form (Phase 2)

Augustus reforms as a flickering shadow of words and memory. He loses spellcasting and gains new disruptive narrative powers.

---

### 🖤 Actions (Phase 2)

**Unwritten Strike.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target.  
**Hit:** 9 (2d6 + 2) force damage.  
The target must succeed a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw or be **silenced** (unable to speak or cast verbal component spells) until the end of its next turn.

**Tear the Thread (Recharge 5–6).** One creature within 30 ft. must make a DC 13 Charisma saving throw.  
**On fail:** Take 10 (3d6) psychic damage and lose your next bonus action.

---

### 🌀 Legendary Action (1/round)

**Glitch Step (Costs 1 Action).** After a player ends their turn, Augustus shifts 10 ft. without provoking opportunity attacks and leaves behind an illusory double. The next attack roll against him has **disadvantage** before the start of his next turn.

---

### 🎭 Tactics

- **Phase 1**: Augustus relies on illusions, manipulation, and spells to disrupt and control.
- **Phase 2**: He becomes aggressive, isolating targets, preventing speech, and warping time and actions.

---

### 🏛️ Battlefield Notes

- The **Unscripted Sanctum** distorts around him. Sentences drift through the air, stone and script crumble, and unfinished thoughts float like fog.
- Consider using environmental effects to flavor failed saves or critical fails.
