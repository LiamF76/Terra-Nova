
The massive hub of the infinite Codex, Shao Daraan's magical city of wonders and possibility. This is a place where anything and everything exists in some facet or fashion. A massive city sprawling across a demi-plane page in the _Infinite Codex_, where people, places, and legends from across time and fantasy converge. Here, stories have weight. The architecture is a blend of epic styles — spired citadels next to modern mage-towers, cobbled bard streets beside eternal markets.

While it is Shao Daraan's city, the Creation God is not the active or present ruler that the city needs. In that void of power a being ascended to act as lord, Lord Palim. Once a crystal, Palim was given sentience because Shao Daraan  believed that a memory crystal deserved to make memories of its own. Palim became a powerful being with Daraan's power, and acts as a guardian and guide for the growth and betterment of the City of Stories.

### Shops

- <mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">The Firefly's Emporium</mark>: Known for its presence in Terra-Nova, this Emporium is run by Bekarys the Firefly, an Abjuration Wizard, this version of him however is not an evil Kairos Replicant, and is just a kind mage & shop owner. The shop sells magical items, mainly pertaining to combat and wizard.
- <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">The First Draft</mark>: A magic shop that sells books, scrolls, and spell books for magic. Run by Inkpot and Wax the golems.
- <mark style="background: #FFB86CA6;">Ash and Echo</mark>: A blacksmith shop run by the ghost of a dwarf named Brannock. He sells many well forged items and some magical armaments. He pours his being into them, giving them both sentimental and magical value. Brannock is known for his Mithril Hammer, a gift given to him by Shao Daraan in exchange for building the doors between pages of the Codex.
