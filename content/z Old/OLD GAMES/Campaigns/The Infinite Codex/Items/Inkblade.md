
### 🖋️ Inkblade  
*Weapon (longsword or shortsword), rare (requires attunement)*

This elegant blade appears to be forged from black metal veined with glowing silver script. When swung, the air ripples with phantom words, and slashes leave trails of ink that vanish a moment later.

---

**You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls** made with this magic weapon.

---

### ✒️ Narrative Edge  
When you hit a creature with this weapon, you may choose to have inky script coil around them. The target must succeed on a **DC 13 Wisdom saving throw** or have **disadvantage on their next attack roll**. This effect can be used **twice per long rest**.

---

### 📖 Arcane Flourish  
Once per day, as a bonus action, you may speak a command word to cause the blade to flare with swirling letters. For 1 minute:

- The blade deals an extra **1d4 psychic damage** on hit.
- When you score a critical hit, your target must make a DC 13 Charisma saving throw or be **silenced** until the end of their next turn (unable to speak or cast spells with verbal components).

---

**Type**: Martial weapon (longsword or shortsword)  
**Rarity**: Rare  
**Requires Attunement**: Yes  
**Damage**: 1d8 slashing (longsword) or 1d6 slashing (shortsword)  
**Properties**: Versatile (1d10), or finesse/light if a shortsword
