# **BATTLEMAP**:

#### *1. The Hall of Whispers*
A large chamber filled with shifting mirrors that reflect illusions of adventurers and sounds that echo unnervingly. You hear them pull at you and whisper, each mirror showing you images of your friends, your party, and doorways, one mirror holds the true entrance, but which one? There is a maze around you, many paths, different ways you could go, how will you solve the maze?

Are you moving or standing still?
If they move, have them make a raw dexterity check to see if they hit a trapped mirror (DC 10), on a failure they trigger a trapped mirror, summoning 1 [[shadow.png]]. Roll initiative!

**SOLVE THROUGH GAMEPLAY**:
If they travel the path trough the mirrors marking paths they have already been on or some other clever plan, they can reach the secret door mirror and escape to the next room!

**SOLVE THROUGH CHECKS**:
You may make a perception check, an investigation check, or an arcana check to search and analyze the nature of the hall.
- **Perception (DC 13)**: Identifies a whisper describing the real exit.
- **Investigation (DC 15)**: Detects that one of the mirrors hides a secret door.
- **Arcana (DC 12)**: Discovers that some mirrors are magically trapped (trigger illusions).
If they fail 3 checks, the mirrors shoot out and slam forming a rectangular wall around you, an area for fighting. 3 [[shadow.png]] leap from the mirrors attacking them. Roll initiative!


#### *2. Guardian's Forge*
As you step through the secret door, the temperature rises abruptly. The air ahead shimmers, warped by waves of heat, and the flickering orange glow of firelight greets them from braziers mounted along the walls.
Before you stretches a wide chamber, filled with the metallic taste of iron and ash. On either side of the room, massive anvils and smelters stand abandoned, their coals still burning dimly as if waiting for a master to return. The walls are etched with ancient runes—symbols of craftsmanship and protection—worn smooth by time and soot. Sparks occasionally pop from the forges, filling the air with small bursts of warmth and light. The steady clang of unseen hammers echoes eerily through the space, as if the forge remembers the work that once gave it purpose.

In the center of the room lies an anvil with a hammer and tongs, pure adamantine shining blue and green in the red light of the room. Behind the anvil a massive iron gateway, the doorway to the next room, but it is blocked by a great suit of armor, standing guard, sword in two hands pointed to the floor as if to say, "You shall not pass".

There are two braziers, and the floor almost glistens as if large sections were slicked with oil.

The armor shudders and snaps at its joints, awakening from its slumber, a coat of ash falling from its shoulders, there is still blood on its blade from the las group who attempted to pass this terror.
You hear a faint cackle as from the great pools of molten iron fly two imps of lava.
**ENEMIES**: [[animated armor.png]], 2x [[magma mephit.png]]

**MECHANICS**: 
- If a player uses the anvil (successful smiths tools check DC 12) they can give their weapon a +1 and deal an additional 1d6 fire damage for 1 minute.
- If they knock over the brazier an area of 15ft by 15ft will be set alight, dealing 1d6 fire damage to anyone who starts their turn there or moves through it (DC 14 DEX save for half damage).

**South Door**. Leads to the Pendulum Blades hallway.


##### *a. The Pendulum Blades*
A long hallway stretches out before you, at the end it turns sharply to the right, the light is dim but you can make out faint metallic glints towards the end of the hallway. 
As you go to step forward, a gigantic metallic blade swings down before you, and past you, every 10ft of the hallway a blade swings across, cleaving through the air waiting for anything it could cut to make the mistake of standing there.

**MECHANICS**: Every 10ft blades, swing every six seconds. If they observe the blades and get the timing down they can make a DC 13 Acrobatics check to dodge them and make it across easily. If they don't observe the blades and just try to run through its s DC 16 Athletics or Acrobatics check. If they block the slow of one blade they stop all the blades, by lodging something in there or otherwise (DC 12 some kinda check). 
If anyone gets hit by the blades its 2d6 slashing damage.

#### *3. The Room of Many Locks*
You enter a circular stone chamber, on the west wall is a massive doorway bearing a large bronze bar across it (the bar appears to be magic if investigated), there are 5 ornate locks on the door. Above each keyhole is an engraving in common, a riddle.
The room is eerily quiet, with only the occasional crackle of arcane energy dancing along the walls. Six stone pedestals are evenly spaced throughout the room, each carved with intricate designs. Atop each pedestal rests a key, each one different in shape, size, and material—some look ancient and rusted, others gleam as if newly forged. The keys seem ordinary, but something about them feels off, as if not all of them belong here.

**MECHANICS**: Must match the keys to the correct locks
Riddles (Keys):
- 1. Not metal or wood, but I open both kinds, so long as an entrance they call home. (Glass key)
- 2. I move wisely, during the day and also at night. (Clock key)
- 3. I carry words without speaking, and messages with no sound. (Book key)
- 4. I am not alive but I grow, I need air but do not breath. (Fire key)
- 5. I fly without wings, and cry without eyes. (Cloud key)
