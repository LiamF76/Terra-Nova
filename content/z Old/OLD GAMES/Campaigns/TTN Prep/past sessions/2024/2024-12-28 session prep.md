## The Forest

As you walk through the pines, the tree cover and trunks lessen the force of the storm, it seems as if the woods are never ending, and the day grows darker. You walk for a full two hours, make two CON saves to resist the cold.

After hours of trudging through the snow and cold, the trees begin to thin out. You steps into a clearing, bathed in pale light that reflects off the pristine snow. The air is still, heavy with the scent of pine and a faint metallic tang that feels out of place.

In the center of the clearing, a massive **dire polar bear** looms, its pristine white fur marred with streaks of red. Its powerful claws rake at the snow as it sniffs around a motionless figure sprawled in the snow—likely a traveler, though it’s hard to tell from this distance.

The bear’s ears twitch as it lifts its head, its icy blue eyes scanning the clearing. For a moment, the party feels frozen in time, unsure if the bear has noticed them. The faint sound of a crackling fire from the traveler’s fallen torch provides the only indication of life beyond the grim tableau before them.

## The Sword on the Wall Tavern

The trees thin out abruptly, revealing a small clearing where a lone cabin stands against the encroaching wilderness. The structure is modest but sturdy, with thick logs forming its walls and a roof heavy with snow. Smoke rises steadily from a stone chimney, curling into the gray sky. A faint golden glow seeps through the frost-covered windows, promising warmth and respite.

Beside the main longhouse there is a medium sized stable with a single old horse tied to a post, feasting itself on a large haybale in the snow. Just past the stable is another small wooden building, dark and devoid of light or life, some kind of storehouse.

The sounds of the wilderness fade as they approach, replaced by the faint creak of wood shifting in the cold and the muffled hum of voices from within. Firelight flickers through the cracks in the shutters, casting a welcoming aura over the snow-covered clearing.

As you stand in the darkness of the tree line, hesitating for just a moment, you see coming from the stables behind the donkey, a humanoid figure, glowing slightly like fire... his hair shining orange and red. He carries a bundle of large logs and enters the building, shutting the door behind him. A sign above the door rattles as it slams shut. "The Sword on the Wall Tavern".
