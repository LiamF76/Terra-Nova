## Pocket Pickers

You all shoot upwards, as the pixies laugh and scurry or fly away, two can be seen lugging Ivys' staff across the dirt as they try to fly away with it unsuccessfully. They look up at you in fascinated fear as you awaken.

The two Pixies are: Cazun and Fitil
- Funny little high pitched voices.
- They are two pixies who live in the Feywild, they are natives and were born into House *Lirienveil*.
- They are kleptomaniacs but friendly and do not want to fight.
- They can lead the party to Sylvaris for an offer of some treasure or a DC 12 Intimidation check.

As they lead you through the forest Fitil flies backwards and lands on Audrey's shoulder, remarking to her, "Just make sure to stay close, and don't listen to the wind! It takes other planers, it eats them! Don't let it guide you away!"

You follow your new friends and a winding trail of silver-blue flowers that appear to guide you deeper into the woods. The petals curl and unfold, glowing faintly underfoot as though alive. A soft hum permeates the air, a melody both soothing and eerie, as though the forest itself is humming a lullaby.

Small, winged lights, will-o’-wisps or perhaps curious sprites—dance between the branches, illuminating the way in flickering bursts. Occasionally, the faint sound of laughter echoes from unseen sources, but when you stop to investigate, there is nothing but the rustle of leaves.

Eventually you reach a strange archway of intertwined trees, the two Pixies fly towards them and kiss each of the trunks before passing through, beckoning you into the archway. Upon passing under the arch they vanish completely.

As you step through the archway of intertwined trees, the world around you transforms into a serene yet majestic clearing that radiates an ancient and profound magic. The air grows warmer, rich with the scent of fertile earth, blooming flowers, and sap. Time seems to slow, and the sounds of the forest fall away into a tranquil silence.

In the center of the clearing stands a towering Treant unlike any you could have imagined. His form is massive and ageless, his bark a deep, rich brown streaked with veins of golden light that pulse faintly like a heartbeat. His "face," though subtle and natural, is imbued with wisdom and calm, with eyes like pools of amber that glimmer with the light of countless stars. His immense body is adorned with a living mantle of greenery—vines heavy with blossoms and fruit cascade down his shoulders like a regal cloak, and his branches stretch skyward, bearing leaves of gold and silver that catch and refract the light.


## The Tree Father

The ground beneath Sylvaris’ massive roots is carpeted in soft, glowing moss, and flowers of every hue bloom in perfect harmony around him. Streams of crystal-clear water snake through the clearing, their soft murmurs joining in an otherworldly melody that seems to come from the earth itself. Small fey creatures, such as glowing butterflies and tiny sprites, flit around him, tending to the flowers or watching the party with curiosity.

Rising from the base of his trunk, Sylvaris’ roots form a natural throne-like structure, with smaller trees and ferns growing symbiotically around it. Encircling him are twelve ancient stone monoliths, each etched with runes in a language the party cannot comprehend but can feel—the words seem to pulse with the essence of creation itself.

Sylvaris addresses you, his voice like the deep groan of the earth mixed with the whisper of rustling leaves. It resonates in the air, bypassing mere sound to echo within the mind and soul.

**“Welcome, wanderers. You carry with you the weight of battles fought and trials endured. Tell me, why have you come to my sanctuary, where the roots of the first forests meet the heart of the Feywild?”**

As he speaks, the clearing seems to respond—his words stirring the leaves, the streams, and even the air itself. The light from above filters through his branches, creating a kaleidoscope of colors on the ground around the party.

Sylvaris exudes an aura of ancient power and serenity, but also a subtle sense of sorrow, as though he carries the burden of countless ages. You feel the magnitude of his presence, as if standing before a god. Your vision is slightly blurred, and quick head movements leave you dazed for half a second, his pressure exerted on you effortlessly.

##### Sylvaris General dialogue:
- **"Time flows differently here, yet it always flows forward. Do not lament what is past, but learn from it, for every fallen leaf nourishes the soil for new growth."**
- **"When you doubt yourself, listen to the whispers of the wind and the rustle of the leaves. The world will remind you of your purpose if you are willing to hear it."**
- **"You carry the spark of creation with you. Do not squander it on hatred or vengeance; let it shine to inspire others."**
- **"When faced with darkness, remember that even the faintest light can guide you home. Be that light for others when they lose their way."**
- **"You are like saplings, growing toward the sun. Take care where you plant your roots, for the company you keep shapes the forest you will become."**
- **"My siblings yes, well... Tiurah, has been long split. You carry two pieces with you already! I am gladdened to see the children of the comet, the shards of my sister's light shines brighter for your company. Thank you for taking care of my nephews."**
- **"Fyrnanth, ah yes... a long time it has been since I saw my eldest brother. He travels often but visits rarely. A paradox I have never reconciled. I trust he will help you in exchange for something, shining, quite like himself. It must be something quite wondrous though."**
- **"Aurathia, the baby. We were quite close for a time, but she became angry at mortals. Destruction of her domain by their machines and wars made her bitter. I love her as I love all nature, but you may not find an agreement with her. If all else fails, I advise theft. Steal what you need from her and escape, you cannot contend with her in battle."**

Sylvaris listens to the party's tale with the patience of a being who has seen eons pass. His amber eyes glimmer as he considers their words, his great branches creaking as he shifts ever so slightly. The streams around his roots bubble gently, and the soft hum of the forest grows quieter, as though the very Feywild holds its breath.

When he speaks, his voice is a deep resonance, as if the earth itself were giving counsel:  
**"The trials you face are threads in the great tapestry, woven by hands far beyond our sight. Yet you stand against the unraveling, and for that, I shall aid you."**

Sylvaris extends one of his colossal branches, and a soft golden glow begins to emanate from its bark. A smaller branch unfurls, then twists and shapes itself with an elegant precision. The wood darkens to a deep, lustrous hue, veined with glowing green light like the heart of an ancient tree. Leaves of silver and gold sprout from its hilt, shimmering as though they hold the light of a thousand dawns.

When the transformation is complete, Sylvaris plucks the object—a magnificent wood axe—and lowers it to the party. As it rests in his immense hand, its size adjusts to fit the one who will wield it.

**"This is Rootsunder,"** he intones, **"a fragment of my being and a testament to the strength of creation. A Prime Vestige Wield it wisely, for it carries the will of the First Forest and the hope of all that grows. Use it to protect, to heal, and to strike down the corruption that seeks to poison the world. Great foes felled by this weapon shall sprout trees bearing my essence, to rebuild the bastions of nature through the corrupted land."**

[Rootsunder](https://homebrewery.naturalcrit.com/share/o-_jWKCukhyz)
The moment the party accepts the axe, the clearing seems to brighten, as though the forest itself approves of Sylvaris' gift. The air hums with power, and the trees sway gently, their leaves rustling like a hymn of blessing.

**"Now, you must seek out your way home, and with that a final gift. Go to [[The Temple of Three Roads]]. You will find further conflict, but with your roots planted firmly, you will grow to the challenge."** He pats you each on the head for singular soft touch by some small flowered branches, **"Obtain the three keys, open the door, and find Tena's boon. May the green of her veins bind you and your enemies, together and apart."**

Sylvaris adds with a gentle yet firm tone, **"Though this axe is a boon, remember: the greatest strength lies not in what you hold, but in the hearts of those who stand beside you."**

With that, he returns to his silent vigil, the guardian of the ancient ways, watching as you prepare to continue their journey with his blessing.


## Travel to the Temple of Three Roads

You voyage through the eternal twilight of the Feywild, finding more and more strange things across your way. Whispering voices seem to drift on the air—whether they are wind or spirits, it’s impossible to tell. Tiny, glowing creatures flit between the branches, their laughter like the tinkling of crystal bells. Every now and then, you spot peculiar sights: a tree bearing fruit shaped like crescent moons, a ring of mushrooms glowing softly with bioluminescent hues, or a brook that runs uphill, its water sparkling like liquid starlight.

As you press on, the path grows more distinct, lined with pale stones etched with runes in an ancient Fey script. The forest seems to guide your steps. Soon, the sound of three distinct melodies intertwining becomes audible—one light and joyful, another wistful and melancholic, and the third deep and resonant. The melodies come from three directions ahead, and where they converge, you catch your first glimpse of [[The Temple of Three Roads]].
