## Ovosath's Lair

The entrance looms ahead, a massive archway of jagged ice and stone, glittering faintly under the muted sunlight breaking through the overcast sky. Hoarfrost clings to the rocky edges, and icicles hang like gleaming fangs from the arch, some as long as a full-grown human.

The sound of the wind howling through the peaks fades as you step inside, replaced by an eerie stillness broken only by the occasional drip of water from melting ice. The walls of the cave shimmer with a bluish hue, the ice so clear it seems to contain frozen fragments of light. Crystals of frost cover every surface, catching and refracting the dim light into delicate patterns that dance along the walls.

Deeper within, the temperature plummets further, and the icy floor becomes more treacherous, slick with patches of black ice. Stalactites and stalagmites form jagged columns of frosted beauty, some connecting to create natural, glittering pillars.

Strange echoes bounce through the cavern, amplifying even the smallest sound—the creak of boots on ice, the faint clink of gear. The air feels charged, almost electric, as if the cave itself is alive with anticipation.

In the distance, faint silver-blue light spills from a larger chamber. As the party draws closer, the unmistakable signs of a dragon's presence become clear: claw marks gouged into ice-covered stone, scraps of bones frozen in the frost, and the faint scent of ozone and metallic cold.

The main chamber opens wide, a cathedral of ice, with vaulted ceilings that vanish into shadows. At its center lies a massive mound of frozen treasures—coins glittering under a thin sheen of frost, polished gemstones gleaming like stars, and the occasional ancient relic encased in ice. Resting atop this hoard, sleek and resplendent, is **Ovosath**, the young silver dragon.

Her scales glisten like freshly fallen snow in the dim light, and his bright, intelligent eyes gleam with a mixture of curiosity and caution as he regards the newcomers. The air around her seems to ripple with cold, and each breath creates a small plume of frost that dissipates into the cavern's chill.

The dragon stirs as you enter, its body rumbles and shakes, a cascade of golden coins fall down its mound of treasures.
