#### Illuman's True Light

Emrys, as you set yourself for bed, doffing your heavy plate and setting down your pike, Solace leaps from your shoulders, engaging in some adorable muscleman posing in the window, basking in the reddish pink light of the setting sun. He pauses and ends with a big lat spread flourish, waiting for your applause. 
As he enjoys your praise and bows to you his head tilts up, as if he hears something from the window that you don't. He listens intently, nods his head, and walks over to your weapon. He runs his little lump of a hand over its edge, a droplet of light falling from his form, coating the tip of the weapon in his power. The light of the setting sun engulfs the room as he leaps up into your lap, placing a kiss in your forehead, and your weapon shifts, the handle becomes slightly thinner, and the blade only slightly longer.
Placing your hand on it, causes it to glow faintly with light, your blade has been empowered by Solith, and is not a +3 magical weapon! As you hold it you get the very clear sense of Illuman's pride in you, as if this change was spurred by a fleeting moment of clarity for the Sun God.

"Emrys Marcell, my precious son, child of my true light. You have made me proud beyond words. I beg of you my son, find my daughter Soleil, bring her back into my light. You are the only one with faith and heart enough deserving for this quest, give her my love, and she will be reborn."

#### Bedtime Story

- Ivys feels compelled to interact with the book, receives an invitation to meet with the merchant of Midnight in the city's gardens that evening. he invites Ivys' friends, but understands he might come alone so as to keep his bedtime reading a secret.

Ivys, as you pour over your spell book this evening, Sozin rolling around in a big bowl of wax and wicks, his candlelight fueling your late night research, you feel a strange pull. It draws your focus away from your spells, the feeling not coming from the book, but from you. It's as if something new, yet very much you, desires what lies in that book.

As you open the tome once again, the first page shimmers and wavers, and text in Abyssal script appears.

*"To those who dare tread these pages, know this: the power within is not a gift, but a transaction. The Tower does not offer—it takes. It does not teach—it consumes. Every secret inscribed here was carved in blood, every spell a wound upon the world. Some say the Tower has no shape, no master, no end. They are wrong. It has all three, and you will meet them in time. Turn back now, or pay the price the dark asks of all who reach too far. But for those who are willing, bask in the splendor of the dark."*

Beneath it another inscription appears, in very different handwriting, as if being jotted down to you at this very moment from somewhere else.

"Greetings Mister Ivys Arth- ah, my apologies, Horus Kent. A lovely name, I'm sure it carries such strong history, a history I would be delighted to hear if you are willing to share. My name is, to be announced when we meet in person. Perhaps tonight? The Merchant of Midnight is very interested in you and your companions, heroes of great reputation and skill are in short supply. The Traxgate Gardens, the riding tracks, find my carriage The Crimson Catalogue, you can't miss it. Your friends are of course invited as well, though it may be best to keep your bedtime reading a secret for now hm? Of course no judgement from me. Whatever you decide, my companions and I will be happy to host you, and hopefully find something in my market of wonders that interests you. Good Evening, my good Wizard."

As you finish your first reading, the message to you flutters into a swarm of inky fluid along the pages, and dissipates from your sight forever, gone. 

Will you continue to read?

The first page you study details a spell, a corrupted and Abyssal version of the Power Word spell, titled: Heartstop. You study its tormented and cruel workings, and form the beginning of an understanding, the life tax being the most major thing. Abyssal magics have lower mana costs (low level Spell-Slots) but they take their payment from the caster, but the language on that front is ambiguous. It will take more time to learn, but the first step has been taken.

## The Merchant of Midnight

- Ivys goes during the night and meets Mister Halloway ([[Malphas]]). He has a large cart pulled by a strange nightmarish horse and a spectral black cat that sits on his shoulder. Write a deep Mister Halloway interaction where he explains the Formless Tower to Ivys and its drawbacks. He also offers him money and power, fame, anything he wants really. He offers Ivys Horizon's reach, saying its not really for him, but someone is going to need it very soon.

As twilight grips the city, the streets quiet yet still lit by the glowing orange lanterns along the road. A faint mist engulfs the  gardens as you enter, as if a spectral fog spirit has decided your evening requires ambience. As you walk through the high hedges, admiring the evening bloom flowers, you hear the faint sound of meowing. A small almost ghostly black cat slinks from the brush, eyeing you curiously with its pure pale blue eyes. It is not of this world.

As it watches you, it darts away, around the bend and into the larger courtyard. The sound of meows echo through the garden, followed by some low chuffs and whinnies. Awaiting you in the courtyard is a massive carriage, beautiful maroon paint with a roof of shale. It looks to be almost a complete shed on wheels. Tied to the front of the carriage is the largest horse you have ever seen, big enough for a Goliath. It has pitch black fur and has 8 legs, two pairs on the front and back. Its muscles are rippled as if it had been climbing the harsh peaks for years all while pulling the massive wagon behind it.

Outside the wagon, sitting on a large rock and seemingly playing cards with the horse is a man clad in beautiful blue and silver silks.
You hear him laugh a soft and gentle laugh, like something from a play. "You got me again Fable! I swear only a fool loses at cards against a horse. Then again you aren't just any horse are you? Remarkable thing you are." He pats the beast on the snout and turns around, smiling and waving at you.

As you approach he stands and you get a better look at him. In addition to his beautiful silk outfit he wears an ornate blue turban detailed with silver stars and moons. Two black ivory horns are affixed to it, as if they were his own horns. His face is somewhat androgenous but unmistakably beautiful and soft, like that of a fondly remembered loved one. He has pure striking silver eyes, and his earrings are like silver chandeliers, many tiny hanging pieces that glitter with even tinier diamonds. 

"Friend('s)! Welcome and thank you for accepting my invitation. The stars whispered to me of your arrival, but they failed to mention how remarkable you would be in the flesh. Come let's see if anything inside captures your lovely gazes, and let's see if we can't make a deal." The cat is perched on top of the carriage, watching you with unnerving focus.

#### Inside the Crimson Catalogue

The moment you step through the carriage door, the world outside ceases to exist. The interior is impossibly vast—a grand parlor of velvet and shadow, lit by floating lanterns that flicker with pale scarlet flame. The air is thick with the scent of aged parchment, spiced wine, and something faintly metallic. Shelves line the walls, stacked with curiosities: jars of preserved laughter, a violin that plays itself, a skull with a silver tongue. Mister Halloway gestures to a plush divan, its fabric shifting colors like twilight.

He speaks as he turns away to a large mahogany cabinet, humming as he selects a large wine bottle of a very deep red color from his stores. "I deeply apologize for my delay in introductions. Its such a hassle remembering what name I go by these days. The Merchant of Midnight is but a title, though one I quite enjoy. You may call me Mister Halloway. Much more proper. Now darlings, I've shown you mine, care to show me yours?"

They talk for a while, and Mister Halloway makes some interesting offers in exchange for powerful magic items. 
- [Robes of the Archmagi](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/magic-item:robe-of-the-archmagi): Asks for Ivys' destiny, give up on the path he chose from the deck of many things, and Halloway will give him the robes of the greatest mage to ever life. [[Vesper]] Fontaine.
- [Defender Glaive](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/magic-item:defender): Asks for Emrys' hope, in exchange for a weapon to protect everyone he loves. 
- **New Cube (For Audrey)**: All he asks in payment is that Elysia gives up her love for Oleander, and Halloway will restore the cube to perfect working order as it was.
- [Scarab of Protection](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/magic-item:scarab-of-protection): In exchange for power against undeath, he asks for life. Someone in the party must give up their soul and afterlife to him in exchange for the Scarab.

He also says that if the party is looking for a really good time, they should visit The Dancing Devil, a club in the undercity. Many there harbor resentment to Hannibal for his even more extreme than Traxgate's usual dedication to war. They are the few but passionate creatives, and one of Hannibal's lieutenants is known to hang out there. Lady Windfang, known as the Dancing Dragon.
