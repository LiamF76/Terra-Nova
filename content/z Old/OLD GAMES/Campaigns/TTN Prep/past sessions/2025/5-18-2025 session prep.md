### An Artist or a Troll?

After leaving Gromm we continue south, there are two possible roads to take. 
- **The Sanguine Road**: A road denoted by its groups of red roses that grow along it from here to The Great Divide. It is the main road for trade and travelers, so its likely to be more busy than an alternative.
- **The Moonset Trail**: A non state road, meaning it is maintained by individuals and is much less busy. Considered an out of the way path to reach the Great Divide, would add 4 days to your journey to the Great Divide, ends in near the same place.
Both roads lead to the Galmer Stairs, which are the common path (and only safe one) through the Great Divide (in this area/reasonable to reach in a good time frame). 
Lumina, you recognize the name Galmer Stairs, as that is the road to the ancient Cloud Giant Temple of [[Nimbara Aeris]], where your Cloud Giant friends lived.

##### The Caravan
As you travel deeper into the Sanguine Road, the patches of red roses grow thicker, climbing on trellises that arch over the path in places, filling the air with their sweet perfume. The road is busy, but not crowded—an elegant artery of commerce winding southward.

On the third morning, the clatter of wheels and a soft lilting melody played on a dulcimer reach your ears. A **caravan of two grand wagons** crests a nearby hill, each pulled by majestic painted drakes—sleek, scaled beasts in brilliant hues of cobalt and jade, harnessed in gold-trimmed leather. A column of **six guards** rides ahead and behind, armed not with common steel but ceremonial polearms and flowing sashes of Drok-Shari silk.
They peddle their wares to you offering not magical items, potions, or weapons, but finery.

After your encounter with the caravan you continue on, and halfway through your last day of travel before reaching the Galmer Stairs, you hear a faint whistling from up ahead on the road. The mountains loom over you, its massive shadow almost reaching you. You see a figure round the bend, a Dragonborn man and a mule.
He hums a strange tune that you haven't heard before, his melodic voice echoing along the road as he waves and approaches. 
"White hair from her shoulders, red blood from her guts, The Celestial lady faced Tyran, and knew she was fucked! But then from behind, a beam from the eyes, of the little boy bot name Locket was shot!" he sings as he approaches. "Greetings kind travelers!"

He bows, as he does his cloak billows dramatically, the fancy red hat on his head tilting and then slipping off his head. He snatches it up and dusts it off:

#### The Moonset Trail

Bridge Troll Encounter. Spirit Encounter (Offering).

##### The Bridge Troll
The Moonset Trail narrows as you travel farther, becoming more twisted and wild. The roses of the Sanguine Road are gone, replaced with ivy, gnarled trees, and thick underbrush. A low mist rolls in at night, and you see far fewer travelers. Occasionally, strange old signposts appear with scratched-out writing in forgotten tongues.

After several days of travel, you come upon a **rickety rope bridge** swaying over a vast almost hundred feet wide gorge. On the far side of the bridge, standing atop a big rock and looking very concerned, is a **Troll wearing a straw hat** and clutching a large, wooden mallet.

As you approach, he waves awkwardly and calls out:

> **Trillik the Troll:**  
> "Hullooo! Travelers! Stop right there please! Trillik says stop! You can't cross! Not safe! My bridge—_my masterpiece!_—is... a bit unwell right now..."

Trillik is a large, moss-covered troll with a deeply emotional attachment to this very rickety, obviously unsafe bridge. He explains that he built it himself, with _no help_, and it used to be a fine structure... until "a fat boar and a wind spirit danced across it" and now it's "all wobble and no woof."

He’s clearly bad at construction and emotionally devastated. He normally would demand a toll, but he's too embarrassed to do so with the bridge in its current state.

- "Unfortunately, Trillik's bridge is closed currently. We are looking into contractors but at this time I'm afraid I can't let you cross, unless you pay the expedited fee of course."
- "It's 500 gold per person, and you have to sign a waiver."
- "Trillik don't know what a waiver is, someone mentioned it when they came by years ago. Trillik just use it to get people away from his bridge. You can just pay Trillik."

<mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">Encounter Options</mark>:
- **Roleplay**: Calm him down, talk him through his bridge woes, or convince him it's okay to ask for help.
    
- **Skill Challenge** _(Optional)_:  
    Help him **rebuild the bridge** better! A group skill challenge using:
    
    - **Survival** or **Nature** to gather wood/stone.
        
    - **Investigation** or **Engineering Tools** to identify structural problems.
        
    - **Persuasion** to keep Trillik calm and focused.
        
    - **Arcana** or **Druidcraft** to use magic to reinforce it.
        
    - **Pay Trillik 500GP** per person to cross and make an **acrobatics or athletics check**, **DC 18**, to not fall into the chasm.

If the party succeeds, Trillik gets misty-eyed and declares:

> "Best bridge ever! You are bridge-friends now! If you ever come back this way, you cross for half price! Small toll, and hugs."

He gives them a **"Bridge-Friend Token"** (a crude wooden carving of a bridge with googly eyes).

If the party mocks him or ignores the bridge issue, the bridge collapses under them. A **DEX saving throw** may be required to avoid falling (or use climbing/flying/rope solutions), but Trillik just whimpers as the bridge snaps.

##### The Shrine

As you near the end of the Moonset Trail, you pass through a serene forest glade where the light always seems to be twilight. At the center of the clearing is a **moss-covered stone shrine**, shaped like a twisted tree with antlers. Fireflies drift in the air, and the air smells of lavender and loam.

There is a **small stone bowl** set in front of the shrine, filled with rainwater and herbs. The carved words, in Sylvan and Elvish, read:

> "To pass beneath the Hollow's Watch, leave a gift in peace or face the dusk within."

#### Session End
 
As the last light of day sinks behind the jagged cliffs of the Great Divide, your path begins to curve toward the Galmer Stairs—those ancient, winding stone steps carved into the mountain by hands long gone.

Whether you came by the blooming Sanguine Road or the quiet wilds of the Moonset Trail, the final stretch is quiet. The wind whistles between the stone outcroppings like a distant voice—one that seems to whisper warnings lost to time.

Then you see it.

Just off the path, half-buried beneath a collapsed cairn of rocks and snow, lies a broken body. A human man, curled unnaturally, limbs twisted from a fall or perhaps something worse. His traveling cloak is torn, his satchel cracked and soaked with old blood, but still intact.

Inspecting the corpse, you find the symbol of the **Courier’s Guild of Dragonswatch** burned into a small wooden badge pinned to his collar. His satchel holds dried rations, a shattered compass… and two **Potions of Resistance (Acid)**, untouched.

But the most important find is a **sealed letter**, wax bearing the mark of a silver skull impaled by a rose. The seal is cracked slightly from age and pressure, but the letter within is legible:

> _"To the Cerwan of the Eldermourne, and to whomever this letter may reach in time—_  
> _A necromancer of no small power has taken root south of the Divide. He is known as the Gravebinder.
> His servants lurk in the night. Whole villages have gone dark. Travelers vanish in the fog.  
> We had hoped to send more, but this may be the last word sent before the path is closed.  
> Be wary. His touch is spreading. Send for help at once, this threat is beyond you."_

The signature is smudged, and there is no date, but the paper smells faintly of grave dust and ash.

As you look up from the letter, a sudden chill rides the breeze down from the peaks above. The path to the Galmer Stairs—and whatever lies beyond—awaits.
