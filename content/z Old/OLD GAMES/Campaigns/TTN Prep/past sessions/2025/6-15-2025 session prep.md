## Up the Galmer Stairs

The wind howls low and constant as you begin your journey into the **Great Divide**, a sprawling network of mist-laced mountains that rise like broken teeth against the horizon. The sun is half-veiled behind streaks of gray cloud, and the air here tastes faintly of iron and moss.

For two days, the Galmer Stairs take you through winding ridgelines and towering stone faces. Crumbling ruins of old watch posts hint at an age when this path was guarded... now, it's lonely. At night, the winds shriek through the passes with voices that almost sound like names you once knew.
You camp on tiny outcroppings, using rope to tie off your tents and selves, to prevent an absent minded roll from your bed off the edge of the mountains. 

At noon during the second day you take a moment to look out over the lands to the north. You see the Berserker Peaks, the mountains you conquered and climbs twice over already. You see the black clouded mountain of Arkenstone, and know that Ivar's Obelask lies shrouded in the smog, hidden from your sight. You look out over the dark kingdom of Arkonia, but most of it is light at noon on this day.
Emrys, looking out over this visage may remind you of how with your friends help you were able to brave the blizzards and snow, Ivys, you may not be able to see it, but you can point in the exact direction of your hometown despite being so far from it.

[[Solace]] walks up beside you, hoisting Thalia into his arms as they both dismount their horses to bask in the view, *"It would take my breath away if I could breathe."*
- If someone wants to leave quickly or get back on the move: *"Can't we spare a moment to enjoy this view? It must be rare that doom driven heroes get to enjoy the beauty of the world. Let's stop and eat. I've been saving this lemon cake for something special."* He pulls a small loaf of yellow sweet cake out of paper in his bag.

After enjoying the glory of Terra-Nova, the world nurtured by the fallen mother Tena, we return to the ancient mountain road. We rest for the night somewhere on these jagged rocks, and awaken on the third day, to crest the mountain and make our final steps towards the downward portion of the journey, over the other side of the Divide.

The road is passable—until it isn't.
On the morning of the third day, you round a curve and find your path abruptly ended. The wide, stone-carved mountain road that leads into a massive tunneled path, the final section of the road leading to the steps finally down this mountain, has collapsed, forming a jagged cliffside drop. The rock is not shattered, but instead looks... melted. Softened. Disintegrated in a slow, corrosive flow.

Beside the tunnel entrance is an old watchtower, seemingly the most restored of all those you have seen. Flags still hand from its wooden struts, though their symbol is strange. A dragon perched atop a tower (Some would recognize it as the symbol of [[Traxgate]]). 

The collapse spreads wide—far too wide for a natural landslide.  
Chunks of **discolored stone**, warped and pocked, give off a faint, unnatural sheen. There's no sign of spell magic in the air, but the destruction feels _purposeful_.

##### 1. The Tunnel and Debris

- **Arcana DC 16**:
You kneel near the edge and study the rock. There's no lingering magic, but the strange erosion pattern feels arcane. This wasn't brute force—it was something alchemical or elemental. 
This was dissolved... not broken. Melted by acid strong enough to destroy stone.

- **Nature or Investigation DC 15**:
The stone here bears signs of **acid burn**—but not from a simple flask or spell. The corrosion was powerful, sustained, and precise.  

You recall rumors of **acid-breathing beasts**, but nothing specific comes to mind.

- **Survival DC 14**:
You scan the nearby ridges and find signs of wind-scoured, ancient trails climbing higher. A faint track veers off toward the east—weathered, dangerous, but still walkable. Perhaps an old pilgrimage path or hunter’s pass. You follow it until it reaches a strange staircase, though its almost not. The steps themselves are massive, almost 10ft tall, big enough for giants.

##### 2. The Watchtower

Just off the collapsed roadway, half-buried in the cliffside, you spot the outline of a **small stone watchtower**, old and worn but still standing. Ivy clings to its southern wall, and its single arched doorway hangs open on rusted hinges. The wind whistles faintly through its cracks, like a breath caught in a dying throat. The entire tower reeks of death, the sickly sweet smell and traces of dark magic stifle your beath upon entry.

Inside, the air is musty with rot and silence. Dust covers old benches, a toppled brazier, and broken crates. A narrow stairwell winds up into the dark.

—a **snap of bone** and a clatter echoes behind you.

From the shadows of a collapsed weapon rack, two skeletal figures suddenly lurch upright, dressed in tattered remnants of old military uniforms. Their eyes flicker with dim green fire as they grab for rusted blades—**silent, but swift**. They lunge forward with their blunted weapons attempting to slash at your faces, their jaws hanging loose.

###### After Skeletons

As the second skeleton falls to the ground you take note of their armor, bearing the same symbol as the flags, a dragon atop a tower. They are Traxian.

The tower quiets once more. As you explore the upper chamber, you find a **water-stained map**, pinned beneath a rock near a dead scout’s pack. It depicts the surrounding cliffs in precise detail.

A **red ink line** marks a spiraling path leading upward into the mountain range—scrawled beside it in Giant runes and Common:
"**Nimbara Aeris — Temple of the Cloudwalkers**"
Beneath the title: "*Giant Territory. Avoid on penalty of death or enslavement.*"

## The Road of Clouds

As your party ascends the **Giants’ Road**, the towering white stone slabs beneath your feet emit a faint warmth—ancient magic pulsing through them like the lifeblood of a sleeping god. The road itself is wide enough for giants of legend to have once walked shoulder-to-shoulder, now flanked by rolling hills clothed in fiery red and gold trees, their leaves spiraling down like embers from a hearth fire.

To your left, a vast stretch of **sun-touched meadow** opens up, dotted with wildflowers that still bloom defiantly in the chill. Birds flit between wind-worn statues of old heroes and saints, each one carved into the cliffs above—faces serene, gazes eternally fixed on the path ahead.

Off the side of the meadow, maybe a twenty feet down a small, dusty path, a **circle of blackened grass** mars the beauty of the meadow. At its center is a small pile of ashes, surrounded by animal bones, arranged too neatly to be accidental. A faint trail of disturbed dirt and faded boot prints leads away from it, vanishing into a small grove of twisted hawthorn trees.

As you pass, the wind changes—briefly carrying with it the scent of **burned myrrh and rot**. Not strong. Almost... deniable. And yet unsettling in this otherwise pristine place.

Within the grove of hawthorn trees there is a nearby **holy marker**, a stone shrine etched with radiant runes and offerings of dried fruit and incense, has a crack running through it. The light once glowing faintly within the stone is now cold. No birds perch near it.

###### RP Suggestions

- **Pilgrim’s Reflection:** The Giants’ Road is a rite of passage for many. Ask players what stories they’ve heard about the road or what they feel as they walk it. Have they been here before? What does this sacred path mean to them?
- **Divine Connection:** If a player is a cleric, paladin, or has any divine magic, they might feel a faint pull or warning from their deity about the shrine’s corruption—or be compelled to cleanse or investigate it.
- **Necromantic Whispers:** Arcane characters might sense a subtle disturbance—a tremor in the weave, a fragment of a soul’s scream echoing in the bones. How do they interpret it?
- **Moral Dilemma:** Do the players ignore the signs of dark magic to stay on schedule, or take a detour to investigate the strange ritual? What does each choice say about them?
- **Memory on the Wind:** Have each character see or hear something personal for just a moment—perhaps a childhood memory, a forgotten face, or a lover’s voice—encouraging them to speak, reminisce, or bond.

After hours of ascent along the mist-wreathed **Giants’ Road**, the clouds part as if by divine will, revealing the mountaintop temple of the Cloud Giants.

**Built directly into the crag of a jagged peak**, the structure appears less like it was constructed and more like the mountain itself bowed to allow it form. Vast stone archways etched with swirling storm patterns and ancient runes loom over you. Winds here sing, not howl, as though whispering a welcome—or a warning.

**Towering statues** of giant warriors flank the path: some serene, some stern, all carved with reverence and humility. One clutches a massive hourglass with sand that seems to fall upward. Another bears a stone lantern alight with cold blue flame, even in the daylight.

As you draw close, you notice the **main entrance**—a massive set of wind-worn doors—lies sealed shut by silver chains and white steel locks that hum with dormant power.

As your party gathers before the sealed gate, **Lumina feels a memory stir**—a conversation with the Cloud Giant Princess, spoken once in quiet confidence while watching stars swirl above the floating city:

- Nobody uses the front doors, they're actually just decoration
- Pass through the crypt to honor the dead
- Someone will be down there to greet you and give refreshments as you enter the inner temple.

As Lumina shares this, she instinctively points to a narrow staircase carved into the rock nearby—**leading downward**, half-obscured by mist. The winds blow warmer here, strangely comforting. Etched into the stone above the path:  
*"To remember is to rise."*

## The Crypt of Clouds

As you descend into the crypt, a cold, blue-tinted light suffuses the vast stone chamber, casting long shadows between rows of carved shelves lined with hundreds of cloud giant skulls—each polished and reverently placed. The air is still and reverent, heavy with the scent of old incense and stone dust. Above, immense skeletons of ancient giants hang suspended by silver chains from the vaulted ceiling, their hollow eye sockets watching in eternal silence. Every footstep echoes like a whisper in a sacred cathedral of the dead.

Lumina, there should be apprentice mage Giants working here, air elementals bound to tasks and functions, and servants attending to the needs of the crypt, yet there is nothing. The air is eerie, and stale, in the temple of the cloud giants air is always moving, so why is this place so still?

You hear a faint creak from behind and ahead, the doors and slammed with a violent gust of wind, both exits shutting in an instant with a wooden snap. As you ponder that thought you all hear a voice, almost like a whisper, gliding across the faintest breeze to your ears, *"You have come to the crypt... you will remain in the crypt... who are you? Why have you come here..."*
The voice trails off, awaiting an answer.

*"You have made a grave error, an error that takes you to your grave... awaiting your binding... now SLEEP!"*

##### Combat Begins!

You watch as the wind kinks up, skeletons erupt from the floor of the crypt around you, you feel a cold deathly wind as two giant ghosts appear from the air, holding crude and haunted ethereal blades, and finally, two of the massive skeletons chains snap, as the large giant undead drop to the floor clad in their armor and carrying black metal swords, roll initiative!
