## Elephant in the Room

The heavy oak doors swing open, revealing a dimly lit dining hall where the air is thick with the scent of spiced incense and slow-roasted meats. A gaunt, silver-masked servant bows deeply and gestures for you to enter, their voice a whisper: *"The General will join you shortly. Please, be seated and partake in his... hospitality."* 
The long table is set with black porcelain plates, golden cutlery, and goblets filled with wine so dark it seems to swallow the candlelight. Tapestries depicting battles both historic and esoteric line the walls, their threads shimmering as if alive. At the head of the table, an empty chair looms—carved from a single piece of obsidian, its high back shaped like a towering, many-armed deity. Shadows dance unnaturally in the corners of the room, and the silence is broken only by the faint, discordant hum of an unseen instrument. A first course has already been laid before each seat: a bowl of deep red soup with long noodles and perfectly cut pieces of fish laid across the top. This is a Traxian specialty, seafood ramen!

Beyond the food in the bowls before you, there is a banquet across the table, turkey legs, veggies of all kinds, savory meats, pastries, and specialty drinks. You could easily engorge yourselves on all the delicious treats available here, what are you guys having for dinner?

Let them talk and eat for a little while, some RP. What do you grab, any comments on what other people are having?

Hannibal arrives with Tarot beside him, Hannibal sits at the head of the table, with a palpable aura of calm, as if when he walks past you your heartbeat slows and your emotions soften. Even Ivys feels a faint internal peace wash over him as the Loxodon walks by.

Hannibal talks to the party as they eat, makes note of commenting o their dinner food choices, Ivys eating lots of red bloody meats, Emrys eating sweets.

Tarot comments that Hannibal is having veggies, Hannibal counters that you still get hungry across the river. More idle talk, war efforts, how things have slowed down since Ivar disappeared (Hannibal affirms he no longer holds any loyalty to Ivar or Arkonil), the coming famine when Traxgate's deep grain stores run out. This famine is due to the havoc of the scar, impossible to eat the corrupted fish from the Sea of Smoke, farmland won't grow, can't protect farmers outside the city, its all chopped.

He asks the party why they are here, and when they answer he says good, and then asks if they really know why they are here. They have been brought here by fate. All things happen as they are meant to. Hannibal has sought out the greatest warriors and mages in the world, including Augustus and Ivar, he has been searching for someone worthy, and through his meditation he realized who the only true challenge was, Kairos. Unfortunately Kairos is no longer a challenge, so his spawn will have to do, the twisted replicant clone. 

"You know Mr. Marcel, I campaigned alongside a cleric of your Lord Illuman for many months in the bloody conquest of Talayal, a very strange land. He was a seer, a future looker. The Seer of 7 Horizons they call him. He is here now in Traxgate. Should you visit, make known that I have offered his services to you, and at your service he shall be."

"Tell me, Alismana, you come from the Mothryn conclave of the Ignalis Forest yes? I have made a pact with your elders in exchange for goods, we harvest a meager amount of your fine trees. They are a gentle and kind folk. I am honored to host you for dinner."

Hannibal talks more, he wants to learn more about the party, to hear their tales.
- What is your greatest achievement?
- What do you believe is your purpose in life?
- What is the hardest battle you have ever fought?

After talking more, he hints at his motivations and knowledge of Kairos. "I would never have reached this point in my enlightenment if it weren't for Kairos, for the compromise of Lazarus, the greatest man who ever lived at the whim of an outsider. A pawn of the creator god... it matters not. My anger and desire are gone, now you heroes are my purpose. Tonight you will show me who you are, and then you will evolve. You are a chemical reaction, and I am the catalyst. That is our destiny."

He ends the conversation, promising to release Emandyr and Augustus to them if they defeat Stygian tonight. He also promises them an honorable battle, and when he defeats them he hopes they will remain in Traxgate and train to defeat him, he knows his end cannot come at the hands of the heroes he faces now. They must grow and change. The river stands too wide for them tonight.

He leaves dinner, and the room around them shimmers and shifts as it turns into a treacherous mountain peak.

## The Arena of Traxgate

#### Fight 1: Above the Clouds

As your eyes resettle you feel the strange air of the arena around you, you can see the crowds of people watching from the stands above, but you are not on a sand floor. You stand on mountain grass and hard stones, you are in the ever changing pocket dimension of Traxgate's arena, able to shift and flow through near infinite different landscapes and battlefields. 

A beautiful battlefield of hilltop grass stretches before you. In the chilly air you feel a gust of wind, as you see ogres rousing from sleep, and a Wyrm of pure cloud coiling in an aggressive stance across for you, roll initiative!
