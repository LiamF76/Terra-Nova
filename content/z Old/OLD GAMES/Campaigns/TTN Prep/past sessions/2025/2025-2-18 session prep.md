## The Early Morning

As you all awaken from your last night of rest here in the Berserker Peaks, before you will reach Lorne and Juno's tavern... Emrys, you see your pack strewn open, its contents spilled out onto the dirt and snow, and your hempen rope is missing! Ivys, you also notice that your staff... is missing! A set of small pawprints, almost alien in their form, lead into the forest.

As you follow the pawprints they head to a large tree with a low hanging branch. You see Ivys' staff with the rope tied around it, thrown over the branch and around the staff again. It appears to be a swing! A small note rests on the flat side of the staff, "Borrowed your rope and stick, thank you!" The handwriting is clear but large, as if written by a child who is still learning to write.
