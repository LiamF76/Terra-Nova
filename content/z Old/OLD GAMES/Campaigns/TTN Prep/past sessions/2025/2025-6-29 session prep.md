## Grave of the Cloud Rulers

As you emerge from the crypt, the cold, sacred silence gives way to the stench of rot and the soft hiss of wind through broken stone. The once-beautiful **Garden of the Skyborne**—where cloud giants once cultivated living thunder lilies and floating blossoms—now lies in ruin. **Cracked marble walkways** are stained dark with dried blood, and **bodies lie strewn across the grounds**—some giant, others smallfolk, likely servants or slaves. All are **contorted in death**, expressions frozen in agony, many **bearing signs of necromantic tampering**—runes carved into their flesh, black veins spidering across their skin.

**Shriveled trees weep sap as thick and dark as tar**, and the ancient fountain of holy water now gurgles with brackish filth. The air feels thin here—like something holy has been **ripped away**. There are swathes of blighted ground, as if waves of poison had been poured in lines across the grasses.

At the far end of the ruined courtyard stands the **grand gate to the inner sanctum** of the Cloud Temple, now **half-collapsed**, scorched by acidic fire. A giant corpse, headless and chained, leans against the wall nearby with a shattered holy symbol crushed beneath it.

This is no battlefield. It is a **massacre**—and a **warning**. 

[[Solace]]. *"In the name of all the heavens... what could have done this? And why? Such senseless and vile acts..."*
[[Thalia]] clutches at his arm, her eyes darting from place to place as she looks terrified at the destruction.

As you all take in the destruction, Lumina, how to you feel seeing the deaths and decay of a people you once called friends and allies?

[[Thalia]] is muttering, whispering to herself, "They were afraid, the breath, not air, they cannot rest, their spirits have been twisted..."

Lumina, as you look over the site, you remember that Princess Sigrid always played with you in the Royal Gardens, which are atop the mountain beyond the clouds through a secret entrance in the fountain... 
You have to put your hand in the water and speak the words:
**"Vael’moraen Vun Thurai."**  
_(“May the skies part for those who walk in trust.”)_

## The Royal Gardens

You step through the narrow, hidden passage behind the desecrated fountain, the echo of the massacre fading behind you like a nightmare sealed shut. As the stone gives way to open air, you find yourselves in a different world—untouched, serene, and eerily silent.

The **private royal gardens** spread before you in a soft crescent of green and silver. **Silken grass glows faintly blue** under the filtered sky above, and **white blossoms shimmer with dew**, suspended like stars among pale-leaved trees. The air here is crisp, cool, and alive with gentle currents, as if the very wind still remembers the joy of this sacred place. No bodies mar the ground. No decay, no blood. Only quiet reverence.

At the center of the garden stands a **circular stone platform**, ringed by six pillar stumps worn by time. Where once a shrine or chamber may have stood, there is now only open air—and at its heart, a **pedestal of cloud-carved marble**.

Above it floats a **belt of massive size**, thick as a tree trunk and woven of enchanted sky-silver and stormhide. It turns slowly within a **sphere of whirling wind**, untouched by dust or time. The very air seems to resist your approach with a hum of ancient power—**A belt bearing the face of a Cloud Giant**, protected by the last breath of the temple’s magic.

As you attempt to reach inside the sphere and take the belt, you feel a gust smack your hand away, and a booming thunderous bleat from above you on a ridge, where a massive cloudy fluffed Ram stands guard! Air elementals manifest above and around you, roll initiative!

## Knights of Traxgate

As you exit the temple you see figures in the air. The flaps of giant wings as three Wyverns approach, bearing flags with the standards of Traxgate, alongside a white flag being held aloft by the lead rider. As they approach you see they are clearly knights, clad in the battle worn dark steel armor of seasoned warriors. They land, and leader, holding the white flag high, points to you all, from atop a close ridge about 15ft up and to the west of you. 

*"Halt. By order of the High Priest of Penance, The Child of the Black Sun. Brother Tarot. You are invited to Traxgate, under the protection of the Black Grail."*

## South of the Great Divide

As you crest the last ridge of the Great Divide, a gust of wind rolls over the cliffside and carries with it the scent of dry leaves, smoke, and thawed earth. Below you, the world unfolds in gold and copper—rolling hills draped in autumn’s fading fire. The snowline is behind you now, and with it, the endless white silence of the northern winter.

You see distant forests blazing with color, rivers unfrozen and glinting in the midday sun, and the faint outlines of villages tucked between crimson trees. The air is still sharp with mountain chill, but it bites less fiercely now. You’ve crossed into a different land—**one alive**, **dying beautifully**, and **waking all at once**.

The road down the mountain is much safer on this side, though it is nigh impassable beyond the path. There is nowhere for horses to travel safely besides the path. This would be fine it it weren't for the very clear gatehouse to the south.
