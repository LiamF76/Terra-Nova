## The Hunters

This is a road encounter.

As you walk down the snowy road you are taunted by the looming cathedral, a great feat of construction in sickly black stone. The entire building radiates darkness, this is where the ancient Abyss mage Qithtar studied the forces of the deep.

The snow around you clearly stops at a point, leaving only the frozen earth, as if the temple itself radiates such heat that the ice melts in its presence.

As you walk you take in this sight, you hear a single twig snap, as a rabid purple magehunter leaps from the trees along the road, shadowy hounds manifest in front of you on the path, and a blast of black energy shatters a section of the road in front of you, originating from a shambling mass of shadow and mutations, roll initiative!

Shadowy archers and robed cultists step forward from the brush and join the battle!
