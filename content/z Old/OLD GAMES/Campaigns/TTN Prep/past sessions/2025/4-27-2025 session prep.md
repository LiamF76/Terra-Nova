## Investigating Zyrathia's Lab

[[Zyrathia]]'s labratory is a medium sized chamber off the side of the massive Underdark portal room where you previously fought her. The air in the chamber is thick with the acrid stench of burnt ozone and something far older—a primal, creeping wrongness that slithers down your spine. The walls pulse with veins of abyssal runes, their sickly violet glow casting jagged shadows across the cluttered room. Shelves along the walls sag under the weight of grotesque specimens—floating organs in blackened jars, eyeless creatures twitching in preservation fluids, and tomes bound in what looks like stretched human skin. At the room’s center, a massive obsidian altar is stained with layers of dried blood, its surface etched with void-touched symbols that seem to drink the light around them. Scattered bones, some still slick with remnants of flesh, litter the floor, and in the far corner, a writhing mass of darkness coils within an iron cage, whispering in a language that makes your teeth ache. The very air hums with suppressed power, as if the room itself is waiting to devour the unwary.

On the large desk you see scattered notes and details of troop movement. [[Hannibal]] appears to be holed up inside his military fortress in [[Traxgate]], [[Thargoth]] has begun something with building or construction in the Shattered Crown, and [[Kor Goroth]] is restlessly awaiting order from his master, [[Arkonil]].

According to [[Zyrathia]]'s notes and detailed documents related to [[Ivar]]'s many surgeries and mutations, they have been slowly making [[Ivar]] as abyssal as they can, replacing his organs, skin, and all manner of bodily parts with those of Abyssal creatures and Tieflings in an attempt to bring Arkonil tot he forefront, and give the Reckoner full control. ##### The Purple Tiefling Rises

**When the chant portion ends**:
Three shimmering forms of angels, agents of infernal justice clad in black armor and crackling pinkish red fire. They carry swords of the hells, shields forged in the infernal forges, and they advance towards Ivys. They are seeking to exorcise their prize, the should of one mage foolish enough to attempt to extract power from their realm without the blessing or permission of their masters.

As one reaches out towards Ivys attempting to reach for his very soul, it recoils suddenly, realizing there is nothing to take. They dissipate into fiery smoke, leaving the soulless replicant to his ritual.

As the ritual completes, Ivys lies in the center of the ritual circle unmoving, his skin cold, lips turning blue from a lack of oxygen. He is completely still, no breathing, no heartbeat.

A few minutes go by, [[Essek]] regularly checking Ivys for signs of life, but there is nothing. He makes his way toward you all with a solemn expression on his face. He shakes his head no, indicating Ivys did not survive the ritual. 
As he lowers his head, disappointed, "I'm so sorry. It didn't work."
[[Essek]] gathers his materials and prepares to leave. His face a mask of immeasurable shame. 

As [[Essek]] departs, the room rubles slightly, the entire temple shaking, and then crumbling, stones fall from the ruined walls as the entire mountain suffers an earthquake. The ritual circle glows red, and black, and then turning to a dark shade of purple.
**Ivys'** skin begins to fall from his body like flakes of ash, revealing his flesh as a new layer of purple scales and skin takes over his form. His body beings to levitate 5ft, then 10ft from the ground, it shudders and convulses as his bone structure changes. He falls back to the ground, his body awake and rising to his feet, **Ivys** you look down and examine you new hands as you feel a piercing pain in your forehead.
You watch long angry purple hors shoot from his skull, growing into his new deep black hair. He looks exactly like a Tiefling, **Ivys** has been reborn. The light dies down as the ritual completes successfully.
