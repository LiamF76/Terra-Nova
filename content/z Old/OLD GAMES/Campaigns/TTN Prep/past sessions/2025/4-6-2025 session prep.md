#### Sunclaw wants paid
[[Sunclaw]] lumbers towards you all, plopping down like a dog sits. "Well met again adventurers, a great battle we had, I shall have it painted upon the great stone halls of my horde one day. A beautiful image of the little paladin leaping from one dragon to another as we ravaged the Grey Ghost!" He laughs heartily, but leans down and speaks more quietly now, "There is of course, the matter of my fee."

"Ryfon, your official representative entered a binding contract with me, promising me a quite significant portion of your current financial holdings, not including your magic items or castle. "There is a contract stipulation that if you find me a suitable horde location and clear if of danger within 3 months, I can return up to 50% and return the difference after today, but initial payment is due now."


## A Celebration of Victory

Party the orcs hold at the Split Lip Tavern in celebration of Zyrathia's death and their liberation.

[[Urzoth]] attends, having been freed by Promise. She is a startlingly tall orc woman with pale green skin and tusks that reach fat past her upper lips. She greets the party with strong handshakes. She says they will be dispatching a few messengers to the Freehold and New Praetoria to indicate their break from the Arkonian Government, and [[Krag Irath]]'s return to independence. 
"We have had a long history of oppression by those who fear our strength, our warrior way. No longer will we be subservient to governments who view us as a wartime resource. [[Krag Irath]] stands alone, and united."

She proposes a toast to the party, "To the heroes who led this assault, vanquished Zyrathia, and managed to reignite the orcish fury in even Old [[Ulgan]]'s heart, TO THE VOIDBREAKERS!" The crowd erupts with a cheer, glasses are slammed together, hugs all around, good vibes for everyone.
