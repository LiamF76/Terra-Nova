#### Standard of the Last Cloudrider

Xeros the Last Cloudrider. 

Tarot returns to the Forgotten Armory alongside Emrys, and introduces him to Xeros. Xeros is a ragged Leonin, his right arm is cut below the elbow leaving only a stump and his right eye is covered by an eyepatch. He wears a simple set of commoner's clothes, and the bloody cloth hand wraps signifying all of Tarot's strange clergy.

He kneels before Tarot without speaking, his labored breaths echoing through the silent chapel as he drags himself to Tarot's feet.

"Be still Xeros. I have no punishments left for you. Your suffering has ended, all that remains is redemption. We must restore your honor, along with the legacy of the Cloudrider Legion. Mr. Marcell is here to help."

Xeros looks up at you Emrys with a look of desperation. "An honor to meet you Sir Marcell. I am Xeros of the Cloudrider Legion. Our knights were of the sky, riders of the fantastic southern Griffons. We protected the lands from enemies above. Two years ago we were sent to defeat a nest of green dragon hatchlings in the Drowning Dream swamp. We disposed of the nest easily, save for two of the eggs. The mother returned and my allies were killed in the blink of an eye. I was the last of our number. I managed to fell the Dragon, but my Griffon was wounded and I barely escaped."

"The reason I need your help Sir, is that I came here to Traxgate. I wallowed in my shame but held solace in the fact that I had saved our standard. A banner of a Griffon and silver clouds. The standard was our sacred and honored duty to protect. After arriving in Traxgate, it was stolen. Tarot has been helping me search... but it ended badly. The thieves are well beyond my means after my injury." He motions to his arm and eye. "Would you be willing to help me? I understand if not, it will be dangerous."

If Emrys says yes: "Thank you so much Sir. I'll repay my debt to you one day, I swear it on the Black Sun. It was stolen by a group of rogues, pirates, thieves. ## Lets Get Deep With Promise

As you return you find promise reading in a large comfy armchair with a cup of steaming liquid beside him. "Reister's coffee again, something about it... wakes up up better than anything else. Care for a cup?"

He wants to talk more, mainly to Elysia, but to everyone. he feels that trust comes from knowing people, so getting to know one another better could help them feel better about the cube situation.

- **Where did you meet the person you love most in the world?:** If he can't say his mom, Promise met his girlfriend Pyre in Arkenstone. She is a princess from the Fire Plane, there on a diplomatic mission with Ivar's representatives months ago.
- **Who was the first person you killed?:** "I was 14. Bandits outside Moonrise had chocked the trade route, we had water but no food coming for weeks. People were getting hungry, too hungry. I didn't kill a bandit. I killed my neighbor. Mr. Wellis. Had known him all my life, considered him a friend. He broke down our door, screaming about my mother hoarding food, said his daughter was going to starve and she had to share or he'd take it. I tried to get him to back off, he wouldn't. Shoved me to the floor. He grabbed her, slapped her, that's when it had gone too far. I just focused on him, I concentrated it, all the gravity. It was slow at first, he clutched his head, increased the gravity on his brain by half of a half of a half of a half of a percent. Just enough for a migraine. He knew I was studying magic. Looked at me with so much fear, so much regret, but it was too late for that. Collapsed his skull in on itself, he was dead instantly. Left the village for Moonshadow the next day, but I brought the body with me to the bandits, the cleared out before I had a chance to speak."
- **Do you enjoy fighting, what about killing?:** Promise dislikes fighting, and despises killing but it is absolutely necessary. He hates it because of what it does to you, every death is some amount of your own life you give away, but someone has to be willing to give theirs for the sake of others staying pure, such is the sacrifice of a true hero. There will always be evil killers, you just have to hope there are good ones too.
- **How long do you plan on adventuring? What will you do when you are done?:** Promise wants to adventure someday, has heard of an island in Akash called Harola, an entire land of ancient mazes carved by beings of forgotten ages.
- **What's the worst lie you ever told? Did it change you?:** Promise lied to his mother that he got accepted into Moonshadow academy with a scholarship for graviturgy research, he was never accepted like that. He left before they replied to his application, and got in through a lucky interaction with the headmaster. It changed his entire life, and he doesn't regret it one bit.
- **When was the first time you realized the world wasn't fair?:** The day Promise learned the truth about his father, that he didn't love his mother, or him, and that he wasn't coming back.
- **If you knew you'd die tomorrow, what's one thing you'd do today?:** Promise would find somewhere to have a nice cup of coffee, find the biggest ruin he could, and try to collapse it with one gravity pulse. Then spend the evening with his mother.
