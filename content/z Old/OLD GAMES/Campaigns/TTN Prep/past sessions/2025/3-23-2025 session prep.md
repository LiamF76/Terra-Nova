## The Black Sun Rises

**MUSIC**: None, let the narration be their symphony.
As you look around to your friends, barely conscious, ragged breath, bloodstained armor, injuries abound and plentiful among you, you know you have lost.

The despair of failure begins to wash over you, a painful bliss that the end has come, and then you begin to feel a presence. A presence that manifests as a weight, a suffocating darkness that falls over the battlefield like a solar eclipse. The air darkens, not with night, but with a dreadful, ashen blackness where only a single burning halo of smoldering light remains overhead. This arrival is heralded by a deep, echoing toll—like the ringing of a colossal iron bell—resonating through the souls of all present.

[MUSIC](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OiuWFxoEx5I&t=835s)
A single word echoes through your bones, down to the marrow, down to the cells that make up your entire being, "Judgement."

And then, he _manifests._

A colossal, shadowy figure wreathed in an obsidian corona of molten radiance, his face an indistinct void with only two smoldering, ember-like eyes. His form is wrapped in a cloak of seething darkness that shifts and twists like a living mass of chains. He does not stride—he looms, his towering presence undeniable, inescapable. His voice, when he speaks, is not heard with ears but felt within the marrow of bones—a sentence pronounced by the ultimate arbiter of judgment.

As he raises his hand, spectral chains erupt from the shadows beneath the witch, burning with divine fire. These chains do not simply restrain, they _judge._ Each link sears through the wicked flesh, forcing her to relive every sin, every cruelty, every vile act she has committed. You watch as Zyrathia's strange eldritch form resists, but the chains only grow tighter. A horrifying rumble of a scream echoes from the many mouths on her body.

A fragment of the Black Sun descends—a brand of pure, punishing radiance. The brand sears itself into the witch’s flesh, marking her as _Damned._ From this moment on, she cannot hide, cannot escape her fate. Her very soul is cursed to bear the weight of her crimes, her magic weakening under the oppressive force of divine punishment.

The figure simply _watches._ And as he watches, the weight of his judgment begins to crush her. Gravity itself twists around her, her bones creaking, her blood boiling as the sheer force of divine retribution begins to erase her existence. Zyrathia collapses in on herself, the blood vessels of her brain exploding one by one as her entire being implodes.

Emrys, as this occurs you feel a warm touch, a fragment landing on your cheek, searing you slightly for just a moment before cooling, and fading. You have been judged favorably. 
You feel a cold hand on your chin, pulling your face upwards gently to look at the visage, the faceless formless shadow of punishment, coddling your jaw in his gentle hand. He watches the fragment fade from your skin, you are judged worthy, and a rumbling chuckle echoes for just a moment. 
"No matter how badly I want to hate you..." The voice trails off for a moment, "Take my hand. You do not belong in this accursed place, my beloved." As if he leans forward and whispers into your ear, a breeze of cold yet loving words, "Of all my scars, you're still my favorite."

You all watch as Emrys embraces the towering hateful God of Punishment, and the world bends around you. 
Emrys, as you touch his ghostly black hand you are all suddenly teleported to the center of Krag Irath. On the surface, a vengeful black cloud fades from covering the sun, and a bright day shines above you all for the first time, shining on a grateful land. The shadowy figure has disappeared.

You see [[Promise]] sitting on the ground desperately drinking water, mounds of Arkonian Knight bodies around him, the dragons ([[Ovosath]] and Sunclaw) sit exhausted from battle in coils beside one another. [[Essek]] dunks his head into a large water trough, running a hand through his long hair as he relishes his hard-fought victory. More and more people around you, orc citizens, Ulgan, Mateo, everyone is here.

Ulgan runs over to you, the necrosis gone from his veins, grabbing Emrys by the shoulders and shaking him happily, "You did it, didn't you? You killed her! Everyone they did it, THE WITCH IS DEAD!!!"
