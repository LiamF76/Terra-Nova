## Meeting Merekas

Tarot leads Emrys to a small training area with a sand floor. A few straw stuffed dummies bearing wooden buckets for helmets are waiting for you.

They are beingly slowly but purposefully attacked by a red skinned Tiefling clad in steel armor. He has dark hair, and is methodically practicing a series of strikes with his longsword, accompanied by his shield. 

As you approach he turns to face you and you notice two interesting features. His legs are dabbled with fur, and end in hooves, less like Tieflings you have met and more like some lesser demons. Second, his shield bears the symbol of a horned wolf howling toward the moon. 

Tarot smiles at you Emrys, whispering just out of the mans earshot, "Good luck, he can be prickly at the start, but he is loyal, brave, and a good teacher."

Tarot claps you on the back, waves to the man, who waves back, and takes his leave back to the Forgotten Armory. The Tiefling stands with his sword pointed into the dirt in one hand and his shield held in the other, staring at you intently.


## Talking to the Moon

Ivys meets Nysria for the first time. Umbral cat fucks with his candles and light, Nysria moon shines through and she talks to him.

Ivys, in your room on the third floor of Tarot's manor, you work by candlelight. You can generate your own lights. magical or by lantern, but there has always been a comfort in candle light. The scents  of tobacco and pine waft from the one on your desk, its light flickering in the cool breeze of winter from the open window. Fresh air, a necessary component when working with heavy and potent spell inks. You are scribing away at your research on the Binding Ritual of Qithtar, hoping to find more information in the issue brought forward to you by Promise and Essek, someone will need to be holding Arkonil in order to seal him.

Your notes are strewn across your desk, the pale yellow light of Nysria the moon cover your desk, barely helping to make your work visible, but present.

The candle flickers slightly and goes completely dark as your familiar, the Umbral Cat, literally plops down on top of the wick, extinguishing the light and giving itself a nice warm place to lay down on. As adorable as it is, you need light to work by.

Ivys gets light again somehow. The Umbral Cat bats it out or eats it. Ivys, you hear a faint giggle coming from your window. You stick your head towards it, but see nothing but the moon in the sky, and a pair of guards asleep on barrels far down the alley. 

Every time Ivys creates a light, the cat paws it out. The giggling gets louder. 

Nysria:
- "He doesn't seem to let you get much done does he Wizard?"
- "Not a very good familiar, perhaps you should get rid of him."
- "Here, a gift, so that you can continue your very important work. A bit of my light." The light of Nysria pierces the window and shines at a perfect level of brightness to illuminate your room without becoming overpowering.
- "Wizard. I've done you a service, I except payment. I demand that you agree to search for something for me. You must find love. Good. It's a promise." The giggling continues for a moment before fading, and the light remains for you to work in. Your familiar rolling in its warmth.

## Sitting on a Beam

The sun hands over the canals, draping the water in those perfect stripes of teal and yellow. As the churning water flows around the scaled body of the massive river serpent that this trolley is built on the back of, you hear a loud clanging and chittering of teeth as massive gears begin to articulate on massive metal pillars ahead of you. It is one of Traxgate's many rising bridges, marvels of engineering that raise an entire center of a bridge nearly 50 ft above the water to allow the river serpents to swim through unhindered.

Augustus looks up at the mechanisms in wonder as you pass under the massive 40 ft wide airborne bridge, held aloft by those sturdy steel arms. "You know we have something like this back home in Akash. Our artificers invented moving platforms that can run  along rails across great distances. This is still pretty beautiful though."

As he speaks he trails off, looking at one side of the bridge quite close to you all, where a ladder rests, clearly intended for maintenance. "When I visited this city first, I found the tops of the bridges to be quite a nice place to sit and watch the city. Come!" He shouts as he rears back and leaps from the back of the serpent, landing hard against the ladder and beginning to scale it up the bridge.

AS you follow you can see Augustus above you, this climb clearly taking an effort from him in this weakened state. he is breathing hard, but determined. Eventually the two of you reach the end of the ladder, and Augustus shuffles himself onto a large steel girder, his legs hanging off the side. He pats it next to him, indicating for you to sit down.
 
Augustus asks Ivys what's on his mind, they talk a while about that stuff.

Augustus tells the story of how his grandfather first found the river. Lazarus was meditating one day, woke up on the banks of a strange river. It was a hot day, when Lazarus was a young man in Praetoria, so he decided to take a swim in the river. As he sawm he saw treasures, gold, and beautiful women waiting for him on the other side. He swam across, picked up a few pieces of treasure, a golden helmet, a necklace of starlight, put them back down. Said hello to the women, but declined their advances, and then got back in the river and swam away. When he sawm back to the other side of the river however, he realized when he left the water he had been pushed back to the side with women and gold. He grumbled in annoyance and sawm back again. This continued until Lazarus begrudgingly took a piece of the treasure, the golden helmet he had looked at earlier, hoping to appease whatever was keeping him there, and he swam back, finally free. 

He wakened in the grass on that hot day in Praetoria. Sold the helmet, bought bread with the gold and gave it to beggars. Then he went to find a real river to swim in. "What do you think that story means?"

When Ivys responds Augustus will nod and say thats a great way of looking at it. If Ivys asks what it actually means Augustus will shrug, his grandfather never told him, when he was little he just wanted to know more about the gold helmet. But now he thinks it means life tends to give us things, sometimes we can't refuse, like responsibility. Pushing away those gifts can hold us back, keep us from moving forward. 

"They say there is no man under the sun greater than Lazarus. That's fine with me. I'd be happy to find 10 men half as great as him, and I think you're one of them Ivys. Like me."

He puts his hand on your shoulder gently. "I may not be champion of Shao Daraan anymore, but I am the Grandson of Lazarus. I look out there and see zombies and demons running rampant across the world, corrupt kings, vengeful gods. This is not the way the world should be. Let's save it."

You two sit quietly for a while, until the sun begins to set, the bloody red hue of the Scar ever present. The day darkens, but the city does not, as lanterns for the festival come alight. Augustus smiles and begins to make his way back down the bridge. "Cmon, you got me out for a good time, tonight is the festival of spirits, the Night Market!"
