
Hello and welcome to the final installment of Tales of Terra-Nova. I am your humble dungeon master and you are my intrepid heroes, say hi intrepid heroes!

Things may not have ended the way we wanted in real life, but they did wrap up in our story behind the scenes. So now I am sure you are all wondering, what exactly happened?

After exploring dungeons in Traxgate and continuing their training, the Young Wolves saw a great beast, the black flame Lion, belonging to Torzod the Scar keeper, child of Arkonil. You captured the lion, luring Torzod into Traxgate where you trapped them, and siphoned their soul in order to destroy them... and keep it for later.

After lengthy delves into Kairos' memories of the future you devised a dangerous plan, but one that was well thought out, and well prepared. 

You met with Hannibal, facing him in combat and defeating him, showing your growth. Emrys stood tall against fear, Ivys controlled his emotions, and Alismana scoured the deepest pages of her spells to bring down the Loxodon foe.

After defeating Hannibal he honored you all, and forfeited his life to you Ivys, did you kill him?

Then we gathered our forces, Kairos, Promise, Egreth, Rosalind, Tarot, and Merekas. We were joined, through a subconscious temporal gateway, by the pair of Melantha and Alabaster, bearing the blessing of Balthazaar and Khal Daraan. Sent by the evil gods of destruction and chaos, your allies brought word, even the enders of the world do not with for this end to come, for when the world does end, they want to do it themselves.

Melantha brought them the promise of victory, alongside the promise of freeing the gods devoured by Khal Daraan, and to usher in a great holy era of Divines, and a hundred new champions alongside them. Champions and priests to shepherd mortals through the darkness and chaos that will follow the great battle ahead.

Now, all reunited, the plan unfolds, and the party attacks. You take Arkenstone in a single night, killing Arkonil's general Kor Goroth, and freeing the last stronghold of Ivar's nation from his control. The city rejoices, and you receive a message from Ivar.

A sparrow carries a tiny letter to the room of Melantha, inviting the party to come, to fight him. He has lost, and he would see it done, but he will not die like a dog, like his brothers and sisters who were slaughtered. He will die fighting.

He sits on a log, hunting rabbits, and waiting for 1 week. While he waits, you prepared. Ivys reunited Kairos with his Temporal blade, a gift from ancient Aelarian Chronomancers that allows him to function through the temporal decay. Emrys became a dual champion, carrying the blessing and power to punish Ivar's wickedness from Aldred, while also carrying Thalia's light to forgive him, and allow him to enter a state of purgatory to atone for his evils. Alismana unlocked the ancient Aelarian transmutation magics, and build a spellfire so large it was said to be seen by every Mothryn on Terra-Nova.

That spellfire began the battle. Alismana fired it across the field, and promise lowered the earth for miles, creating a flaming pit for Ivar to suffer in, but when Ivar fell in, the Reckoner flew out. Arkonil fought like he should, clawing and screaming to the very end. He did melee against Merekas, Emrys, and Alabaster, even trading blows with Kairos. Ivys controlled the battlefield, and Alismana healed and burned in equal parts.

Eventually the standstill broke, and many fell in battle, leaving Kairos and Melantha alone standing. As Arkonil cackled, driving his blade down through Ivys, the world stood still. Kairos utterd words to his child that he had said once before, in a moment of pure joy sitting beside his sons and enjoying life. "Make every second count."

He sacrificed his own life to create a strong enough temporal jump forward, both saving Ivys, and shunting Arkonil painfully forward, aging and weakening him further as Ivar wilted in his 80s. 

Melantha watched in awe, and with a resounding final blow, she finished Ivar for good, cleaving his arm from his body as Rovan destoryed the Uncrowned King.

A moment of peace and calm fell over the battlefield, but it did not last. The Reckoner was not dead, and still needed to be sealed. As Ivys had discovered, that required a mortal to hold the blade. The sword of the demon lay still for a moment, but it called out to each of you, which hero takes up the blade to hold it, and sacrifice themselves to end this once and for all?
