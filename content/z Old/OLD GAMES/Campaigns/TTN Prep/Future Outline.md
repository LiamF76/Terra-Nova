## Hannibal Arc

This will be an outline of the 'Adventuring Days' that make up the bulk of Traxgate/Hannibal arc stuff!

- Thalia gets captured by Eythora, rescue her.
	- Kill Hannibal's Lieutenants:
		- Calliope Windfang & Eythora Thunderblade: Eythora is a Blue Dragon in disguise, Calliope is Lynette's plot hook and a magic bard with a flute and she sings. Together they follow Hannibal. Calliope because Hannibal freed her from life as a prostitute and showed her there is more to life than gold. Eythora because Hannibal is a great general, and where he goes conquest follows.
		- Stygian and Aeros: Stygian has bound the comet elemental of Air to himself, granting him flight and speed. He fights the party in the arena. Give them a primer combat, then a puzzle, then Stygian.
- Hannibal sends the party a messenger that he will fight them if they can defeat stygian
- Pyrahn Dungeon, Emrys gets his wings baby!
- Handle the thugs who stole Xeros' standard too. Turns out it was stolen by pirates hired by Karegor to sell to angels who need its power to get back into heaven.
- Clockwork Dungeon
- Hannibal Fights:
	- First fight in the Arena after defeating Stygian, have him wipe the floor with the party, show some indication of weakness. When someone does something heroic, willing to put their body on the line, that is the only time they can really hurt him (have strange hooded figure watch, its Kairos in disguise). They Lose.
	- Second fight in his throne room, that's when Kairos and the PBP gang shows up. Hannibal watches them fight it out. This can go lots of ways, but should end with them teaming up to kill Hannibal and then bring him back with divine magics. That's when the real boss fight starts. PBP gang can't fight him as ordered by Vulcan, but Kairos will help. They Lose.
		- Kairos has a vision of the future during the battle, says killing Hannibal will delay the Day of a Thousand Dawns for generations. Also joins up with the YW on their side, desperate to learn more about Ivys. He teams up with them and gives them some information he wrote in his journal about Lazarus, that Hannibal's enlightenment can be weakened. They have to get to him while he replenishes his spiritual energy.
		- Promise offers to have himself and Augustus cause a distraction to lure his armies away, so that the party can make their way into Hannibal's sanctum (Tarot tells them where).
	- Third fight in the vault, Hannibal's lair/pocket dimension where he meditates. This is where they have a chance to really win. Hannibal is disrupted.

- **DOWNTIME WEEKs:** Hannibal tells the party they are weak, and not strong enough to beat him, they must train and develop their power if they are going to save the world. Tarot demands that the party take downtime to train and learn, they cannot beat Hannibal yet, and that requires practice.
	- Tarot helps the party get in to rescue Augustus and Emandyr
	- Emrys trains with Merakas of the Black Grail, one of Tarot's followers. He is actually Ferran of Felldoor revived under a new identity to serve as punishment for allowing Ivar to become who he is. He trains Emrys in the use of a shield.
		- Merakas fights with Emrys using a spear and shield, teaches him footwork and style, but mainly teaches him about toughness, that fighting through pain is the essence of the front like, not bravery against fear, courage against damage.
		- Second training comes where Xeros stands behind Emrys, Merakas attacks and Emrys protects himself, but Merakas goes after Xeros, now Emrys has to train to protect someone else behind him, his shield protects him, and he protects them.
		- Final day Merakas brings in 6 soldiers and pits Emrys in a wide doorway to the stables. Thalia plays with the horses behind him. "Would you fight for her, die for her? Prove it. Hold the line Marcell." He has to defend against the attackers for Thalia, he must be the shield.
	- Ivys practices practices meditation and studies the river with Augustus, and studies warfare and strategy with Emandyr.
		- Studying with Augustus, has to practice casting while laying on his back in shallow water, using magic without emotion, no anger, no love, no hunger, the path of the hollow mage. Difficult and contends with the influence of Craxivdan.
		- Learning warfare with Emandyr, studies Hannibal's past battles, learns his habits for grappling, notices the pattern of his construction of a sanctum in each city he rules from, this indicates he needs them for some reason.
		- Has to practice letting go, cannot feel anger towards Hannibal or anyone for what happened, it simply did, act without clouding his thoughts with emotions, his goal is to defeat Hannibal, he cannot as long as emotion floods his mind.
	- Alis studies war magic with Promise to learn more magic, gets a feat.
		- Has to learn to concentrate and fight in groups as a war mage, gains the War Mage feat.
		- Promise takes Alismana to the night markets, and teaches her about focus and intent. Understanding the agreed upon beauty of magic, but also its danger.
		- Promise talks about what magic and learning it means to Alis, and wants her to focus in on that feeling when she trains. Remember why she loves it, and remember the power it holds. He can mention his time at Moonshadow and hos his classmates never really liked him. He thinks it came from his intensity, he looked at that school like a chance to change his life, a pathway to learn about the beautiful mysteries of the world, they saw it as an education.
- What's up with Emandyr? He was a Kairos backup plan as a body, and then was given the mission to understand the Konduell barrier, as Kairos used to believe it was connected to his problem with Shao Daraan. After escaping the barrier, Emandyr was cut off and trapped in the wider world, and spent his life trying to build the power to get back through warfare. He deteriorated and after Hannibal took him as a prisoner he has basically lost his mind. Only useful information he has left is that Ivys is not one of them, he is not a Kairos replicant. He sees Ivys and thinks Ivys is Lisa.
- Traxian soldiers return on ships from fighting demons coming from the scar. Many are traumatized by what they saw, horrific beasts that are nearly incomprehensible, debauchery and vile acts that defy the gods themselves, blood rituals committed in battle by devils.
- Shadowfell Griffon imprisoned by a Traxian Mage, party frees it and helps it get back to its realm. (Based on Roc from WWW) This occurs at the menagerie merchant with [[Gossamere]] in [[Moment Prep]]
- Little freak demon steals stuff from the party, total coward, bends to their will instantly if they discover him and can act as a spy for the party, his loyalty only eists so long as he is in danger. Complete rat.
