We find ourselves on the deck of the Violet Pirate, Egreth's personal ship, manned by the loyal men of the Winterlands that have long allied with their guardian, the Purple Wizard. 
We see Ivys, a young man on his first mission, awkwardly adjusting his slightly oversized purple robes. Beside him is Egreth, in her iconic purple garb and purple lipstick, standing at the prow of the ship surveying the horizon.
You are headed to the Jawripper Chain, a series of small islands off the west coast of Estor, south of the purple tower and near the south end of the Caspian Bay. Known for its underwater geysers that produce boiling water at the bottom of the ocean, and warm waters around the island. Apparently a portal has opened, and some creatures have made an incursion into the material plane.
As you stare across the water Egreth, the islands come into view, a tall jagged stone coasted, crude land structures, you turn to your distracted new apprentice and tell him the good news, you've found good water and reached the islands sooner than expected.

As the sun reaches its highest point, noon, you hear the thump of metallic boots and armor as the knight who was assigned to this mission makes his way up from the depths of the ship. His strange winged helmet and longsword enter your sightline first, as Ivys, you think to yourself that Egreth never mentioned his name.
He walks up the deck, the sailors giving him a decent berth as he passes, and makes his way up the prow to stand beside Egreth.

As they talk, outline what they see on the islands, normally an uninhabited holy site for locals from the Caspian Bay, a place where their dead are buried, is now infested with a hive of exotic and terrifying plants. One larger than the others can be seen atop the many leveled plateau of an central land mass, which stretches 20ft into the sky, a large green mass that appears to have veins going down its sides.

As the ship approaches, Alabaster, you point out a ridge that you may be likely to reach, that isn't too infested, a landing point for your merry band.

They make landfall, and slowly make their way up the jagged cliffs, climbing and walking the long path up to the top level. 

They fight some plant creatures: https://www.reddit.com/r/UnearthedArcana/comments/qq82cr/expanded_sentient_plants_by_sonixverselabs/

And they reach the top level of the island, a massive green bulb opens and out grows a beautiful 50ft wide flower, its petals orange and pink. From the center steps a humanoid woman with green skin, pink hair, and a beautiful flowing verdant dress.

"I am the mother, you have slain my children. Tell me why your world has sent this evil to our world. You liquid burs my children, cease your aggression and close your portal at once."

She thinks they are lying about not being responsible for the portal, the only way they can stop her from attacking is if they lay down their weapons and surrender.

Together Ivys and Egreth can traverse the tunnel inside the plant and reach the portal where the geyser boiling water is leaking into the Verdant Plane.
