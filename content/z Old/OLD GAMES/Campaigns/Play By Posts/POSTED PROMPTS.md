
### Prompt 1

As you step through the fiery red portal, you enter into an alien yet somewhat familiar world. The air drips with warmth and the faint smell of brimstone, volcanoes obscure the vivid orange and red setting sun on the horizon. You see grassy fields and hellish wastelands around the balcony of pure ruby that you stand on. 

Around you is a strange assortment of beings, you see Novis, after leading you through the portal. She stands alone across the balcony, waiting beside the hundred feet tall iron doors.

To your right, looking over the grassy fields and seemingly meditating as the cool wind drifts over its face is a strange 12 foot tall creature with skin like birch bark, and on his back looks to be some form of wooden lance. He appears to be some kind of nature spirit.

To your left lays the large obsidian black fur wolf, Onyx, Vulcan's prized hound. It lays with its ears tucked back facing towards you, its eyes patiently studying you as its massive form slowly rises and falls with each breath.
