Melantha and Alabaster's duo mission through Drok Shah and Thalun. They search for Rovan and the answers of the lost gods. Melantha is searching for a different solution to the Arkonil and Khal Daraan situations, and she will find that through the dead gods story.

**Information Points**:
- There are hundreds of dead gods that have been lost and forgotten, that can never be brought back
- Rovan, Oteus, and Calina can be brought back. Bravery, mercy, and love.
- In order to bring them back they must find their artifacts, their remains, and rebuild their divine souls in their fallen planes: Rovan in The Hall of Black Stars, Oteus in the Castle of Cruelty, and Calina in the Temple of Debauchery
- They need to find an astrolabe they learn of from Hayyel, and an artifact of hers is with the Astrolabe, so they can help her as well.
**Who is after them?**:
- Geisor, The Axis Devil, The Hellstorm, One of the Timekeeper's Toys.
	- Working for Kairos, wants to turn Alabaster to their side to use his influence over Tena as her "champion", will help free Khal Daraan from Terra-Nova
- Ithuriel, The Wrathful Light, Servant of Illuman.
	- One of Illuman's angels, sent to capture and bring Melantha to Elysium to face judgement before the gods for her disrespect.
- Novis, The Burning Blade, Angel of Vulcan
	- Vulcan's angel, former warrior and mortal general who became a saint and angel upon his death. He is sent to kill Melantha for betraying Vulcan's wishes.


**What is going to happen?**:
- Melantha and Alabaster journey to the Godsgrave, which is the remains of Oteus. In the Godsgrave they speak with Orica, "The Old Woman". She is a seer who is said to be able to speak to the dead, including angels, which Melantha can sue to speak with one of Oteus' angels. (Which one? [[Hayyel]])
- Hayyel is a loyal angel who has fallen from the heavens with Oteus when he was killed by Arkonil, and she can tell them that the principle of divine rebirth is based in existence. They must find artifacts related to the gods, stories, items, and songs, and the more they are remembered the more they will return. Angels can help as well, and finding their artifacts may outright bring them back, which could help even more. She has only one answer to any question about why gods don't come to Terra-Nova or what is up with Thalun, "The Moon and Earth are prisons. The prisoners are the only two things Shao has even been afraid of. The law of the heavens dictate I can say no more." She also has a warning for Melantha, "You go into darkness not just against the abyss, but the light chases behind you as well. There are wolves in the shadows hunting you, but there are angels in the light that chase you as well. Ithuriel is coming, the Dawnfather wants you dead."
- She bids them find her artifact, [[Sheara]], the Weightless.
- After speaking with Hayyel the Old Woman tells the couple to seek out the [[The Celestial Astrolabe]]. It can help them navigate the planes and find the artifacts. It is said to allow you to walk the Infinite Corridor as if it was just a hallway with a door to every would you could imagine.


## Geisor Interaction
- Geisor has caught them in a little desert town in Drok Shah. He was sent to capture them by Khal Daraan, whom is one of his many masters now that he has been mutated and corrupted.
- His main goal is to convince them that Khal Daraan is the way forward, and that they can use Khal's power like Aldred did to mantle their dead gods back into existence, but this is not readily apparent. He is trying to do this through subterfuge and speculation, he wants to corrupt and mislead Alabaster instead of outright tell him, because Khal needs control over Tena in order to be free.
- Geisor seeks to plant the seeds of this idea the first time they meet, by showing them his power in combat, and then by talking to them, see those points below. [[the hellstorm.png]]
	- The Divine are liars, just like men. Khal told me the truth they would hide from you all.
	- They do not love you, they own you, they love to control you. You are not their prophets, you are their slaves.
	- Tena will return, but to the arms of her true father, the one who would never have let her die in the first place, the real creator, Khal.
	- Illuman betrayed his brothers for pride, he must be destroyed the same as Fulgrim and all the rest. Khal will cleanse the evil of the heavens and set us all free.
	- It is possible, and likely... you will see, you will join us before the end. The Timekeeper can control him, he will spare us small beings and raze Elysium of the divine liars.
