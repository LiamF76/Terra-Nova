At the base of a mountain trail gathers a massive group of spectators. Mayor Kenton is conversing with a few townsfolk. 
The Valorbond Mercenaries are present, along with a few other individuals. The lone people are Zalyn the Silent Hand (Martial Artist), and Molly Quilt (Rogue), and off very far by himself, a man in humble gray traveler's robes, with simple light armor and a mace on his hip, Tyran. 

Tyran, as part of his repentance, has been assigned to travel with Zalyn the Silent Hand, a monk of the Sliver Star, by King Adrithyr. He's kind of like Tyran's parole officer, to watch him and "eliminate him", if he shows signs of treason or betrayal. Tyran is excited, he wants to compete against Melantha and Reister in a friendly manner, and tries to shake their hands hesitantly. 

Mayor Kenton: "Welcome friends and competitors! This is a familiar competition, we host it yearly in the summers to test our physical strength. You all must make the perilous climb, from this clearing, to the Old Temple in the mountains. It is dangerous, and perilous, those of you in heavy armors may struggle to reach the top! This year for the Eclipse we have had something special added, some dangerous beasts have been placed in the course, defeat them to quicken your pace! The race begins in 30 minutes, prepare friends!"

As you stand at the base of the mountain looking up, seeing the ruins of the temple high in the hills, Melantha, Solstice speaks, "I know this place... Bring me to the temple... that is worthy."

The race begins and the party launches themselves up the hill, ROLL INITIATIVE! We take the lowest players initiative, unless we split up, got to come up with how that works on the fly. If they split up, the people left behind will be 1-2 rounds late to combat.

### **THE TREK:** 
- **THE START**: The competition begins, the first section of the trail is difficult and stony pathing with rough winding turns and steep steps. Athletics checks for all participants. The lowest group is last to reach the crossroads.
- **THE CROSSROADS**: You travel for a short time, reaching a sheer rock face to be climbed, around 60ft tall, difficult to traverse, DC: 20 Athletics check. The DC will go down to 15 if you have a rope set up at the top. As you reach the top you see the path splits into 3 different paths. There are footsteps going left and right, the center path appears to not have been taken. 
	- LEFT: The left path appears to be littered with heavy foliage, and looks as if it had to be cut through to get there, the sounds of animals and nature can be heard, but it is dark with a heavy cover of leaves and vines.
	- CENTER: The center path appears untraveled, there are jagged rocks and it is very steep, appearing to be the fastest way up but there is a broken sign with the image of teeth that says monsters on it.
	- RIGHT: The right path appears very safe, a clearly traveled road (kind of like a safe hiking path), but it runs along the outside of the mountain with a slow incline, meaning it probably takes the longest to reach the temple. You are already behind, the only chance to catch up would be the center path.
- **THE TRECHEROUS PATH**: Taking the center path allows you to make up a ton of time. You follow the path as you climb these gigantic carved steps in the side of the cliffs, the path narrows into a tiny rock shelf along the mountainside, requiring you all to travel single file, DC: 15 Athletics check. After traversing the cliffs for a short while the path separates into two shelves leading towards a single large landing in the hills, where it appears to widen again.
- **THE BATTLE**: As you all traverse these cliffs you see in front of you the large clearing, a stone outcropping with high jagged walls on each side, filled with rubble and stones. You go to take your first steps into the opening and hear a loud thump as two gigantic stone roaches fall from the rocks above, roll initiative! 
	- ENEMIES: 2x Giant spider
	- After defeating the rock roaches, something happens. Maybe mid fight they bump into some rocks, but there, from the hills themselves, wrestling themself from the stones and walls around you, a STONE GIANT! ROLL INITIATIVE AGAIN!
		- ENEMIES: [[stone giant.png]]
- **THE GATE**: After the battle, you see a gigantic stone carved archway. This is clearly not natural, but made by mortal hands. The stones are ornately carved and engraved with writing in a language you do not understand. The message is [[Door Message]] (translation: [[Door Messaged Translated]]). Melantha, you recognize one word, one word you recognize though you do not speak the language, as GOD. The rest is completely different, a language even Reister's translation orb cannot comprehend. 
- After this you enter the gateway and find a large cave, you find a large set of carved stairs. This is the LONG ancient ruins of some kind of massive temple that was once carved into the mountain itself. The halls are thin and the stairs are again gigantic, a strange ratio of height to width. The halls are very dark, and you are blind without light or dark vision. You climb and climb through the ever ascending hallways until you reach a gigantic hall within the mountains. There are thin carved windows in the side of the rock, ancient windows of open air, allowing tiny beams of sunlight inside the temple. Inside the massive chamber your footsteps echo, and you hear a faint rumble, and warmth. The room is dark, and you see something that you cant quite make out on the other end of the room, but is large, looks like a mound of rocks or dirt. As you approach, a small rock is kicked by someone, rattling through the room. As the rock tumbles across the stone floor, a wave of gas flows towards you all, DEX saving throw everyone! ROLL INITIATIVE!
	- ENEMIES: [[young blue dragon zombie.png]]
	- As you enter combat you hear a crashing and thunder of broken stones, the passage you entered from has been collapsed. 
	- The side door is arched stone, and the entrance is blocked by rubble, there is enough room for someone small to get through but nobody big. 
- **THE TEMPLE**: As you finish the battle against the Zombie Dragon, you see the room begin to light up, tiny magical spirits escaping from the walls, they sing and chirp as they fly around all of you. Fargrim, you understand the spirits, and they call themselves the "Children of the Star", they thank you for defeating the cave dragon and saving their home. 
	- LOOT: Pile of gold and gems worth 50,000GP, inside the pile of gold you find a shiny bladed knife (Shadowglass Dagger, Antigone, you would recognize this as Shadowglass, a material that can hurt elder vampires), Chest: Inside the chest is a huge stone tablet with writing on it. The writing is that same unfamiliar language, but at the top it says once again the word GOD. The tablet is 4ft tall by 2ft wide and 4 inches thick, and weighs 440lbs. The tablet can be compared to the writing once a day, DC:20 Investigation check, each successful check grants a letter translation. This can be done once per day. 
- THE RUINS: Through the blocked door and up the stairs leads you to a clearing, the archway you pass through is covered by vines and foliage, as you cut through you see a small raised stone dais, the ruins of the temple you were sent to reach for the competition, and you were the first ones here! You see a ton of not yet blooming flowers around the dais, the Twilight Lily flowers. 
- Melantha, as you stand here in this place, the light of the moon reaching down from the sky hitting the center of this ruined temple. This place is littered with the imagery of stars, and the floor of the dais is engraved with the constellations. As you look around this place you hear a tap tap on the stones, and a warm nudge on your shoulder as Orion manifests beside you, he walks towards the center and lies down comfortably, neighing happily in the moonlight. You hear the voice of Solstice: "This was... my home." The sword flies from the scabbard on your back, floating forward and landing on its point at the center of the temple. It speaks again: "What is my name? I cannot explain it but I feel, some peace here. Thank you Melantha. You are worthy."  Again he speaks: "I was once a king... I shined brightest in the night sky... beside my father?" He says almost like a question.
