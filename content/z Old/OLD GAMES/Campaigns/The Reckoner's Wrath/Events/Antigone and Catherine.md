### Blood Pact

Catherine takes a knife, carves a circle into her right palm, and cuts a line into yours. She takes your hand and both symbols are burned into your flesh. You both bear a circle with a lie through it vertically on your palm.
The blood pact is complete, you cannot lie to, betray, or seek to bring harm to your bonded person. You always know which plane and the general area (down to the specific location) of your bonded partner. 
She smiles warmly, handing you a beautiful pan flute of ornate carved dark wood. "This will be your instrument, your binding will to your younger sibling. You will be the herald of the new age Antigone, and our child's name will be... Dante."
