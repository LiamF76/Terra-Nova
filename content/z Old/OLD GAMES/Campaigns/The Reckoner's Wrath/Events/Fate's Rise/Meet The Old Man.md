He speaks as if you have had this conversation already, barely waiting for you to answer before nodding his head and responding. His face is wrinkled, deep smile lines on his cheeks, and in his forehead, as if he spend his entire life with his eyebrows raised in amazement. He wears a simple brown fur cloak over a tan garb and travelers boots. They are dirty with mud and moss from walking. His hood is pulled up but not in an attempt to obscure his face.
This is [[The Old Man|The Old Man]]

**CONVERSATION:** question/prompt: response
- Ask his name: Well most just call me the Old Man. It became a bit of a nickname, but you can call me Haso. I can't actually remember the last time I was asked.
- Ask why he is there/ abt the fates: Oh I just came from up that way myself. I was meeting someone... she's helping me find my son.
- Ask abt son: Oh, with the war and all... we got separated years ago. She's been helping me look for him. We thought the fates might be able to help. I miss him terribly, so much potential... this whole war was wrong, a bit mistake.
- Ask abt the woman: She's an old friend. I knew her when she was just a little one. I'm very proud of how much she's grown. If anyone can find my son its her.
- Ask abt the paths: 
	- LEFT: Well they both go where you want. This one here (*he points to the left*) it goes right up, sun shines on your back as you walk, a lovely way to spend the day. It'll get you to the Fates sure as you could ever ask for, but it won't get you where you want to go.
	- RIGHT: This path here (*he motions his walking stick to the right*) this one is a difficult trek. You'll find all manner of monsters, memories, and hardship here. It goes up before it goes down. You will feel like giving up before you even make it halfway, but this path takes you where you really want to go. Its up to you to choose.
- Unprompted: You know something Antigone, I met a young lady who was very much afraid of failure. I might give you the same advice I gave her, in not so many words of course. You can read all the stories you want, but you'll never have your own to tell unless you are willing to live it first. It might be dangerous, scary, downright suicide, but those who are able, must. For those who cannot.
- Leaving: Well I had best get moving. I wouldn't want to waste a day watching everyone else move around while I sit on my ass haha! Too many stories to tell.
