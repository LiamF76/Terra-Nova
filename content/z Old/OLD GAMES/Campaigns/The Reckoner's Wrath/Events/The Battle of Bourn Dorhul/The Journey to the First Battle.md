##### DOWNTIME OPTIONS:
Along this two month journey, the party has some downtime. There are almost limitless ways they can spend this time but I have a couple special options:
- Study the battlefield, giving you advanced information on the areas the conflict will take place in (Your troops will have doubled movement)
- Prepare your troops, training with/ spending time with your army will give you advantage on attack rolls and death saving throws with them
- Analyze the enemy, understanding your opponents tactics and abilities can aid you in battle. (You will gain advanced info on enemy soldiers and commanders present)

#### THE SECOND MONTH:
- **THE LADY OF THE LAKE**: At the end of the first month, the day that we reach The Lakefort arrives. Syrell's airship meets up with yours and the plank falls, joining the two ships with a 40ft long 5ft wide bridge. Elena makes her way across the plank along with her mage and commander Sara of her guard Tomen. She walks directly up to your group, "We need to meet, downstairs, *alone*." She storms downstairs and makes her way to the war room, standing with her arms crossed at the map table. 
	- Elena is angry that Essek was captured, he was a valuable mage who had some of the most detailed battle information of the royal army. She is also concerned, because as far as she can tell from her communication with other champions, they are losing their powers and nobody knows why. They can't get a clear message from their gods and they are losing faith, emboldening the problem. 
	- This should be obvious, but without the power of champions, the chance to defeat Ivar is essentially gone. She encourages the party to make a stop at the Tears of Stone. Its a sacred site within the ancient landmark The Cage. It is rumored that the fates reside there, and that if there's a way to defeat Arkonil without the power of the Champions, the Fates will know. Without champions, Mervin or the others at Bourn Dorhul, they may stand no chance against the unknown enemies Ivar has sent, especially considering they barely beat Thargoth with Melantha and the Cube.
