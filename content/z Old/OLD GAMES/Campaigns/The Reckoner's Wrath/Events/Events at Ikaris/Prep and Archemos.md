The party arrives in Archemos 2 days early for the Eclipse Ball to prepare the Champions Convergence.
Accompanying the party is Maple Obliteros, for her duel against Vivienne Ironhawk.
When they arrive at Archemos, they see it decorated with festival lights and there are small bales of hay with little candles littered through the town. Its a tradition to light the bales on fire and watch them burn through the night, families that are able to stay awake until the bale has burned way will have good fortune in the new year.

They reach Fighting Tiger's Rest, the tavern [[Clover]] told them to meet at. The owner, an Aarakocra named Amin, gives them keys to rooms, on the house! He leads them to a large table at the back of the tavern and gives them a free meal. He says they are happy the party is here, and its an honor to host them, [[Clover]] is expecting them 

In the morning they travel up the mountain to the Castle, the road has been recently redone and is about an hour ride, but it is a calm and easygoing path.
[[Clover]] awaits them at the gate, he greets them kindly, bowing deeply and spreading his wings (a show of great respect among his people). 
He updates them on which champions are known to be attending:
- Melantha Celestialshield, Champion of Vulcan
- Reister Metamorph, Champion of Kronos
- [[Augustus]] Auranthor, Champion of Shao Daraan
- [[Mervin]] Morningstar, Champion of Illuman
- Elena Syrell, Champion of Mani
- Brondar Silkhammer, Champion of Iros
- Tonitru Hammerhand, Champion of Fulgrim
- Magnus Whitefur, Champion of Skuld Ysor
- Kijma Kimina, Champion of Oras
- Julius Carnthos, Champion of Aureon

He mentions that very tragically, Elaris (the champion of Erebos) was found dead on the road to Terraven just 3 days ago. He also tells the party that the dragonborn champions unfortunately declined the invitations (Ramses and Bloodhorn). Igan also said he could not attend due to increasing Agamarthi aggression at his boarder. 

He shows them around the Castle, giving them a look at the meeting hall for the Convergence. It is a separate room from the war council, a massive chamber with a circular table and a large covered balcony. There are 20 seats, although there will not be that many champions present, and more are available just in case. Clover says he will personally be on call and present if they wish, to help with anything they need. Maps of the continent updated just days ago are available for planning as well. 

Clover also remembers that a courier came with a letter, and hands it to Reister (whom it is addressed to), it is a small note that says there is a Belt of Fire Giant Strength in the possession of Cerkil. It is signed with the name: Ouroboros. 

Clover says that unfortunately, it has been mentioned by a few of the champions whom he will not name, that it ONLY be a meeting of Divine champions, and that the other young wolves may not be welcomed at the meeting.
