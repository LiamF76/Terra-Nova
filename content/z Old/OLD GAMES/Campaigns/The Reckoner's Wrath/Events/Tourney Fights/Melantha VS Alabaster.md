Melantha, with Maple's help you don your entire suite of armor, and kneel at a small table where you place your blademaster's chain, and symbol of Vulcan. You pull from your pockets, a few small incense reeds, harvested from a small hidden grove deep in the waterfalls of Thylor Aethyl.
You place your blade along the table, unsheathed, and set it alight just slightly, warming the reeds like incense. They fill the room with the scent of cinnamon and feel warm. 
It reminds you of home, of the ritual taught to you by Sarasoar, the old arms master and the man who trained you in secret. The moment reminds you of home, and brings you a sense of calm, a sense of bravery. You are ready for this tournament, to show the lords and Kings that you are worthy to lead the divine champions against Ivar and his devils.

Maple helps you don your final pieces, you shoulder pauldrons and bracers, and you are fully armed and ready. 

Ivys, as you sit in waiting for the battle between Alabaster and Melantha, you are approached by Queen Annabelle. She motions Kaela up and away again, taking the Red Wizard's seat without making eye contact with you. 
- "Tell me White Wizard, do you believe we are the right side?"
- "The weight of the crown is heavy, but I bear it with grace. Can you say the same of your burdens?"
- "Power is a delicate thing, easily lost but seldom forgotten. Ensure you are worthy of it."
When she gets an answer she is satisfied with she departs, returning to her husbands side.

A horn sounds, summoning Melantha to the arena, and Annabelle back to her husbands side.

Maple walks with you, and you make your way confidently to the arena, where you are met by the waiting Alabaster. He stands across from you with his arms crossed, no helmet, sword at his side.
"Well met my Lady. I propose a test of skill, to the draw, let's avoid any of your elven blood touching the cold ground today hm?" He says with a cheeky smile.

The two take the field and draw their weapons, reading themselves, the herald nods to you both, getting your signal that you're ready, and he blows the horn. ROLL INITIATIVE!
