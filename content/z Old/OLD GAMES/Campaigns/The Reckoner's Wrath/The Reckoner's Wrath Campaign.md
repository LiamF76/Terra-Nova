The Reckoner's Wrath is a large scale political intrigue, civil war and war against demons campaign. There are a ton of components with the main 3 being:
- The events at Castle Ikaris
- War against Arkonil/ Ivar
- Augusts' rise as the Lumithiil

The first part of this campaign is the massive gathering at Castle Ikaris, featuring the Eclipse Ball, the War council, and the Champions convergence.
