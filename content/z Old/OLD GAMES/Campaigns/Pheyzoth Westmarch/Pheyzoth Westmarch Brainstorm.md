	- Westmarches, players decide who is playing and when, I prep only what they tell me they want to do

- Westmarches style campaign based in Akash/focused on the Pheyzoth Ravine
- Dungeons, exploration, and mysteries to be explored
- Cult of monster worshippers that are awakening the leviathan that originally made the ravine, final boss leviathan/ cult leaders?
- Party is based out of Camp Mistwatch. Camp is called Mistwatch because it was built on a sturdy section of ground surrounded by waterfalls, where there is a lot of mist. Hence Mistwatch.
- Ways into Pheyzoth from Mistwatch:
	- The Switchbacks: Treacherous carved footpaths used by only the poorest and most desperate adventurers.
	- Gearlift: Mechanical elevator that lowers to the first level of the ravine, most commonly used way in, but costs a bit.
	- 
- <mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">The Wahellian Curse</mark>: The Pheyzoth ravine is the birthplace of the Wahellian curse. The curse holds the land hostage, and any who would seek to rule or be called king in Wahellia dies along with their claims and their lands. Long ago King Ren Omrotva took the beast races of Wahellia into slavery, forcing them to toil in his name. He choked the land and its people for wealth and power, and ruled as a violent and cruel tyrant. He became so powerful that through his mages rituals, when is time to die came he bound the spirit of death sent to kill him. He lived for hundreds of years in undeath. Eventually he was overthrown by the Beastfolk, and they released death, killing him. The angel of Erebos lay a curse on the land, preventing another from calling it king forever. It is said that any who attempt to do so will face an army of the dead called upon by the spirits of death, and Wahellia is littered with the ruins of civilizations and rulers to prove it.
- [[Kaelor the Graveknight]], powerful undead knight that followed King Omrotva, said to wield an axe called <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Willbreaker</mark> that could cut through freedom itself, and he rode a great dragon that he bound to his will through Omrotva's power. He follows the Wahellian curse as divine justice, believing all choice and personal freedom to be a sin. Power comes from binding oneself to a cause beyond even life.
- <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">Cult of Kaelor</mark>, known as the <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">Obedient Thorns</mark>. They worship and revere Kaelor, believing in the law of binding oneself to a cause beyond death, and sacrificing your soul and free will to that of your master.
- Unique NPC cult leader needed, someone the party knows and is revealed to be leading the cult later on.
- <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Pilarn</mark>, undead spirit of a legionary who followed Kaelor. Uses a spear and shield, his tomb is connected to the goblin mines. Party sees it and can interact with it but is clearly outmatched and cannot conqueror it at first.
- <mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">Roymar the Rogue Mage</mark> is a wizard that lived in Camp Mistwatch, he stole some magic tomes that contained wards needed to protect the Camp, and fled to the Drowned Library. He created and released some gargoyles into the switchbacks to prevent the Camp from sending adventurers into the Ravine. He wants to use the tomes to break <mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">The Wahellian Curse</mark>.

## Plot Hooks

- <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">The Clasp of the Caster</mark>: The party is originally given the missive to find the Clasp of the Caster in the Pheyzoth Ravine. The Clasp is a magic item that empowers mages who wear it, it is desired by someone in <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">Camp Mistwatch</mark>. They don't know where it is, can find out from <mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">Thirjis Rootflame</mark>. 
- <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">The Obedient Thorns</mark>: Cult of Kaelor, worshipping him and trying to summon him and his dragon. Led by Farryn Bryralei in secret. She wants to bring back Kaelor because he was her husband. Thinks she can break the spell/binding on him with their true love.
- <mark style="background: #FFB86CA6;">Broken Gearlift</mark>: The Gearlift elevator used at Camp Mistwatch tog et into Pheyzoth has broken, and the large enchanted metal gear used to operate it has fallen into the Ravine. Someone needs to go get it. It fell into the goblin infested mine.
- <mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">Goblin Infested Mine</mark>: Clan of Goblins has made a home in an abandonned silver mine near the end of the Gearlift. The enchanted gear from the elevator fell in there and they are using it in a dangerous mechanism. The gear is being used in a ritual, goblins led by a goblin leader and a human cultist (from the Obedient Thorns). The party encounters this when they clear the dungeon. Deep within the mine is an entrance to the Tomb of <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Pilarn</mark>, a powerful lieutenant of [[Kaelor the Graveknight]].
- <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Drowned Library</mark>: <mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">Roymar the Rogue mage</mark> who defected from Camp Mistwatch stole tomes needed to protect the settlement using mist magics, he has gone hiding in a cliffside library built behind a waterfall alongside the edge of the Pheyzoth Ravine.
- <mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">Gargoyle Nest</mark>: Nest of Gargoyles have taken over the Switchbacks, making it dangerous to travel down into the ravine. They took note of the broken Gearlift, making the Switchbacks full of easy prey. They were released by <mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">Roymar the Rogue Mage</mark>.
- <mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">Lost Forest Spirit</mark>: <mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">Thirjis Rootflame</mark>, is the Green Dragonborn Druid of Camp Mistwatch that helps keep balance and peace with the spirits of nature in the Ravine. They have recently lost contact with an important spirit, the <mark style="background: #FFB86CA6;">King of the Birch</mark>. He normally guards a glade in the Ravine that leads to the <mark style="background: #D2B3FFA6;">Ruins of Volth Tower</mark>, but Thirjis saw evidence of corruption, and fled to back to Camp. - <mark style="background: #D2B3FFA6;">Ruins of Volth Tower</mark>: Once home to a powerful wizard king, this tower sunk into the earth when his reign was ended by the <mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">Wahellian Curse</mark>. This place holds the treasure the party is originally sent to find, the <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">Clasp of the Caster</mark>.
- <mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">Dragon Mystery</mark>: Mystery about a dragon in a river, it is said to show the way to treasure for any who can answer its riddles. 
- <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">Crypt of Riches</mark>: Ancient Tomb holds lots of undead cursed to guard it, but is said to contain vaults with noble family riches of great value.
- <mark style="background: #FFB86CA6;">Wizard Tower</mark>: A Wizard named Rowan has a tower in the Ravine. Someone visited him but the doors were locked and sounds of agony and pain could be heard inside. He was mutated in a failed experiment. Now he is a weird eyeball monster that summons more little eye monsters in a fight.
- <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">The Weaver</mark>: Hollow knight ahh is inviting challengers to take a test and fight them.
- <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">Midnight is Missing</mark>: Tark's pet / mount Griffon, Midnight, went missing after he passed away, upset at its long lived friend's death. It has made a nest in the Ivory Grove and can be brought back to Camp Mistwatch after resolving a territorial dispute with another beast. 
- <mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">Bullywug King</mark>: Guplup(A Bullywug) calling himself king is stirring up the undead, related to the curse, someone needs to go stop him before the curse manifests and kills lots of innocents.
- <mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">Rogef's Favorite Sword</mark>: Someone stole Rogef's masterwork sword, his greatest creation. It was stolen by a bird spirit called Fanihi, and he wants it back. Fanihi knows that Rogef does not fight and also is a bit of a coward, so he took it to protect the creatures of his forest, seeing it as a better use, and says he was going to give it back (he def wasn't).
- <mark style="background: #FFB86CA6;">Poachers in the Ravine</mark>: A group of bandit poachers have entered the ravine and are devastating the local wildlife populations. They are hunting a highly endangered family of Painted Pandas. 
- <mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">Imp Demon</mark>: An Imp Demon meets the party on the road, and gives them directions to a tomb full of riches, he can't clear it himself and offers them 99% of the horde, he just wants a nice knife. He saw one in the treasure he wants. There is actually a treasure horde guarded by a dragon, the party can defeat it or reason with it. If they defeat it the imp shows up with more demons to kill them and take it all. If they reason with it the Imp and other demons will be revealed as trying to rob this dragon for a long time, and they can work together to defeat them outside. Dragon is NOT GOOD! Will later come back to kill the party/raid Camp Mistwatch.
- Living Building: There are rumors of a living building that is destroying much of the Ivory Grove.

## Ravine Areas

- <mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">Lvl 3-5</mark>: 
	- <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">The Wesclif</mark>: The western cliffs of the Pheyzoth Ravine. Littered with lakes, waterfalls, and caves.
	- <mark style="background: #FFB86CA6;">The Ivory Grove</mark>: A forest of 50-80ft tall birch trees. Thickly wooded and inhabited by monsters such as spiders, goblins, and all manner of other beasts.
	- <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">Ashbog</mark>: A festering smoky swamp of deep water layered with lily pads and muck. massive trees and even larger beasts call this place home, alongside the dangerous Bullywugs.
	- 
- <mark style="background: #FFB86CA6;">Lvl 5-8</mark>:
	- <mark style="background: #D2B3FFA6;">The Black Crypt</mark>: Massive underground crypt network containing vaults of noble families. Full of undead guardians and monsters.
	- <mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">Bloodspires</mark>: Crimson tipped pillars of rock. This area is thick with fog and danger such as wyverns, monsters, and all manner of danger.
	- <mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">The Hexgarden</mark>: A magical domain where gravity bends, there are giant stone hexagonal pillars that make up a twisting and latticed 3d landscape.
