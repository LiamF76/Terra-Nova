## Puzzle/Roleplaying Challenge

Entrance is a trap/puzzle.

There are two pillars toward the end of the hallway that spit fire when a lever is pulled. A goblin is hiding back there pulling it to attack the party when they advance.

The goblin has a mirror placed beside him and on the ceiling to see the party.

The fire covers the entire hallway except the alcoves, where you get cover from the fire and half cover from other projectiles.

<mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">Enemies</mark>: 1x Goblin

## Entrance and Guardian

Big cave room north / mineshaft south.

<mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">Traps</mark>: 
- Tripwire Alarm: On the north path there is a thin fishing line tripwire
- Fire Pit: On the south path there is a smoking pit where the goblins are burning bodies. The floor in front of the fire pit is covered with dead logs and dried leaves. Stepping on it causes the floor to tilt upwards, sending you sliding into the burning pit.

<mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">Enemies</mark>: 4x [[Goblin Warrior.png]], 1x [[goblin warlock.png]]

## Trick or Setback

Statue puzzle

<mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">Statue / Gate</mark>: The way forward is blocked by a heavy enchanted iron gate. It appears to be more intended to prevent things from getting out than getting in.
The statue is a woman with her hands down. Her right hand bears the symbol of a blade, and her left bears a symbol for fire.
Right hand needs to hold a weapon, left hand needs to hold a torch/fire.
