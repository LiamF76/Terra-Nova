## Campaign Focuses

- lost history and power, new magic items, new spells, lots of lore and cool information/learning
- close the scar. study magics and rituals to seal the scar, work to some degree with a wizard (Ivys if alive, maybe Promise)
- recover specific artifacts to close the scar, one from the sky land and sea respectively:
	- True name of the demon mage who keeps the scar open (powerful ahh abyss mage)
	- Soul Coil (magic rope made of hair from a demi-goddess)
	- Last Tear of the Sky (tears from the spirit of the sky)
	- Seed of Tena's Heart (magic seed from Tena's Peak)
	- Blood of the Tidebringer (blood from the greatest leviathan in the oceans)

## Early game

Early on they are hired to retrieve some smaller things/ gather some information. These jobs come through an NPC that is a facade for The Wizard Council/Wizard to test if they are worthy of the quest.

What might the early game look like?

- find a clockwork songbird he lost many years ago in a dungeon (strength, problem solving)
- map an abandoned mine full of dangers and mystery (bravery, curiosity)
- escort a caravan across a dangerous area (loyalty, protecting others)

These quests need to be accompanied by travel and NPC interaction with RP to develop the relationship between party members.
Tie in player motivations. Someone's loved one is on the caravan/on the other side of the dangerous area? 

Who is the BBEG of the early game? The Wizards Council led by Ivys/Promise is hiring the party, but someone on the council is working against them. They set the party up and cause havoc at many different points. Party discovers this betrayal and the Council believes this is a perfect test, hunt down their opponent and handle them however they see fit.
