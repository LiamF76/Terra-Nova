
Short campaign where the party works under the new government after Adrithyr in Estor. They have gained control of the information on Kairos' "Project Tiamat", and have tried to continue it to create a superpowered dragon weapon to use and retake the lands of Escarth for their nation. Government official like a General or Lord is leading the project, hires the party to obtain items or info needed for the project but they are unaware of the nature of this job.

Ideas:
- BBEG is a government official who uses the weapon
- This could take place after a political intrigue campaign, showing aftermath of party choices
- Trying to seal dragons like Betrayers in a staff to create a limitlessly powerful arcane focus/ WMD
