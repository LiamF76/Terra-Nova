
King Ivar has summoned elite soldiers, mages, and thieves, the members of the party, from across his lands and from his allies. After successfully capturing his father, Ivar and his General Hannibal seek further humiliation and destabilization of Escarth, wanting to end support for King Adrithyr. 
He summons the players an an elite group, dispatching them into Escarth on a quest to eliminate a secret order known as the Keepers of Solomon. This order of Hyperion knights is highly secretive, but was deeply involved with King Adrithyr's killing children and absorbing their life force. Lady Zyrathia has learned that the Keepers of Solomon were in possession of Worldshatter, a long forgotten weapon bearing another Betrayer God like Arkonil, but lost it. Ivar sends the party to destroy their last temple, and find out what happened to Worldshatter.

The party will be sent to find the Keepers of Solomon, learn the location of the weapon, then find the vault containing Worldshatter and retrieve it, and use it to destroy the temple of the Keepers.

She is the Hyperion Oracle, though the players don't know it. Ivar reveals she was instrumental in locating Adrithyr's escaped children to be killed, and he wants her and her cult dead.
