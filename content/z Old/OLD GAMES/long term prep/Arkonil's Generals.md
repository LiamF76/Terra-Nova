## Generals/ Design Concepts
- [[Verrow]]: dragonborn/reborn lesser demon, bow user, extreme range assassin, use him to terrify the party, fire a few shots from somewhere they can't see and disappear before the even know where he was. 
- [[Stygian]]: Human/reborn lesser demon, uses a spear, can either teleport to his spear target or teleport the spear back to him, has armor and helmet that let him breathe underwater, use him in a fight where the party gets trapped underwater and he is at a strong advantage. Maybe Hannibal and Stygian together?
- [[Hannibal]]: [Loxodon](https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/lineage:loxodon) (elephant man) general, master of war and has huge beasts and warriors he fights with, this is a multiple medium guys fight instead of one big guy. He commands soldiers but himself isn't the strongest, just a great mind for tactics. He is ruling over the city of Traxgate, he will send soldiers constantly to distract and weaken the party. Maybe Hannibal and Stygian together?
- [[Thargoth]]: big Balor/ abyssal dragon war pig, he hits shit with his hammer harder than almost anyone, get them somewhere they can't run, try to get them to use someone else to distract him like another neutral actor in the war who is strong
- [[Zyrathia]]: Zyrathia is a human/reborn greater demon, she has two brains and can cast multiple leveled spells in one turn, her max level spell slots should be lower but she has twice as many slots and uses so it'll balance out. She has an honor guard of demons and probably resides in some kind of mages tower.
- [[Kor Goroth]]: Kor Goroth is the most exalted general of Arkonil, formerly a powerful fire dragon who was once the largest in the Wyrmspire, he rules Arkenstone from his Flying Fortress. Biggest baddest fire mf there ever was or will be, he has a dragon form and a Abyssal form, one is melee and one can fly and has breath attack. He changes from based on a round damage threshold. 

### Locations

- ❌ Verrow: Roaming assassin, harasses and hunts the party regularly, no set location this fight could happen anywhere.
- Stygian: With Hannibal in Traxgate, working as his personal guard (without Hannibal Ivar's hold on Arkonia and military might would collapse)
- Hannibal: With Stygian ruling over the garrison city of Traxgate. They defend the southern boarder against repeated attacks by the Legions of New Praetoria
- ❌ Catherine Rex: Ruling from Ironpetal alongside her Daughter Scarlett and nephew Valen Drakovar. Currently working on creating a new blood god child for herself to control and allow her to rule the world.
- Thargoth: Is building a gigantic fighting pit in the ruins of Oligoth called the Shattered Crown Arena, where he wants massive gladiator battles against slaves and warriors from all around. Is still under construction because he gets angry and tears it all down randomly. Is supposed to be protecting the northern border but doesn't care.

- Kor Goroth: Ruling from Dragonswatch, he has begun a venture into the depths of Nivolthar's Horde, seeking the legendary dragon's remains and hoping to find a worthy opponent.
