
Here I want to outline allies or friendly actors that the party can build relationships with. They are looking to feel less alone against the evils of the world so let's give them some kind people they can work with or know are helping them as well.


**The [[Blades of the Raven]]**: The Blades of the Raven are a mercenary company, but they fight for what the needy can provide, not a price they demand. Currently they work for the Freehold as the standing army after Augustus has abandoned the throne. They are the only line of defense against the demonic incursions against Arkonil's armies moving further west. They are honest, they do demand payment, but fair compensation and they often refuse unjust causes.
