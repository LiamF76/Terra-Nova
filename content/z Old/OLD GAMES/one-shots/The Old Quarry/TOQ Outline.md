## Introduction
Set the scene. The players already know each other, adventurers making their way through the freehold exploring and doing contracts where they can.
The town of Rockfall is a small village built near The Crosspeak Mountain, a small peak in the Freehold. The town's industry is based around labor, logging and mining. The town's mine has been shut down in the last few years due to collapses and a strange disappearance of 7 miners from a work group. 

You make your way down the dirt roads, the market bustles with modest activity, its stalls crammed with handcrafted goods, fresh produce, and the occasional glint of mined crystals. The air smells of baked bread and earth, mingling with the faint tang of metal from the blacksmith’s forge nearby. Townsfolk exchange hushed whispers about a missing girl, their worry palpable as they glance toward the looming silhouette of the abandoned quarry just outside town.


## People in Town
*Characters*: 
- **Mara**: Jenna's mother, middle aged half-elf woman, says she has no idea where she could be. Says she thinks Old Gregor, the town drunk, may have done something to her.
- **Jenna**: Young half-elf girl, loves exploring and has been obsessed with the forbidden quarry and exploring it for months
- **Tom Brickhand**: Local fletcher, Jenna's mentor, teaches her the trade of arrow and bow making. Thinks she would be around the quarry, wouldn't stop talking about exploring it. 
- **Old Gregor**: Old Silver Dragonborn, town drunk, says he sees little lizards and strange crystal lights at the quarry near his house at night
