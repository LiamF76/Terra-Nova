In todays one-shot we will be exploring the Summer Games in Akmu! A vibrant cultural festival and celebration of life and blood in Drok Shah, and now involving the lands beyond Konduell! This is a year to remember.

You all are members of TBD, and have arrived in the city of Akmu on this glorious morning, just as the festivities begin. The whole city is alive with color and celebration as you walk the streets. You follow the road towards your benefactor's lodging, a pleasure house that he owns.
Vendors shove fine woven silks and various delicious treats in your faces, attempting to capitalize on the merrymakers and drunks to score as much coin as possible over the course of the festival.
As you reach the pleasure house, lovingly named on its large blue sign, "The Serpent's Sheath", you see your benefactor, the skinny middle-aged half-elf, **Thabit Al-Hara**. He leans on a second floor balcony, two scantily clad women one human and one orcish laying themselves over him, "Ahaha! Look friends!" he says, swinging his wine glass about and beckoning to all in the streets and those in his establishment, "There they are! The Serpent's Sheath's new gladiators! Heroes! They are here for me, for you, and for THE GAMES! You are welcome friends, enjoy yourselves, I shall be down in just a moment to see you off for your grand adventure! Haha!"

The crowd now forming around you of city dwellers cheers and applauds you all, and games break out around you. A large golden dragonborn huffs at you all, almost a challenge, as he sits down at a sturdy stone table build for arm wrestling. You see a half-elf woman use a single finger to beckon you towards a tied up Morellotops, a sign beside it says, "10GP to any man who can stay on for 30 seconds!"

#### **Festival Games**:
- Morellotops Riding:
	- You get on the back of a Morellotops with no saddle, make 3 athletics or animal handling checks, DC14 increasing by 2 each time. Winners get 10GP.
- Arm Wrestling:
	- Contested set of 3 athletics checks. Losing takes you back one space, winning takes you forward one space. Goes until someone reaches 3 in a row.


After a little while of them playing games, **Thabit** makes his way downstairs and introduces himself:
- "My warriors! I am glad to finally meet you in person, you are even more impressive in person. We here at the Serpent's Sheath are proud to sponsor you in these traditional Summer Games. It is a great honor for us to be able to participate."
- A bell rings, echoing through the city as more chime out in response, "The games are beginning! Come, we must make our war to the spike pit."
- As you walk he hands you a necklace, "Wear this one of you, an insignia of our sponsorship. That way if you fall, your families can be compensated, and if you win, well it will be worth much much more!"

You make your way to the spike pit, where you are entered into the gladiator tunnels. As **Thabit** walks you towards the arena he warns you, "The organizers warned me about some kind of race, but to get to your vehicle, you must first defeat beasts. I do not know what kind of race, but I do know what kind of beasts. Exotic creatures from Sauria, the land beyond Konduell, south east of here. They are gigantic and strange lizards, good luck!"
