### Part 1: Setting the Stage
As you make your way through the city you feel as if you are being watched, small crowds make their way down the streets in this early morning, as you make your way up to the Dragonfire Hotel.
As you enter the shining foyer, the glass doors open before you, the gleaming white crystal front desk before a massive 20ft tall interior waterfall. The staircases on either side lead up to the grand dining halls and various attractions this fabulous place has to offer.
What will you do?

*Talk to the guy at the front desk*: At the front desk stands a very well kept Orc in a fancy hotel uniform, a collared white shirt and a red velvet vest with a funny little hat. He smiles as you approach, "Welcome to the Dragonfire Hotel, Lightbearer Guide you. How can I be of service today?" He will let Omroc know you are here, then you are brought up a few flights of stairs to the private rooms on the third floor that have been bought out by the Westlight Trading Company.

*Omroc Dialogue*: Omroc shakes each of your hands and bows to the group deeply, he appreciates you being here.
"Welcome friends! I trust your journey here was not too difficult, Mira knows her seas are tough bastards! I am so glad you are here. Let me inform you on our goals for these negotiations."
- He wants a 30% cut on goods sold in Calixos using their suppliers in exchange for a non compete.
- Omroc's company will agree to not trade in Calixos in exchange for the tax, but he knows he won't get 30%, its a highball to get them to agree to around 12, but he will not settle for less than 10.
- He also wants them to agree to allow Westlight to buy some of their minerals directly from the Silver Seal at a discount of 2%, he is set on 2% and will not take any less.
- The negotiations are in one hour, and he knows that the Silver Seal will be down at the hotel bar if they wish to introduce themselves first.

*The Hotel Bar*: If the go meet at the Hotel Bar they find Crawford sitting at the bar alone. He is very pissy and doesn't want to talk to any non human. He will tolerate them if they know about fine alcohols or if they say they are from Westlight.
His goals for the negotiation are to supply food to Calixos, because they have very little farming and trade with Estor has collapsed since Adrithyr's capture. He is a bad guy doing a good thing. His company can only afford a 9% tax, and higher and the deal can't go through. He has been authorized by King Calix himself to pay the party a one time fee of 30 thousand gold pieces and a promise of safe passage to wherever they choose should they be willing to turn on Omroc.


### Part 2: The Negotiations
That afternoon around 2pm is the meeting, but the party needs some fancy clothes for the occasion. They head out on Omroc's recommendation and dime to, Revelation Threads. A wonderfully fancy all expenses paid shopping trip! The shop keep Alyssa is happy to help. She tailors everything and they can walk out of the store int heir new duds.

#### At the Hotel

Back at the hotel you gather in the meeting room. A large circular table where Crawford and a shady looking woman sit, opposite them are Omroc and his assistant.

The negotiations take place in rounds. The first round starts with the team that rolls higher on a d20. Whoever's turn it is make an offer, and the party can discuss making checks to push their agenda. After an offer is accepted or declined the next round starts with the opposite team where they make an offer.

### Part 3: The Unveiling
As the final deal is reached between the groups, a bargain is struck! 
If they side with Omroc he is overjoyed and asks them to be official Ambassadors of the Westlight Trading Company, and wants them to accompany him on a journey home to Akash.
If they side with the Silver Seal they are invited to Calixos to join the criminal organization of the Broken Seal.
