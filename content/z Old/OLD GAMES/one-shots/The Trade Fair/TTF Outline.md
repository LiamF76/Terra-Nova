**Who are the players or companies**?
- *The Westlight Trading Company*: A pillar of the trading community, they have risen greatly as aa once small company in Akash, that came over Konduell's Grasp and began operations in Escarth. They trade in all goods but mainly in food products from the Freehold (grains, meats, veggies), and building materials. They want favorable terms from the Silver Seal, mainly they seek a tax on goods purchased from the Freehold in exchange for a non compete on price gouging.
	- [[Omroc]], The Owner of Westlight Trading
	- **Durlan Petic**, his High-Elf Assistant
- *The Silver Seal*: Known for their dealing in precious minerals (silver, gold, gems), the Silver Seal is a HUMAN ONLY company based out of Mythria in Calixos. They are seeking access to The Westlight suppliers for grains and preserved meats to get more supply to Calixos. Historically they are very snooty and they care a lot about heritage, bloodline, and wealth.
	- **Crawford Heathcliff**, Chief Acquisitions and Negotiations Officer for the Silver Seal.
	- **Marla "Blackheart"**, Enforcer for the Silver Seal, known pirate, violent criminal pardoned thanks to political string pulling.
