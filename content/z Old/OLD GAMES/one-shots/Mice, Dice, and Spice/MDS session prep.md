Hello and welcome to another exciting installment of Tales of Terra-Nova I am your humble dungeon master and you are my "scurrying scoundrels", say hi "scurrying scoundrels" ! Today is a special day, as you all preen your fur and trim your whiskers, because we're Mousefolk! A special little species in Terra-Nova known mostly for their love for cheese, a love you all share together, and that is what has brough you so far east, far far from home in the Underhill, to the mountain castle of Ikaris.
	In the church of Hermod, the Lord of Mousefolk, the lucky and cheese loving folk, there is a legend that one day an elf woman from heaven shall sit for a feast, and be served the most holy and divine cheese in all of history. All of you have dreamed of eating just one nibble of this divine fromage, and you have that chance.

After months of travel, hard days avoiding cats and other monsters, you met up with Buttons, the hardened and violent leader of the Mousefolk crime syndicate, The Whiskers. They have agreed to work with you to obtain the divine cheese in exchange for 50% of the profit. 

Buttons smiles wickedly as you all convene in his tent, built high on the cliffs of the mountain just below the castle.

### The Tunnel

The tunnel is a small carved hole that goes from Buttons' camp all the way up to the Castle Kitchens. You travel for over 30 minutes until you reach a crossroads, with two seemingly identical paths left and right.

As you move down the path you chose you begin to see spider webs, scattered and growing thicker and more common, until you come to a small arched doorway that leads to a large open room. There is a massive gorge in the center with two little rickety bridges crossing it, they sway in the wind.

**ENEMIES**: 2x [[giant spider.png]]

**Northern Exits**. Both northern tunnels connect and lead back to the main path, taking them to the Kitchens after 5 minutes of walking.


### The Kitchens

The room is alive with chefs and cooks, boiling pots and chopping vegetables. You all find yourselves standing at the edge of your mouse tunnel, beneath a small table, across from you is a large oven that you can hide beneath. 
- Make a stealth check to run across the tiles unseen.
	- **Failure**: You stumble and fall as you run, a chef turns and his massive boot comes down towards you, make an acrobatics check to avoid being crushed!
	- **Success**: You masterfully and stealthily scurry across the kitchen, underneath the warm stone oven you can hear chef arguing about sauces.
		- DC 18 perception check: You overhear one of the Chef's mention Lord Chester not eating his wet food this morning... he must be hungry.

As you convene under the oven consulting with the layout of the room around you, you map out a path to the pantry. You'll have to get in through the barred window, because the underside of the door is sealed, meaning you need elevation.
- Get from beneath the oven, across the other side of the room to the kitchen supplies table, climb up to the table and leap from that table to the cabinet, where you can easily drop from the cabinet into the pantry.
- From there, its all about finding the Cheese, which should be identifiable by your strong little mouse noses!

### Travel to the Pantry

- **Under the Oven**. You begin your trek, skittering across the floor beneath the oven in the large center island of the kitchen. You reach the edge of your cover, the many chefs looming above you, frantically preparing a feast, "Nononono, Lady Melantha won't eat that she's very picky. REFIRE THE STEAK! IT MUST BE PERFECT!" As they work, a chef knocks over a large bag of flour, crashing to the ground in front of you creating a massive white cloud between you and the kitchen supplies cabinet.
- **Up the Supplies**. You see a few grooves and handles as you all try to climb up the cabinet op supplies, the wood is slick from the air of frying oils and other ingredients. Everyone make an Athletics or acrobatics check (DC 14). 
	- **Failure**: if more than 2 people fail, one of the sous chefs turns and sees you, shouting, "VERMIN! LORD CHESTER!!!!" The clock is ticking, Chester is coming.
- **Across the Counter**. As you peer across the counters covered in utensils, pies, boiling pots and cutting boards, you see the immensely difficult trek ahead of you. Make a dexterity check to make it through the chopping knives of the sous chefs.
	- **Failure**: On a failure you take 1d8 slashing damage. 
- **The Meats**. As you make it through the cutting board assembly line you are faced with a large goats leg, freshly cooked and being sliced to plate. Give me a stealth check to slip beneath the bone of the leg undetected. 
	- **Failure**: You hear another sous chef call out as he bends over and picks something up, "There you are Chester, handle the squeakers for us please my Lord."
- **Past the Pots**. You scurry around a boiling pot of stew, make a dexterity saving throw (Dc 12) to dodge boiling liquid as a bubble pops and flies some fiery soup at you.
	- Failure: On a failure, a spot of stew lands on you, singing your mouse fur. 1d6 fire damage.
- **Across the Pie Swamp**. You trek through a massive raspberry pie, its sticky and tries to pull you under, make an Athletics check to get through it. (If two people fail they get stuck stuck and end up in an encounter with Lord Chester)
	- **Failure**: You becomes stuck for a moment, and you feel the soft thumps of paws echo through the kitchen, growing closer.
	- **Failure by 5 or more**: You become fully stuck in the raspberry jam. You cannot move unless two allies spend an action pulling you out.
- **Lord Chester Arrives**. A massive tuxedo cat makes his way across the counter, receiving loving pats and scritches from the chefs as he approaches, fire burning in his eyes. He plops down in front of the pie, licking his paw and staring at you intently, observing you as you struggle. [[lord chester.png]]
	- "Who might these little rodents who dwell in my kitchen be?"
	- "Uninvited guests to a very special feast. I know why you are here."
	- "You came for the divine fromage? The cheese of legend? Well I have to inform you that I am its guardian. What could you offer me besides your lives as payment for a taste of heaven?"
- **Into the Pantry**! You reach the windowsill beyond the pies, a massive gap between you and the cabinet top that you need to reach to enter the pantry. Everyone make an athletics check to jump across (DC 16).
	- **Failure**: You leap across the gap, barely managing to reach the edge and slip, holding yourself up by just the arms, dangling high above the floor, sure to be turned to mush if you fell.
- **The Window**. You stand at the edge of the counter, ready to crawl across the small ga and drop down from the window. You stand on the ledge of the window and look into the pantry, a heavily light shines from the ceiling, illuminating foods beyond your comprehension. You see artisan breads, chocolates, soups, veggies, salted meats, and on a platter in the center of the back wall shelf, a silver domed plate with a label that says, "Illuman's Fromage."
- **The Forks**! As you reach the shelf, make a perception check (DC 14).
	- Success: There is a thin veil of magic lines cutting across your path, leading from a set of utensils on either side of the cheese platter.
	- If they hit the trap, 2x knives, 1 fork, 1 spoon animate and attack! Roll initiative!
	- **ENEMIES**: 12AC, 30HP, +3 to hit, 1d6 damage, slashing knives, piercing forks, bludgeoning spoons
- **ESCAPE**: It takes a group check on athletics DC 25 to open the silver dome, if they have Lord Chester's help he opens it for them. They now have to escape with the cheese, make a series of athletics, acrobatics, and stealth checks as the chefs burst into the pantry, knives in hand! 
- Wing it from here, they make it back to the tunnel and escape. If they eat the cheese its really yummy, totally worth.
