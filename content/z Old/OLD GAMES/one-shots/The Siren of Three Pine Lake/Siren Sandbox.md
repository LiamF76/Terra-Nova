#### Story: 
The party is a rag tag group hired to investigate the mishaps within the town. Ships have been going missing for months and it's getting more frequent, and creatures have risen from the lake and attacked the West Docks, making them unusable. The Mayor, Beatrice, has hired the party to investigate the problem and solve it before the town runs out of money, and everyone leaves, or worse. 
Bullet Points:
- There are ships going missing, fisherman, and even the largest town docks were attacked. There are strange songs echoing across the sea during the night. People are lured to the docks and never seen again. The Mayor wants the party to investigate the disappearances and report back.
- Siren is angry because the Mayor broke the ancient pact she made with the founders of the town. As long as she allowed Riverrun to stand, they would never build a lighthouse, and any ships that fell to the rocks were hers along with their crew and cargo. 
- Mayor Beatrice Crosswind had a new lighthouse build and construction on it finished 2 months ago. She was encouraged to do so by Old Man Marlin, one of the shipmasters of the village and owner of 3 fishing boats (all of which are unharmed).
- Old Man Marlin wanted the power of the Siren for himself, so he attempted to bind the once benevolent spirit to his will, in a failed ritual, not the siren is angry at him and wants to destroy him, and gain back the power he stole. Marlin then encouraged the Lighthouse to cause trouble, hoping the Mayor would solve the problem for him.
- The monsters that attacked the docs are mind controlled/ undead sailors from the ships the Siren has taken. The Siren is using them to hunt Marlin for his betrayal. She wants the lighthouse torn down, and Marlin for his treachery.
- The party has to meet the Mayor, investigate by talking to Marlin (directed by Mayor), go to the docks or investigate the records in town, this is where the path diverges. If they investigate the docs they fight the drowned men, and they discover Marlin's treachery. If they go to the town chapel they can find an old scroll that tells how to bind the Siren and talks about the origin of a town, being that of a bond between a benevolent sea spirit and the founders. Marlin can give the info where the Siren is, if they don't go to Marlin they need to get that information organically by following people possessed by the siren's song. The party can then go defeat her, or talk to the mayor and see her thoughts. 
- The Mayor wants them to kill the siren, and says that the caves on the island are filled with water, gives the party each a water breathing potion that lasts 1 hour. If the party burns bridges with her she will NOT give them the potions. If the party kills Marlin without bringing him to be questioned or without learning info she will need to be persuaded.
- This can end with destroying the lighthouse against the Mayor's wishes, or fighting the Siren.

#### Key Locations:
- Riverrun
	- The Mayor's Manor
		- Mayor Crosswind's Manor. It is a large mansion filled with valuable heirlooms and icons from the towns history.
	- Chapel Naki
		- The ancient chapel holds town records and is dedicated to Mira the Goddess of the seas and oceans. The chapel has details of the ritual to bind the Siren (hidden, have to get it from the priestess), and records of the towns founding with the benevolent singing sea spirit.
- The Pine Lighthouse
	- A newly constructed lighthouse, it is made from the trunk of an old tree that once stood on an island in the massive inland sea of 3 Pine Lake.
- The Drowned Wharf
	- The largest dock in the town, was attacked by many monsters said to have gills and fins. The docs are destroyed and the townsfolk say that there are strange sounds and creatures lurking there.
- The Abandoned Cove
	- An old cove just 10 minutes outside of town. Said to be long abandoned and dangerous, on the stone pillar in the center is an ancient inscription, telling of the pact between the founders of the town and the Siren. There is also a shattered mirror in the cove. 
- The Fathomless Cave
	- The final boss room, this cave is a half underwater dungeon where the Siren resides, beneath the island the Lighthouse was built on. The Siren and her minions hide here.
