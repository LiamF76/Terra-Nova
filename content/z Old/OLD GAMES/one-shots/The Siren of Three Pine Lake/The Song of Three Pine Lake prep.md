Ok, let's get ready, time to set the scene.

The world of Terra-Nova, while always enduring danger and primordial chaos, is relatively quiet, for the armies of the Abyss have not yet risen, and the forces of the divine have not been called. A world before the defeat of Kairos, and a world where Tyran the Obliterator still sits upon the obsidian throne of Obelask.

However scary, dark, or bright the world is was or will be, doesn't matter to us today, because we find ourselves in the seaside town of Riverrun. In the north of Estor, the sandy banks of the city most north in all of Adrithyr's kingdom sits quietly, save a small whispered song across the waves. In this once quiet town that housed some of the most profitable fishing waters for years longer than the life of our beloved king, there is malice, danger, and mystery...

As you all travel you notice signs along the road, the driver mutters as you ride past one, the image of a broken sword carved crudely into the sign marking your path. 
Glen - "It's a blessing, asking Illuman for protection, this town's been falling on hard times, danger, some say the sea has turned sour, gone cursed. It's this damned war."

As you ride for a while eventually you see the houses and docks, the smell of salty air begins to fill your nostrils, and over the horizon you see the sprawling port town of Riverrun. You hear the gulls in the sky fly overhead, seeking the bounty of the sea, but as you look up in the sky, you realize they are flying away from the town, not towards it. 

Glenn stops about 100ft from the town, pausing the cart and saying, "This is where I let ya off friends. Walk with the light, I pray ya bring some peace to this town." He cracks the reigns and turns the cart away, riding slowly down the road back into the forest.

#### Enter Riverrun
As you walk under the wooden archway to the town, the dirt road beneath your feet slowly turns to gravel and sand. The houses are nice, but most look empty or unused for weeks. Few guards can be seen walking past you, as they do they give you a strange but hopeful look. You guys would ask for directions, but the way is clear, considering the Manor is visible from almost anywhere in the town. 

#### The Manor
You make your way to the door, where you are met by 2 armed guards in heavy plate armor. They go to cross their spears and stop you from entering, but they just shrug and step aside, allowing you in. You make your way into the manor, walking into a large entryway with a dining table, at the table sits a halfling woman in purple robes sewn with the images of ships and waves. 

**Beatrice**: "Welcome travelers, you come highly recommended by the Old Wolf. Here's the deal, this town's been the best fishing port in North Estor for the last hundred years, but now something changed. The water's gone dark, boats are turning up shattered on the rocks, and there are strange happenings. I want you to investigate and find out what's happening to my town."

"Now I won't stoop to spreading rumors... but some say there's music in the night, tempting people to the waves, and it might have something to do with that harlot Caeleana. Things were bad before she got here, but they haven't been much better with her around."

"She hangs out at the Briny Barrel, ya might try there. I'd also talk to Marlin, he knows these waters better than anyone. Find the source of our troubles and report back to me, then we can talk about solving the issue at the source."
