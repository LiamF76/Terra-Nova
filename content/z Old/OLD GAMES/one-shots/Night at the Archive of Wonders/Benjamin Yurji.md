Name: Benjamin Yurji, Curator of Artifacts at the Archive of Wonders

Voice: charismatic type, vibrant and loud, "Welcome friends! We are so lucky to have some more help. This institution is the heart of Terraven, history! It MUST be protected."

Description: Half-Elf male, middle aged with black hair that is going grey in a few spots. He wears simple brown pants and shoes, a white shirt and brown vest jacked with lots of pockets. On his back is a book almost 2ft tall.

Important Info: 
- Curator, the one who goes out and gets the items for the museum.
- Large book is a magic item called, "The Inventory", its a magic tome that can tell you if any registered museum item is in the museum and where.
- Cursed by the "Curator's Contract", a special curse applied to official curators, they are bound to obtain the items and give them to the Director, unable to keep them for themselves. 
- THE THIEF! Makes mimics with a potion of mimicry. He has the mimics eat magic items and transport them to his apartment.
