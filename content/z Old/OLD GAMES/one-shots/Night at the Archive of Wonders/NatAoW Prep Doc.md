
### Ideas/ Working

[[The Archive of Wonders]], the largest and most prestigious museum in Estor, has been infiltrated by mimics, that come alive at night and eat other displays and artifacts. The party is a rag tag group hired to be night security guards and stop the mimics. There are lots of them and they are dangerous! The party will be given some items to assist them but they will largely be on their own to find and exterminate the mimics. 
Many priceless artifacts have gone missing, and both the curator and director want this to stop ASAP!

They get a tour from the Director before their first night, where he points out a magical chest that was the most recent discovery, the Treasure of Aegir Galewind, a Storm Giant King of long ago. The curator brought it in about 7 days ago. Things have been going missing for 5 days. They are supposed to work with Bluster to find whatever or whoever is taking things from the museum.

## First Meeting

You hold the flyer in your hand, it reads in large bold lettering, written as if it was desperate to be read, "HELP WANTED! Overnight Security at the ARCHIVE OF WONDERS! 5000GP a night!"

The massive white marble steps leading up to the front entrance before you. You look at the large looming building as the last few patrons make their way out, at 5pm, the time you were given to arrive here. The bustling city of Terraven is alive around you, as the sun begins to set you watch the red banners of House Realmcrest fly from the towers and streetlights along the road.

Have them describe characters in clockwise order.

As you stand outside talking, almost hesitant to enter the massive building, a pair of figures emerges. A middle aged half-elf man in travelers clothes and a large book strapped to his back, and an elderly silver dragonborn in blue and purple robes.

The silver dragonborn meekly raises his walking stick and waves, as the half-elf makes his way down the steps towards you.

**Benjamin**:
- "Greetings friends! We are closing for the day but we open tomorrow at 8am, tours begin at 11, you can purchase tickets at the front desk! We just got unveiled a new exhibit from the hills of Obelask, a generous donation by Ivys Arthur, a fully intact Minotaur Skeleton!"
- "Oh apologies, you're here about the security job? Of course... Well I'm happy to introduce you to the director and please, I'm available for anything you need. We are happy to have someone here."
- "It's been difficult to get any assistance, the royal army is all dispatching west to Castle Ikaris, and the Hyperion Knights did pledge one member, but he was a squire, and sadly he was lost to whoever is stealing the items."

**Myrninth**:
- "Well uhm, yes I suppose it's good you're here. We have had a total of 7 items be stolen... a tragedy. There items are highly magical... highly effective... highly dangerous."
- "You are welcome here I suppose, please try not to get your fingerprints on anything. Our cleaning staff is the best in the city, don't want to create more work for them." As he says that he points to the side and a floating wooden staff is seen pushing a broom across the steps, sweeping off dust.
- "I have a list inside, I left a copy with Mr. Wobblenob and one in my office, come, I'll introduce you and we can get you started tonight."

The two lead you inside, Myrninth visibly annoyed at your arrival, while Benjamin smiles ear to ear. As you enter a wall of sparkling water ceases in front of you, allowing you to pass, and resuming after you enter.

At the left desk you see a halfling asleep with a hat pulled over his eyes and his feet up on his desk, snoring loudly. [[Bluster Wobblenob]]

Myrninth points at Bluster, indicating to the Cleaning Staff, which swoops over and smacks him on the nose with a decent amount of force. Bluster jumps upwards with a "YOOOOWWWWW" pulling his club off his back and his eyes hurriedly darting around over his thick mustache. "WHERE ARE YA? HUH YOU THEIVIN BASTARD, ILL BREAK YER LEGS!"

**Benjamin**. "Calm yourself Bluster, and please try to stay awake. We brought you help with the last of our budget. The lease you could do is be awake at work. Can you give them a tour and outline security, and what's been going on? It's already 5:15 and I really need to get home to feed Winston." He turns and looks at you, "Winston is my owl."

**Bluster**. "Whatever you say Benji, just make sure to get to work on time tomorrow. I may sleep here but you've been late for a week straight."

Benji laughs and walks out with Myrninth, waving goodbye to you as they go.

**Myrninth**. "Oh and please, be careful, the displays, I take very very special care with them... nobody in the world loves this museum more than me. Nobody. I cherish these items like my own children..."

Benji looks down at Myrninth a bit worriedly, then smiles awkwardly and shrugs his shoulders. Waving goodbye as he leaves for the night.


## The Tour

Bluster stands up at his staggering height of 3'2, fixes up his belt and puts his club on his back. He twiddles his mustache while pushing things around on his desk, pulling a small glowing orb out from the papers and writing tools. He holds it out to you to look at, it is a clear white ball with a small red dot in the center.

**Bluster**. "This here is the anti-magic wall of force control orb for the whole museum. If ya shake it, and want it to turn on the field it does, the dot turns blue when its on." He takes the orb and shakes it, as he does the wall of water behind you vibrated for a moment and stops. The dot turns blue and a faint field can be felt around the entire building. "The field is on, magic can't come in and can't go out, and the entire campus is protected from physical entry, in theory people can still teleport in but they shouldn't be able to teleport out. I keep this as the head of overnight security. Follow me and I'll give ya a tour."

[[The Archive of Wonders]]

**Bluster**. "There's basically 3 main exhibit rooms, the Skeleton display they just put up der, the hall of ancient peoples, and the hall of warriors. The skeleton and ancient peoples are on the first floor, just off the main entryway here, and the hall of warriors is up the stairs in the hall of ancient peoples."

He walks you through each room pointing out artifacts of interest, specifically the most valuable.
By room:
- Skeleton Room:
	- The Giant Minotaur Skeleton
	- The Dragon Scale display
- Hall of Ancient Peoples:
	- The Plates from the feast after Lazarus defeated Arkonil
- Hall of Warriors:
	- The necklace of Aralin (fine white metal necklace said to have jewels made of pure starlight)
	- The Blade of Thaldrax (massive ruby/purple/black blade said to have been wielded by the legendary evil, Thaldrax the Hand of Night)

After Bluster describes the artifacts he says they are welcome to hang out in the cafeteria or the Curator's office, but NOT the director's office as he keeps it private.

The night begins, they can patrol, making investigation checks, hang out making perception checks, or study the artifacts potentially gaining information or remembering things.


### First fight!
As they patrol everything seems normal, until you hear a faint clatter in the Hall of Heroes.
As you rush up the stairs you hear another clatter, and as you top the staircase you see a single katana from one of the displays sitting along on the floor in the center of the room. As you go to pick it up... it flies upwards towards your face!
Roll initiative!

After the fight they notice that a magic staff of medium value is missing from a display.
They fight the shield and sword mimics. Bluster comes and talks to them, says they can take a rest in the office if they need a break.

In Benji's office there are papers detailing the curse he has to work as the curator. If they go in Myrninth's office they will find a list of items on some note paper, Thaldrax's Sword, elven necklace... Minotaur statue. (This list is planted by Benji)

The Minotaur awakens, second fight! Roll initiative!

After the minotaur fight they see a portal inside the ribcage, and going through it takes them to Benji's apartment. Benji is a wizard who has been stealing the artifacts because he believes he deserves them more than the museum, he's the one who got them after all. 

They fight him in his apartment, then when they win they either find a note in his chest or he tells them (if he lives) that the last mimic is still inside the museum... its A GIANT MIMIC!
