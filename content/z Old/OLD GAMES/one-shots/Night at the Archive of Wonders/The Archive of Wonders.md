

## FIRST FLOOR

### The Entryway
- Beautiful bright stone makes up the room, the floor is cool tile and the walls are covered in posters about new exhibits dating back many years. At a desk on the right is a halfling man with a security hat asleep with his feet up on the desk. [[Bluster Wobblenob]]
- **North Door**. Leads outside to the street and Terraven
- **South Door**. This arched doorway beneath the stairs leads to the [[#The Central Hallway]]
- **West Door**. A small room between the Entry and [[#The Skelton Display]]
- **East Door**. A small room between the Entry and [[#The Hall of Ancient Peoples]]


### The Skelton Display
- A large chamber with vaulted glass windows for a ceiling, in the center is a 5ft tall wooden platform holding up a gigantic minotaur skeleton display. The plaque on the front of the display reads, "Donated by the White Wizard Ivys Arthur and the Young Wolves after their battle against the rogue Warlord Tyran Obliteras." There are a few taxidermized Scavenger Dogs from Drok Shah and assorted bear and dragon wyrmling skulls along the walls of the room.
- **Stairs**. The twisting wooden stairs lead up to the [[#The Creatures of Lost Ages]]
- **Southeast Door**. The small door leads to the [[#The Central Hallway]]


### The Hall of Ancient Peoples
- This quiet room houses many relics of ancient civilizations. There are plates and pieces of architecture from the Praetorians, all the way back to some Aelarian pottery and clothing. The statues depict a praetorian woman holding her child, a half-elf carrying a bow and arrows, and the largest statue is the likeness of the legendary hero Lazarus.
- **North Door**. These doors lead back to [[#The Entryway]]
- **Southeast Door**. This door leads to the [[#The Record of Modern Royalty]]
- **Southwest Door**. This door leads to the [[#The Central Hallway]]
- **Stairs**. These stone stairs lead up to [[#The Hall of Warriors]]
