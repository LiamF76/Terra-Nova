#### Post-training

As train, the clockwork automatons suddenly freeze, assuming their default positions motionlessly. The training area loses its hum as the systems deactivate and in from a doorway steps a well dress warforged, wearing his bowtie and monocle. You all know this is **Pendulum**, the Warforged caretaker of the Eon Spire. He bows at the hip as he greets you all, "A wonderful showing sirs, and especially from you Master Metamorph."
"Might I suggest you all take a visit, once you have washed, to the command center? I believe Grimphor has found a temporal anomaly he wants investigated."


## The Anamolies

They go to this futuristic command center with a giant glass floor to ceiling window and massive paper thin crystal screens where a White Dragonborn, **Grimphor**, works using magic spells to alter numbers and calculations. Grimphor is the architect of the tower alongside Kronos himself, using his adept arcane abilities to construct an almost perfectly invisible and functional bastion of temporal defense.

He points out an anomaly, small but very slowly growing. He models a ramp, a quadratic equation for overall misalignment if it is left alone. He says here let me pinpoint it, and pulls it um in a weird wavy map of the surface of Escarth, where it appears to be directly on top of a village, he searches the records and finds it is called "Sunset" a small young settlement subservient to a red dragon. As he models it you see another anomaly dot, which appears to be right near the Eon Spire.
When you point it out he says, "Strange, shouldn't be possible for them to naturally approach the spi-"
He gets cut off by a massive shudder as the entire tower shakes for a moment. Alarm bells start blaring, red lights flashing, "BREACH BREACH BREACH, TEMPORAL GATEWAY HAS BEEN COMPROMIZED."
Grimphor runs to look out the glass and sees a strange blue purple and white tinted shadow, then another, then another, and some strange black and white winged kobolds begin to emerge from your Temporal Gateway outside the tower, "Get out there and shut it down, its been overridden, I'll do what I can here to slow them down. Be quick, the longer it takes to turn it off the more likely we lose the shields, and then we are completely exposed!"


## The Battle for the Gateway

At one point during the battle you feel the bridge begin to shudder and shake as the strange watery shield that encompasses the area around the tower flickers for just a moment.

The commander pulls a small white flute from his pocket and plays a short tune, as the spectral form of a dragon rises in smoke from the instrument. It puffs out a cloud if strange steam as it speaks in a disembodied voice, "Behold... Tzor'Vael..."

## Search for the Stopwatch

After the battle they return to the command center, Pendulum passes out orange slices, "Fresh from the land of Sauria, pre human colonization. An excellent food for recovery after battle sirs."

Grimphor ponders over his crystal screens concernedly. "The shields went down for just a moment. Long enough for a problem. We detected a breach in the vault, and the Ruby Stopwatch is missing. There isn't any time to waste, literally, that thing can transport a decently large area outside of the timestream, serious damage, and look, that anomaly from earlier has grown more that 50 times in size. They have to be related."
"I'll activate the gateway, get down there and find out what is happening, and fast. Remember, don't interfere with world events, just find out where the break is, and follow the trail."

## Battle for the Ruby Stopwatch

##### Battle Mechanics:
- *Orbs*: Tzor'Vael and the apprentices are inside orbs of connected frozen time. Each orb has an AC of 18 and 20HP, breaking it releases the mage, and they must break all three to open Tzor'Vael's, Tzor is invulnerable to anything while inside her orb.
- *Time Inversion*. When Tzor is freed she will use her first turn to activate time inversion, which will reverse initiative order completely.
