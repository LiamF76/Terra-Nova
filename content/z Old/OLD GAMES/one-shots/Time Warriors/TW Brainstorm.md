Eon Spire base is attacked by a time dragon known as Tzor'Vael, an ancient dragon trained by Kairos in using Temporal Magics.

Dragon sends her minions to attack the Eon Spire as a cover while she steals the Ruby Stopwatch, one of the legendary artifacts of the Knights of Continuum's Hold.

She steals it and escapes, fleeing into the timestream around the Eon Spire, and the party must chase her. 
First, they have to return to the control room and be informed by their warforged helper Pendulum, that there was an alert in the vault and something appears to be missing, go check and find ruby stopwatch has been stolen while they were defending the tower.
Second, they have to find where she want, go and consult with the Chronal Eye, a magic artifact that can see where breaks in time occur. Couple investigation checks to determine where and when, they why. Where: Sunset village, When: around the middle of the second age, Why: She appears to have invaded and created the break inside another dragon's horde near the town.

They end up in a small town called Sunset, while it is still ruled by the Red Dragon Landrogal, Tzor'Vael has infected him with a necrotic disease in order to have him die, altering time and wanting to take his horde for herself.

They investigate the town and learn something has happened to anger the dragon lord Landrogal, and they ask the party to talk to him and possibly fix it.
Landrogal is angry but talks to them, and informs them of his problem. Tzor'Vael has stolen his horde and created a temporal rift in his lair. The horde has been transported to her Twilight Caverns on the moon Thalun. The party has to follow the distortion from Landrogal's cave to hers.
