The Infinite Armory stretches out before you like a vault without end—rows upon rows of shelves, racks, and glass cases filled with every conceivable magic item known to existence. The ceiling soars beyond sight, lost in a swirling expanse of glowing glyphs and shifting constellations of arcane runes.
The floor beneath your feet is smooth, dark marble, faintly reflective, capturing glimmers of light that seem to ripple across the shelves. ### The Green Gateway

You run through the maze, winding pathways and halls before you, almost magically identical, you simply move, as fast as you can, trying to find something unique, something that differentiates one path from another to give you a reason to go done one path but there is nothing.
As you run you reach finally a wall, but its a dead end. Nothing before you but a wall of thick tangled and thorned vines. Behind the vines you can see the faint outline of a door with glowing red writing above it. There is a small stone table to your right where you find a small watering bucket with water, a pot of soil, and a scroll of daylight. The vines are withered and weak, the bloody thrones and leaves are devoid of moisture and life. 
**PUZZLE**: They have to use the items in the right order, water, soil, sunlight. If they use the items in the wrong order they reappear on the table refilled as if they were never used. Touching the vines causes you to lose 1d4 HP and you are disoriented, halving your movement speed for 1 minute.
**SOLVED**: As the rays of sunlight touch the hungry plants, the grow and blossom, blooming into thousands of roses as the vines retreat upwards, opening the way to the door.
