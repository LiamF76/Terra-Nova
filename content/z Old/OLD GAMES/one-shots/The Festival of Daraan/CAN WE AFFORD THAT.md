
This is the price is right but with GP and magic items. The game is set up where each player takes a turn but they are a team, while the play against an enemy, Karegor. 

THE PRIZES:
- [Sword of Zariel](https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/wondrous-items:sword-of-zariel): 2,250,000 GP (2mill 250 thousand)
- Finality (Reister's Scythe): 508,000 GP (508 thousand)
- [Sozin](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1XHHPlH4l_03d_P3wqpELlzfnGU2pxhFpZaQApSVlkUo/edit?usp=sharing) (the little guy): 50,500 GP (50 thousand 5 hundred)

The game goes like this, in clockwise order ending with Karegor, the party must guess the value of the item. 

At the end of the round the farthest guess is eliminated, and people have a chance to edit their bid going in the same order of turns, they are also told if the guess is higher or lower than the one of the eliminated person. The catch is that if they wish to edit their guess, anyone who does so has to fight a monster.

MONSTERS:
- [[clay golem.png]] Loot: 2 shards

The player with the closest guess who doesn't go over the price wins the item and can use it for the rest of the game.

"OK folks, lets run down the rules for our lovely contestants here! This is, CAN WE AFFORD IT! Today we are playing for 3 very special items!" 
She sets to the side as a curtain pulls back revealing a pedestal with a crystal ball, and 3 more off to the side bearing a glowing golden sword, a large alien looking scythe, and a little fire baby in a bird cage.
"For those of you who don't know how this works, each of these items has a set price based on their cost in the legendary FIREFLY's EMPORIUM! The players have to guess the price of these items, and the closest one wins it! But, if you guess over you are out! And if you guess the worse you get a PENALTYYYYYYYY!"
She walks across the stage and stands behind the crystal ball, waving her hands around it as it projects a screen.
"The game consists of two rounds, the first round is the first guesses you make against the value of an item. Each player takes a turn guessing, and then the guesses are displayed above their head. The player with the lowest guess is ELIMINATED from that round, and they take some DAMGAGEEEEEEEE! In addition I will let the players know if the value is lower or higher than the guess of the eliminated player."
She smiles evilly as she says damage.
"Then the second round begins, here's where things get tricky, you can make a second guess. However! What do we say to the players who wanna make a new guess?"
