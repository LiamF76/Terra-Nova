
Attending a wedding between Prince Thorgall and Lady Pondessa. King Slog is present to celebrate his son's wedding with his friends and subjects in their castle, Fort Alabaster. The reception takes place in the grand hall of the Castle, a room with high wooden ceilings and the floor is water, traversed by leaping between lily pads.

They are partying, traditional frog games of Hop Hippity, and drinking an alcoholic beverage called Pond-Water. The honored warrior Glorp is present, a legend in the swamps and bogs of the Eldermourne forests. 

They party for a little while before the waters suddenly darken and grow menacing, the Prince shouts out as he is attacked by a cultist. There are 5 in total, they toss magical spells as all the non Bullywug people become transmuted into hopping croaking frogs!

Battle commences!

After battle a single black lily pad rises, and King Slog explains that it leads down to the dark temple ruins below, where the cult must be summoning their lord Zothog'Gol the Shadow-Hopper. Prince Thorgall was stabbed with a cursed blade and the cult must be defeated to heal him.
