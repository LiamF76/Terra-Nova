
A baby Pegasus has escaped from its stable and the local lord has hired the party to find it. 


Blue Butterfly, teashop (Sydni)

#### Stages:
- **Intro**: Meet the lord of the village (Pedro Pascal, Pedrovan Pelkian), he hires the party, promises them a reward for finding his foal
- **Search for clues**: Investigate the stables, ask the Stablemaster questions, find a trail of feathers or another clue to follow the foal
- **Enter the Mystic Woods**: The party enters the woods to follow the trail and has three encounters
	- **The Ranger**: A ranger who works in the forest as a hunter of dangerous beasts, "That foal trotted right past me, into the Fey Glade! I hunt wolves, I'm not going anywhere near that place its magic!"
	- **The Owl**: A Fey owl as they near the Glade, talks to the party as the trail disappears, says they saw the foal but will only show them the way to the glade if they bring the owl a Silver Acorn from the Silver Oak. A tree protected by a forest guardian
	- **The Forest Guardian**: A half centaur half bear creature named Ursalon, Guardian of the Forest, secretly guards the tree, approaches from behind as the party attempts to steal the Silver Acorn. RP interaction, wants them to not steal the acorn, but leave something valuable or respectful as an offering to him.
