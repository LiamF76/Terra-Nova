Description: light skinned half-elf woman, 5'4, has a gemstone shaped like a water droplet in her forehead. Her hair is braided and lays long down her back. She wears a white dress with a heavy black fur cloak over her shoulders, her body is covered in tattoos of golden ink and her left arm is a gauntlet made of pure purple and blue ice.

Important Info: Apprentice of [[Zyrathia]], Evocation mage specializing in Void Abyssal and Frost Evocation. She has served as Zyrathia's apprentice since she was released from the Nightfort. She was arrested for suggesting a class be taught about defending against abyssal incursion and avoiding contact with demonic artifacts.
- In the Nightfort she was branded an Apostate, given the name Shadowfrost as a branding punishment.
- She suffered in the prison, which doesn't function with cells and guards, it is basically an inescapable free for all with criminals and monsters. Most people who get sent there either die or go mad.
- She made a case to the Aitor Guild, but they denied her claims and threatened her with further incarceration, so she fled the country, and was enlisted by Zyrathia while living in Dragonswatch.
- She hates the Hyperion Knights and believes they are the evil not Arkonia.
