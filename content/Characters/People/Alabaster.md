Name: Alabaster Ironhawk

Voice: human knight, chivalrous and kind, 'Well met sir, and you my lady. May fortune favor you in the days to come.'

Description: 6 foot human. Knight, decent physique but a little skinny from not training for a while, wears steel plate armor with an iconic winged helmet. Longsword with a cross guard shaped like wings. 

Important Info: Former follower of [[Aton]], raised in the Serpent's Sons cult, broke free from the cult and dedicated his life to hunting [[Balthazaar]]'s followers. Was driven mad by his forced servitude to Aton's [Champion](obsidian://open?vault=Terra-Nova&file=unsorted%2FDivine%20Champions) [[Kairos]]. Betrayed the Young Wolves in the Battle against [[Characters/People/Tyran]] at The Monolith, former Lord of Obelask

POST MADNESS: Alabaster was healed slowly being around the Oakenheart and thanks to Ryfon, he healed as much as he could but  his wounds were not just magical but also mental. To aid that he went on a pilgrimage to Tena’s peak, and has dedicated himself to Tena. [[Clovis]] sensed a great darkness in the Temporal Plane, and asked Alabaster to go with him to help the Young Wolves

He is much weaker than he was, but he is willing to fight with them if they will have him.
