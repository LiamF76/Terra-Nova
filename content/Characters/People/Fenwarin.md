Name: Fenwarin Dusklight

Voice: elvish but crazy, think WHERE IS OMNIMAN! guy, "Well met Timekeeper. You reek of lies and half truths, the influence of your forebear I am sure."

Description: A large elf like man with slightly dark purple skin, his hair is thick and woven greens and purples almost like moss. He has long pointed ears but they point more sideways than upwards (he looks like a Firbolg). He wears steel plated armor with patches of moss and mushroom caps along it and his hammer is gigantic solid silver with a small crackling bolt of energy held in a cavity in its center. On his back are a small white wood shortbow and a large ornate lance.

Important Info: Codeword = "To where is the dragon's last flight? Wherever the wind takes him."
