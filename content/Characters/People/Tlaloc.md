Name: Lord Tlaloc of Redsong, the Red Warlock

Voice: Saruman, high society and ingrained upper class person, "We push forward not because we can, but because we must. The limits of magic must not be respected, but tested, and broken."

Description: 5'11 dark skinned half elf, black hair lined with red silk bindings and red ruby rings, he wears blue and red robes and has a tattoo of a burning eye on the back of his right hand. He is kind and interested in what mages and great warriors have to say, especially Reister, as he is interested in the Church of Kronos. 

Important Info: Lord of Redsong, member of the Redsong Wizards, distant cousin of Igan, member of the Order of the Arcane Eye, close friend of Shin Tzu and [[Annabelle]] Realmcrest. Tlaloc is loyal to the crown, believing that although Adrithyr is a bad person, he is the rightful king and the laws must be followed or else chaos will take hold of the world.
