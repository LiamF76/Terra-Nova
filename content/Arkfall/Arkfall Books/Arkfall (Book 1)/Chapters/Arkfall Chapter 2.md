# Arkfall Chapter 2

`Author Notes: Lowell August and Priest escape the archive and end up outside the wreck, a Warlord Vector arrives and so do Consortium pirates. Lowell recognizes the Warlord's horned helmet, one of the Fallen Star Coalition leaders who betrayed the cause. They fight and Lowell steals the Vector's gun when it gets dropped. The Priest is killed in the crossfire and August grabs the data stick.`

The ringing is awful, and I can feel my eyes rolling lazily, everything looks like I have dirty glasses on, can't focus. I can feel pressure on my chest, heavy, something metal. August?

The screaming around me is hard to hear, something about shocking.

Another hand hits my chest, and a massive bubble, black as night, fills my vision. The hand twists and lightning fires through my body. I feel myself lurch upwards as my senses return with violent speed, my hearing fills with the sound of metal against metal and August shouting my name. An open hand flies across my face, bringing me back to reality.

"There he is, hey boss. Listen, you're fine, I taped up your arm, but we have to go. Pirates are here, and judging by the sounds outside, they're throwing Lattice with extreme prejudice."

I look back at forth between the Priest and August as the shipwreck lurches again, some explosion below. "Did this bastard shock me with magic?"
