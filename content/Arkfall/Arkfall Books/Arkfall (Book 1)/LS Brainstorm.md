## General

- the core of the story: A man who believe power inherently corrupts is about to become very powerful against his will. His world view is that Vectors cannot be trusted, there is no saving the world, and never trust someone with a cause. How does he cope with becoming a Vector that he despises? What does he do with his power?
- First Arc: Lowell is hired for a job to infiltrate a lab. When his crew gets there something is clearly off, someone dies, there is high security and evidence of Lattice experimentation. They get into danger and someone is in trouble, Lowell bonds with a sprite they find (very concerning the job brought them somewhere with a Sprite), to save the person in danger

## Characters

- [[Lowell]]: human protag. Mercenary, former soldier who does jobs to get by in New Mumbai. Lowell character traits:
	- doesn't like killing but keeps that to himself
	- was studying mathematics as a soldier but the college was destroyed by an alien attack, wanted to be a teacher (skill outside combat that shows he was hardened, not always the way he is)
	- false job comes first, can't help but assist those in need when it comes down to it
	- became a mercenary after working as a soldier for the Fallen Star Coalition. Used to believe in retaking the stars and saving humanity, but after Vector Warlords broke the coalition into violent tyrants, he abandoned justice and accepted being a gun for hire
- [[August]]: Module ally, mercenary, works on jobs with Lowell. August is optimistic and hopeful where Lowell is cynical and downtrodden.
	- August was activated for war, was part of the Fallen Star Coalition with Lowell, Lowell helped August escape and they have been inseparable since.
