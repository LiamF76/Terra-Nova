This document is where I will outline Lowell, who he is, how he acts, and how he will change.

## Building the Core

**Enneagram Type:** Type 8, The Challenger. 

**Core Fear:** Lowell is afraid he is correct about the world. Humanity's Golden Age has ended, and now there is a desperate struggle to survive that actually only delays the inevitable.

**Core Motivation:** Never get caught in a cause he isn't strong enough to do himself. Relying on other powerful individuals will end the same as the FSC did. It ends in ruin, so be strong enough to do the saving yourself and there's no need to worry. Stay self-reliant, engage battles on his own terms.

**Core Values:** Fairness. Lowell believes in helping others, but he has a twisted sense of justice. He does his jobs right, even when alone, it's his code. Eye for an eye. He would never call himself principled, but he stands on his beliefs. Hurt an innocent, and you are no longer innocent. The world is ending and there isn't time for growth and forgiveness.

**Lie:** "Trying to save others will only get you killed. Take care of yourself and your own, and leave the rest to their own devices." He lies to himself that he doesn't care and that it isn't worth it, but when push comes to shove he tries to help others.

**Ghost:** Lowell's ghost is the fall of the Fallen Star Coalition (FSC). People he though were magically empowered heroes turned out to be greedy Warlords and betrayed the cause. It confirmed his lie, that you can only protect yourself, and that trusting those in power / those with a cause leads you to ruin. Lowell was a commander, and he told his troops it would be ok, it wasn't. He doesn't say it will be ok anymore, even when he does help.
