- Lowell, human protag. Mercenary, former soldier who does jobs to get by in New Mumbai. Lowell character traits:
	- doesn't like killing but keeps that to himself
	- was studying mathematics as a soldier but the college was destroyed by an alien attack, wanted to be a teacher (skill outside combat that shows he was hardened, not always the way he is)
	- false job comes first, can't help but assist those in need when it comes down to it
	- became a mercenary after working as a soldier for the Fallen Star Coalition. Used to believe in retaking the stars and saving humanity, but after Vector Warlords broke the coalition into violent tyrants, he abandoned justice and accepted being a gun for hire
- the core of the story: A man who believe power inherently corrupts is about to become very powerful against his will. His world view is that Vectors cannot be trusted, there is no saving the world, and never trust someone with a cause. Now he has the power, does that change him?
