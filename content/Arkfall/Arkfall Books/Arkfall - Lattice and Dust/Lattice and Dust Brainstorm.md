# Brainstorm

- space western
- set on mars
- MC: old Vector cowboy / lawman
- abandoned town, people show up looking for somewhere safe, need someone to protect them but MC is downtrodden and reluctant
- people are fleeing swarm meteor strikes, and another one is coming, but now for the MC's town
- MC once protected the town, but a power hungry Vector destroyed the town when they went mad from Lattice withdrawal, still holds guilt over not being able to save them
- story ends revealing the MC was the Vector who destroyed the town
- Titles:
	- The Last Gunslinger in Marineris
	- Dust over Marineris
	- Red Frontier
	- **Lattice & Dust**
- Line idea for when Lincoln's backstory is revealed: "When the rampage had ended, all that remained of the town was Lattice and dust."

# Characters

- Lincoln Grae: AKA 'The Grey', the main character. Former sheriff / lawman. Old Vector who harbors guilt he cannot overcome. The hero, fights through his guilt, recognizes his evils, and chooses to save those he can still save. Deep down he wants to protect people, but his guilt makes him fearful to accept that responsibility so he stays isolated.
- Buck: Lincoln's Sprite, Lattice spirit formed and with a nature tied to justice. He and Lincoln are close, but even they have drifted apart the last few years, and he is the only thing holding his mind together on fleeting reserves of Lattice.
