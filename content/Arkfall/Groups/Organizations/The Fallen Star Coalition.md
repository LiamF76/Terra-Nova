## The Fallen Star Coalition

#### "Astra intra. (The stars within.)" - Alex Menodora

The Fallen Star Coalition, also known as the FSC, is the governing council of Earth's largest city, [[New Mumbai]]. The Coalition are made up of [[Vector|Vectors]] who lead the city and its defenders against the threats of Warlords, Pirates, and Aliens. The FSC is recognized by the people of New Mumbai as their leaders and protectors. Generally defer to their authority as the superhuman individuals with the power to fight against the advanced threats of the post fermi universe.

The FSC's main duty is to protect New Mumbai from external threats, sending Vectors to protect resources such as communication relays, reactors, outposts, and Golden Age technology. Protecting Mumbai is often done by preventing its enemies from advancing their military strength, meaning much of the Coalition's work happens outside the walls of the city. The FSC believes it is the duty of those with the power to protect others that have none. This applies heavily to Vectors, who they recruit , believing the power of [[Quantum Lattice]] to be the ultimate tool for protecting humanity and Terra.

## History of the Coalation

The Fallen Star Coalition was formed by a group of Vectors led by [[Kaz Axis]], who held great power on Terra during the Dark Age. With the power of the first Vectors in the Coalition, they fought through hordes of Aliens and Warlords, carving a safe haven into the ruins of Earth, and building New Mumbi as a bastion for Humanity. As New Mumbai grew, so did Kaz's ambition. This ambition led to infighting, a break in virtues. Vectors who followed Kaz believed it was their right to rule and do as they chose, others believed it was the duty of Vectors to protect others.

The infighting came to a head when Kaz took it too far, shooting and killing a non Vector member of the Coalition who opposed him.
