| Race           | Pillar      | Quote                                                                                              |

| The Consortium | Capitalism  | *“They already own your world, you just haven’t missed a payment yet.”*                            |
| The Shards     | Wasted Time | *"You never really win a fight with a Shard, because they only lost fights they planned to lose."* |

| The Legion     | Militarism  | *"They have endless soldiers, and twice as many guns."*                                            |
