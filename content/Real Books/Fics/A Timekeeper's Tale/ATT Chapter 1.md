
His legs were shot. There was nowhere to hide, she was going to catch him. He stumbled backwards as his foot caught on something. As he fell he heard footsteps from the kitchen, he would be in her grasp within moments if he didn't do something.

Magic? It was that or be caught, so with a hesitant flick of his wrist Kairos felt his skin become translucent. Invisibility, a very useful spell when one needed to hide. Against someone this perceptive however, it was worthless.

The wind was ripped from his chest as a humanoid figure tackled him, throwing him onto his back on the hardwood floor. He gasped as she attacked, surprised not only that she had seen him, but that she had leapt over the couch to attack, concealing her footsteps by using the soft sofa.

Kairos exclaimed as he fell, feigning some great and deadly wound, and when he was inspected by the young girl, he laughed. "You have me darling! The evil wizard is slain!"

She was overcome with giggling, pretending to blast him repeatedly with spells from her pretend wand as Kairos mimed agony at their casting. He wrapped the girl in his arms, lifting her up as he stood and placing the both of them on the couch. "You are quite perceptive Lisa! I thought invisibility would win me the game!"

Lisa reached up to his face and pulled his glasses off his face, fiddling with them as she grumbled, "Magic is cheating." The poor thing was upset.

Kairos gave her a few gentle pats on the head before admitting, "It was. I cheated, and you won anyway. Let that be a lesson, people will cheat in this world, you can still succeed despite them." Hopefully wise words, he wanted to impart better morals onto her than he had taught to him. How can you know if you are doing right by your child? It's impossible. Just do your best, and they will be as good as you are to them.

He held out his hand, indicating it was time to return his lenses, which Lisa obliged. "Thank you very much Lisa. I love you, you know that right?" He tapper her on the nose, eliciting a giggle from his adorable daughter.

She nodded in response, but then she stared up at him with a painfully sad look on her face. "Then why did you leave Daddy? You left me alone."

What was she talking about? "I am right here my child. I am here with you, always, I would never abandon you."

Kairos reached out to hug her tighter, but there was nothing there. She was gone. Kairos tumbled to the ground as the whole house started to tilt, and he rolled along the floorboards. He snatched and grabbed at anything he could to hold on to, but found no purchase. He fell through the front window, glass shattering and tearing at the skin on his hands and arms as he screamed. He had fallen into an empty shadowed void. "LISA! LISA I AM HERE!"

Kairos was desperate, he shouted and pleaded to her, but he wasn't there, and he had left her.

He awoke screaming like a knife had been plunged through his heart. 'Add it to the pile' came as a fleeting thought before he stayed his shaking hands, picked up his journal, and chronicled his 87th nightmare. 

Ten thousand curses upon you Shao. On you and all your children, your creations, and your world.

He knew Shao was watching, he was always watching. What was the Elder God thinking? Kairos chuckled, thinking about how many times old Daraan must have been cursed in this manner by men who had far less reason than Kairos to hate him. 'Add it to the pile'.
