
The Queen summons her agent. The Cabal dispatch the censors, who scour the many dancing halls of Museen. Through the thick vapors and addicts puffing exotic dust, they find him. The Queen's greatest Dancer.

His skin is black like obsidian, a devil child whose mother tried to drown him for his coloration, for he was of hell, and she could not abide it. Now the baby's violent thrashing is called dancing, and he is quite renowned. His body is perfectly thin and toned, his dancing is radiant.

He dances through the halls of Museen, the Queen's city, until she calls upon him. His skin coated with glimmering golden tattoos, magic seals to bind him to her will, and imbue him with her power. His dance is her grace, and her grace is death.

The city of Museen lies hidden within the deep and tall walls of the darkness. The entire society tucked within the continent consuming maze of Iramo. The maze is all the land, and all the desire, for it holds all magic the Queen desires. She carries power over all her slaves will, but more is her right, and her Dancer will bring it to her.

In the Dancer's own blood they write upon a slave's skin. A girl of age 12, still pure, untouched by men and women in the way of love. She is a holy sacrifice, and her death will give the Dancer power. They cut into her skin and write the name, the name of the one that must be danced upon, the one that the Queen wishes to die.

**Agmahg**, the Goliath, the Titan, a slave to the Queen's enemy, the Master.

The Dancer takes lives for the Queen, for he is hers, and her will is his. His horn tips glow a faint blue and green, as the girl dies. Soo too does the Queen's center eye glow, embedded in her neck, her face veiled in black, for those who look into her empty sockets become hers and die, and she does not with her Dancer dead. She loves her Dancer.

His hair is thick and curled into the tightest locks, like tendrils of night itself, they are black as he is, and all of his beauty. He wears nothing but his skirt, flowing black silks, for that is what the Queen gives to him. No slave of the Queen has anything that is not hers given, and treasured. For all things belong to the Queen, be they things or lives, all is hers in Iramo.

If it is not hers it is the Masters, and he will die. If it is not the Masters it belongs to another ruler, and they too shall die, but first the Master, for the Queen hates him most.

But before all things must come the dance, and the Dancer departs with a kiss on his feet, the Queen's blessing, two painful jabs as nails are driven into the tops of his feet. If her should fall they will be driven up, the points sticking out from his soles. It is a blessing to be given the Queen's pain. The Dancer must dance from his toes, and stay poised, tall as he can be before Agmahg, for the Titan is tall, and the Dancer must stand tall as well.

The Dancer cries tears of love as they break him, and the Cabal grant him a blessing. A blade forged from the bones of his mother, a perfectly white knife, drained of marrow and sharpened with hate. It will be his instrument.

"Go and dance for me." The Queen decrees, and the entire city of Museen erupts with manic revelry, they cannot resist, for it is the Queen's will. They dance until they drop, and they will drop until they die.

---

Agmahg is angry. He swings his hammer in fury as he tries to dance, he fails, for his movements are sickly and dry, he does not glorify the Queen. He is disgusting, a warlord who brazenly breaks down the walls of Iramo: Seeking to fight through the challenge of the maze rather than meet it. He has never been blessed to know beauty, and now that it is before his eyes, his unholy spirit rejects it.

The Dancer shows him what love is. Falling from the rafters of the fortress, ten cuts along his arms before he knows what is happening. Twin blades, a scimitar gilded in gold in black, matching its wielder, and the Queen's gift, the bone knife.

In parallel they spin, the Dancer moving deftly on his toes. He pirouettes and chasses his way between Agmahg's guards as the Titan recoils in pain. The Dancer trips one, tossing another on top of him. The scimitar cleaves down, severing two heads from two necks in one blow. He licks the blood from the blade, drinking in their pain. 

"Glory to the Queen, Agmahg, you Dance terribly, you disgust me. Now in the end I grant you beauty. Be beautiful, and die for her."

His voice is like honey and oats, sweet, hearty, deep, and loving. What he says is not loving, it is benevolent. What a chance Agmahg has been given, to die for the Queen is the honor of heaven, and he rejects it wholeheartedly.

The Dancer answers with greater love than can be comprehended by the faithless. Agmahg swings his warhammer down, shattering the brutalist stone table where he maps the maze for the Master. He has no respect even for his lord.

A pivot and leap land the Dancer on the Titan's massive shoulder, easily eight feet from the ground. Agmahg screams in agony as the nails of the Dancer's feet pierce his flesh, they are coated in the strongest of incense, the Queen's blessings bring fortune!

How powerful was her eye, her foresight, that she knew to drive the nails through his flesh, to kill the Titan? Truly her love is boundless. As the nails pry open his skin, the Dancer leans down, bending at the hip with expert flexibility. He licks the inner ear of the Titan gently, drinking in his scent.

He whispers a blessing into the ear of the Titan, and slits his throat with the dagger. 

*Thank you mother, thank you for dying, thank you my Queen.*

In proper fashion, he returns to his Queen with the body, her strength helping him to carry it through the long winding and maddening halls of the maze.

He sets it before her, and the Cabal begin the ritual. The power of his soul must feed the Queen.

They dig out each organ, piercing it with the bone dagger, draining him of his blood, and carefully removing his veins. The dancer stands in the center, the cavity of the body. He balances on one foot, agonizing on pointed toes, his arms raised above his head gracefully, his other leg pointed and held behind him.

He must stay this way until Agmahg is empty of all things but the Dancer, for then his soul can be devoured.

---

The Dancer returns to the halls of Museen, the city of scum and revelry. The Queen is finished, he has no more love to give. He could not feel the broken bones, cuts, or poisons that he had suffered during the assassination. The Queen's love protected him. 

The Queen's priests will heal the Dancer, for he must be strong to love again. The next name will not lie within Iramo. The Dancer boards a ship and embarks across the great seas. The next death must come, and the Dancer will give the Queen's grace to any she wishes.

But as the Dancer strays from the shores of Iramo, the glow in his horns flickers, and his forked tail pauses for just a moment.

*What is my name...*

The fleeting thought fades, as the image of the Queen's insurmountable beauty returns, and her love along with it.

---

The voice and love of the Queen softened with distance, and her influence along with it. Though the Dancer's mission did not change, his fanatical worship of the Queen faded with time away from Iramo.
