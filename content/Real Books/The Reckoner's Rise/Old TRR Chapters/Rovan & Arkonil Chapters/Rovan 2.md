Rovan's hands quivered as he knelt, trying as he might he couldn't raise himself from the ground. His body tore and shuddered as he wiped golden blood from his eyes.
The trees beneath his hands were crushed, the buildings around him lay in ruins, how did this happen, how did he get here? As his head rang in pain he recounted what he could remember. He had made a formal challenge, called out Illuman before the gods, a battle broke out, they were dying, everyone was bleeding, and the next thing Rovan knew he was on Terra-Nova, the forbidden lands. Gods were sworn to never enter the plane of mortals for fear of that which lies within. Illuman seemed not to care for his own laws any more than he did for the lives of his fellow gods.
As Rovan forced himself to his feet, avoiding crushing a huddled mass of mortals screaming in fear as gods clashed around them, he saw the Dawnfather. Descending to the earth, his feet refusing to touch the dirt as he hovered. How could Illuman walk as mortals did, it was beneath him, everyone was beneath him.
Rovan spat blood as he spoke, forcing the words out with his ragged body, "You... there are mortals here... our children... spare them, you will destroy everything, PLEASE!"
As Rovan begged Illuman simply laughed, raising his hands as thousands of tiny light spears materialized around him, flying at insane speeds and piercing each mortal around them, sending their souls to the sky and to Elysium. Trophies for his collection Rovan thought as he stewed in anger at the greedy God King. As if in the same movement of raising his hands Illuman snatched Rovan by his wings, dragging him into the sky as Rovan fought against the rising desire to sleep, to fall unconscious.
Illuman gloated, smiling with those stained red teeth form millennium of wine, "Congratulations Rovan, you did it, you made me care about killing you, but don't worry, you Kallista and Kimutan will not die today. I am giving you a far worse blessing from your God Illuman. You are being banished to the Abyss. I would tell you not to worry, but my scholars tell me that it has a somewhat adverse effect on the divine. You may be... different when you wake up. But there is some solace, I will take very good care of your precious mortals, enjoy the pit, little reckoner."
Rovan's eyes widened as he saw Kallista carried by Vulcan, tossed down the great pit, and he watched Illuman laugh as he hung the limp starlight body over the gateway to the Abyss. Rovan reached out to the Dawnfather in a singular moment of weakness, of fear, desperate to not be sent down to the darkness, the most evil twisted place so far from the heavens that he would never see the light of his beloved North Star ever again, "Illuman, don't, I am... I am afraid.."
Illuman laughed even louder, his uproar shattering the land around him as his angels dragged Rovan down to the hells.
Rovan's eyes closed, the darkness of the Abyss consuming him, and those starlight eyes would never open again. The God of the North Star, of Bravery and Heroism was dead. In his place rose the Reckoner, the Betrayer, Arkonil, God of Rebellion, Lord of the Abyss.
