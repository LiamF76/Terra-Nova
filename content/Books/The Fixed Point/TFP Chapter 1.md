## Chapter 1: The Moment After Nothing


There was no light at first. Only an absent pressure - a crushing weight on my back and chest, like I was inside of a black hole. I only learned later that I had been floating in the epicenter of the birth of a universe. All I could muster in my mind was a need to piss.

As I tumbled through darkness, feeling no force but my momentum carrying me through nothing, I was blinded by the first star. Nova-Magnus as it was later named, by me. The light brought my vision, and confusion. I was among the stars then, as more lights bloomed into the world, and that should have killed me. Men cannot breathe in wild space, starlight and heat should kill me, my body should explode from a lack of gravity, and yet nothing. Something was protecting me.

That was when I saw it, scanning the 'horizon' of this place led me to what I assumed was its creator, my benefactor, whatever had pulled me from that infernal pillar of sunlight.

The being was boundless, blackened scale skin like a dragon, with hair of tentacles and a slender body. It was almost feminine, if not for being so alien to me. Its 4 arms were raised above its head, cradling an orb larger than my home world in its grasp.

My mind could barely handle the sight, what man can comprehend the actions of the infinite gods of the world? For that is what this being was, I could feel the words echo in my mind, as if this being was so beyond mortal communication that simply adding memory of its intent to my mind was the only option for talking.

*I AM SHAO DARAAN, I AM THE ELDER, YOU ARE MY WITNESS*

A witness. I had been meant to die... why had this Shao Daraan the Elder chosen me to be its witness, and to what? Someone to watch it create... everything?

*YES. KAIROS. YOU ARE MINE, MY CONSTANT, YOU SHALL BE THE FIRST AND THE LAST OF THIS PLACE. I SHALL SHARE IT WITH YOU, AND WE WILL WAR IN WONDER AND AGONY. I SIMPLY NEEDED YOU TO COMPLETE MY DESIGN. YOU SHALL COMPLETE MY WORK WITH YOURSELVES.*

A mad god it is. Scattered thoughts about the first and the last, either I was incapable of discerning whether the god was simply mistaken, or I was incapable of understanding its meaning. I refuse to acknowledge the latter, no god is greater than the mind of a mage, certainly not than myself.

The Elder's presence pressed against my skill like a second heartbeat. Thoughts that weren't mine pulsed through me, they were vast and cold. Yet beneath all that immensity there was a violent curiosity, the kind a storm feels when it notices a leaf caught in its winds.

*YOU ARE ANAMOLOUS. I HAVE MADE YOU IN EVERY WAY, YET NONE SHALL BE NATIVE, FOR YOU ARE AN OUTSIDER.*

"You could at least give me something to stand on!" I call out, my words echoing into nothingness. Does it hear me? There is nothing for the sound to bounce off of... I chuckled to myself in joy, truly this place is a wonder.

It must have heard me, because suddenly I was astride the shoulder of the Elder God. A wonderful vantage point as I watched this Shao Daraan push and pull the earth of this globe, creating his mountains and rivers.

The Elder drifted lower, folding its immense form until it hovered only a few meters above the ground. Its four arms moved independently—one sculpting the sky, another smoothing the land, another stirring oceans into existence, and the last tracing symbols in the air that I suspected were laws of physics.

The space around me rippled like water, was it laughing?

*YOU HAVE PURPOSE HERE. ASSIGNED BY YOUR NATURE. YOU WILL FULLFIL. YOU WILL COMPLETE, YOU WILL ALWAYS EXIST.*

I make a daring move, my head wobbling as i begin to comprehend the invention of gravity, and I sit on the God's shoulder. "And what nature is that? What purpose?"

*YOU LOVE. AND FOR LOVE YOU WILL STAND. AND FOR STANDING YOU WILL FALL. AND FOR FALLING THEY WILL RISE.*

"Do you know love Shao Daraan?" How could this eldritch thing that shapes worlds ever comprehend compassion, connection? These are factors of the human condition. Its head turns to face me, the hair flicking in the emptiness, and stars come alight behind it. They move... alive? Observing us?

It is smiling at me, some strange toothless grin of a monster.

*I HAVE A BROTHER.*

Strange.

*LOVE IS FORCE. FORCE OBSERVED IN YOUR ESESNCE. YOU WERE MADE FROM LOVE. A BOND BEYOND ENTROPY AND YET TIED TO IT.*

"You have known me for what feels like 30 seconds."

*TIME DOES NOT EXIST YET. SHALL WE BUILD IT TOGETHER?*

Building time? HA! What a lucky God Shao Daraan was. It could find no better expert than  wizard specializing in Chronurgy. "We shall then. Soon I will be able to tell you hurry up. And we need to teach you to speak, this thought transmission is giving me a proper headache."

---

If Shao Daraan had allowed the time we invented to pass in that place, it would have been 1 year. 1 year to build a world, stars, sun, moons, and lands beyond Terra-Nova. Truly a magnificent journey it had been.

We grew close, as close as two beings so different could become I suppose. I could feel some bond from the start, but this was different. I asked for a mountain, not too large, a river beneath it, and pines along its slope. I asked, and it was. No bargain, no demand, it simply was. I was grateful for that.

Shao Daraan’s four arms slowed. The last moon settled into its orbit with a soft, cosmic hum. The sky, once a blank canvas—now shimmered with constellations I had named, though Shao insisted names were "decorations from the mortal mind."

The Elder turned toward me. Its vast form dimmed, as if the act of creation had exhausted even a being like it.

*THE WORK IS COMPLETE.*

"Done already, but there is still the matter of my dau-" My words ceased, as they did every time I had tried to ask. This God did not enjoy questions of other beings. I had hoped this would soften, but I was running out of chances to being my precious Lisa.

*THE GAME IS READY. YOU MUST ENTER IT. I AM REGRET. YOU WILL DESPISE ME. YOU WILL DESTROY MUCH OF WHAT WE BUILT.*

"Why would I ever do that?" I ask as I feel my body begin to move towards the land.

*BECAUSE I MAKE YOU SUFFER. YOU SUFFER BECAUSE I LOVE YOU.*

My words tore out of me raw, ragged, useless. "You are a God. You get to choose! Bring Lisa here, I would not harm this world in vain. Just give me my daughter."

*I DO NOT CHOOSE.*

"THEN WHO DOES?" I am screaming now in vain as I fall towards the world. "WHO CHOOSES IF NOT GOD?"

*KAIROS. YOU HAVE ALREADY CHOSEN.*

"You will regret this Daraan! You are cruel! She is just a little girl you cannot deny her even the right to exist! I will find her! I WILL ESCAPE THIS!" The Elder God did not answer. It only watched.

"LISA!" I cried, reaching for a child who wasn’t there, who had never been allowed to be here.

The wind answered with silence.

The ground surged closer, the valley yawning open like a cradle or a grave. My body burned with the force of the fall, but something, some lingering protection from the Elder, kept me from breaking.

I am in a sudden moment of clarity, a red hand, a warm hand, the hand of someone I love, reaches out and soothes my troubled mind. Then I hit the ground.

---

On the first day, of the first year, of the new world, it rained.

I lay in dirt, and saw the invention of mud around me. It rained hard, which was no doubt some twisted and humiliating joke by Shao. I lay there, gasping, shaking, staring up at the empty sky where it had vanished. My fingers searched the ground for something to hold on to, finding roots and underbrush that I gripped until my knuckles were white.

"You will regret this." I whispered, a smattering of tears falling down my cheek as I force my body upright on the soil. "I will unmake your design. I will bring her back to me. I will scour your lands, I will silence your song, I will darken your stars, and I will defy your laws."

The wind stirred, carrying the scent of pine from the mountain I had asked for. A gift. A kindness. A mockery.

"Do you hear me, Daraan?" I shouted into the newborn world. "I will not be your witness. I will not be your constant. I will not be your pawn."

The sky did not answer.

And so the first mortal, me, a wizard of time, stood in the first world, abandoned by the cruel first god.

And so our long war began.
