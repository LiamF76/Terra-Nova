As we draw closer to the swamp, our encounters with people grow fewer, while those with the wilds become more and more common. Thanks to Pollux and Coffee, we have managed to avoid fights with wolves, the dogs are scared off by a couple big horses and a pair of men. 

The bigger monsters though, have made a few passes. A pair of giant snakes came at us one night, but they had poor timing. Petlia was still tending to his new axe, freshly oiled and shined. They met a quick end. I would have hoped for a painless quick end for Lukas, but then he found that weapon...

To say his vitality had returned was a vast understatement.
