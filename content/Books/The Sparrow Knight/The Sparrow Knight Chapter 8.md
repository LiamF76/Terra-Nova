As we draw closer to the swamp, our encounters with people grow fewer, while those with the wilds become more and more common. Thanks to Pollux and Coffee, we have managed to avoid fights with wolves, the dogs are scared off by a couple big horses and a pair of men. 

The bigger monsters though, have made a few passes. A pair of giant snakes came at us one night, but they had poor timing. Petlia was still tending to his new axe, freshly oiled and shined. They met a quick end. I would have hoped for a painless quick end for Lukas, but then he found that weapon...

To say his vitality had returned was a vast understatement. His wound was unchanged, blackened and vile, festering with necromancy as always, but he no longer held his side when exerted. He had his breath, his strength, and vigor back, twice as strong as he had been in years. I had thought I would be carrying the load, especially when it came to fighting. I was wrong.

Lukas was fighting like a man possessed. As that axe met his fingertips, it was like he held the strength and skill of all his ancestors in his hands, and he swung it like he was trying to tear the world itself in two. We had snake stew that night, didn't even have to remove the heads to cook them, Lukas decapitated both.
