
The Third Age was easily his least favorite to travel to. Full of conflict and complication, none of which Kairos had orchestrated, which meant in the end it was pointless. 

His hands were freezing. Fucking Winterlands, sub-zero temperatures 11 months out of the year, what kind of thick skulled small minded ape would choose this as a home?

The ape he imagined in his mind was not far enough off from the man that approached him to remove any of Kairos' bias against this land's natives. He was tall, stocky, and quite strong looking, with that potent stench of Northern Ale and bravado that Kairos hated oh so much.

His accent was thick, slogging through the Common words like they were thick mud against his teeth, "So, you are the foreign Wizard! You came here for what? You seek our weapons in a war against the Elder God?" As he spoke he paused, taking a nice lock slog of his drink before slamming it down on the table.

The slam had an intended effect, causing Kairos' cup to leap and fall to the floor, spilling the red wine across the stone tiles of the Inn. King Trygtor was famous for his ability to ruin alliances in just one meeting. Kairos would wonder until the day he forgot this oaf just how he became King of the entire North.

The King wanted a response, he would have one. Kairos sighed, looked down at his cup, and then locked eyes with Trygtor. Don't back down. He is but a man, he stews over his plans for tomorrow, our plans lie in centuries and ages. With eyes locked, Kairos held his hand over the cup on the floor, twisting his hand palm down, and rotating it counter clockwise. As he did the wine seemed to reverse, un-spilling from the cup as the cup began its journey back onto the table, and into Kairos' hand. Trygtor watched as Kairos controlled the very time of his drink, undoing the King's slight, and taking a calm sip from his cup as if it had been quite the simple trick.

"The greatest weapon in all the world is my mind King Trygtor. However my magic can only do so much. My mission is not to take your weapons. It is to create one worthy of being wielded by my intellect. Do you feel you are up to the task?" He wasn't. Kairos had known it, but he wasn't truly here for Trygtor, he was here for the blood-suckers that lived in his land. And besides, this would be entertaining.

Trygtor laughed, and in a single motion he flipped the table, sending Kairos along with it into a wall. It hurt like hell, but he had been through far worse than hell. Perhaps a couple broken ribs, surely if he stayed here he would die, the King was advancing with axes ready. "They said you were smart wizard, they said you were dangerous! I will tell Catherine that you dies poorly, and I will let her drink from your corpse!"

So that's why the bitch needed his help. Vampire noble ladies, always fucking their way into problems, she was quite lucky he owed her a favor. Oh, look at that, the large man had finally reached him, time to dispose of the trash. Kairos pulled his staff up and slammed it into the ground, twisting it counter clockwise, but this time the target was himself.

He felt time shift backwards, a blindingly familiar feeling after all these years. He phased through Trygtor and found himself where he had been sitting. Unfortunately the chair was gone, and he had to stand himself up using his staff. The King could at lease have left him a chair.

No matter, he had a job to do. As the King turned around to witness his assailant cast out a barrage of magic missiles piercing through his abdomen, leaving the King with quite a few holes, fatal ones.

Strong men, fast men, warriors of all kinds had faced Kairos, and they all made the same stunned noise as their bodies fell. The exhale of genuine surprise at being felled in a single strike after their understanding of time being torn to shreds. It had been enjoyable at a time many years ago, not it was grating like nails against a chalkboard. A symptom of the sickness of murder.

The barman walked over, asked for Kairos' cup, and filled it again. "You aren't at all concerned with the dead King on your floor or the fact that I murdered him?" Kairos asked with genuine surprise. He had expected some kind of fear or retaliation.

The Barman shrugged and began cleaning some plates at the counter. "He raised my taxes last year."

A small laugh was followed by the clink of coin as Kairos tossed the King's purse from his belt to the Barman. "Suppose you take it back now then hm?"

A knowing nod was exchanged between the two, and Kairos pulled his cloak off the hanger by the door, draping it over his shoulders again. It was heavy, which meant it would be warm. The black fur of some cruel Northern beast that had cost him a whole gold piece to get made contrasted his pale white and gold robes, but it was function over form. Better to be warm and slightly less stylist, than be dead and perfectly dressed.
