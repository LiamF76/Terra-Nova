## Terra-Nova Wiki Homepage

LAPTOP TEST!

Welcome to Terra-Nova! This is my Cloud stored Obsidian vault for all things Terra-Nova, my Homebrew Dungeons & Dragons setting and campaign world.

Please feel free to browse these pages and read up on the setting. Complied here is information for Players in Terra-Nova. You may research locations, read up on myths and history, learn about religions, and pretty much anything else this world has to offer! Always feel free to message old Shao Daraan (maiLfps) on discord if you have further questions, or if you think something should be added! Please keep in mind this is an ever-evolving project, and some things will be in construction or entirely undeveloped before or even during campaigns.

Enjoy, and I hope to play through your tales in Terra-Nova very soon!
