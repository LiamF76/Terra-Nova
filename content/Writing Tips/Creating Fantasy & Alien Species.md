## 3 Pillars:

1. Food
	- What do they eat, what do they have to compete with to eat
2. Reproduction
	- What makes them more likely to reproduce, what traits are selected to survive their environment, birth / spawn / eggs, life expectancy
3. Not dying
	- Who are the major predators, how do they defend themselves, and how to they survive harsh weather / environment

Answer those 3 questions, and you will have the fundamentals of a species.
