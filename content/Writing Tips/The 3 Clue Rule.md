notes on: https://thealexandrian.net/wordpress/1118/roleplaying-games/three-clue-rule

- For any conclusion you want the PCs to make, include at least three clues.
- But, for any _chokepoint problem_, I make sure there’s at least three solutions.
- By a chokepoint, I mean any problem that must be solved in order for the adventure to continue.
