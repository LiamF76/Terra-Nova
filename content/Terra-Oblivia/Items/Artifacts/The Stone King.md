# The Stone King

The Stone King is a timeless artifact. A 6ft tall stone statue of an elderly human male, holding a demonic sword pointed into the ground. It is said to hold the soul of a demon king from the time of the Old Gods, granting demonic power to those who worship it, at a heavy price. It is a forbidden and heretical artifact that has been lost, its location unknown.
