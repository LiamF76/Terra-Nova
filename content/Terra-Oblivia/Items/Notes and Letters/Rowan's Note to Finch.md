# Rowan's Note to Finch

![[Rowan's Note to Finch-1792634194170.webp|600]]

## Transcript:
Finch
If you are reading this then it is likely something quite terrible has happened to me. I write in haste, but I am with you always. This amulet is the key to everything Finch. Protect it, do not let it remain in Citadel, they will be hunting for it. You are in danger. Let the light of the new star guide you home. I am sorry. Remember, the sun will rise tomorrow, and we will try again.
