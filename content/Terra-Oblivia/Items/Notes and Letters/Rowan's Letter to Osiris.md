# Rowan's Letter to Osiris

![[Rowan's Letter to Osiris-1793297672438.webp|600]]

## Transcript

Mr. Arzen Osiris,

I write with admiration, as your work on the occult is considered the most expert and well documented amongst all scholars, from your land to mine. I assume you our corrispondance has been greatly beneficial, even rewarding me with an artifact. I cherish this amulet deeply.

Were you able to discuss with the other scholars on the subject of our ‘Stone King’? I know you are busy with your own delves into the ruins of Walima’s lost empire, but I would appreciate any information. Our last testimoy landed the artifact somewhere in Escharra, but I have been able to locate no record of it outside of myth, It has been many months since we began, and I believe my research is starting to draw unwanted eyes. I may have to make an expedition and locate it myself, as you did with your vault.

On your request for information about this so called, ‘Min Jadid’ or as I say, The Reborn, I have nothing. No doubt you are aware of my connection to this topic as a learned and holy man. I have nothing to say on this matter. Respect that I will protect the Reborn, and allow it to do as it sees fit. Destiny is not yours to play with, we trust the Gods, and the Weaver to care for tomorrow. You would be wise to abandon this path, searching for the past, so far in the past... it will involve forces we are all better off without.

Previously I made a promise to tell you who this Reborn is, and where they are. I am breaking that promise. May Lumena punish me properly for breaking my word, but if you are ment to find him you will. Do not meddle with the world.
