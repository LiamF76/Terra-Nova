- **General Daegel Lathir**: Elf turned construct. Inspired by General Grievous, son of Admetus the Warlord of Ashkarad. Born with a bad leg / needed a crutch to walk / physically disabled. Transformed himself into a construct over time to abandon the weakness of his flesh. Current strongest Warlord in Ashkarad. Killed his father and stole Admetus most prized possession (a wish dragon), bonded with this Wish Dragon and became a massive superpower in Ashkarad. Daegel seeks to create an army of constructs loyal to himself that he can use to conquer the entire continent, to prove he was not born to die as his father said and to affirm the weakness of flesh and reliance on Elven magics.
- **Hunger-Of-Kings**: Wish Dragon, believes in Dragon supremacy and will not grant wishes to mortals. Made a deal with the land of Terra-Oblivia to grant a wish to the one who can conqueror all of Ashkarad. Pride + the chance at a wish keep the Dragons & Warlords in constant conflict instead of invading the rest of the world.
- **Lord Dastan**: Human leader of Bastion in the Triarch. Dastan believes the Triarch is awaiting its own doom by not attacking or fortifying its defenses against the Empire and Ashkarad. While the Empire is busy quelling rebellion within its own lands and Ashkarad's dragons war amongst themselves, he knows they will not ignore the Triarch forever. Dastan leads towards war, and believes the Triarch must enforce its will on Terra-Oblivia, before others do so on the Triarch.
