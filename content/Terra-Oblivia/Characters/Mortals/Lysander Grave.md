## Lysander Grave
**Book-Keeper, Myth-Hunter**
*Pronouns*: he/him

---

## Voice
Lysander has a strange tone, speaking slowly to those he believes less intelligent than himself, and quickly to those he considers of worthy intellect. His demeanor and ego is often off putting, but he speaks confidently and honestly, and is respected as a public speaker across Citadel and the Triarch.

> [!quote]
> "There are no secrets my Mistress cannot find, and there are no learnings I would not die to bring her."

> [!quote]
> "Knowledge is knowledge. You must be intelligent to understand it, you must be motivated to learn it, and you must be cunning to beat others to it."

---

## Description
6'3 Elf male. Lysander has pale skin with a strange green tint, often thought to be the product of his deep bond to Vespar Telarum, his Verdant scaled God. He wears simple black robes and wields an arcane orb of black and green energy as a conduit for his divine magics. His eyes have a pale green tint and he has no pupils, this magic effect referred to as 'Verdant-Sight.'
