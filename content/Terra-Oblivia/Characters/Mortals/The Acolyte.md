## The Acolyte
**Kasayt's Chosen**
*Pronouns*: he/him

---

## Voice
The Acolyte whispers with the force of a Dragon's voice. He speaks with the power of Kasayt, his words laced in the shadowed-flame. It is said he can scream so loudly it causes men's ears to bleed.

> [!quote]
> "Do you not know death when you see it? This is my hour. Kasayt is rising, and I carry his flame."

> [!quote]
> "So long as I carry Kasayt's flame, no man can kill me."

> [!Quote]
> "All power in this world belongs to those who can take it, and Kasayt shall have everything."

---

## Description
The acolyte is a 7ft tall humanoid. He always wears his black robes and armor, draped in Kasayt's flame, and always carrying the Orb of his sealed master.
