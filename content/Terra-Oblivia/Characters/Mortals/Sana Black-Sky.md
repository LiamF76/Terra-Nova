**The Blood Sovereign**

> [!quote]
> "Cut them to pieces, boil them in the oil. The children of Kasayt shall feast tonight."

> [!quote]
> "It is not a matter of how think they build their walls, only if you are willing to shatter your arms hammering them down. Follow me, and I will show you my splintered wrists and ravaged biceps."

## Description
Sana is a 6'10 dark skinned Elf with blood red eyes and pure white hair. They are muscular, wear little bits of armor and fur. They are known for their gold skull belt, said to have been forged from golden Giant bones. They always carry a massive weapon, rotating between the largest thing they can find and swing.
