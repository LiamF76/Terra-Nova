## Ayla Syros
**Stargazer, The Blind Seer**
*Pronouns*: she/her

---

## Voice
Ayla has a wispy voice, like stardust carried across the night sky. She speaks with a strange sadness, like an ancient soul within her has never forgotten their lost love.

> [!quote]
> "Time heals all wounds. The wounded forget, or are forgotten."

> [!quote]
> "Do not follow the hands across the clock unless you are willing to reach the most important moments. Do not follow the stars across the sky if you are not willing to reach the correct place. Do both, and you will find yourself exactly where Krokha Inam means for you to be, as I do."

---

## Description
Elf female, 5'9, slender build. Ayla wears a vast set of Priestess robes almost like a gown of silk, it holds a mystical shine of gold and silver, and appears to twinkle in moonlight. To commune better with the Spirits of the Stars and space, she wears a mask to cover her physical eyes and force her spiritual eye open. She has deep black hair and striking facial features. It is said while wearing her mask she can see into the very near future.
