## Bellum Grail
**Winged-Spear, Spirit-Slayer**
*Pronouns*: she/her

---

## Voice
Bellum is calculated, willing to lie and cheat her way to any victory. She will say anything to get what she wants, and kill anyone. Her voice ranges from her goals, if she wants a powerful man to do her bidding, she will act sweet and soft. If she wants her soldiers to rise to a challenge she will give powerful and rousing speeches.

> [!quote]
> "No masters but Kasayt. No Gods but Kasayt. Let those who would stand against the Shadowed-Flame face me, and I will show them the power of the Red King."

> [!quote]
> "Quote"

---

## Description
Bellum is a dark-skinned female human standing 6ft tall. She wears heavy metal armor modeled after an ancient empire. Atop her brow is a silver crown adorned with green gemstones that glows when she invokes her magic. Over her shoulders lays the pelt of a Lion Spirit she slew when leading armies for the Rocan Empire, a trophy she carries proudly. She is able to sprout arcane iron wings from her back that flow with green energy and allow her to fly.
