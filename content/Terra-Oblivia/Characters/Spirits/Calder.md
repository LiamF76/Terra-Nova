## Calder
**The Spirit of Autumn, The Autumn Knight**
*Pronouns*: he/him

---

## Voice
Calder is loud and proud, he has a deep masculine voice that echoes through every room he speaks in. He is respectful and honorable, never threatening those who do not deserve it.

> [!quote]
> "My honor demands I protect this place. I shall redden the leaves with the blood of any intruders."

> [!quote]
> "Let it be known I stand as the last of the Green Knights, and I will keep my oath to their memory until my final days."

---

## Description
Calder takes the form of a knight clad is leaves and armor. He wears a cape made of orange, red, yellow, and brown leaves. His helmet carries the horns of a great beast, and he wields an axe whose edge is carved from the setting sun itself.
