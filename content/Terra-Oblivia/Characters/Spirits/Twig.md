# Twig

![[Twig-1795643776896.webp|400]]

---

## Twig
**Leafborn**
*Pronouns*: they/them

---

## Voice
Twig speaks like a child, high pitched innocent baby. They are easily excitable, and just and easily let down when others are rude or aggressive.

> [!quote]
> "By the bark of my stick and the blade of my grass, I am Twig, and I am of the forest!"

> [!quote]
> "My mom has lots of swords."

---

## Description
Twig's 1'8 body is a thin stick covered in a vaguely humanoid shape of leaves. They have horns made of sticks they picked up and stuck into the leaves, and small eyes made of acorn shells.

---

## Important Info
- Woodland Spirit, very distant descendant of [[Lumena]]
- Mother: [[Iseul]]
- Father: Unknown spirit of Lumena
