## Working List:

- *The Stormseer*: `To see the way forward is to see everything.`
	- Mage, can navigate Abyssal Storms. 
- *The Scarred*: `There is always power for those willing to pay the price.`
	- Mage who has Demonic / Abyssal connections or use Demonic / Abyssal magic like blood magic. 
- *The Magus / The Thaum*: `All secrets can be uncovered through study, and dedication.`
	- General mages, name is determined by social standing. Wizardry / Alchemy. 
- *The Blessed*: `The Spirit is with me, my cause is righteous.`
	- Holy / religious mages. Can be paladin or cleric style. Usually pact bound with a Spirit of divine authority.
- *The Griffin Knight*: `Dragons may rule, but in the clouds the are not alone.`
	- Warrior who fights alongside / mounted on a bonded pet Griffin. Code of chivalry.
- *The Marauder* `Life is a dirty fight, kick sand in their eyes, carry a hidden dagger, poison whoever you want, who cares? They're dead, you aren't.`
	- Warrior of Ashkarad's lawless land, fights using wit or brutality. Doesn't care for honor. 
- *The Legionary*: `Fire burns wildly, but we are control. Allow your foes to make their mistakes, and strike when they are already falling.`
	- Soldier of the Rocan Empire, fights using discipline and tactics. Strength is the only virtue.
