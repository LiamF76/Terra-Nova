This is a list and description of all the skills in the Terra-Oblivia Tabletop game.

- **Survival**  
	Enduring and navigating hostile conditions, reading terrain, finding food and shelter, tracking, and withstanding environmental hardship. Covers travel through Ashkarad's wasteland, overland Abyssal Storm exposure, and general wilderness competence.

- **Lore**  
	Academic and historical knowledge, history, geography, politics, known creatures, and known magical theory. Covers "book knowledge" a character could plausibly have learned through study, education, or oral tradition, regardless of whether they practice magic themselves.

- **Devotion**  
	A character's standing, ritual competence, and willpower in matters of faith — maintaining favor with a God, Great Spirit, or other patron, performing rites correctly, and resisting spiritual corruption or temptation. Also covers raw faith-driven resolve in moments of crisis.

- **Combat**  
	Physical fighting competence in a fight, melee or ranged, armed or unarmed. Covers tactics, weapon use, and reacting under direct physical threat.

- **Influence**  
	Social effectiveness, persuasion, deception, intimidation, negotiation, and leadership. Covers getting people to believe, trust, fear, or follow you.

- **Craft**  
	Making, repairing, modifying, or understanding constructed things — tools, weapons, armor, structures, and Artifice-based magical effects requiring materials and formulae.

- **Stealth**  
	Avoiding detection, sneaking, hiding, moving quietly, and infiltrating places or situations undetected.

- **Insight**  
	Perceiving what isn't obvious, reading people's intentions, sensing danger or deception, and noticing details others miss.
