# Tithe

> [!Quote] An Unnamed Spirit of War, Child of Rocan Ashar
> *"You kill in my father's name, and offer the heads of your enemies at my shrine. You pay in prayer and offerings, and I drink from that gift. I take what I need to live and a bit more to grow. The rest flows upwards to Rocan Ashar, and thus the Tithe is paid."*

Tithe is the divine law of worship, the system that dictates how [[Terra-Oblivia Religion|Religion]]flows into the [[Terra-Oblivia/Magic/Spirits|Spirits]], and how that flows from the Spirits to the [[Terra-Oblivia Gods and Pantheon|Gods]]. This Tithe is not a spell to be cast or rule to be followed, it is a truth of the universe.

## The Flow of Tithe

**Mortals -> Spirits -> Gods.**

Mortals worship their Gods through the Spirits, making their offerings and prayers. This worship sustains the Spirits life, and the Spirits take what they need to survive, then a bit more. This sustains and allows the Spirits to grow. Then any extra is given upwards to their Gods, which sustains and grows the Gods. Spirits who receive more worship will feed and grow stronger, while those who do not will starve, along with their Gods.

## Mortal Tithe

Mortal mages who are Spirit Bonded gain magic through this Tithe. If their Spirit grows stronger, the mortal mage can receive greater [[Terra-Oblivia Magic|Magic]]. It is beneficial to these mages to spread their Spirit's greatness to gain might themselves.
