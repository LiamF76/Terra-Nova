# The Sacrament of Oblivion

The Sacrament of Oblivion is a heretical magic that uses [[Terra-Oblivia Gods and Pantheon#The Only Pantheon|Old Rites]], and communes directly with the Old Gods.

This ritual is an inversion of the magic Oblivati-Mori, instead of making you forgotten, and dead, it realizes the magic of your soul and shows who you truly are when alive.

Beings whose souls are tied to the Divine, the Old Gods, or anything significant from the time of the Old Gods who cast this ritual will have their true soul revealed.
