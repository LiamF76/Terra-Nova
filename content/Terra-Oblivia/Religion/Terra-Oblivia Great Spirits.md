## Terra-Oblivia Great Spirits

Great Spirits are divine beings tied to a God's domain. Each one governs one narrow aspect of it, and mortals often pray to a Spirit more directly than to the God. Below the Great Spirits are Minor Spirits, who govern smaller or local things.

This list of spirits dynamically pulls from the folder of Spirit Characters. Spirits whose page has not been published yet will not appear here.

```dataview
LIST FROM "Terra-Oblivia/Characters/Spirits"
WHERE publish = true
```
