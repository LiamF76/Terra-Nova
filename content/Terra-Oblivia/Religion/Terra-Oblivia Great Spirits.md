# Terra-Oblivia Great Spirits

Great Spirits are divine beings tied to a [[Terra-Oblivia Gods and Pantheon|Gods]] domain. Each one governs one narrow aspect of it, and mortals often pray to a Spirit more directly than to the God. Below the Great Spirits are Minor Spirits, who govern smaller or local things. These Spirits act as conduits for [[Terra-Oblivia Religion|Religious]] worship of the Gods through the [[Tithe]] system.

Some Spirits may not be added to this list, see the Spirits folder under Terra-Oblivia -> Characters -> Spirits to see all Spirits in Terra-Oblivia.

## Great Spirits

- **Astor**, *The Summer Eagle*: A Spirit of Lumena, Astor was a great guardian of the valley that houses the Triarch kingdom for centuries. He guarded those lands long before battling a Red Dragon Kasayt, which weakened him greatly. Now Astor resides in the valley, his worship and legend fade as his shrines slowly become forgotten.
