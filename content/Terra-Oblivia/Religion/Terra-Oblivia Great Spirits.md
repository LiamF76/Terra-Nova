# Terra-Oblivia Great Spirits

Great Spirits are divine beings tied to a [[Terra-Oblivia Gods and Pantheon|Gods]] domain. Each one governs one narrow aspect of it, and mortals often pray to a Spirit more directly than to the God. Below the Great Spirits are Minor Spirits, who govern smaller or local things. These Spirits act as conduits for [[Terra-Oblivia Religion|Religious]] worship of the Gods through the [[Tithe]] system. 

Though Great Spirits are the main conduit of worship, they are rare to see, often times only attending gatherings or stopping at temples to gather their accumulated Tithe, and send it onwards to the heavens. It is a blessing to see a Great Spirit and an honor to speak with one. Many of the Great Spirits travel across the world, moving from temple to temple and rarely staying in one location for long, seeking to gather and guard as much of their domain as possible.

Some Spirits may not be added to this list, see the Spirits folder under Terra-Oblivia -> Characters -> Spirits to see all Spirits in Terra-Oblivia.

## Great Spirits

- **[[Astor]]**, *The Summer Eagle*: A Spirit of [[Lumena]], Astor was a great guardian of the valley that houses the Triarch kingdom for centuries. He guarded those lands long before battling a Red Dragon Kasayt, which weakened him greatly. Now Astor resides in the valley, his worship and legend fade as his shrines slowly become forgotten.
- **[[Paga]]**, *The Winter Bear*: A spirit of [[Aevarus]], Paga is a great bear who awakens during the winter, protecting those animals that hibernate and sleep during the cold times. It is said he is the largest bear in the world, and he has the horns of a reindeer.
- **[[Tayox]]**, *The Golden Bull*: A Spirit of [[Oa Maton]], Tayox is a solid gold bull bearing feathered wings. Tayox serves as a judge between Spiritual disagreements, and is known to have a deep love for mortal children who ask him for aid when parents institute unfair bedtimes.
