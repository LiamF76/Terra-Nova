# The Law of Oblivion

> [!Note] The Law
> The Law of Oblivion, born from the chaos of the past and the folly of Dead Gods to prevent such a violent end that was once thought unavoidable.

The essence of the Law of Oblivion is that the [[Terra-Oblivia Gods and Pantheon|Gods]] must not meddle in the mortal world directly. They must allow mortals their ways, and let the [[Terra-Oblivia/Magic/Spirits|Spirits]] guide them. All Gods must follow the Law, and those that fail will be punished by garnishing their [[Tithe]].

- Gods must not set foot on Terra-Oblivia in their true form.
- Gods must not exert their power on Terra-Oblivia directly.
- Gods must follow the Tithe, and the Spirits must pay accordingly.
- Gods must allow the Spirits to grow.
- Mortals must not become Gods.

## History of the Law

When the [[Terra-Oblivia Dragons|Dragons]] rose up and devoured the Old Gods, they brought with them the might to rule, and the Law. The Old Gods meddled and walked upon Terra-Oblivia, and it brought ruin everywhere they stepped. Now the Gods are distant, and the world lives on because of that.
