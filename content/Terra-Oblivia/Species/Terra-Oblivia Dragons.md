### Dragons, The New Gods

The New Gods are dragons, and Dragons are divine. Many dragons are worshipped like living Gods, respected and feared in all lands and cultures. Even those who do not worship or revere Dragons still recognize their divine might, and know they must defend themselves against it.

#### Religious Views on Dragons

Some religions view Dragons as the Divine test of mortal strength. To honor their Gods, followers must do battle against the Dragons and prove their worth.

Others believe Dragons should never be fought at all, only served or appeased. In this view, mortal strength was never meant to be proven against the divine, only offered up to it. Warlords who pay tribute to dragons (as in Ashkarad) are not seen as cowards, but as the truly devout — those wise enough to recognize their place beneath a god, rather than presuming to test themselves against one.

#### General Sentiment

The prevailing wisdom is not to fight or oppose dragons, and to seek to appease them instead:

- If you do not worship them, you are respectful and avoidant.
- If you are not respectful and avoidant, then you have a death wish.
