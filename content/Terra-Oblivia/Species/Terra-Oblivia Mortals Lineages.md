# Mortal Ancestries

This is a list of the mortal ancestires in Terra-Oblivia. This is a WIP, a rough list is available at the bottom of this note with any that have not been added yet.

- **Constructs**: Built rather than born, Constructs are artificial people given life through artifice and Rune Magic. The oldest were made in an age when the world’s magic ran clean, and no one alive fully understands how they were given true minds. Modern artificers can repair and maintain them, but creating a new thinking Construct is nearly impossible, so their numbers only shrink. Each Construct runs on a mana core, and as the world’s magic grows more poisoned, those cores dim. A Construct feels this as a slow fading of memory and thought, until one day it simply stops. Many cities still debate whether Constructs are people or property, and in some lands they are bought and sold like tools. Dwarves tend to treat them with quiet respect, seeing their own rune-craft reflected in them. They are uncommon, and a Construct walking freely in a city will draw curious, and sometimes greedy, eyes.
- **Dragonborn**: Revered and respected (or reviled by Elves), Dragonborn are the literal children of Dragons. Bred specifically to rule and enact the will of Dragons, these people are powerful, strong with might and magic, they command respect and fear in mage craft, warfare, and government. Which dragon fathered or mothered them, and why, is often a matter of political or religious importance in itself. They inherit some trait or motif of their dragon parent (a color, an element, a temperament) but not full draconic power. All people recognize Dragonborn, either respectfully or otherwise, their power and status is undeniable, and their presence in a city is always of note.
- **Dwarves**: Short and stout, Dwarves live beneath the surface and within mountains. Dwarves have no magical bloodlines and cannot be born with magic. They have a unique magical growth property, they can weave gemstones into their hair in braids, and as their hair grows it will imbue mana into the gems, making them useable for artifice magic. These braid-stones are considered sacred and personal. Dwarves are the most talented at Rune Magics. Dwarves still rule underground kingdoms and cities, along with the greatest mines in Terra-Oblivia. They are not uncommon in general society, and seeing one is  a regular occurrence.
- **Elves**: Tall and old, Elves have been on Terra-Oblivia longer than any other mortal species. Elves once lived near endless lives, but that has faded as the world's magic was poisoned, along with the magic in their blood. It is a slow decline Elves feel personally. Elves carry the strongest magical bloodlines, but it has faded all the same. The most powerful Elf mag bloodline carries a fraction of the power it did centuries ago. Elves are generally solitary, believing Humans specifically are responsible for the downfall of the world and its magics. They are not uncommon in regular society, but their opinion on other species can influence how much public attention they will tolerate. Most Elves live in secluded family groups or settlements, and some make their homes within larger cities for resource availability.
- **Giants**: Giants were once a superpower across the north of Escharra, but Dragons despise Giants. The Warlords and Dragons of Escharra decimated Giant society over the years. Now Giants are nearly extinct, being used as high value slaves in Ashkarad and the Rocan Empire. They were a proud people once, well versed in magic and martial skills, but now those traditions are lost.
- **Goblinoids**: A family of related peoples who share a common ancestry, though each branch has grown into something very different. Goblinoids are hardy and quick to breed, and they survive where others cannot. In the Age of Dragons they have found use as soldiers, laborers, and fodder, and few rulers think of them as anything more. Many Goblinoids resent this deeply, and old grudges run between the branches as often as between Goblinoids and everyone else.
	- *Goblins*: Small, clever, and endlessly resourceful, Goblins live in the cracks of the world: sewers, slums, ruins, and forgotten tunnels. They are rarely born with magic, but they have a knack for scavenging and tinkering, often rigging broken artifice into something that works, if not safely. Most people see them as pests or thieves, and Goblins in cities tend to live in tight-knit gangs or warrens for protection. They are very common, and very often ignored.
	- *Hobgoblins*: Taller and stronger than their Goblin kin, Hobgoblins are a disciplined and martial people who value rank, order, and loyalty. Many Warlords and Dragons prize them as soldiers, and whole Hobgoblin legions serve in foreign armies for pay or by oath. They see themselves as the proper leaders of all Goblinoids, a claim Goblins often dispute. Seeing Hobgoblins in a city usually means a garrison, a mercenary company, or a lord’s guard.
	- *Trolls*: Huge, long-limbed, and nearly impossible to kill, Trolls heal from almost any wound that is not fire or acid. Some scholars believe this regeneration is a remnant of old magic in their blood, now souring as the world’s magic is poisoned, and Trolls today heal slower and age harder than the ones in old tales. They are often feared as brutes, though many Trolls are patient and thoughtful. Most live in remote clans in swamps and forests, and are rarely seen in cities except as hired muscle or prizefighters.
	- *Bugbears*: Large, hairy, and surprisingly stealthy, Bugbears are hunters and ambushers who live on the edges of Goblinoid society. They are often used by other Goblinoid groups as scouts and enforcers. Bugbears keep to themselves and are uncommon in cities, where their size and quiet nature tend to make others uneasy.
- **Humans**: Numerous and adaptable. Humans are the most common race. They are the least likely to be born with magic, even those born to magical bloodlines have a chance to be without it. Humans have risen to power in the Age of Dragons, often acting as puppet Kings for the Dragons and Dragonborn, and as such they have an inflated sense of self importance and ego. Most though exist in poor conditions fighting to survive.
- **Scarborn**: Mortals corrupted by the touch of the Abyssal Scars that covered Terra-Oblivia in the days of the Old Gods. They have many colors, reds oranges and even black, alongside demonic horns. They are viewed by others as cursed from birth, the product of evil and corruption in the world.
