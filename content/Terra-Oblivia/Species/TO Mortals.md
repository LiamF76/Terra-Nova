- **Constructs**: 
- **Dragonborn**: Revered and respected (or reviled by Elves), Dragonborn are the literal children of Dragons. Bred specifically to rule and enact the will of Dragons, these people are powerful, strong with might and magic, they command respect and fear in mage craft, warfare, and government. Which dragon fathered or mothered them, and why, is often a matter of political or religious importance in itself. They inherit some trait or motif of their dragon parent (a color, an element, a temperament) but not full draconic power. All people recognize Dragonborn, either respectfully or otherwise, their power and status is undeniable, and their presence in a city is always of note.
- **Dwarves**: Short and stout, Dwarves live beneath the surface and within mountains. Dwarves have no magical bloodlines and cannot be born with magic. They have a unique magical growth property, they can weave gemstones into their hair in braids, and as their hair grows it will imbue mana into the gems, making them useable for artifice magic. These braid-stones are considered sacred and personal. Dwarves are the most talented at Rune Magics. Dwarves still rule underground kingdoms and cities, along with the greatest mines in Terra-Oblivia. They are not uncommon in general society, and seeing one is  a regular occurrence.
- **Elves**: Tall and old, Elves have been on Terra-Oblivia longer than any other mortal species. Elves once lived near endless lives, but that has faded as the world's magic was poisoned, along with the magic in their blood. It is a slow decline Elves feel personally. Elves carry the strongest magical bloodlines, but it has faded all the same. The most powerful Elf mag bloodline carries a fraction of the power it did centuries ago. Elves are generally solitary, believing Humans specifically are responsible for the downfall of the world and its magics. They are not uncommon in regular society, but their opinion on other species can influence how much public attention they will tolerate. Most Elves live in secluded family groups or settlements, and some make their homes within larger cities for resource availability.

- **Humans**: Numerous and adaptable. Humans are the most common race. They are the least likely to be born with magic, even those born to magical bloodlines have a chance to be without it. Humans have risen to power in the Age of Dragons, often acting as puppet Kings for the Dragons and Dragonborn, and as such they have an inflated sense of self importance and ego. Most though exist in poor conditions fighting to survive.
