# The Story of the Wolves
*A story passed down from the ancient past. Held in memory through oral tradition, it is said that once when the world was in need, Wizards could summon a great army of wolves to protect the world and its people..*

It was said once that in the land of Escharra there stood a great many towers. Towers of beauty and color, and atop each tower was a Wizard. These Wizards were protectors, those who loved and cared for the land and it's people.

From atop their towers, the Wizards would cast their spells, but when the land was threatened, they would enact their greatest magic. The Wizard stood clad in white, atop a tower so tall it stretched beyond the stats, further than even time could reach. From this celestial throne, the Wizard would summon his Wolves.

The Wolves would race across the land, tearing evil men to pieces. The largest wolf was white, and her hair flowed so long and radiant it is said she became a lion, her fur dipped in the blood of her victories. The smallest wolf was black, and could take many shapes, but his favorite was that of a friend. He is fried to all things, animal and man. 

There were many Wolves, and they were all heroes. One day they will return, under [[The Light of a New Star]]
