# From Dust To Dust

It was a complicated plan in all honestly, but I never expected them to get it this wrong.

Being that I had no soul of my own, I made one. That's what Wizards do after all, souls are magic just like anything else, all I needed was the right materials. Granted it needed to be powerful enough to hold my form and immense magical power, so the only real option I had during the Abyss Wars was... a Lesser Demon.  It was feisty, but Ryfon got it in a bottle for me.

So I have a demon soul that will be reborn in the Abyss with all my magical power and knowledge, but none of my morality, the moment I die, which is coming quick! As Wizards do, I made a plan.

I had 4 goals:
1. Preserve my consciousness, not my soul, my knowledge and ability, with none of my personal memory, a pure Temporal Wizard mind database to power the Elegy. I call it Graveglass.
2. Split my soul in two, the Demon part, and the part that was me.
3. Destroy the Demon soul, so that it doesn't get to keep any of my badass powers and come back to bite any of the Geriatric Wolves (or anyone else) in the ass after I am no longer taking care of everything at every moment forever.
4. Die. I wanted my mortal soul, whatever had been allocated to me while fused with the Demon, to die. For good. No reincarnation, no time tricks. I had a good run, the kids turned out alright, and Nysria was content with the amount of time she got with me (though she would never say to to another few centuries, immortals am I right?)

My body was weird looking from above. I don't exactly know what *me* was looking at it. I was frail in those days. Age had gotten me, white beard, missing horn from that experiment, general wear and tear, body was in rough shape. The skin too... the pale purple, I was almost lavendar.
