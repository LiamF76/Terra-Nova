# Leave Coward

I wipe my nose, scratching an itch that had risen while desperately trying to stay still hiding from the Warlord's patrols. Scratching an itch is a pleasant thing, removing a nagging pain leaves you warm and fuzzy inside. [[Dastan]] had a different kind of itch tonight, and he was about to start scratching. 

"They don't even have walls, and those raiders... looked more like farmers to my eye. One was carrying a pitchfork and a net for Lumena's sake." I say casually, trying to cast as much doubt as he would tolerate on Ember-Flag's military strength. I disagreed with an attack, and he knew it, this was clearly not a military base.

"They're vulnerable now. Who cares what weapons they carry, Scarborn savages. They'd kill you with their horns and eat your heart. They do that with malformed babies you know, tear out the heart and offer it to demons. Monsters, and now we have one of their Warlords, weak, we can take him out before he invades the valley."

I grimace at his description of the Scarborn. I had known plenty of their kind, and I knew many more Elves Men and Orcs that would sacrifice a child before a Scarborn would even dream of such a thing. "Is he even a Warlord anymore? He didn't bring much of an army."

Dastan laughs from his belly. "Not much of one that is, but trust me [[Amory Tau|Amory]]. He is as bad as they come. He fought alongside [[Sana Black-Sky]], rode on the back of a dragon against [[Daegel Lathir|Lathir]]. He killed thousands of people, innocents, and he wants to invade the Triarch and make himself Warlord here now that his reign in Ashkarad is over."

I shrug, placing a hand on the pommel on the hilt of my rapier. "People can change."

"Not killers." he responds matter-of-factly, as I eye his sword and armor, his maps of Ember-Flag and how to destroy the infant Scarborn city.

"Don't look at me like that Amory." He sounds slighted.

"Like what?" I ask as I draw my blade, practicing my forms against a dummy.

"Like you're better than I am. You're a wonderful swordsman, but you lack the courage to do what is required of great men." He nods towards my blade. "That's why she gave you a magical sword, she didn't have to worry about its impact, gave it to a man with no will to use its power."

"Lady [[Iseul]] is an honored Spirit and you should grant her far more respect." I sheath my weapon. "She decreed I would die in a war worth fighting, honorably."

"Then perhaps you will die on that field tomorrow, as you follow me into Ember-Flag. If not, you can leave, coward." Shaking his head as he snaps through a piece of bread.

"No." I say as I turn to leave. "I won't."

He laughs again, but this time its hollow, it's a laugh of effort, trying to convince himself he isn't unsure about his motive for attacking this town. "I don't need you Amory Tau!"

He was right. He stormed the city, burned it to the ground, and killed every Scarborn he could find. It was remarkably easy, almost as if there was no Warlord army to fight against. The only one he failed to catch was [[Crux]], his terrifying Scarborn Warlord adversary. What a shame.
