# The First Flame

The logs crackled before me, the wood floor cool while the head began to radiate through the foyer.

It was freezing outside, I remember mother holding me back as father made his way into the snow to check the horses. I needed no encouragement to take a seat by the fire and warm myself.

I stretch my hands out and feel its warmth. A loud snap echoes through the room as the largest log collapses into the growing flame. My sister flinches, darting backwards. She is little and scared, but the fire is nothing to fear.

I grab her hand gently, letting her feel the warmth. Nothing to fear little sister. Slowly I inch her hand towards the flame, we are too little to know better, to know pain and danger. Nothing to fear from the flame. 

The fire warms us, protects us, guides us. It lights our torches, and brings us safely through the night. Fire is life.

Before I realized what I was doing, mother has grabbed me, dashing me outside, shoving my hand into the snow. It is freezing, icy cold. Why did she do that? My hand is numb, red, it had been so warm just a second ago.

"You mad child? You've burned your hand terribly! Gods why did you do that?"

She seems to be mad to me. The fire would set her straight. I do not meet her eyes, I am focused on something else in the distance, glowing.

She slaps me across the face, but I do not respond. "What in Lumena's name are you looking at now boy?!" She screams, following my gaze.

The barn is ablaze, the whole town is, as a raider carries a torch and my father's limp body towards the cabin. My hand is freed from the snow, and the cold retreats, as the righteous warmth returns.

I scramble back inside to the fire as she screams. My sister is gone, shame. The fire would have liked her. A wet crunch fills the home and I hear a thud in the doorway.

I don't look. I focus on the fire, on the dancing licking flames, and on the twirling dragon that forms in its center, so warm, so inviting... so powerful. The fire calls me, and I answer.
