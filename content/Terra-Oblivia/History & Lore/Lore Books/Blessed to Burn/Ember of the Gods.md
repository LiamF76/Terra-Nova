# Ember of the Gods

Golden blood and black fire tore open the sky. My ancestor, the greatest of the Red Dragons, ascended into the heavens and ripped the old God of war asunder. They were weak and fickle Gods, unworthy of worship or the heavens they rested in. Now he is Rocan Ashar, fire and war.

The Dragons took divinity from them, killed them, and destroyed their memory. It is the natural order for the strong to devour the weak. Those who call my crusade heresy fail to understand nature, for it is natural the strong will seek to become stronger. I will bask in the power of the Stone King, and through it I will become a God.

Thus is the word of Kasayt, The Lord of Shadowed-Flame.
