# The Burning Throne

This is my sermon, my prayer and my promise.

They ask why we serve a dragon. I ask why you serve anything else. The fools in the valley pray to gods who never answer. We pray to one who burns. Power is not cruelty. It is honesty. The strong rule, and the wise kneel to the strongest.
