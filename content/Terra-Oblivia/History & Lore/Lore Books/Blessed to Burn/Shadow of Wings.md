# Shadow of Wings

There is no sun but the fire. There is no sky but the shadow beneath his wings. There is no God but the one I kneel before.

His body is perfect, smooth and glass, roiling within the orb like an untamed fragment of creation itself. The Abyssal Storms answer his call, and he burns hotter than any Rocan priest.

In the sunlight men hide from themselves. They use Lumena's bright eyes to shield themselves from the truth. I do not hide from the truth, and in the Shadow of Kasayt's wings I am free.

I am honest beneath his form. I am hungry for death, I thirst for violence, and I burn with ferocity. I am fire, and I am death.

My skin is gone, melted and warped. Carrying my Lord is a heavy burden, but power comes at a price. My price was faith in mind and flesh. I pay greatly, and am rewarded in kind.

Now fire is mine to command, and I breathe it forward as effortlessly as a Dragon. I can call the shadows to my side, and calcify them with my flame. My Aspects are fire and death in a smaller margin. The Black Riders of Kasayt, the Aspects of his champion.

I am renamed in his image, now I am the Acolyte, my old name, my family, past, burned away. I am nothing but his servant.
