This is an overview of Terra-Oblivia's history.

# Ancient History

- *Time of Reckoning*: Wars with the abyss and the world is dark and dangerous. 
- *Dawn of the Dragon Gods*: New Gods are born as the Terra-Oblivia and the Abyss are torn apart after having been fused together. The Old Gods die, and their ways are forgotten.
- *Last Great Kings*: The Last era of great Kings from empires of the past. Their magical power and martial authority fades as the world is in turmoil, their lands and monuments fall into ruin, leaving secrets and treasures to be found.

# Recent History

- *3 Fronts War*: In the protected valley of the Triarch, the 3 Cities were born. In a struggle for ruling power between the 3 young cities, a new order was born. In the wake of a many years long war between the 3 settlements, a council was formed to combat the impending cataclysm of Kasayt, the Red Dragon of Ashkarad. The leaders of the Triarch chose to work together to face a shared problem instead of fighting amongst themselves. This council still rules the Triarch to this day.
- *Kasayt's Storm*: A Red Dragon called Kasayt fused her fire with an Abyssal Storm, and led that storm towards the Triarch, hoping to destroy it and establish her new domain there. Through the allied leaders of the Triarch, combined with the magic of the Storm Seers, they diverted the storm. Kasayt was deterred by the efforts of a Great Spirit, a bird who took to the skies and fought Kasayt, but died, and their name was lost.

# Modern History

- *Ember-Flag Massacre*: Ember-Flag was a small settlement of Scarborn established under the rule of a weak Warlord who claimed to be a protector of the Scarborn people. It was obliterated by Lord Dastan and Bastion forces. Thousands of people died. Scarborn often refer to Dastan as 'The Butcher' or 'Flag-burner'. Many Scarborn refugees fled to the Triarch, where they face adversity. Dastan called it a preemptive strike to protect the Triarch.
- *Surge Wars*: Series of wars between the Triarch and Ashkarad. Young Blue Dragon children of the Ashkari Dragon, Lasini, and her Warlord minions invaded the Triarch. Luther Scorn rose as a hero and defeated a Blue Dragon with his forces, saving the valley.
