Once in the days before the shadows, there was a King. This king did not rule land and throne, but over magics and great moments all throughout the world. All things knew him, and he reflected through them.

The King had a Son, a Son who was alike him in all manners and none.

One day the Son decided he would leave the King, because he no longer wished for the tomorrow the King envisioned. The King hunted his son tirelessly for endless days, until they fought atop the King's castle beyond the stars.

The King admonished his Son's wayward hopes, and vowed that if he fell he would simply rise again. They fought, and the King lost, over and over the King lost to his Son.

The King fell a final time, and the Son built his own castle in the stars, hidden in darkness, waiting for the day the King would return, to stop his father once again. 

When the King returns, so too will the Son, under the light of a new star.
