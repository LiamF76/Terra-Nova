# The Triarch Council

The Triarch's Council is the government of the Triarch / 3 Cities nation. Each of the 3 cities nominates a member of their government to represent their city and territories interests on the Council.

The Council makes choices for the entire nation, so its members hold a great deal of power.

## Council Members

#### Bastion Councilor: Lord Dastan, General
#### [[Citadel]] Councilor: [[Rowan August]], Arch-Priest
#### [[Gate]] Councilor: Kifrag Beast-Bane, High Pilgrim
