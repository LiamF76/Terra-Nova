# The Cult of Kasayt

The Cult of Kasayt is a group of warlords and cultists that worship the [[Terra-Oblivia Dragons|Dragon]] [[Kasayt]] in Ashkarad. They are ancient enemies of [[The Triarch]]. The believe in strength through fire, and that anything that can be obtained must be obtained, and offered to their God Kasayt in exchange for favor and power.
