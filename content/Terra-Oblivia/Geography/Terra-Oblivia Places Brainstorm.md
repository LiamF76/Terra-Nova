## Nations

| *Nation*                                   | *Ideas*                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [[The Triarch]] (AKA: The 3 Cities)        | Collection of 3 cities that create a triangular 'safe' area. Territory outside that is lawless and deadly. (city names: Citadel, Bastion, & Gate). There are smaller villages in the territory as well that pay tribute to the Cities. Each city has its own government and elects a representative to a 3 person council that governs the overall region. |
| <br>[[The Rocan Empire]] (AKA: The Empire) | Violent fascist expansionist nation that permits fire magic. Strong themes of conquest and fire. Has a Phoenix Spirit they revere and worship, give their Magus Phoenix feathers to empower their fire magic. The Empire rejects Dragons, Dragonborn, and their worship, believing them to be false Gods.                                                  |
| [[Ashkarad]] (AKA: The Wastes)             | Lawless wasteland ruled by competing Warlords and Dragons. No central authority just a patchwork of disputed territories. Warlords serve or pay tribute to Dragons. Dragons and Warlords are in constant conflict with one another, never unify enough to war against other Nations.                                                                       |
| [[Liberos]] (AKA: The Free Cities)         | Collection off 2 cities along the western coast that are allies. Were once part of a larger nation conquered by the Empire, but rebelled and became free. (city names: Sovereign & Seer's-Star) They are port cities governed by trading companies.                                                                                                        |
