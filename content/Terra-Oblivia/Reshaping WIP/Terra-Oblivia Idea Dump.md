- The 3 Cities: collection of 3 cities that create a triangular 'safe' area. Territory outside that is lawless and deadly. (city names: Citadel, Bastion, & Gate)
- The Empire: Violent fascist expansionist nation that permits fire magic. Strong themes of conquest and fire. 
- Culture that respects necromancy, honoring dead / burial rites includes raising chosen people (great honor) and sending them into the world to fight monsters and protect the living.
