1. The world is fallen, dark fantasy, anyone and anything can die
2. Power is law, and dragons hold the greatest power amongst mortals.
3. Every situation holds hope despite the impending doom.
4. The Cosmic struggle of Khal seeking to end the world and Shao seeking to continue it drives conflict.
5. Rare magic is important magic. It is a sought out and valuable resource, not an abundant power source. The world is highly magical, but not as time traveling wizards or fireball casting randoms. Great mages are rare, and significant.
6. Ambiguous agents. Servants of Khal and Shao do not always know what they serve or which side, and that confusion is a playground for writing.
