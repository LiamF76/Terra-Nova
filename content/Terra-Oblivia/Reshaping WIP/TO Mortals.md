- Humans
	- Numerous and adaptable. Humans are the most common race. They are the least likely to be born with magic, even those born to magical bloodlines have a chance to be without it. 
- Dwarves
	- Short and stout, Dwarves live beneath the surface and within mountains. Dwarves have no magical bloodlines and cannot be born with magic. They have a unique magical growth property, they can weave gemstones into their hair in braids, and as their hair grows it will imbue mana into the gems, making them useable for artifice magic. These braid-stones are considered sacred and personal. Dwarves are the most talented at Rune Magics.
