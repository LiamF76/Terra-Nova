# Act 1

Act 1:
- Levels 1-5
- explore the Triarch
- dungeons
- uncover secrets of the Green Knights in relation to the Stone King
- **Dramatic Question**: Can the heroes find the location of the Stone King, and protect / destroy the Formless Tower before Sana reaches both and obtains the demonic power of the Stone King?

## BBEG Stuff

- **BBEG**: [[Sana Black-Sky]]
- **Additional Baddies**: [[Kasayt]], Hartwyn Syros
- *Goal*: 
	- Locate the Stone King in the Triarch, then find out how to unseal the demon within. 
	- Free their brother. The brother was a defector who tried to join another Dragon's warband, now held captive by the Cult.
- *Timeline*: Hartwyn has given Sana 2 years to locate the Stone King. 
- *What is stopping them*: 
	- Location of the Stone King has been lost, only recorded by an extinct Knightly Order called the Green Knights. Have to scour their ruins and relics for the location.
	- Ritual tied to the Stone King is forbidden, Abyssal magics. Lost to time. Have to get the ritual from a demonic tome called The Formless Tower. This book is kept somewhere secret I haven't decided yet.
- *What happens if they succeed*: 
	- Sana's brother will be set free, and returned to them unharmed.
	- Sana's wish is to be given the Triarch as spoils, to rule and do with as they please. Hartwyn will pledge the majority of Kasayt's forces to Sana's warband.
- *What happens if they fail*: If they does not locate it before that ends, Hartwyn will kill Sana's brother that the Cult holds captive. Sana will also be hunted down, and punished further.
- *What power do they carry*: 
	- Sana carries a fractured piece of the Stone King called 'The Bloodstone.' It holds demonic power, granting Sana strength, speed, blood magic, and a chronal shift. 
	- Sana can use this blood magic to mutate her followers, granting them hideous demonic forms with enhanced power.
	- This Bloodstone was given to them as a guide by Hartwyn. The Bloodstone leads its wielder towards the Stone King, as it wants to be reunited with its origin.
