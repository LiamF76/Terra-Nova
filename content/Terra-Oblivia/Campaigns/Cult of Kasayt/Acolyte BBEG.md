- **BBEG**: [[The Acolyte]] / [[Kasayt]]
- *Goal*: 
	- Make Kasayt a living God. Steps / parts required
		- Find the Stone King, need it's blood magic / demon soul to fuel Kasayt's ascension
		- Needs to find the demonic / abyssal ritual tome to learn how to make Kasayt a god (The Formless Tower)
		- Restore Kasayt's essence before the ritual, requires the killing of [[Astor]] to absorb the Great Spirit's essence into Kasayt's orb
- *Timeline*: As soon as possible. Every day Kasayt's essence weakens. The highest priority is preserving Kasayt, so killing Astor is PRIORITY 1! This must happen within 2 years because that is how long Kasayt has before he 'dies' for good.
- *What is stopping them*: 
	- Doesn't know where the Stone King is besides that it is somewhere in [[The Triarch]].
	- Doesn't know where the ritual (The Formless Tower) demonic tome is
	- Has to amass enough power to kill a Great Spirit
- *What happens if they succeed*: 
	- Kasayt becomes an unholy demonic dragon God. He would destroy much of the world before he could be stopped, if he could even be stopped.
- *What happens if they fail*: 
	* Kasayt stays in the orb, the cult fractures, and the Warlords turn on one another. 
	* If the ritual is interrupted late, Kasayt emerges half-made: a demon dragon with enormous power and none of the divine order, which is the impending monster the party has to face. 
	* The Stone King may be partly woken and left to the Triarch.
- *What power do they carry*: 
	- Power of Kasayt, bonded to the orb that holds Kasayt's essence, carries the shadowed flame and draconic magics from Kasayt. Can summon spirits of shadow and flame, cast fire magics, enhanced strength and speed
	- Rides a red wyvern that can breathe fire
