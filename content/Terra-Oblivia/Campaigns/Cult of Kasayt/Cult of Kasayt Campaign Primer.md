# The Cult of Kasayt

Welcome to Cult of Kasayt! The first campaign to be run in Terra-Oblivia. If you are reading this you're probably playing in it, and I hope you are as excited as I am!

If you haven't already please feel free to browse the Terra-Oblivia Wiki or DM Liam with more questions about our setting, but everything you need to 'create a character' should already be available. There will not be as much info about the entire world already available as you may be used to in the past with Terra-Nova. Apologies, I am still building this setting out and being 100% honest Terra-Nova was pretty half baked when you guys made your first characters too, so don't worry about it.

## Campaign Setting & Length

This campaign will begin with a 'revolver campaign', so please keep that in mind when creating your characters. If people enjoy the initial revolver campaign, I am very happy to continue this adventure. I am hoping for another multi-year adventure alongside my friends!

> [!note] Revolver Campaign
> A revolver campaign is a campaign that is usually 5-6 sessions long (the common ammo capacity of a revolver cylinder). They generally serve as starter campaigns or introductory campaigns, to help players get a feel for a game or setting.

This campaign will take place on the Continent of Escharra. Escharra is one of the oldest continents in Terra-Oblivia, having a long history dating back thousands of years past known history. Specifically we will be starting in the valley nation of [[The Triarch]]. 

The Triarch is a country protected from outside threats by a large mountain range around its boarders. It is a coalition of 3 Cities that protect, supply, and advise one another to survive in the deadly land of Escharra. This cooperation is necessary, as the Triarch is bordered on the East by the vast expansionist [[The Rocan Empire|Rocan Empire]], and on all other sides by the lawless land of [[Ashkarad]]. Ashkarad has no central government, in a constant state of war between Dragons and Warlord Cultists that worship and serve them, and they have come into conflict with the 3 Cities many times before.

Other lands such as Zarus and Drokon lie across the sea, but they will not be the focus of our campaign.

## Campaign Content & Expectations

As you may know, Terra-Nova and Terra-Oblivia are both creations of Daraan. You may feel a sense of deja-vu exploring this world. Follow that feeling, a large part of playing in this world (for those who have played in Terra-Nova), will be about understanding the new order of the world.

> [!note]
> How did we get here? Why is the world this way? What influence did we have on this? The are endless secrets, be bold and discover them all!

It will be encouraged to hunt for information. You will need to travel and *think carefully* about who you are, and what you are willing to do. Understanding will not be free, and all information comes at a price. This campaign is brutal. Character deaths are a real possibility, and the best way to prevent that is to *learn* and *explore*. You will not be able to defeat great threats without understanding them, this is not a button mash hit point battle, figure out what you're up against or you stand little change.

We will be having fun. We will be dungeon delving, exploring, solving mysteries and puzzles, talking to interesting NPCs, and dealing with some serious topics. We will have player safety tools such as consent sheets, none of your boundaries / consent will be breached.

The tone of this campaign will be nearly identical to Terra-Nova for player attitude. We can have jokes and fun, everybody's character does not have to be grumpy and moody all the time. The tone is more serious though, I enjoy a focused game, let's try and have the right energy for gameplay!

In terms of content specifics, there will be combat against monsters and mortals, RP between party members and NPCs, high stakes, and intense moments. General typical story driven D&D stuff, be prepared for a more RP focused campaign, and when combat arises it will be dangerous. This world wants you dead, fight with everything you have.

## Our Quest

Here is a very brief overview of our quest. This is brief on purpose, you will learn more as you build characters, ask questions, explore the Wiki, and adventure through the world!

> [!note] The Cult of Kasayt
> Long ago, in the infancy of the 3 Cities, the Red Dragon Kasayt attacked the peaceful valley. Using his terrible fire magic, Kasayt fused his breath with an Abyssal Storm, creating a hellish typhoon of demonic fire, and led that typhoon to the Valleys edge. There Kasayt was halted by the great bird Spirit and protector of the Valley, Astor. Kasayt and Astor battled, and both were greatly weakened, but Astor diverted the Storm from the Valley's edge, and saved the people of the Triarch.
> 
> That was over a hundred years ago, and the Spirit and Dragon who battled for the fate of the 3 Cities have faded, but are slowly returning. Scouts of the Triarch who make their way into Ashkarad have heard rumor that the fallen Cult of Kasayt, fanatical worshippers of the Red Dragon, carry the essence of their master. For reasons unknown, they are amassing a great army once again, and are making their way towards the Valley of the 3 Cities. Now the Triarch must once again face their ancient enemy, and defend their Valley against the violent Ashkari barbarians, who bathe in blood across their vast wastelands.

Your quest begins in the Triarch. You will travel the continent of Escharra, seeking to understand your enemy, why they are moving on the 3 Cities, and how Kasayt survived the battle against Astor.

## Party Roles

For this campaign we will be trying something new. Party roles inspired by the runbooks from Powered by the Apocalypse. Each player will be required to select one of these to build their character and backstory around. Multiple players cannot select the same role. This will also factor in the 'Knives' backstory system for character creation. Both will be outlined below.

---

### Backstory Knives

Backstory knives, in my own words, are unresolved pieces of your character's life that can be used in the future. There is a clear outline available [here](https://www.reddit.com/r/DnD/comments/775caq/my_friends_and_i_have_something_called_knife/) in text, and [here](https://youtu.be/jWtDBHYl06E) in video format. Please use this to develop your character's backstory, and maintain it / send it to me somehow. This can be done in a google doc, a discord dm, or any other form of shareable text.

For reference, I feel 7 knives is best, if you have more that's ok, just understand I may not get to use all of them. Also, if you have less that's ok, just understand your character may not be as involved with their backstory if I have less to work on.

I save these backstories and knives when you tell me they are finished in my Obsidian vault, and I reference them frequently to develop sessions and the story. If you make updates or changes, let me know. if you need help making updates or changes, talk to me! We can do it together.

Additionally, as a REQUIREMENT for character creation, I need your character to have a goal. What do they want? Examples:
- Revenge
- Redemption
- Power
- Validation for a mistake
- Justice for being framed

What is this character chasing? Do they want treasure, to kill a former mentor? This should tie into your knives, give me threads to pull on.

### The Risen Dead

> [!quote] Ayla Syros, High Priestess of Krokha Inam - 
> *"My chosen, you passed on to the heavens. Krokha Inam has deigned through my blessed hand that your purpose on this world has not ended. Rise, my anointed champion, the shadows of the world grow long, and I require your service once again. You may rest when the world is saved."*

**Description**: In a long abandoned tradition, you have been raised from the dead in service to the High Priestess of Krokha Inam. You are the champion of Ayla, and have been sent out into Triarch territory to enforce her will and protect the land. Ayla has trusted you to act bravely, justly, and honorably, will you honor that, or take this second chance at life into your own hands?

**Questions to Answer**: Who were you in life? How did you die? Your memory is fragmented, how can you get it back? What powers does your undead form give you, and an you return to life again? 
When playing the Risen Dead, ask yourself what your motive will be, and how that will change as you begin to recover who you were.

**Boons**: 
- Undead, you cannot die permanently unless Ayla is killed.

**Bonds**: 
- You are tied to Ayla Syros. If she dies, you die. 
- If Ayla commands you, you are compelled to obey. To disobey may incur a toll upon your being.

---

### The Triarch's Chosen

> [!quote] Lord Dastan of Bastion- 
> *"By the Authority of the Council of the Triarch, you are now appointed as Chosen of the 3 Cities. Go forth into the Valley and beyond, protect this land, and adhere to the Council's will. You are the hand of the Lords, be strong, and be fearless."*

**Description**: You have been chosen by the Council of the 3 Cities to represent their interests as an agent of the Triarch. You have been given a small amount of authority similar to a Noble, able to get away with small infractions in the name of protecting the Valley.

**Questions to Answer**: Which member of the Triarch's Council supports you most? What deeds did you accomplish to become chosen? Were you a hero of a battle, saved lives, or so respected by people that they appointed you to help? How do you plan to use your new position?

**Boons**: 
- Council's Authority. You carry the insignia of the Triarch on an item of your choosing. It grants you the ability to sway and influence others who answer to the Council.

**Bonds**: 
- You are beholden to the Council, and must report your actions to them when asked. Council members may disagree on what you should do.

---

### The Made 

> [!quote] You - 
> *"I was made for something even I do not understand, but I know the answer is out there, waiting for me."*

**Description**: You are a Construct, found dormant or newly awakened with no clear purpose. You have no need to eat, sleep, or fear time. Some people see a person, others see property.

**Questions to Answer**: Where were you found? What do you remember? Do you want to know your purpose, or are you afraid of it? How does your purpose and function show in your design?

**Boons**: 
- Tireless. You don't need to eat, sleep, or breathe, and you are hard to wound.

**Bonds**: 
- You carry a dormant directive. The GM decides when it awakens.
- Some people will try to claim, sell, or dismantle you.

---

### The Hunted

> [!quote] You - 
> *"The people of this world despise what I am. Let them fear, let them hate, it is because they do not understand how great I can be."*

**Description**: You are Scarborn, or have Abyssal power. Across Escharra, people like you are arrested or killed, except in the Rocan Empire, which has begun offering a place to those who serve. 

**Questions to Answer**: Who knows what you are? What have you done to hide it? Has the Empire contacted you? What would you give for a place that accepts you?

**Boons**: 
- Abyssal Sight. You resist Abyssal effects and sense things touched by the Abyss.

**Bonds**: 
- If you display a dangerous nature, Triarch law will hunt you. 
- The Empire has made you an offer, and it's a temptation.

---

### Spirit-Touched

> [!quote] Your Bonded Spirit - 
> *"I grant you my power, my blessing, and my mission. Tie yourself to my purpose, and I will leash my abilities to you. Go forth, with my divinity, and make your will manifest with my magic."*

**Description**: You are bonded to a [[Terra-Oblivia/Magic/Spirits|Spirit]] of Terra-Oblivia. It grants you magic in exchange for your service to it. You may choose the Spirit from those who already exist in the world, or work with the GM to create one. You may have been a worshipper of this Spirit, a pilgrim to their shrine, or a child whose parents asked for their blessing.

**Questions to Answer**: When did the Spirit first reach you? What can its gift do? What does it want from you? Does the Spirit travel alongside you, or does it travel on its own journey?

**Boons**: 
- Spirit's Spark. You sense when it is in danger, and when other Spirits are near.
- Blessed. You are respected and revered by those who worship your Spirit's Divine parent, akin to a Disciple of the Heavens.

**Bonds**: 
- As the Spirit weakens, so do you. If it dies, the gift dies with them. 
- You feel it whenever the Spirit uses its power. 

---

### The Cult Defector

> [!quote] You - 
> *"I Knelt before Kasayt every dawn. I can hear the lies on the Priest's tongue, there were no gifts awaiting his followers, only death... and Kasayt's fire."*

**Description**: You were raised or recruited into Kasayt's cult in Ashkarad, and you got out. You know the symbols, the rites, and some of the faces, and the cult remembers you too.

**Questions to Answer**: Why did you join? Why did you leave? Who did you leave behind? Did you know the Priest? Do you still believe, even a little?

**Boons**: 
- Inside Knowledge. You recognize cult signs, rituals, and ranks, and you can pass among cultists for a short time.

**Bonds**: 
- The cult hunts you as a heretic. 
- Someone still in the cult knows you, and you owe them a great debt. They may call you back or call in a favor.

---

### Ruin Delver

> [!quote] You - 
> *"The dead don't need riches, they just left them there for us to take. What's the harm? Besides, there's more than pretty jewels down there... the ruins are where the old world left its secrets."*

**Description**: You are an adventurer, you have explored deep caves and ruins, high hills, and tall forests. While exploring, you learned of a treasure just beyond your reach, either due to money, distance, or some other factor. Now you work to earn your way to that treasure, no matter how far it runs.

**Questions to Answer**: What was your first big find? What did you see that you can't explain? What treasure do you most greatly desire?

**Boons**: 
- Delver's Eye. You can read ruins for traps, hidden doors, and old inscriptions.

**Bonds**: 
- You are obsessed with a specific treasure from your past, and will put yourself or others in harms way to obtain it.

---

### The Pilgrim Knight

> [!quote] You - 
> *"I kneel to no lord and no city. My oath is to the road, and to any soul who needs a sword when no one else will lift one."*

**Description**: You are a free knight of the Pilgrim Knights, sworn to no noble family and no city. You travel the valley and beyond, carrying a knight's code without a lord's banner. Some see you as a hero, others as a sword for hire in better armor. You may have given up a noble house, been born into the road, or earned your spurs by serving the people the great houses ignore.

**Questions to Answer**: Why did you take the pilgrim's road? Who, if anyone, did you leave behind? What oath do you hold to above all others? What deed made you known, and who remembers it? Which lord or city would most like to claim you?

**Boons**: 
- Right of the Road. Common folk, innkeepers, and many lords will offer you shelter and a fair hearing on the strength of your knightly standing.

**Bonds**: 
- You hold to a code of aid. When someone in real need asks for your help, you are compelled to answer, and breaking that code costs you your standing. 
- You have no house or city to protect or avenge you. Powerful people may try to recruit you or remove you.

---

### The Noble Descendant

> [!quote] The Head of Your House - 
> *"You carry more than a name. You carry everything we were, and everything we owe. Do not let it be buried with us."*

**Description**: You are born of a noble house with a long history in Terra-Oblivia. Your family name opens doors, and your bloodline may carry the old magic hoarded by great houses. You are away from the family seat, whether by choice, duty, exile, or secret purpose, and your house still expects something of you. You decide whether your house is thriving, fallen, or hiding something.

**Questions to Answer**: Which house are you from, and where does it stand now? Does your bloodline carry magic, and what does it draw on? Why are you away from home? What does your family want from you? What did your house do that its enemies have not forgotten?

**Boons**: 
- Name and Blood. Your family name carries weight with nobles, officials, and those who owe your house. If your bloodline carries magic, you can draw on a small measure of it.

**Bonds**: 
- Your house can call on you for a favor, a marriage, or a service, and refusing may cost you your family. 
- A rival house, an old debt, or an old feud follows your name, and enemies of your house may treat you as its heir.
