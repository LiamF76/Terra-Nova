Welcome to Cult of Kasayt! The first campaign to be run in Terra-Oblivia. If you are reading this you're probably playing in it, and I hope you are as excited as I am!

If you haven't already please feel free to browse the Terra-Oblivia Wiki or DM Liam with more questions about our setting, but everything you need to 'create a character' should already be available. There will not be as much info about the entire world already available as you may be used to in the past with Terra-Nova. Apologies, I am still building this setting out and being 100% honest Terra-Nova was pretty half baked when you guys made your first characters too, so don't worry about it.

## Campaign Setting & Length

This campaign will begin with a 'revolver campaign', so please keep that in mind when creating your characters. If people enjoy the initial revolver campaign, I am very happy to continue this adventure. I am hoping for another multi-year adventure alongside my friends!

> [!note] Revolver Campaign
> A revolver campaign is a campaign that is usually 5-6 sessions long (the common ammo capacity of a revolver cylinder). They generally serve as starter campaigns or introductory campaigns, to help players get a feel for a game or setting.

This campaign will take place on the Continent of Escharra. Escharra is one of the oldest continents in Terra-Oblivia, having a long history dating back thousands of years past known history. Specifically we will be starting in the valley nation of [[The Triarch]]. 

The Triarch is a country protected from outside threats by a large mountain range around its boarders. It is a coalition of 3 Cities that protect, supply, and advise one another to survive in the deadly land of Escharra. This cooperation is necessary, as the Triarch is bordered on the East by the vast expansionist [[The Rocan Empire|Rocan Empire]], and on all other sides by the lawless land of [[Ashkarad]]. Ashkarad has no central government, in a constant state of war between Dragons and Warlord Cultists that worship and serve them, and they have come into conflict with the 3 Cities many times before.

Other lands such as Zarus and Drokon lie across the sea, but they will not be the focus of our campaign.

## Campaign Content & Expectations

As you may know, Terra-Nova and Terra-Oblivia are both creations of Daraan. You may feel a sense of deja-vu exploring this world. Follow that feeling, a large part of playing in this world (for those who have played in Terra-Nova), will be about understanding the new order of the world.

> [!note]
> How did we get here? Why is the world this way? What influence did we have on this? The are endless secrets, be bold and discover them all!

It will be encouraged to hunt for information. You will need to travel and *think carefully* about who you are, and what you are willing to do. Understanding will not be free, and all information comes at a price. This campaign is brutal. Character deaths are a real possibility, and the best way to prevent that is to *learn* and *explore*. You will not be able to defeat great threats without understanding them, this is not a button mash hit point battle, figure out what you're up against or you stand little change.

We will be having fun. We will be dungeon delving, exploring, solving mysteries and puzzles, talking to interesting NPCs, and dealing with some serious topics. We will have player safety tools such as consent sheets, none of your boundaries / consent will be breached.

The tone of this campaign will be nearly identical to Terra-Nova for player attitude. We can have jokes and fun, everybody's character does not have to be grumpy and moody all the time. The tone is more serious though, I enjoy a focused game, let's try and have the right energy for gameplay!

In terms of content specifics, there will be combat against monsters and mortals, RP between party members and NPCs, high stakes, and intense moments. General typical story driven D&D stuff, be prepared for a more RP focused campaign, and when combat arises it will be dangerous. This world wants you dead, fight with everything you have.

## Party Roles

For this campaign we will be trying something new. Party roles inspired by the runbooks from Powered by the Apocalypse. Each player will be required to select one of these to build their character and backstory around. Multiple players cannot select the same role. This will also factor in the 'Knives' backstory system for character creation. Both will be outlined below.

> [!note] On Party Roles
> These roles are not classes or jobs, they are just predetermined story hook starters, meant to help you ceate characters connected to the world, and better suited to the story & setting.

### The Risen Dead

> [!quote] Ayla Syros, High Priestess of Krokha Inam - 
> *"My chosen, you passed on to the heavens. Krokha Inam has deigned through my blessed hand that your purpose on this world has not ended. Rise, my anointed champion, the shadows of the world grow long, and I require your service once again. You may rest when the world is saved."*

**Description**: In a long abandoned tradition, you have been raised from the dead in service to the High Priestess of Krokha Inam. You are the champion of Ayla, and have been sent out into Triarch territory to enforce her will and protect the land. Ayla has trusted you to act bravely, justly, and honorably, will you honor that, or take this second chance at life into your own hands?

**Questions to Answer**: Who were you in life? How did you die? Your memory is fragmented, how can you get it back? What powers does your undead form give you, and an you return to life again? 
When playing the Risen Dead, ask yourself what your motive will be, and how that will change as you begin to recover who you were.

**Boons**: 
- Undead, you cannot die permanently unless Ayla is killed.

**Bonds**: 
- You are tied to Ayla Syros. If she dies, you die. 
- If Ayla commands you, you are compelled to obey. To disobey may incur a toll upon your being.

### The Triarch's Chosen

> [!quote] Source - 
> *"Quote"*

**Description**: You have been chosen by the Council of the 3 Cities to represent their interests as an agent of the Triarch. You have been given a small amount of authority similar to a Noble, able to get away with small infractions in the name of protecting the Valley.

**Questions to Answer**: Which member of the Triarch's Council supports you most? What deeds did you accomplish to become chosen? Were you a hero of a battle, saved lives, or so respected by people that they appointed you to help? How do you plan to use your new position?

**Boons**: 
- Council's Authority. You carry the insignia of the Triarch on an item of your choosing. It grants you the ability to sway and influence others who answer to the Council.

**Bonds**: 
- You are beholden to the Council, and must report your actions to them when asked. Council members may disagree on what you should do.

### The Made 

> [!quote] You - 
> *"I was made for something even I do not understand, but I know the answer is out there, waiting for me."*

**Description**: You are a Construct, found dormant or newly awakened with no clear purpose. You have no need to eat, sleep, or fear time. Some people see a person, others see property.

**Questions to Answer**: Where were you found? What do you remember? Do you want to know your purpose, or are you afraid of it? How does your purpose and function show in your design?

**Boons**: 
- Tireless. You don't need to eat, sleep, or breathe, and you are hard to wound.

**Bonds**: 
-  You carry a dormant directive. The GM decides when it awakens.
- Some people will try to claim, sell, or dismantle you.

### The Hunted

> [!quote] You - 
> *"The people of this world despise what I am. Let them fear, let them hate, it is because they do not understand how great I can be."*

**Description**: You are Scarborn, or have Abyssal power. Across Escharra, people like you are arrested or killed, except in the Rocan Empire, which has begun offering a place to those who serve. 

**Questions to Answer**: Who knows what you are? What have you done to hide it? Has the Empire contacted you? What would you give for a place that accepts you?

**Boons**: 
- Abyssal Sight. You resist Abyssal effects and sense things touched by the Abyss.

**Bonds**: 
- If you display a dangerous nature, Triarch law will hunt you. 
- The Empire has made you an offer, and it's a temptation.
