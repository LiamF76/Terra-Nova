# Dungeon and Quest Ideas

- Metal men have appeared out of the ancient castle, they are chopping down the forest and bringing the lumber back to the ruins. Nobody knows why.
- An aging wizard wishes to give back to the community and start his own wizarding school. He urgently needs his first spell book back, but he sold it some years back while traveling the land. Return it and he'll pay handsomely.
- A Lich who lived for a thousand years has finally decided he wants to die, but he can't remember where his left his phylactery.
- Party has to be tested against a powerful forest spirit (leaf tree green knight thing) in order to be allowed to adventure north or into a dangerous land.
- A deadly necromantic virus is spreading across the land. It kills people within days and immediately revives them as undead. They are violent and dangerous, the Necromancer who started the virus needs to be stopped.
- A gigantic earth elemental mountain has awakened and is migrating, the wizard who awoke it resides in a dungeon on the top of the mountain. He must be defeated to stop the elemental before it crushes a settlement, and it must be done somewhere safe foe the mountain to land.
- A land is the only place a specific kind of magic crystal grows, and a caravan is traveling there to obtain and sell them, they offer to split profit and pay the party to accompany and protect them.
- flying Island dungeon that has to be summoned from the sky using an obelisk/ritual/lost spell
- tiny dungeon built in the tower of a mad wizard, forces the players to enter it shrinking them with abyssal magics. They have to fight their way through to get out. Once done they are enlarged again and can fight the wizard or forgive him cause he's cool
- Underground ruins/caves that have been taken over by nature. Holds unique creatures to the massive underground ecosystem. Mana Crystal Lizards that live in the caves and eat regular crystals, producing mana crystals on their backs. Mana Eater Bats that attack the Lizards and eat the crystals on their backs. Null Wyrms that nullify magic to eat Lizards and Bats.
- Town is under attack by little insect hive that has become sentient/intelligent. Party is hired to shrink down and destroy it so that the insects stop destroying the humanoids town.
- Living forest that has grown and overtaken a town. Party is hired to retrieve heirloom from noble family's home inside the walls of ivy and vines. Town was consumed by a corrupted Wyrmwood Tree that was convinced it needed a horde (like a real dragon), set up plot hook for whatever messed up the tree.
- Forgotten Prince's tomb, a test built by a long forgotten king as a test for heroes. Whoever could reach the grave of the King's son will receive a Kingdom's treasure and power. Traps and tests of character, monsters and shit left down there that can survive hundreds of years (which is what? golems and stuff probably)
- Ancient Wizard/King library. Constructed as a hoard for knowledge and is guarded by eternal minions. Possibly taken over by a dragon? Loot includes spells and magic.
- Elemental Plane break caused by amateur wizard. Rooms are in different elemental planes.
- Giant treehouse built by ancient Aelarian tribe. Now inhabited by monsters.
- Ancient castle haunted by animated armors, once enchanted by the court mage to guard it for eternity. Boss is an Iron Queen Armor
