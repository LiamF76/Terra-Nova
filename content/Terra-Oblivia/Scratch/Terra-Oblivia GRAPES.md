# G.R.A.P.E.S. World-Building Checklist

> Six aspects of every civilization: Geography, Religion, Achievements, Politics, Economics, Social Structure. Use this when your world doesn't feel quite "full enough."

---

## Geography

_How does the region and climate affect the characters in your story?_

- [ ] Primary exports and imports
- [ ] What natural resources do the people have at their disposal?
- [ ] How does the society's placement affect their chances of survival?

---

## Religion

_What do your characters believe in?_

- [ ] Names of God/Gods/Goddesses
- [ ] Worship practices
- [ ] Myths and legends
- [ ] Rites of passage
- [ ] Primary tenets and ideals
- [ ] How do religions clash? Do they clash?

---

## Achievements

_What has this civilization created that has withstood the test of time?_

- [ ] Works of art
- [ ] Literature
- [ ] Agricultural stability or other resource allocation
- [ ] How are these achievements honored/maintained?

---

## Politics

_Who controls the society and how do they control it?_

- [ ] System of government (Socialist, Democratic, Fascist, etc.)
- [ ] How are laws created?
- [ ] How are laws enforced?
- [ ] How is power exchanged?
- [ ] Who is in power?

---

## Economics

_Resources and money._

- [ ] What are the most valuable resources?
- [ ] How are those resources bought and sold? (Barter, coin, paper, etc.)
- [ ] How "wealthy" is this society compared to others?

---

## Social Structure

- [ ] Who are the most "important" people?
- [ ] Who are the least important people?
- [ ] How do these classes live together?

---

## Using G.R.A.P.E.S.

- Take one item above and use it as a writing prompt
- Work methodically through the list as a reference while writing
- Keep the items in the back of your head and look for chances at spontaneous world-building
