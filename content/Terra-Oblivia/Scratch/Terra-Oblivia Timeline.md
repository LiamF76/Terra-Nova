## The Shapeless Era

- **Years Unknown - 0**: History is fractured and unwritten.The Old Gods rule, the Abyss invades Terra-Oblivia, and chaos rules. Millions die and live, and the world is slowly poisoned and dying.

## The Dragon's Age

- **Year 0**, *Dawn of the New Gods*: The Old Gods are forgotten, and the New Gods are born. It is this way andhas always been, and the people know their Gods as they did before. The new order is established, and Terra-Oblivia is named for the Oblivion of the Old, and the birth of the new. The world's Abyssal wounds are closed but never fully cured, and its stain remains. Abyssal storms ravage the land, warring kingdoms destroy everything in their path, and order fights to be established. Millions die in the following years as Kingdoms and Religions collapse and are rebuilt with new visions.
- **Year 20**, *Atros Reigns*: The Ancient Red Dragon Atros establishes dominion over the wasteland of Ashkarad, naming it for himself and his Dragon Kin. Ashkarad becomes violent and hostile to all mortal life as monsters flock to it for treasure and food.
