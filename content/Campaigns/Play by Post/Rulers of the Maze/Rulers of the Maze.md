# The Game

Just outlining my ideas, what will happen, detailed transcript is in the discord channel, as that's where we play.

---

Audrey awakens on the ship, a slave ship, having been on the boat for two days. She had left a city called Deggon, from the coast of Southern Xerathos. All the slave are hungry, and scraps of stale bread are thrown to eat. Troughs of water are desalinated by a mage, and given to drink

One person is nearby, a man, who doesn't eat the bread, he does drink the water though. He thinks they lace the bread with chemicals, not surprising given where they are going. Iramo. The land where slaves go to suffer, not die, they don't let you die.

**TIMESKIP MESSAGE**: The journey to Iramo is violent and dangerous. The ship takes on water, braves storms, and runs low on supplies. By your  estimate, of the 500 who boarded in Xerathos, at least 150 have died, from sickness, starvation, or being thrown overboard, as they were no longer useful or worth selling.

Sheen continued to antagonize you along the journey, often spitting in your food, and sometimes at you, though it ceases the moment he draws the eye of Kazeer (pictured below), the slaver who owns the ships, and you. 

You begin training with the slavers on deck, Kazeer sanctions you to work with practice swords, wooden sparring staves, instead of the real thing. If you want to fetch your value you must stay in shape.

Do you willingly maintain your abilities?

One morning, the air smells different, you can smell smoke, the braziers on the deck are lit. You know this means you are close to Iramo. What do you feel Charlotte, as you approach the land where slaves go to die? Where gods abandon you, and only the Maze remains?

Arrives at Iramo and the slaves are forced to their knees before a shrine to the Queen. The shrine is an 80ft tall faceless statue of a nondescript woman with a massive green eye painted on each of her breasts. The Maze stands before you as you kneel on the stony shore of Iramo, it is at least 300ft tall,  it is covered at the entrance before you, daunting darkness awaits inside its walls.

Each slave is blessed with a cross of golden powder on their foreheads. This is when Audrey meets [[Delfine]], the Priestess. They take a liking to one another, she senses Delfine's strength and whispers to meet her at the temple during the night. "If you survive the passage, I shall see the Queen's blessing on your fingers."

As they are being blessed, the Wyvern is unloaded from the ship, eats two slavers and 10 slaves. [[Kael]] arrives, strange skeleton man, radiates an aura of violence. He tames his new mount, and rides it into Iramo, disappearing into the Maze.
