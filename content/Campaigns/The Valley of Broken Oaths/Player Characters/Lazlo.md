Lazlo was left on the doorstep of a church where he was trained in both holy arts as well as warfare turning him into a paladin and acolyte of (Aldred? the primary god of the valley). After embarrassing the head of his division in a duel he was sent off to the valley of Westwood. Lazlo does not know his parents or cares to meet them. He, by virtue, does not know his last name. He only cares to achieve glory to redeem himself and earn himself a proper station. While he does not hate Westwood he seeks a higher status position and think he needs to prove himself to get it. Lazlo has two holy symbols, one public he displays around his wrist and the other private that nearly nobody has seen.
