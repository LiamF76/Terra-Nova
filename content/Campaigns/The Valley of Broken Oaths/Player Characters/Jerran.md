
## Backstory

Gavin went to clear out an encampment of kobolds that had been causing trouble expecting an easy mission when he encountered a Carrion Crawler. In a difficult battle he slayed the monster but had sustained serious injuries. His druidic magic kept him stable on the trek back to Starnlow, but going so long without proper treatment allowed the crawler's poison to fester. With the help of Starnlows Priest he would ultimately survive the encounter, but was left paralyzed for nearly a week. A few days after Gavin’s return, the surviving Kobolds rallied their remaining numbers for a counter strike, expecting the Knight to have been mortally wounded and the town of Starnlow to be defenseless. They came late in the evening, battering the gates with their crude weapons and tools while the village's militia attempted to pick off whatever warriors they could with their arrows. Knowing they would not fend them off for long, Jerran ran to the church infirmary to try and wake Gavin. He shook him, pleaded and begged him to come save them the way he always does, but to no avail as he lay there slack and clammy on the cot. From the cracked door Jerran could hear the shouts from the gate, desperate cries to gather the townsfolk to the church and retreat before the gate collapsed. A sickening crack of splintering wood, a clash of steel, draconic cackling followed by shrieks of terror. Jerran was young, slight, and afraid, but he found himself donning Gavin's heavy armor. The chainmail draped around his trembling frame almost seemed to rattle as he set the helmet upon his head, picked up his sword, and stumbled out of the infirmary. Both the monsters and civilians halted as he emerged, waiting for the knight to act. Jerran wanted to speak, but as he opened his mouth no words came forth. Maybe it was fear, or unfamiliar weight of the plate mail he was wearing, or perhaps just how awkward he felt holding this sword in both hands tight against his chest. It felt so unnaturally heavy and foreign to him. If he couldn't speak, maybe he could send the right message with just the blade. As Jerran drew the blade and discarded its scabbard and felt a wave of warmth wash over him. The sword's weight felt perfect in his hand, the armor unburdening. He noticed a sort of low murmur at the edges of his perception, as if someone meant to speak to him without demanding his attention. They were whispers of kindness. Confidence. Inspiration. Courage. The whispers had served their purpose and left as quickly as they had arrived, fading to a low hum. Before, Jerran knew he could not face the monsters alone. Now, he knew he was not alone. He raised the blade into the air, drew in breath, and exhaled impetus. A single word escaped his lips. "Rally." Every townsfolk in earshot was overcome with a surge of strength and assurance that purged their fear. The fighting spirit of Starnlow convened into a tidal wave, crashing against the monsters in the form of pitchforks, lumber axes, fire pokers, and smithy hammers. The people of Starnlow suffered no casualties, and the kobold forces were eradicated. After the battle, Jerran crumpled into a heap of armor. The sword had stopped speaking and the burst of strength had left, but the courage had not. Hearing about the events of the battle after his recovery, Gavin would begin training Jerran as his apprentice.
