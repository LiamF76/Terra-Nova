## Overview

The Territory of Vesponous is hostile to life itself. Warped by hundreds of years being influenced by the Ancient Dragon, it has become alien to the natural inhabitants of what was once the Goldgrove.
The territory consists of swamps, forests, jungles, lowlands, and a few scattered mountains.
