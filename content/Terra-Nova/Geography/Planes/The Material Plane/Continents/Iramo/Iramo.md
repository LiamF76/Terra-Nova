
This document covers the Continent and Nation of Iramo, as the nations therein are all part of one system.

Iramo is a strange and alien land, where powerful beings rule as kings and queens of slave nations. Demons, Eldritch monsters, and Fey force hosts of servants to build shrines in their honor and scour the maze of Iramo for artifacts and ancient magics.

Iramo was built by the Ancients (Aelarians), a race of elves who possessed the greatest and most terrible magics. They constructed the maze of Iramo to hold their darkest secrets, powers, and monsters. Some say Iramo is a prison, built to hold those who carry greed in their hearts, trapping them in the maze to search hopelessly until they die. 

# **Iramo, The Land of Mazes**

## **1. Core Identity**

- **Nation Name:** Iramo
- **Nicknames / Epithets:** The Maze, The Land of Mazes, The Land of Greed
- **One‑Sentence Summary:** A continent covered by an ancient magical maze built by a lost civilization where powerful rulers force slaves to search for its secrets.
- **What Makes It Unique:** The entire continent is a massive maze. Cities have been built within the many miles high walls, people scour it for its endless secrets. Every mortal citizen of Iramo is a slave to their master, and foreigners who come there often end up as slaves, bewitched by the power of the maze, desperate to scour it until death for its vile secrets.
