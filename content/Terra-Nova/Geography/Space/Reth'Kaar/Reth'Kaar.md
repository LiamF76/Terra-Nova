
## Campaign Title: _Ashes of the Forgotten Star_

### Premise

The players begin on a dying desert world called **Reth'Kaar**, where ancient ruins lie buried beneath sandstorms, water is more precious than gold, and warlords rule from city-fortresses. Religion is rare, and gods are whispers of a past age. Magic is feared, its use regulated or forbidden by the powerful.

But the deeper the party explores the ruins, the more they learn: **this is not a distant world**, but **a forgotten shard of Terra-Nova’s cosmos**—a planet sealed off after a divine catastrophe.

---

## Setting Details

- **Environment**: The world is sun-scorched, with vast salt plains, glass dunes, sunken cities, and buried starcraft. Sandstorms charged with arcane energy sweep across the wastes. The sun here burns red and slow, and the nights are filled with aurora-like skies.
- **Magic**: Magic is rare, dangerous, and deeply mistrusted. Casters draw from “echoes” of an ancient mana source now dormant or poisoned.
- **Technology**: Mix of primitive and scavenged arcanotech. Think rusted skyships, relic plasma-lances, and energy cells only found in ancient ruins.
- **Society**: Tribes, merchant guilds, and warlords fight over access to water, power, and ancient ruins. Civilization clings to a few oasis-cities built atop the ruins of fallen empires.

---

## The Three Gods of Zhaar'Reth

Reth'Kaar has one deity, **Reth**, that bears three unique faces, remnants of a greater pantheon that once guided it:

1. **Aen'thal, the Face of Fire** (God of Survival and Sun)
    - Symbol: A cracked sun disk
    - Tenets: Endure. Adapt. Protect the flame of life.
    - Worship: Through survival rituals and flame-keeping.
    - Secret: Aen’thal was once an _aspect of Illuman_ from Terra-Nova, split during the divine war.

2. **Thressai, Face of the Sandmother** (Goddess of Death and Change)
    - Symbol: A shifting spiral
    - Tenets: All returns to dust. Accept change.
    - Worship: Rare, often done by mystics or nomads. Her priests wander the wastes, bringing peaceful deaths.
    - Secret: She was once a moon spirit exiled from Terra-Nova after refusing to choose a side in the war of gods.

3. **Kzorr, the Face of the Hollow Star** (God of Knowledge and Silence)
    - Symbol: A black void inside a star
    - Tenets: Knowledge is power. Power must be hidden.
    - Worship: Via hidden texts, meditation, and riddles.
    - Secret: Kzorr is actually a **Prime Creation**, now corrupted and banished to this world to keep dangerous cosmic knowledge buried.

## The Big Reveal

### Clues Throughout the Campaign

- Glyphs and ruins use **ancient Terra-Novan script**.
- A crashed ship mentions a planet called “Akash” or “Terranova Prime.”
- Items or artifacts reference **familiar divine or arcane names** the players recognize.
- A powerful artifact (or NPC) refers to “the Sundering of the Celestial Gate” as the reason Reth'Kaar is isolated.

### The Twist

The climax of the campaign involves:

- **Finding a divine vault**, sealed away to prevent the spread of corrupted god-magic.
- The vault’s gate bears the **symbol of the gods of Terra-Nova**, revealing the truth: Reth'Kaar is a **sealed twin planet**, a terraformed world once meant as a divine experiment.
- Perhaps the gods used Reth'Kaar as a **testing ground for divine ascension**, and when it failed, they erased it from history.

---

## Factions and Threats

- **The Cinderclads**: Warlords with scavenged divine armor, worshipping Aen’thal through strength.
- **The Echo Cult**: Madmen who tap into magical echoes, often causing mutation or insanity.
- **The Dust Courts**: Merchant empires that control water, tech, and transportation.
- **The Star-Silent**: Secret archivists of Kzorr who know of Terra-Nova and want to reconnect... or destroy it.
