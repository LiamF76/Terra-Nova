Also commonly referred to as Ascended Vampires, Elder Vampires are a subsection of regular Vampires, and they are only produced by very specific means. While anyone can become a vampire, you must be born from an Elder Vampire to become one.
Elders are essentially just regular vampires with some exceptions:
- Enhanced senses
- Enhanced strength
- More resistant to sunlight and common vampire weaknesses
- Inherent access to Hemomancy
- Requires unique methods to be killed

**How can they be killed**?
While Elder Vampires are strong, they are still able to be defeated through a few unique methods including:
- Prolonged Sunlight Exposure: If you can expose an Elder for many consecutive hours their resistance and power will weaken so much they can be killed like regular humans.
- Shadowglass Weapons: Shadowglass is a special alloy made from Obsidian and other rare materials that is extremely deadly to Elder Vampires, it can kill them outright.
- Another Elder Vampire (Using their Hemomancy or bite/claw)

These conditions are difficult but achievable, however their specificity is what allows most Elder Vampires to live as long as they do.
