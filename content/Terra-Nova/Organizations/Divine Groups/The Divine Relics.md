
Divine Relics are unique magic items created in special magical moments such as a solstice or eclipse. They are given divine power and a piece of the celestial energy belonging to a god by that gods choice or some kind of ritual. There are many ways to create a Divine Relic with the help of a God. One example is Vulcan's Soulsunder, a blade imbued with Vulcan's might after his champion defeated twenty enemy commanders in one battle.
