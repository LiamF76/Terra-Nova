In the Abyss (also known as Hell or The Hells), there is a constant and unending war between the evil powers. There are multiple groups of highly powerful beings, including Gods, Demi-Gods, and the great demons that war against one another for control.


### [[The Betrayer Gods]]

Known as the Betrayers, led by the powerful Arkonil, the Betrayer Gods are known for their attempt to usurp Illuman and the Divine Council after the deaths of many Gods during the Titan Wars. Though defeated, they were not outright killed, and were banished to the Abyss for thousands of years. While there they ruled the Abyss with an iron fist, overpowering the demons who ruled there for many years. When the Betrayers led their armies to the surface to conqueror the mortal plane, they were stretched too thin to maintain hold over the entire Abyss, and were ousted from their seat of total power.
The Betrayers were defeated and sealed within magical artifacts, not having returned to the Abyss since that defeat. Their followers still remain, many of which are Tieflings, also known as the Children of Arkonil or the Blood of the Reckoner.


### [[The Demon Princes]]

Known as the Demon Princes, devils and fiends born of the Abyss, demi-god sons of the divine magic of [[Balthazaar]] and the demonic magic of the Abyss, they are astoundingly powerful and cruel. Having been born in the Abyss they are twisted, corrupt, and many say inherently evil. They rule over different regions of the Abyss in council with one another, though it is supposed to be democratic they rarely come to actual agreements. They usually just lie, steal, and cheat to get what they want. Most are technically in open war with one another, but ultimately they are aligned against the forces of the Betrayers, and seek to dominate the plane themselves.
