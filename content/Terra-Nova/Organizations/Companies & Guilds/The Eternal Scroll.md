The Eternal scroll is the Lorekeeper's guild, a group dedicated to the chronicling and recordkeeping of all the world's histories.

Headmaster [[Rhas'Vinul]] is the leader of the order, and has been for many hundreds of years. He is a lich who obtained his power through a knowledge seeking patron. Now he operates the Historians guild, and weeks to chronicle and make knowledge of the past available to all.
