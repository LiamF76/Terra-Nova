The Pact of Shattered Fate is an organization similar to the Dark Tribunal and Young Wolves, but they are not good or evil aligned, they represent a more neutral, albeit good, interest.
They have many members, but are made up of a council of 4 major leaders, some with their own organizations beneath them.

**Members**:
- [[Waft]], The Ionic Word
- [[Brynja]] Shield-Maiden, The Raven
	- The Blades of the Raven (Mercenary company currently allied with the Freehold)
- [[Omroc]] Bhakax Shazire Hiqiroth, The Courteous Flame
	- The Westlight Trading Company (merchant company of caravans and ships based out of Annevar)



## Origin & Goals

The group was formed by Brynja Shield-Maiden, with the goal of filling the void left by the death of the fates. With Destiny and fate broken, killed, she brought together the wise monk Waft and powerful speaker and merchant Omroc to form a group, invested int he future of the world.
Together they vowed to protect the interest of the many, of the mortals and beings of Terra-Nova, and to use their power to protect those who resided with them on this world as fate no longer could.

In the words of Omroc, "A toast friends, for if fate no longer guides this world, and we shall inevitably fall to shadow, I will do so fighting, and knowing I worked towards the greater good. To The pact of Shattered Fate!"

Their goal is basically protect the world now that the kings are corrupt, the wizards council is gone, and the fates are dead.
