 
 An ancient abyssal ritual said to be able to seal objects, creatures, and even souls in stone for eternity. Designed by the brilliant Abyss Mage, Qithtar the Black, known for his ability to walk the Abyss without mutation or corruption.
 
 **School of Magic**: Abyssal Transmutation

### **Materials Required**

1. **Dragons Blood**: The blood of a dragon or other draconic creature, freshly drawn. This serves as the catalyst for the ritual’s dark energy. (1 vial)
   
2. **Stone of Binding**: A large, unworked block of obsidian or basalt, at least 5 feet tall. The stone must be untouched by tools or magic before the ritual.
   
3. **Soul Stone**: A gemstone (such as a black diamond or onyx) capable of containing a soul. This gem will act as the anchor for the binding.
   
4. **Infernal Chains**: Chains forged in the fires of the Nine Hells or the Abyss. These chains are used to bind the creature physically and magically.
   
5. **Ritual Dagger**: A dagger made of abyssal wood, inscribed with abyssal runes. It is used to draw the blood of the target and carve the binding runes.
   
6. **Sacrificial Offering**: A living creature of significant power (CR 5 or higher) to fuel the ritual. The creature’s life force is consumed during the process.
   
7. **Abyssal Runes**: A set of runes carved into the ground around the Stone of Binding. These runes must be drawn with the Dragons Blood and glow with a faint red light.
   
8. **Incense of Oblivion**: A rare incense made from the air at the top of the Void. When burned, it creates a thick, acrid smoke that helps to suffuse the area with abyssal energy.


### **Ritual Process**

9. **Preparation**:
    
    - The ritual must be performed in a place of great darkness or corruption, such as a ruined temple, a deep cavern, or a site where abyssal energy is strong.
        
    - The Stone of Binding is placed at the center of the ritual space, and the Abyssal Runes are carved into the ground around it using the Ritual Dagger and Abyssal Blood.
        
    - The Sacrificial Offering is chained to the Stone of Binding using the Infernal Chains.
        
10. **Invocation**:
    
    - The caster begins by chanting in Abyssal, calling upon the dark powers of the Abyss to grant them the strength to perform the binding. This chant lasts for 10 minutes and grows louder and more intense as the ritual progresses.
        
    - The Incense of Oblivion is lit, filling the area with thick, choking smoke.
        
11. **Binding**:
    
    - The caster uses the Ritual Dagger to draw blood from the Sacrificial Offering, allowing it to flow into the Abyssal Runes. The runes glow brighter as they absorb the blood.
        
    - The Soul Gem is placed at the base of the Stone of Binding. The caster then uses the Ritual Dagger to carve the name of the target creature into the stone, channeling abyssal energy through the dagger as they do so.
        
12. **Sealing**:
    
    - The target creature is brought before the Stone of Binding. If the creature resists, it must be subdued or restrained using the Infernal Chains.
        
    - The caster completes the chant, calling upon the Abyss to seal the creature within the stone. The Soul Gem glows brightly as the creature’s essence is drawn into it.
        
    - The Stone of Binding begins to crack and shift, forming a crude likeness of the creature as it is absorbed. The creature’s body turns to stone, and its soul is trapped within the Soul Gem.
        
13. **Completion**:
    
    - The ritual is complete when the creature is fully transformed into stone and the Soul Gem is embedded within the Stone of Binding. The stone radiates a faint, ominous energy, and the creature’s essence is sealed for eternity.


### **Risks and Consequences**

- **Abyssal Corruption**: Performing the ritual exposes the caster to abyssal energy, which can corrupt their soul. The caster must make a **DC 18 Charisma saving throw** or gain a level of exhaustion and a random flaw tied to abyssal influence.
    
- **Ritual Failure**: If the ritual is interrupted or performed incorrectly, the target creature may break free, or the abyssal energy may run wild, summoning demons or causing a localized explosion of necrotic energy (8d10 necrotic damage in a 30-foot radius, DC 15 Dexterity save for half damage).
    
- **Soul Gem Fragility**: The Soul Gem can be shattered, releasing the trapped creature. If this happens, the creature will be enraged and seek revenge on the caster.


### **d6 Table: Abyssal Corruption Flaws**

14. **Whispers of the Void**
    
    - You hear faint, insidious whispers in your mind at all times. They urge you to commit acts of violence or cruelty.
        
    - **Mechanic**: You have disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing, and you must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw to resist acting on the whispers' suggestions when stressed or provoked.
        
15. **Eyes of the Abyss**
    
    - Your eyes glow with a faint red light, and you occasionally see visions of the Abyss overlaying the real world.
        
    - **Mechanic**: You have disadvantage on Wisdom (Insight) checks, as you struggle to distinguish reality from the visions. Creatures that can see your eyes are often unnerved, giving you disadvantage on Charisma (Persuasion) checks.
        
16. **Mark of the Damned**
    
    - A dark, twisting mark appears on your skin, pulsing with abyssal energy. It burns faintly when you are near holy symbols or consecrated ground.
        
    - **Mechanic**: You cannot enter holy ground or wield holy objects without taking 1d6 necrotic damage per round. Celestial creatures are hostile to you by default.
        
17. **Hunger for Souls**
    
    - You develop an insatiable hunger for the life force of others. You feel weak unless you drain energy from living creatures.
        
    - **Mechanic**: Once per day, you must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or gain one level of exhaustion. You can remove a level of exhaustion by draining the life force of a living creature (dealing 2d10 necrotic damage to them and regaining half as much HP).
        
18. **Paranoia**
    
    - You become convinced that everyone around you is plotting against you or hiding something.
        
    - **Mechanic**: You have disadvantage on Charisma (Deception) and Charisma (Persuasion) checks, as your paranoia makes you come across as untrustworthy. You also struggle to trust allies, potentially complicating group dynamics.
        
19. **Unstable Magic**
    
    - The abyssal energy has warped your connection to magic, making it unpredictable and dangerous.
        
    - **Mechanic**: Whenever you cast a spell of 1st level or higher, roll a d6. On a 1, the spell triggers a wild magic surge (use the Wild Magic Surge table from the Sorcerer class).
