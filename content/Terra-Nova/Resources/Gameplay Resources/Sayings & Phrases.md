This is a list of common sayings and phrases in Terra-Nova and their meanings/ origins. Those that are common across the world will be in the general list, and from there they will be organized by the areas they are common in.

---

## General

- *"Carry a shield, meet the sword. Carry a sword, meet the shield."* Whatever stance you choose, the world invites the opposite. This comes from the ancient foes Arkonil the Betrayer God of the sword and Melantha Celestia-Shield, the Shield of Heaven.
- *"Keep the Road's Honor."* Respect fellow travelers and adventurers. This comes from the Rising Sun Church of Illuman
- *"Arken."* A term for someone dangerous, violent, or malicious. Often used for Tieflings and Fire-Genasi due to their alignment with the Rebel King of Estor's ancient history.
- *"A worm who thinks himself the serpent, is no Balthazaar."* Just because someone thinks they are something does not make it true. Originated from an ancient champion of Balthazaar whose name is forgotten, because in truth he was little more than a worm.
- *"Auranthian."* A term used for someone who is or their deeds are greatly heroic and legendary. Originated from the legend of the Hyperion Church's Messiah called the Auranthor.
- *"In Daraan's eye."* A term for someone who is chosen / destined for something. Someone who will experience powerful events and live great stories.
