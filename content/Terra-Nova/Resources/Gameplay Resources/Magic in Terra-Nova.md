Magic in Terra-Nova functions similarly to many other settings, with a few of its own tweaks and quirks of course. This document will outline magic from the perspective of the world, not the players. Magic used by players is determined by the game / tabeltop system you run, such as D&D or Pathfinder, and that will not be covered here. What willbe covered is magic through the lens of the NPCs in my games and characters in my stories.
