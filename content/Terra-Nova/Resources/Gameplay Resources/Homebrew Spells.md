## Kendoltric's Storm Orb

| Spell Level | School    | Casting Time | Range | Components                       | Duration      | Classes          |
| ----------- | --------- | ------------ | ----- | -------------------------------- | ------------- | ---------------- |
| 2nd         | Evocation | 1 action     | 30ft  | V, S, M (a diamond worth 50+ GP) | Instantaneous | Sorcerer, Wizard |

You summon a 5ft by 5ft orb of storm clouds and crackling lightning at point within range. At the start of your next turn it detonates, each enemy within 10ft of the point must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw, taking 2d8 lightning damage on a failure, and half as much on a success.
