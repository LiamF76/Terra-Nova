## Overview

What is Terra-Nova? This is my world. It began in my college dorm room during a dark period of my life, where I poured passion and love into it and it became something much larger than I had anticipated. 

Like may D&D settings I draw heavily on inspiration, and the major setting inspirations for Terra-Nova you may already know about.
- Middle Earth / Lord of the Rings
- The Dark Crystal
- Faerun & The Forgotten Realms
- Malazan Book of the Fallen
- Frieren: Beyond Journey's End

![[Terra-Nova Primer-1784516592913.webp|300x507]]

You may notice some diverse and conflicting themes and inspirations from the above sources. I do too. While many elements from these sources can be found in the world, it is not overly or exactly like any of them. It is organic and unique in many ways. Depending on location, the tone ranges from dark to cozy fantasy, and high to low magics, though generally it leans towards lower magics as compared to traditional D&D. The tone of the setting depends very heavily on the continent and even nation or plane your adventure is taking place. 

Here are tow examples from Terra-Nova. Take for instance the land of Escarth, full of legendary stories, fantasy knights and castles, classic monsters, tyrant kings and agents of the heavens fighting against demons and dragons , it could be an Elder Scrolls game setting. Compare that to the land of Iramo, a continent entirely made of a Maze covered and devoid of sunlight, hundreds of miles wide and tall, with many levels, filled with the most horrific monsters and traps imaginable, where a trio of Eldritch beings rule and war eternally over the secrets and powers held within left behind by the ancient Aelarians.

These tow places are so different they are almost entirely separate worlds, but look outside. If you in the Unites States or wherever you live, woke up one morning in Palestine, Russia, the Philippines, or Antarctica, would it not feel like you had traveled to another planet even for a moment? This world is vast and diverse, and yours to explore.

## Divine Magic

The core of Terra-Nova is the powers of divinity and mortality. [[Shao Daraan]] and [[Khal Daraan]] created Terra-Nova, the material and all other planes. Now Gods do not walk or directly interact with Terra-Nova, as they are forbidden from doing so. Gods choose Divine Champions, blessed heroes who carry a large sum of their benefactor's power, and enforce their God's will on the world. Throughout history champions have influenced and altered history. One such champion was Lazarus, who defeated Arkonil in the first Abyss Wars, or Melantha Celestia-Shield, who helped free many dead Gods from the grasp of Khal Daraan.

Champions are the bridge between divinity and mortality, meant to shepherd and guide mortals through turmoil and great change. Gods often interact with Mortals through their Angels, Priests, and Paladins. Followers of the Divine are very common, and most are raised in households that revere one God or another to some degree. Nearly every town has a priest, and most major cities have multiple temples, some are home to holy orders and religious capitals or monuments.

Divine magic is usually viewed as favorable or a blessing. People look upon Divine magic users favorably, and will not judge your use of it.

## Arcane Magic

Magic is extremely potent in Terra-Nova, having been responsible for land formations and many of the most important historic events in the world's history. Arcane magic is different than Divine Magic, in that mortals created arcane magics all on their own. Arcane magic invented by the ancient Aelarians protected them against the forces of dangerous Primordials on a still changing world in the Dawn Era.

Telura is the Goddess of Magic, but she does not govern it. That is handled by the Wizards Council. Once made up of a specific set of Wizards, White, Blue, Green, Red, Purple, and Brown, it has in the last few hundred years been opened to a wider group. From far away lands there is a Gold, Silver, Brass, and many other Wizards.

Arcane magic is generally equal to Divine magic in power, though not in commonality. In the last few hundred years Arcane magic has dwindled substantially due to The Scars, Pillars of Dark Magic that drain Terra-Nova's leylines and funnel it into the Abyss.

In the modern day, magic users have become much more rare. In the Third Age you could find a cantrip peddler in every circus, now you will have to visit a city to find someone who can truly cast a spell. Small towns often have those learned in magics, but none who can use it.

People are often wary of Arcane magic, and may not attack you outright, but will demand you do it away from or not to them. Some nations have laws regulating the use of Arcane magic.
