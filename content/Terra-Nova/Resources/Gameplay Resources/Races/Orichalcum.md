Orichalcum is an arcane material of dubious origins. Thought to have been invented by Horus of the Temporal Plane, and utilized by Ivys Arthur / Kairos.

Orichalcum is calcified and form driven time. It is literally temporal energy made into a solid form, melted down, and crafted into a metal that could be forged. It is extremely rare, wildly expensive, and even harder to create than it is to find.

It can only be crafted in specific forges. It cannot be created outside the Temporal Plane.
