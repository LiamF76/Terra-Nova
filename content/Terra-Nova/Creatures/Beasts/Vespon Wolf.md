## Overview

Vespon Wolves, also called Tox Hounds by locals, were created by the mingling of magical venom in the air with wild canines. Dire and regular wolves alike were mutated by the influence of Vesponous, and the Triumvirate Scales are now home to a unique subspecies of genetic accident.
