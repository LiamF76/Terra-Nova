Lazarus was the greatest mortal king in history. He was the first recorded champion of Vulcan and the divine (excusing the anomaly of Kairos) and called "The Leader of Men" for uniting all the Kingdoms of Escarth into the Kingdom of Aetoria. His legacy and titles are nigh endless, but that led him not to hubris, but into humility. He gave up his power as Vulcan's champion, and put down the sword for good, taking a single rowboat, and entering Konduell's Grasp, seeking to see where the sun goes when it sets.

He disappeared forever, never returning to the known world. He was presumed dead, but Lazarus was not dead. He traveled across the unknown, seeing far away lands and mysteries and wonders like nothing he had ever seen. He sailed across the great Veil of Thalassa, along the Red Rivers of the Shadowlands, and through the great rock walled gateway to the land he soon ruled, now called the Lazarian Straights, the doorway to the Kingdom of Lazarus. He landed on the vast green and beautiful land, not knowing what he would see, expecting beasts and plants as he had seen, but he found people. The ancient tribes of Ogodei, nomads and warriors who lad lived and traveled the lands for many years. Their roads crossed from the cold mountains and snowy lands of Karakorum, all the way to the south eastern deserts of Kublai. Lazarus was the first man to come from within the great "Forbidden Waters", they were fascinated by him, and after displaying his prowess he was welcomed into their ranks.

To earn the trust of the Ogodei Lazarus had to show them his power, his mastery in their ways, the way of Ogodei was wrestling. Lazarus challenged the biggest man in the caravan, a legendary hero of the tribe called Kojima, a great warrior. They wrestled for three days, before Lazarus finally subdued Kojima, and was bonded with the tribe forever.
Lazarus learned their ways, the art of horse and bow, of travel and nomadism, unlike the stationary lifestyle of his people in Aetoria.
