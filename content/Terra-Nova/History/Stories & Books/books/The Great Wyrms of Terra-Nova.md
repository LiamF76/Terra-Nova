# **A History of Dragons**
**Author**: *Herodotus Quill* of the **Obsidian Scriptorium**
**Modernized and Translated by**: *Talinor Runehand*, Apprentice to *Lorekeeper Drezin* of the **Ivory Spire Institute**

Throughout history, every natural system with flora and fauna exists on some kind of natural hierarchy, often described by a tiered ranking called "The Food Chain". In Terra-Nova, many years have been dominated by mortals, in the beginning the Aelarians created the magical infrastructure we know today, Elves brought us the legendary age of magic items led by Aethos the Enchanter, and today Men rule, their ability to adapt and evolve is unparalleled. And yet throughout all these eras, there is one creature that remains dominant, sent down from the heavens, the metallic dragons, and risen from the heart of the world, the chromatic dragons, these beings, often referred to as Wyrms, stand above all others.
Dragons were designed by minds we cannot comprehend from beyond our world. We do know this though, the metallic breed were sent as mortal guardians, and the chromatic as forces of chaos.

## The Timeline of Dragons
Dragons are stated and known to have existed through thousands of agreeing accounts since the beginning of time. In the earliest Aelarian days settlements were built around metallic dragon lairs, as they were known to be safe places to live. Under their protection the ancient elves were able to thrive, one such location being the floating island city of Thylor Aethil, once the home of Basarym. Dragons existed in an eternal conflict between themselves, the balance of power shifting between metallic and chromatic over centuries. 
Dragon population numbers dropped slowly over time, as more mortals became powerful and populated the world, along with the extinction of the Aelarians, eventually dragons became more rare than they already were, keeping to the Wyrmspire or their isolated hordes. While they were were they were still known and respected, until that changed. During the time of the rise of the first Khan, Hara Khan, seemingly out of nowhere all the dragons on the Wyrmspire vanished. Still to this day it is unknown why or how, but the chromatics that lived there were gone. Metallics and chromatics through the world slowly died out, unable to withstand the evolution and consuming nature of expanding mortal societies.

## The Elder Chromatic Dragons
While the first generation of dragons was full of valiant and dastardly wyrms, there are some of special note. Those listed here are the ones who are known, and are especially documented for their either good of bad impact upon the world. 

#### Thrynax, The Keeper of the First Flame, and the Kings of Fire
The first of the lineage of flame dragons, known as the Keeper of the First Flame, the King of Fire, Thyrnax. Thrynax is known for his incursion into what is now known as the *Wyrmlands*, where he and his brethren constructed the largest centralized dragon next is history, the *Wyrmspire*. He is known for his unquenchable thirst for gold, power, and his dominance. Challenge is common and encouraged in dragon society, but Thrynax was never contested, his power and rule as the King of Fire was absolute.
In his time he was fierce, setting the precedent that dragons were a terrible and dark force upon the world. Thrynax is credited with defeating one of the earliest demi-gods, Elenaria, a daughter of Illuman. He sowed terror through the land, and prevent Aelairan colonization of what became Escarth for thousands of years. He was eventually defeated by the terrifying power of Wishes, through another demi-gods pact with Essebaav the genie.
His lineage extends longer than even the most ancient Aelarian houses, but only one other from his line is noted in history, that being Scaldor, Prince of Fire. Scaldor was similar to his ancestor, nigh unstoppable and imbued with the power of the First Flame, but he was not unchallenged. This was much to Scaldor's liking, as he often invited, and sought out challengers to face him. He is said to have defeated over a hundred other wyrms, both metallic and chromatic before he fell in one of his duels in his old age. 

#### The First Tiamat
Tiamat, the embodiment and Goddess of Greed and mother of chromatic dragons. She was defeated after allying herself with the Reckoner in the war against the heavens. Though she was destroyed her essence was never truly killed, and she lives on in the spirit of greed. Now Tiamat exists as a concept, formed when multiple chromatic dragons rise to power in Terra-Nova and form a Tiamat, taking from their god immense power and embodying her greed and malice. 
The first Tiamat was made up of 4 dragons
- **Noxia** the Green, a three headed serpentine dragon known for her venomous breath and words. She dominated the jungles of the Pridelands.
- **Glaceria** the White, a massive fur and scaled covered mother of a massive ice dragon next who ruled over the Agamar Mountains for years.
- **Voltarion** the Blue, the largest of the four and known for building an army of Kobolds and Dragonborn, he led the Tiamat and loves warfare.
- **Terravox** the Brown, the most brutal and violent of the Tiamat, almost more like a dinosaur than a dragon he waded through the sands of Drok Shah and amassed a horde of gold and legendary warriors he kept as slaves.
This council of chromatics reigned through terror and violence, conquering almost anywhere they pleased. Though they were immensely powerful, they were summarily defeated by a group of heroes who became known as the **Wyrmwardens**. These heroes had legendary magical items forged by none other than the Enchanter, Aethos. Using the power of these items:
- The Staff of Sunrise, **Dawnheart**
- The Might of Thunder, the **Stonbringer's Fists**
- The Doom of Dragons, **Scaledancer**
- The King's Blade, **Excalibur**

#### Kezurdur the Pale
Known as the last dragon, hatched from an egg under the caring and watchful eye of the Witch Queen Kagerra, Kezurdur is a force to be reckoned with. 

## The Ancient Metallic Dragons
While the chromatics were agents of the end, the metallic lineages have been guardians of humanity and defenders of the natural order forever. Many great metallic dragons have existed, while not all completely benevolent they are generally aligned with mortals and the idea of justice.

#### Basarym the Bright
Basarym was a gold dragon, said to be descended directly from the very first dragon, Fyrnanth. Basarym is the legendary dragon that allied with some ancient Aelarian heroes to defeat and  sunder Thrynax, ending the reign of terror the the red dragon inflicted on the world. He was said to be kind, and lacking in fury, but he covered that weakness by being the beacon lighting the way of the Aelarians. He assisted them using his magic to allow their dimensional expeditions through celestial gates, he guided and mentored the ancient Aelarian demi-god [[Tiphion]] (a child of Khabris), and had no hordes of gold or treasure, but of knowledge and riddles.

#### Lervandel the Ferocious
Lervandel, the Bronze beast as he was known, was the greatest dragon general that the empires of Ikon have ever seen. The last dragon Kezurdur is the only living dragon general today, but any scholar could point to the exploits and power of Lervandel and know that he would demolish the white dragon. Lervandel was smaller for an ancient dragon, but was much more than his size expressed. He bore the breath of divine fire, a gift from the forge master Iros himself, and when he entered battle his armies could rest easily. He kept his own casualties in wars that lasted decades below the numbers of conflicts that last months today. Lervandel built a great tower in the land of Escarth from the melted metal of his enemies weapons and crowns, and his horde was that of rank insignias, enemy banners, and their leaders souls.

#### Eikied, The Cloud Guardian
A rare (even for dragons) and curious story is Eikied. Said to be the only recorded or even mentioned Electrum dragon. Eikied was a benevolent guardian of the skies, protecting ships that traveled through Terra-Nova's airways with its speed and skill. Eikied would divert the paths of sky titans such as cloud tyrants, and assist lost voyagers through chaos and storms. She was not one to talk, but was studied with her permission by an Aelarian adventurer named Cyrus and his sister Aubrim. The two recounted tales of Eikied's bravery and mystery, a ponderous and deep thinker on the functions of the world and the roles of gods and primordials.
