Long before the Kings of Estor bore the name Realmcrest, before Kagerra ruled the Elves, and when dragons still ruled the skies, there was a man called Thal. Thal was an accomplished wizard, but he had always believed that traditional magic was, limited. He was gifted in the crafts of artifice and necromancy, using the power of summoning and death to power his creations.
Thal grew stagnant in his pursuits, unable to become more powerful than he was, and it filled him with rage. He delved into ancient and forbidden texts of dark and alluring power, throwing away his integrity for a chance at greater power.
