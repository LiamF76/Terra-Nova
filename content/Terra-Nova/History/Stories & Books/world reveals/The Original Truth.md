Long thought to be lost to time and obscurity, there is a story. This story tells the truth of history, of creation, of magic, and it has been wiped from almost every record.

The spur myth and origin of the world begins with the war in heaven, the battle between the gods and Balthazaar, but what came before is where this story begins. 

In the times of peace, for almost a thousand years the world, in the light of Illuman's sun, the cool breeze of Tena's winds, and the majesty of Skuld Ysor's mountains, the Aelarians lived in the great lands of Terra-Nova. They lived in peace with the land, but the conflict was above them, in their skies, in the wild space, in the realms of the divine.

Due to the law of Ytragof, the gods could not enter the moral plane, but the primordials, the precursors, were unbound by such laws. They caused issue with the mortals, pain and suffering, using their power over the weave (magic). The gods granted their children the ability to use divine magic, to protect themselves and match the power of the precursors. This is the origin of divine magic, but that which was not divine, was not yet granted. 

This angered the primordials, who made a bargain with the celestials, as long as they only gave mortals divine magic, they would no longer trouble them, and were removed from the gods protected plane, known as the Sacred Realm. The greatest of the primordials, Konduell, submerged himself deep into the waters of the world, creating a barrier so that no Aelarian could leave, and the bargain held. 

For thousands of years there was peace, Balthazaar and Illuman bickered, Shao Daraan watched his mortals play, his gods rule, but balance could not last. A god whose name has been long forgotten, known to the Aelarians as "The God in Glass", who had been offering the mortals his divine magic, began to offer them more. The precursors discovered the God in Glass' treachery when some of the Aelarians were strong enough to escape Konduell.

Enraged, the primordials began to destroy everything in their path, invading the Sacred Realm. In a stunning betrayal, as the gods faced their enemy primordials in the heavens, and Balthazaar tore into his brother Illuman, wounding him eternally, turning his golden skin pale white and causing a never-ending bleed. The gods reunited and fought against the primordials, but quickly all realized that Balthazaar would destroy the sun, and the home of all gods creations and all the primordials themselves. 

The primordials surrendered, and were stripped of their divinity as punishment, turning them simply magic, although still immensely powerful. The Gods then united to stop their brother the Night Serpent, as he rampaged through creation, seeking to devour the sun. Then begins the history we know already.
