
The truth of Tena and Illuman. Illuman is not evil, prideful, or wrathful, in truth he is the Redeemer, the Guiding Light, the Dawnfather. He came to be angry, weak, and enraged, from the venom of Balthazaar, in his unhealing wounds sustained in their battle. 

In his twisted state, he murdered Tena, and in fear of retribution from his siblings and father, he made a pact with Khal Daraan to hide his lie, in exchange for giving Khal's followers Tena's body.

Khal honored their pact, casting the 10th level spell, *Oblivati Mori*, on the world, casting their true memory of the events into darkness forever. The only ones to escape this were Illuman, Khal, [[Hyperios]], and those within the Feywild.

Khal's follower Thaldrax worked alongside the remaining druids under a disguise to build the shrine to the dead goddess, imbuing it with the dark power of Khal, allowing him to use it to devour gods who walked the mortal plane. Khal was pleased, each god devoured brought him closer to awakening, and the gods couldn't stop him without freeing Balthazaar, a perfect paradox. The Oakenheart is Tena's heart, but it powers both Balthazaar's chains and Khal's awakening. Illuman violated this pact by ruling that no god should return to the mortal plane, and could only exercise their power through mortals known as *Champions*. This greatly slowed Khal's resurrection, but Illuman continued to falter as he fell deeper into madness and wrath due to his poisoning.

In the Feywild chaos brewed even more deeply that it normally would. The lords of the Fey were divided, on whether to ally with Khal or spread the truth back into the world. but Khal offered a pact to a young Fey Prince, who seized it and murdered the dissenting lords, resulting in only Three Great Houses remaining. He became known as Oberyn the Feylord, and was blessed with eternal life. He then purged all information and deniers, and the number of Aelarians left in the Feywild dwindled so low they almost went extinct. 
One creature that couldn't be killed by Oberyn and his followers was [[Kerro]] Greenstride, a demi-god son of Tena, who refused to forget his mother and patron. Kerro was imprisoned in [[The Temple of Three Roads]] for eternity, using his own mother's veins as chains.

Hundreds of years later a Demi-God son of Illuman known as Hyperios went on a quest, after meeting his father and discovering his venomous wounds. He realized his father had been corrupted and delved into the deepest reaches of the Abyss searching for a cure. As Hyperios adventured the maddening whispers and mutations of the Abyss took him, and ruined his mind with rage and jealousy. He found the cure for his father, but took the artifact and destroyed it, casting it into the nothingness beneath Balthazaar. He became the first Hyperian, and his cult was named after him. He sought to destroy his father and the gods, twisted into believing that the only way to fix the wrong his father had done was to destroy the gods and allow an untainted reality to rise.
