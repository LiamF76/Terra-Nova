## The City of Prophecy

---

In the city of Arthos there are 3 great wonders. The Oracle, the ultimate seer, born from the fusion of the last 3 mortals to bear the gift of prophecy, now the Oracle exists in fragments of their former self. The Gate, a wonder of divine architecture, a massive semi-ring built to house and act as conduit to the divine power of the Oracle. And the faith, held so deeply in Arthos that it is often referred to as Little Elysium, for its people are so holy they believe their home to be heaven.

Of all those wonders though, the greatest is truly the Oracle. Through a ritual designed by the holy mage Mervin, using the remains of the Fate-Weaver and the 3 mortal oracles, the Oracle was born.

It is said that looking upon the Oracle's face sends a man into madness, so be thankful that their face is no longer in once piece. Through the city's history it has been attacked by the agents of Iramo and its cruel masters on many occasions, assassins, thieves, all manner of evil operatives. The Oracle has been killed and revived many times over, but now it has been fractured. Most pieces reside in Arthos, each held in a separate section of its vast temples, slowly dispensing prophecy to the Illumanic priests who listen.

Some however, were lost. Thrown to the side of history's road, their location, abilities, and importance are forgotten. Those who carry them beware, for with knowledge of the future comes desperation to change it, and none, even the Oracle, can change destiny.
