## The Third Lesson

Vesponous had taught him to kill, to cleave through and unmake through effort. She had taught him to die, to be rended gone, and rise again, but the third lesson was far more delicate.

She raked her claws, dripping in venom, across his chest. "To be duplicitous my supplicant, you must know how it is to BREAK!"
