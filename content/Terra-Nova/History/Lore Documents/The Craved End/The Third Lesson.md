## The Third Lesson

[[Vesponous]] had taught him to kill, to cleave through and unmake through effort. She had taught him to die, to be rendered gone, and rise again, but the third lesson was far more delicate.

She raked her claws, dripping in venom, across his chest. "To be duplicitous my supplicant, you must know how it is to BREAK!" Her voice was poison like her breath and claws.

His eyes shut as he split, and as he was taught, he halted his breath to pure stillness for a moment, and his mortal eyes turned from this world to the Nothing, and something was there waiting.

She towered over his twin forms, one shuddering with violent desperation, the other still and unmoving. Aldros' breath ached in and out, in through the still, and out through the shaking. He was then still in both forms, and two arose, for Aldros had become his own Aspect, and his Aspect had become him.

It is not recorded here what [[Aldros]] saw when he died, when he became duplicitous in the teaching of [[Vesponous]], but when he returned Aldros was endless.

His body shuddered and split, spectral Aspects of himself surrounding the pair, the Dragon and her student. They multiplied into the hundreds, then shunted back into [[Aldros]], again and again.

[[Aldros]] and his Aspects spoke a simple proclamation. "I am [[Aldros]], and I am many, but of many, we are one."

The Dragon laughed and raised her clawed hand to kill him, for there were many lessons yet to come.
