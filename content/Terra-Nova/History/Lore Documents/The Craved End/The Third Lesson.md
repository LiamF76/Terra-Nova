## The Third Lesson

Vesponous had taught him to kill, to cleave through and unmake through effort. She had taught him to die, to be rendered gone, and rise again, but the third lesson was far more delicate.

She raked her claws, dripping in venom, across his chest. "To be duplicitous my supplicant, you must know how it is to BREAK!" Her voice was poison like her breath and claws.

She towered over his twin forms, one shuddering with violent desperation, the other still and unmoving. Aldros' breath ached in and out, in through the still, and out through the shaking.
