the Shardbound, an order of mage hunters in New Estorias who wield shards of an abyssal pillar, in New Estorias they believe abuse of magic to be the reason for strife in the world, conduct 'audits'  on mages to see their use of magic, often ends in the mage being killed. Only Five Exist – The Shardbound are always five, no more and no fewer. Each wields a shard of unknown origin, passed on only through death. When one falls, they are not replaced.
Eternal Wardens – The Shardbound do not age. They live indefinitely, bound to their duty until killed, at which point their shard is lost to history.
Doctrine of Excess – In New Estorias, it is believed that abuse of magic is the root cause of the world’s suffering. The Shardbound exist to enforce restraint, not to end magic entirely.
Magical Audits – The Shardbound conduct audits on known and suspected mages, investigating patterns of spell use, accumulation of power, and reliance on magic. These audits are rarely survivable.
Feared by All Casters – Even restrained and benevolent mages distrust the Shardbound. Their arrival is taken as a death sentence, regardless of guilt.
Shard Resonance – The shards react to spellcasting, growing warm, vibrating, or shedding dark dust when magic is used nearby, betraying even hidden or subtle acts.
Magic Starvation – Prolonged proximity to a Shardbound causes spells to falter and unravel. Casting becomes unreliable, then impossible, often without warning.
Permanent Scars – Bearing a shard inflicts lasting damage on its wielder: blackened veins, dulled physical senses, and recurring dreams filled with hostile, alien patterns.
Judgment Without Appeal – The Shardbound do not answer to mages, councils, or churches. Their judgment is considered final, and their methods are tolerated out of fear rather than trust.
Myth of Origin – The true origin of the shards is unknown. Stories contradict one another, and no official account claims certainty. The Shardbound themselves offer no clarification.
