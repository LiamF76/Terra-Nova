Only Five Exist – The Shardbound are always five, no more and no fewer. Each wields a shard of unknown origin, passed on only through death. When one falls, they are not replaced.
Eternal Wardens – The Shardbound do not age. They live indefinitely, bound to their duty until killed, at which point their shard is lost to history.
Doctrine of Excess – In New Estorias, it is believed that abuse of magic is the root cause of the world’s suffering. The Shardbound exist to enforce restraint, not to end magic entirely.
Permanent Scars – Bearing a shard inflicts lasting damage on its wielder: blackened veins, dulled physical senses, and recurring dreams filled with hostile, alien patterns.
Judgment Without Appeal – The Shardbound do not answer to mages, councils, or churches. Their judgment is considered final, and their methods are tolerated out of fear rather than trust.
Myth of Origin – The true origin of the shards is unknown. Stories contradict one another, and no official account claims certainty. The Shardbound themselves offer no clarification.

IMPORTANT: As agents of New Estorias, they have been tasked with hunting sorcerous bloodlines such as the Realmcrest, Tarn, Ferran, and Celestia-Shield.

## Fragments of Below

---

Six were the pieces, six shards of the ancient magic, six to carry and six to bind. And with the six shards, six were their knights, the Shardbound.

The Phoenix Emperor, who carried these shards sealed and safely within his form dispensed them to his six greatest warriors, and sent them unto the world to preach a sacred doctrine. From the Book of Fire, the Doctrine of Excess, a pillar of the Paragon of Rebirth. The Doctrine stated abuse of magic was the greatest source of the world's suffering and its misuse was the reason it had faded. The Shardbound were tasked to end this excess, and bring the Paragon to the wretched and unrepentant mages.

The Shardbound were perfect for this tasks, chosen from the elite followers of Horus, those who were with him when he ascended, his perfect and devoted warriors. There were mighty before the shards, and greater after. The shards granted them eternal life, strength, and power to match. They could drink magic from their foes, and return it to the world.

Horus decreed in his doctrine, that when the Shardbound would fall, so too would the shards, their magic quelled, and no new knight could replace them. there were six only, forever.

As they were born, infused with the shards into their flesh, they were given new names, and honors.

- **Alren**, Destined to Burn
- **Malor**, Destined to Fly
- **Lorn**, Destined to Move
- **Kell**, Destined to Conquer
- **Eram**, Destined to Navigate
- **Fall**, Destined to Change

The origins of the shard are hidden, the unwritten secret of the Doctrine of Excess, but their power is obvious. Mages grew to fear them, as they tore down the walls and libraries of ancient and honored places of learning. Towers, monasteries, anywhere the arcane lived, the Shardbound killed. Their arrival was nothing short of a death sentence.

The shards are hungry things, and mages who spend too long around them without proper protection suffer from mana starvation. Your body begins to devour itself, you shrivel, shrink, and starve.
