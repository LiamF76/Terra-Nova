Khal Daraan created from his power 4 mantles
- The Weapon
- The Spell
- The Voice
- The Book
These mantles were left open, to be worn by those who claimed them and their power. It is said that Khal devours creation when all 4 mantles are worn and united in purpose with Khal, the Oakenheart dies. The mantles are governed by Iridescen, a primordial.

As Khal lay beneath all, and one with nothing, his most chesished son, Iridescen, toiled in rage and singular purpose. The law of all was strength, and that strength like all things could be cut and made in pieces.
