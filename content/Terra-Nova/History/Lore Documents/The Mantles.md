Khal Daraan created from his power 4 mantles
- War
- Death
- Plague
- Famine
These mantles were left open, to be worn by those who claimed them and their power. It is said that Khal devours creation when all 4 mantles are worn and the Oakenheart dies. The mantles are governed by Inten, a primordial.
