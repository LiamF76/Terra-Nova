Fading Wonder, Narrated by [[Rakesh]] 

---

It was said once that for every bakery on the corner, you could find a magic shop across the street, and for every two bakers, there was one wizard. From the ancient magical institutes of Redsong to the Ivory Spire, there were thousands of mages. Secular casters who drew power from ritual, knowledge, and runes, and did their learning in libraries lined with history and incantation.

Today you will find no such places. Sovrentia holds only one place of mages now, [[Tamora]], and even it's power fades by the age. There are clerics, paladins, and holy healers in decent supply, but admittedly our numbers dwindle too, because no matter the source, divine or arcane, all magic fades in this world.

---
![[Fading Wonder-1777920806661.webp|500x294]]
The Scars. Originally, a terrible line carved across the entire continent, like Arkonil had plunged his blade into the stomach of the world and cut, hoping to spill the guts of all creation into the void. He found no organs or flesh, but magic, magic poured out like blood and bile from Terra-Nova's gut.

For a time there was no loss of magic, actually the opposite. The White Wizard at the time, Ivys Arthur, testified that the Scar had birthed a new era of magical potential, through which Terra-Nova had significantly *more* mana than prior to the event. If only that had remained true.

Nearly 50 years after the Scar was created, the decline became undeniable. Children born with sorcerous or arcane capabilities outside of those from potent magical bloodlines had become nearly nonexistent. Most children born into powerful bloodlines were nonmagical, and if any were it was limited to the firstborns. Tradition, and power, were dying, and mages were dropping like flies.

In an effort to quell the combined magical and societal decline of a gigantic portal to hell, a campaign was launched. Now known as the Scar Crusade, multiple armies composed of mages and warriors fought, entering the Abyss itself through the scar, and closing the portals to the Abyss.

The Scar, luckily for us, was not unbreakable. It was formed by a chain of Abyssal stone pillars, connected by demonic transmutation and teleportation magic. It was in effect a big open hole in a wall that had never been meant to break down. Led by great mages such as Ivys Arthur, and the guidance of the nearly passed Promise Hemsley, the crusade was largely successful. The massive Scar was reduced to a number, at the time 14, smaller localized portals to the Abyss. While not ideal, this was a vast improvement.
![[Fading Wonder-1777920885789.webp|500x500]]
Each of the Scars now was centralized around the remaining pillars that were not reached and destroyed. When mapped, a revelation was made, each of the pillars was built on a magical leyline intersection. Some had more leyline overlap than others, but all were built where the magical blood of Terra-Nova intersects. 

Whoever planned these locations was a mastermind, meaning Arkonil or one of his mage advisors. The Scars were deadly, and after the effort of the first crusade, there weren't enough great warriors and heroes left to make another push, so the Scars remained, full of demons, portals to hell nobody could destroy. The sit now on the surface of the world, poisoning our leylines, and stealing the magic right out of the ground.

Today you are lucky to meet a mage in your lifetime if you don't live in a capital city, those you do meet in the wilds probably aren't friendly, and will kill you with the magic they so deeply covet. Often times in my youth I despaired over the fading wonder of this world, and how it felt the only magic left lay in the Elven lands, or far away in the shadowed wilds of Wahellia, but I am glad now to say I have hope.

Divine magic. The future is bright in Illuman's light, and all the Gods will shine on us one day. Pray and keep faith, and the holy spells of healing and guidance will lead the way forward, for all peoples.
