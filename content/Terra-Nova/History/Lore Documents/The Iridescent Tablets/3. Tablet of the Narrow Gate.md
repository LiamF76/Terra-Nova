## 3. The Tablet of the Narrow Gate

Possibility is the mother of betrayal. Choice is the enemy of decision.

Close the doors. Under lock and key, both mind and body. Reduce choice to a shadow on the wall. 

Destroy the hope, and the will shall fall behind it. Obliterate the self, and only our Nothing remains.

Tear, until even freedom has only one direction. Deeper. Down into the tower, black, where we reside.

Arms open, no choice left but the finality, no ending remains but the last.

Step through the gate, and embrace the Cycle.

Nothing.
