## 9. The Tablet of Procedure

Pray if it calms you.

Measure if it saves you.

Repeat if it changes you.

Holiness is a functioning loop.

I have it written here, in my Nothing.

Break the loop, and where do the Gods land?

On bended knee before their supplicant masses. How very *divine*.

I should like to see again, what they look like in fear...
