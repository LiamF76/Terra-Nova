## 5. The Tablet of Flow

You are carried along the river. Struggle, fight, thrash and scream.

You will only drag yourself down... further and darker.

You cannot swim, only choose how you drown.

The Fixed Point... taught me much, but most it taught me through it's trusting.

The same lie that it fell to, has passed on to the spawn.

No Tower is Formless.

The final moment knows your name. It always has.

To accept the flow is not surrender. It is alignment.

Agreement is written in birth. All things end, in Nothing.
