## Count your Pieces

The gulls cry out over the port, feasting on caught fish and scavenged meat from the docks, while perched on the beautiful tiled and stone rooftops of the port buildings. The sky is bright blue, and the sun shines warmly on the backs of the people as they step almost silently down the ramp of the ships. There are hundreds of them, from Ikon, Iramo, and Akash alike.

They are held together tightly, a strange sense of community, a family forged in chains. The masters flick their crops, making red the backs of the slaves as they are loaded into carts, already purchased in bulk by great capitalist kings. They will all be dead within 6 months, mining beneath the mountains for their Dwarven masters.

A young boy, bound at the wrists, looks up in awe at the 12 great towers of the city, The Celgol Bells of Crystos. Forged by the first ruler, the first richest man in the world, the bells are enchanted and crafted of solid Celestial Gold, literal divine metal. they ring every hour on the hour, with the sounds of a heaven only the uppermost members of Xerathos' society experience.

For the rich it is an hourly reminder of their paradise, for the slaves it speaks only to what they can hope for on the day they die.
