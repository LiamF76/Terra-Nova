## The Son of the Storm Queen

---

A Dragonborn will tell you that [[Lanxaad]] is a Demi-God, born from the divine womb of [[Garthrax]], a shining blue example of her might. [[Lanxaad]]'s wings and strength would tell you the same thing, for his speed is unrivaled, and his power is immense. Lanxaad is the first recorded child of the Storm Queen of the Triumvirate Scales, and likely the only one who will ever be named in history. His father remains unnamed in every account, perhaps on purpose, or perhaps not.

From birth, delivered in that terrible castle by the sea that Garthrax built to rule from, Lanxaad was different. He was massive, born 5 feet long and with a tail nearly full grown. His scales were sharper, running along his spine like knives, cutting every midwife who held him. He would snuggle up to his nurses feasting on both the milk, and their blood. Garthrax would make thunderous laughter at the savage baby, the noise never bothered little Lanxaad.

---

As an adult Lanxaad was much the same, desperate and frothing at the mouth for two things, and nothing else. The approval of his mother, and to kill every enemy he faced. Garthrax would often send her beloved barbarian on great quests, assassinations and conquests impossible for mortals to achieve, each one more deadly than the last. Lanxaad faced them all without fear.

It took years, nearly 100 years of slaughter, but finally Garthrax found something that Lanxaad couldn't kill, at least not at first. The Dragon had found a beautiful prize in the hands of a dead Elf, an exile who had fled into hiding in the Triumvirate, and met their end. Garthrax took this gift and sent Lanxaad with it, a scroll of teleportation to the Feywild, on his greatest hunt yet. He followed her orders, but the realization spread as he passed through the planar gateway.

Lanxaad faced down his foe, taller than any building, with branches laden in golden leaves, and armor of divine bark, the Prime Creation: Sylvaris the Forest Guardian. Greatest of all woodland spirits, he whose honor is bound to the roots of the world itself. Lanxaad stood no chance. He was easily defeated, mocked by the great tree before all the fey and pixies, a pure mockery of his might, and captured. Sewn into the bark of the tree itself, he was imprisoned. He would escape, time and time again he would rest himself free from his prison, and duel with Sylvaris, and each time he would lose.

Sylvaris would entertain the Dragonborn, saying he liked the blue of his scales, the points of his claws, but he wouldn't kill Lanxaad. Enraged Lanxaad fought harder each time, throwing himself further into danger while he screamed in rage. His mother, she had sent him here to die, to fight the things she feared to face herself, she was a coward, and a traitor to her only son, and he hated her. He ripped and he tore, and each time he fought he grew stronger, and stronger, until he and Sylvaris were equally matched, and the suddenly... Lanxaad was stronger.

He called forth a stream of lightning so fierce, so violent, so rooted in hatred, that even a Prime Creation could not survive it. Sylvaris was slain, and Lanxaad drank from the dying Prime's sap. He hoisted himself upwards, renewed by the blood of his fallen foe, and with a club made from the very bark of the spirit, he tore open a gateway home.

Imbued with fey magics Lanxaad harnessed a power few would relish, a power unique to such a bloodthirsty and egotistical warrior who had drank from the wellspring of the Fey. In the presence of Lanxaad, those who consider him foe are subtly charmed, not into fear, or into submission, but into confidence. He does not want you to run,. and he does not want you to hold back, he wants you to fight him with everything you have, and you will lose.

Newly returned to his mother's side, she did not embrace him, nor praise him. She simply cited to her generals, that her son would always do her bidding, and sat him down next to her as if nothing had occurred. Lanxaad obeyed, but only to bide his time, because as his mother and the court of the Triumvirate Scales all know, one day Lanxaad will move for that throne. He believes he can take it, and he hates his mother that sits upon it, so he shall fight. 

Whether it ends in his heart impaled by her claws, or her skull decorating his new throne room, he will fight.
