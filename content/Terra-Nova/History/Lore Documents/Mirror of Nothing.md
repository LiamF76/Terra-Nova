## Mirror of Nothing

I dip my fingers into the river, it is not water, but it flows the same. Once it was cool and calm, now it barely flows, and the trickle of this creek is fading, my veins are stricken and tight. The creek is clouded and dark, warm, what has burned so deeply the lines that I laid upon myself?

I had forgotten what I made, a gift for someone else? They are thankful, and no matter how many there are, they thank me. It must have been a good gift. I have friends and children, I have never met them, and their faces are empty, but not hollow, simply empty.
