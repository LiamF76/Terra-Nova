## Mirror of Nothing

---

It is perpetual twilight, everything, myself and the worm, are frozen in this endless between, ever moving, and trapped.

I dip my fingers into the river, it is not water, but it flows the same. Once it was cool and calm, now it barely flows, and the trickle of this creek is fading, my veins are stricken and tight. The creek is clouded and dark, warm, what has burned so deeply the lines that I laid upon myself?

I had forgotten what I made, a gift for someone else? They are thankful, and no matter how many there are, they thank me. It must have been a good gift. I have friends and children, I have never met them, and their faces are empty, but not hollow, simply empty.

My eyes glimmer, I feel them twist and shudder at the mirror, but when i look forwards at that glass face, I see nothing. i have no reflection, because I am not. I told Daraan's children in their words, Cogito Ergo, but it was a lie. I am gone, and I have abandoned my children. 

What is my name?

Did I die? Did I ever live? Something happened... something painful, they took from me a life, and tore from me my love. Right from my chest it was ripped, and they left me here... in the deep, with this hollow mirror and shapeless form.

I feel the claws in my skin, tearing at my skin and heart, they want something from me... is it not enough to have killed me? What can they tear now from my corpse that they have not already taken? I scream out to leave me, but none hear me.

I turn my faceless head upwards, and I clasp my fingerless hands, I meditate and commune upwards, and upwards my darling communes with the deep. My favorite leaf. Oh my darling leaf, both perfectly browned like the bark of my trees, and beautifully laden in green like the spring of my soul. The leaf knows I am here, and he speaks kind and sweet comforts.

I am terrified for him, because something is moving. I feel it slither along my legless torso, twisting and tightening around me. Somebody woke up... this vile thing that holds me, what monster holds the mirror before me, forcing me to look upon the NOTHING I have been rended unto. Why would my spawn awaken such a thing? I shall scold them, because soon I will be free. When this thing can no longer hold and suckle from my soul, I shall be free, but so too shall it. I only hope my favorite leaf will be there waiting for me, in the morning.
