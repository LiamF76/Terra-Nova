## To Read You Must be Blind

Being the smallest had its benefits. You need less food, less blood, and less tithe. [[Vesponous]]' horde was nearly smaller than herself. Barely holding more than a hundred coins, and a ceremonial knife she had stolen from a merchant.

It would not be long before her siblings, the big ones, would decide she looked tasty enough for a snack. Her eyes remained low as her mother passed, bring no attention and you will remain unharmed. [[Vesponous]] however did not go unnoticed, as mother passed, her tongue flicked in hunger, perhaps it was not her siblings [[Vesponous]] needed to fear...

That night [[Vesponous]] reminded herself of the benefits of being so small, her mother had always joked she had short fangs, dull and brittle. It seems fangs so small are hard to feel, even when you are being eaten.

[[Vesponous]] puffed soft soothing poison into her mother's nostrils, and ate her bit by bit. She melted her mother's flesh with her breath and slurping it up like a dog drinking muddy water by the road. It was delicious, and with the taste came secrets, which [[Vesponous]] would share to her siblings, killing them with dark magic.

She awoke the next morning alone, and fully grown. Still small, but much more deadly.

---

The tablet was something beyond the world. Like a stone plucked from the earth of a foreign plane. It carried two distinct traces, smells both arcane and material.

One stench caused her to retch and hiss, the smell of Chronality, the smell of a Wizard. Tricksters and deviants, they were not to be trusted.

The other was deep, like the taste of the sand from the ocean so deep the sun have never touched a single grain. It was fermented with secrets, boiled in hidden knowledge, and oh so enticing to her salivating maw.

She tried, but the tablet's icons were unreadable, they were carvings not of this world, and as she tried to read, she failed to see. The longer she attempted, the more she understood, but the price was taken without consent. 

Her scales grew upwards, inwards, piercing the soft whites of her eyes, they bled and were lacerated as she desperately darted her gaze across the tablet. It called her to read more as blood filled her eyes, and the scales grew so tall, they were covered, and eventually removed forever.

She lost her eyes, but gained something more valuable, she could hear the words of the tablet, and felt the call of the deep. [[Vesponous]] answered, and was glad to be blind. What she learned would kill most to see, but to hear of it is not so difficult.
