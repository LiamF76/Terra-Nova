Fading Wonder, Narrated by [[Rakesh]] 

---

It was said once that for every bakery on the corner, you could find a magic shop across the street, and for every two bakers, there was one wizard. From the ancient magical institutes of Redsong to the Ivory Spire, there were thousands of mages. Secular casters who drew power from ritual, knowledge, and runes, and did their learning in libraries lined with history and incantation.

Today you will find no such places. Sovrentia holds only one place of mages now, [[Tamora]], and even it's power fades by the age. There are clerics, paladins, and holy healers in decent supply, but admittedly our numbers dwindle too, because no matter the source, divine or arcane, all magic fades in this world.

---

The Scars. Originally, a terrible line carved across the entire continent, like Arkonil had plunged his blade into the stomach of the world and cut, hoping to spill the guts of all creation into the void. He found no organs or flesh, but magic, magic poured out like blood and bile from Terra-Nova's gut.
