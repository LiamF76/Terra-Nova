
During the last years of the Young Wolves adventures, Maple was reunited with her father Tyran Obliteras. After having trained with Melantha, she had become quite a capable warrior, and this continued with her father's training.

Despite his mistakes, she accepted him when he returned, and together they broke a cycle of violence. They forsook the birthright Tyran earned of the Obelask fortress, and built a new life together.

The plains north of Arkonia, despite being defended by the bastion city of Dragonswatch, were overrun with demons and monsters of all kinds, and together Tyran and Maple made it their mission to cleanse the land of evil. As they traveled and cleared dungeons, the pair made a home of a ruined castle in the peaks along the region's southern boarder, naming it Fort Tyranis. It became their stronghold, and a safe haven for travelers through the mountain range and the entire land.

After many long years of defending Fort Tyranis, alongside cleansing the land of demons and monsters, Tyran Obliteras died in his sleep. Maple held a funeral for her father, attended by many important figures such as Melantha Celestia-Shield (her former master), Alabaster Ironhawk, Ivys Arthur, and many other notable heroes. Maple mourned her father for many months, but reunited with Melantha, she found a new vigor in training once again, and defended her home from many attacks.

During this period, one notable was the Sacking of Tyranis, where the fortress was assailed by a small demon army led by Kor Goroth, the recently returned Abyssal Dragon of Arkonil's army. With the help of Melantha and Alabaster, the Dragon was defeated, and sent back to the Abyss.

In honor of Maple's triumphs, and as a sign of their love, Melantha adopted the growing woman, and named her Maple Celestia. Maple continued on her adventures becoming a renowned warrior through Escarth, and married later in life. Her descendants carried the surname Celestia, and became known knights of the Freehold, which became the Nation of [[Sovrentia]]. 

When Maple passed at the age of 99 years old, in her honor the people of the Freehold renamed the mountains she called home as the Maple Mountains, noting her achievements and the maple tress that dotted the southern slopes of the range.
