The Night of Twisting Stars is a wondrous and powerful magical phenomena. After the rebirth of the Gods by the hand of Melantha the God Mother, the heavens came once again into power, and balance.

As the oceans move with the current, and the moons orbit around Terra-Nova, so too do the stars move about the heavenly sources of magic. Nearly every 100 years, this occurs, and the stars themselves are dashed and pulled through the night sky by an unseen powerful magical current through the space above. What were once discrete points of starlight stretch into continuous lines, each star dragging itself across the sky. Together these streaks curve inward, forming a vast, spiraling pattern overhead, less like a turning dome and more like a wound opening in the firmament.

The motion is not smooth. The star‑lines wobble, kink, and overlap, bending at impossible angles before correcting themselves, as though the sky itself is struggling to remember its shape. The spiral appears deepest at the center of the heavens, where the light grows dense and layered, creating the illusion of depth, as if the stars are arranged on a great, rotating plane rather than a distant sphere.

Below, the world reflects this distortion. Light spills unevenly across stone and water, creating faint, broken reflections that ripple even when the air is still. Shadows stretch improperly, elongating toward the same unseen center that claims the stars above. Vertical structures such as towers, pillars, and spires, stand in stark contrast to the sky’s movement. They remain unmoved witnesses framing the celestial rotation and emphasizing how unnatural the motion truly is.

Observers describe the sensation not as fear, but as disorientation. With no fixed stars left to anchor the eye, direction becomes meaningless. North and south lose their authority. Time feels suspended, measured only by the steady, inexorable turning of the twisted heavens until, slowly, imperceptibly, the lines shorten... and the stars relearn how to be points again.

---

It was said in legend that the Twisting Stars was created once, by the ancient and powerful Timekeepers. An event of astral anomaly meant to reassert and define the linear flow of time. Some who watch it say they can still see the tip of the mystical tower between worlds, rising at the epicenter of all the stars. And atop it there is a figure, a mage who created this event to protect the world evermore after his death, and beyond.

People travel across the land to reach holy sites and watchpoints, hoping to see the Twisting Stars in all their beauty, for it is a once in a lifetime opportunity. Cherish the moment, and remember the sacrifices of your ancestors during the twisting stars, for Elysium is never closer than during this phenomenon.
