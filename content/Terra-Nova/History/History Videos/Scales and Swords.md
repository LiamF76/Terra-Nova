Scales and Swords, Narrated by [[Maro]]

---

I have lived many years, but not long enough to have witnessed any of the Scale Wars with Sovrentia save the third. 

Though the first two conflicts had their heroes and their legends, they actually boiled down to one thing only. In the first, the Triumvirate dragons sent their amries to take the Maple Mountains, and consequentially the province of Ark. In the first war, they succeeded, and controlled the mountain range, though thanks to the Mages of [[Tamora]], the Scales were never able to push further south.

In the second war, Sovrentia took them back, and the Scales were routed past the Mountains, and they haven't been back since.

The last war though, 20 years past, the Third Scale War, that was a change. Many made their legend during this war, heroes, and veterans alike claim this war as their great achievement. I fought myself, though I am less than proud of that, the blood spilt, and the violence required. 

Each of the dragons, the Blue, Green, and Black, took a different front, and with them came three terrible armies. [[Garthrax]] led her legions, regimented and strong enough to rival any army in history, into Sovrentia. She took them through Lightbear, and battled into the hills around the peaceful valley. They broke through the magical protections of the Valley on multiple occasions, but never took full control of the valley.

[[Vesponous]] rallied her cultists and shamans, and called in favors to hire mercenaries, which she then took into the hills of Crest, where they sieged cities and towns for months. Many towns were destroyed forever, and some places are still laced with her poisons. 

[[Gravebinder]], with no army, slithered into the province of Ark. With no forces, Sovrentia could not prioritize fighting a lone dragon, so they left the defense of Ark to the mages of Tamora. The mages armed themselves, and stayed ready, but no fight ever came. The Black dragon came with no army, but left with one, after slinking through the cursed ruins of the city, Arkenstone. He left with a horde of undead, and that ended his participation in the war.

The skirmishes in Westwood were deadly, but small. [[Garthrax]] has a tendency to enjoy doing the fighting herself, and she brings smaller armies to test herself and her skill as a commander. Apparently my own skill in such areas was unknown to me, because I led the front against her. I am proud to say we held that valley, and as the sun rose on the third month of that war, she was blinded by Illuman's wrath, and send flying back to her mountain in defeat.

The real war was in Crest. Having taken the mountains and built a stronghold there, the Green Dragon delighted in the chaos she built. They cut off supply lines, ravaged farms, and destroyed every bit of infrastructure they could find. Her mercenaries, The Blades of the Raven, were foreigners hired from Wahellia. They had strange tactics, and took no prisoners. Many were beastfolk, more like monsters than men to the people of Crest, and they fought like demons. The fighting there lasted two weeks longer, but it ended the very day I had the chance to reach Crest.

[[Thalanil]] of Crest, a descendant of great kings, kings once believed to be dragons, challenged [[Vesponous]] to single combat after a particularly crushing defeat. Naturally the dragon accepted, always one for sport, but she did not fight. She sent a great champion, [[Azark]] the Pale Minotaur. He was champion of the Blood Circle, a warrior bred for blood and ruin who made his wealth in the arenas, and made his strength in the wilds of Wahellia. Terrible and powerful, but [[Thalanil]] was grace and perfection. Azark fought because he loved it, Thalanil fought because he was born for it. Hundreds of years of warrior history, legends and kings, it made him perfect.

Azark was slain, and Vesponous fled north once again.
