The Story of the Uncrowned and Realmcrest, Narrated by Marvos Ferran

---

Some 400 years ago, before the Scar and before Konduell opened the world to us, there was a rebel.

King Adrithyr Realmcrest was long lived, thanks to his family's sorcerous dragon blood, some 200 years he reigned over Estor. Estor spanned the entirety of Southern Escarth, and Adrithyr did as well. He took mistresses, bed them but never wed them, and sired hundreds of children.

Now if you hear that and think Adrithyr is not a man of honor, brace yourself for the worst of it. Adrithyr didn't have a taste for things like steaks and chickens, he loves blood. To good King Realmcrest, there was no sweeter treat than the flesh of a child, specifically those of his own children. He was a twisted and sick tyrant, but he fucked and feasted as he ruled.

He went uncontested for many years, had a legitimate son called Prince Lukas Realmcrest who he almost loved, and a wife who tolerated his barbarity named Annabelle Mournraine. His court kept his sick practice a secret, hunting down any children the King didn't eat, but one slipped through their grasp.

A baby named Ivar. See Ivar's mother, well Adrithyr had somewhat of a fondness for her, so when the poor girl took pregnant he locked her up in the tallest tower he could find in his castle. She cried and wept every day, they said she cried so loud the gods heard her, and so long they almost cared.

Eventually she gave birth, and King Adrithyr held his boy, looking down at his tan skin and dark hair, he must've for the first time in his fucked up life, felt some kind of pity for a kid he planned to eat, so he decided to wait.

Ivar's mother held him and sung to her boy, thinking she had been chosen by the Gods for some divine reason, to be spared the cruelty Adrithyr usually dispensed to his harem, but she wasn't so lucky. King whispered to his Court Wizard, Kaela, and she waltzed over and slit the girl's throat as she sang, covered the baby in his mother's blood.

Ivar lay in his crib, still covered in that blood, for a day, until my ancestor Lord Ferran of Felldoor, snuck in and saved him.

Took many years, but Drew Ferran raised Ivar, and taught him how to fight, how to live, and how to be a good man. Ivar worked as a caravan guard and adventurer for years, never knowing where he came from, but when King Adrithyr visited Lord Ferran, unaware of what had happened to that baby, everything changed.

Ivar saw that bald headed fire wizard Kaela, and it triggered something in him. He couldn't place it but he knew her, and he pestered Ferran every day after the King left.

Eventually of course, because he loved Ivar like his own son, Ferran broke, and told Ivar everything. He showed Ivar the records, all the mothers and babies killed, every single one, all dead and devoured, except Ivar.

Ivar fought Ferran in a rage, took every ounce of willpower the boy had not to kill his adoptive father. He had Adrithyr's rage in his heart too it seemed, but he spared my ancestor thank the Gods. He left Felldoor and took to the wilds of Escarth. The priests will tell you Ivar's rage was so deep it shook the earth itself. The historians will say it was the day Estor's doom was sealed.

Ivar swore vengeance on the very bones of the world, he swore it to every God. He begged for the strength to kill the Dragon blooded tyrant Adrithyr, he begged for justice, for revenge, and for peace for his siblings those who had died and yet to be born.

The Gods in the heavens didn't answer... but a God from the Abyss did.
