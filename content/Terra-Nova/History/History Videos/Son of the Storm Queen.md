The Son of the Storm Queen, Narrated by Abraham

---

In my lifetime I've seen two wars with the Triumvirate Scales, but in total there have been three. Each has been worse than the last. Many great warriors and terrible beasts have fought on both sides of these conflicts, including the Dragon Rulers themselves. One mortal however, matches the cruelty and savage nature of those flying serpents. Lanxaad, the Son of the Storm Queen.

Half-Dragon son of Garthrax, he must've been a big egg born from some unholy type of breeding, because that man came out eviler than sin itself. Scales blue like his mother, and with breath to match.

Lanxaad led the Storm Dragon's honor guard, a group of elite Dragonborn knights known as the Azure Battalion. They were fierce, and their leader set that example.

First time I saw him on the field was in the Gilded Peaks, they were making through the mountain pass, hoping to seize a gold mine. My regimen and I had to hold the pass until reinforcements arrived. We were blessed by Vulcan to make it through the night and see dawn.

The other men thought Garthrax had landed on the mountain herself, because Lanxaad pierced the watchtower with a beam of lightning from his maw before we even knew we were under attack. He tore down the entire fortress without being in range of arrows, then he leapt into the sky on scaled wings. That's when I thought I was going to die.

He whipped trough the air with blinding speed, arrows glancing harmlessly off his skin, like a cascade of death he landed before me, killing two of my companions before he had even drawn his axe.

He noticed my officer's pin, because he smiled, terrible toothy grin, "You will make a fine trophy for my mother." I sweat I shit right then and there.

We fought, but barely. Many people know me, the Sword Sage, the man who dueled Melantha Celestia-Shield to a stand still in the tournament at Highstride, and many other bullshit titles. I could take any man on level ground, give us both a sword and by the Gods I can beat anyone. Not Lanxaad. Because he is not a man, he is a force of nature.

I escaped, managing to survive the 15 minutes it took for reinforcements to arrive up the pass, but I only survived. He broke both m arms, all my fucking ribs, hell he broke any bone in my body he could find.

Worst part was, he ripped the officer's pin off my cloak clasp during the fight, he was so fast. Bastard was playing with me. So if you learn anything from this, confidence is key. You could beat any man or woman in single combat, but you'd still be torn to pieces by Lanxaad.

Someday heroes greater than I will venture forth into the Scales and finally slay those dragon fucks. My best advice, wait until Lanxaad croaks from age.
