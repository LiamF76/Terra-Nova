## Hunters of Kabir
*A Campaign of Ruins, Wishes, and Reckless Ambition*

![[Hunters of Kabir Primer-1784234085686.webp|500x750]]

---

Hundreds of years ago, a Noble of an Elven house carried the key to an ancient vault, within that vault lay untold riches and power. That Noble was slain in a great rebellion, and the key was lost forever, destroyed. Now generations later, a new Elf Noble, Kilyn of House Araran, has inherited ownership of the vault, and desires its bounty. Kilyn has made a dangerous bargain with an ancient enemy of her House, a Wish Dragon. Kilyn has bargained for the Wish Dragon to open the vault, in exchange for Kilyn discovering the Wish Dragon's true name. The only link to the Dragon's true name was held by one of the nearly forgotten members of her house, Kabir Araran. To decipher the Dragon's name, you must assist her to find his relics, and discover the secrets of the past.

### The World, Briefly
Set on the continent of Ikon, this campaign features diverse settings and themes. From the enchanting magical city of Thylor Aethil, flying within the clutches of a hundred waterfalls, to the thick wild jungles, laden with monsters and magical spirits, you will deal with danger and mystery alike. 
The wilds of Agamarth are the harshest in the world, laden with more monsters and magical anomalies than any other land. You are as likely to find a nest of Wyverns as you are a zone of enchanted air, waiting to explode when you light your campfire.

### Making Your Character
Standard PHB races/classes, plus [any sourcebooks you allow]. Every character needs: a professional reason to be chasing treasure (academic, smuggler, disgraced ex-something, treasure hunter for hire), and a personal reason the Sable Compass matters to _them_ specifically.

### Table Tone
Deadly traps, breathless chases, banter heavy combat. Death is possible but we're leaning "swashbuckling" over "grimdark" — expect near-misses more than tragic ends. Adjust to your table's actual taste.

### Three Questions for Session Zero
1. What's the closest you've ever come to real treasure — and what did it cost you?
2. Who taught you to do what you do, and are they proud of you now?
3. What's one rumor about the Sable Compass you've heard that you _don't_ believe... yet?
