Hunters of Kabir is a treasure hunting D&D campaign set in Terra-Nova, on the continent of Ikon.

## Ideas

- Artifacts of Kabir, need to be gathered to locate the wishing dragon
	- Kabir's Arrowhead: Weapon that killed Kabir, not one he wielded
	- Kabir's Lantern: Magical lantern that shows another map when its light touches the original map the party was given
	- Kabir's Eye: Magical eye that can see the entrance to the vault
- Mysteries surrounding artifacts / story
	- Magical temple that only appears under certain phases of the moon, spirit tied to it has been stalking the party on only New Moons.
	- Blessed relic that turns out to be cursed
- 


## But and Therefore

- tbd *BUT* tbd **THEREFORE** tbd

- The Elf Noble wants to open the vault *BUT* the key (the only way in) was destroyed **THEREFORE** he makes a deal with a Wish Dragon who can open the vault.
- The Elf Noble wants to open the vault *BUT* the Wish Dragon's bargain dictates he has to find the Dragon's nest using a map to earn his prize **THEREFORE** he hires the party to help him find the Relics of Kabir to read the map.
- Campaign starts here.
- The party needs to find the Relics of Kabir *BUT* they don't know where the relics are **THEREFORE** they have to find Kabir's last student who disappeared in the jungles of Agamarth hundreds of years ago. (Elf Noble will give them the place to go look)
- They party searches for Kabir's last student in the jungle *BUT* his path leads into a dangerous section of the jungle where he hid himself (he betrayed Kabir's teaching, believing Kabir's connection to Wish Dragons was wrong and that Wish Dragons are evil and dangerous) **THEREFORE** the party has to brave dangerous combat with beasts to reach his hiding place.
- Kabir's last student agrees to tell the party where the relics are *BUT* he promises they will regret finding them **THEREFORE** the party can travel to the location of one of the 3 artifacts.
