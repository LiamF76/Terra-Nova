
## Backstory

1. start with thing I sent in our discord Character Building, followed by this: 3 years after above event. Gavin and Jerran are on an excursion to clean the shrine of a nearby elk spirit and make an offering. The elk spirit is a bit aloof and it took them a lot longer than they expected to finish their task. When they return to Starnlow, the village is a mess. Several villagers lay bruised and unconscious on the ground, doors to buildings are left ajar or ripped off their hinges. Gavin tells Jerran to start tending to the villagers and getting them somewhere safe while he investigates more. While Jerran is tending to the wounded, Gavin starts toward the elder's(?) house when they hear a crash from inside. Gavin gets a large head start and Jerran follows after, but arrives as the confrontation is ending. Jerran only gets a brief glimpse of the intruder before he disappears, escaping the two of them (not sure what would be appropriate for how this guy would escape). The elder lay dead on the floor, the interior of the home absolutely trashed. Gavin refuses to tell Jerran anything he may know about what happens, insists they gather the townsfolk and make sure everyone else is okay. He tells Jerran he has to pursue the intruder, alone. Insisting that Jerran would be in too far over his head and that Starnlow will need someone else to watch over the town. Gavin says he will return to Starnlow, but hasn't been seen for 4 years. In that time Jerran has taken 3 students of his own to be more capable protectors of Starnlow, so that he may leave on his own quest to protect people of [[Sovrentia]] and find Gavin.
2. then here's two briefer lists
3. Primer - Jerran Helt is an orphan and has effectively been raised by the entire village of Starnlow. Jerran's father died in the war in which [[Garthrax]] led troops into Lightbear while Jerran's mother was still pregnant. His mother contracted an illness that was improperly treated and led to her death when Jerran was 6. Gavin Ferwig is a wandering knight who would occasionally stop in Starnlow and help the townsfolk, his stays became longer and more frequent over the years, and he became a positive role model for Jerran. 15 years old - Gavin is temporarily incapacitated from an injury. In a monster attack, Jerran takes Gavin's sword and armor to inspire the townsfolk into taking a stand and they are victorious. Gavin takes Jerran as his apprentice and begins to train him. 18 years old - Jerran and Gavin return from an excursion to find an intruder who had harmed many villagers, killed the village elder and ransacked his house. He escapes unharmed, Gavin decides to track him down alone and leave Jerran in his stead. 18-22 - Jerran trains a few students of his own to be capable protectors of Starnlow, during this time he meets a Druid named Selphie Faquinal. Selphie asks for Jerran's help in a few ecosystem related missions (Overbreeding Ochre Jellies, Culling diseased deer population). 22-23 - Jerran sets out on his own to help the people of Lightbear and investigate Gavin's disappearance. Jerran runs into Selphie once again and assists in her dealing with an elder tree that was spontaneously Awakened and has demands. 23 - Jerran meets Thalia Celestiashield
