## Backstory

Dragons are as dangerous as they are unpredictable, a fact that Sobek, child of the Living Storm, embodies beyond his knowing. To him though, he is a simple refuge of the Scales' most recent war, someone who through his trauma desires to be the protector for others that he never had. In the Westwood Valley he is seen as a kind and gentle figure who gives more than he takes and always has a story to tell the children. Outside of home, however, he has a more monstrous side. The blood of a pure dragon flows through him, granting him great strength and power at the cost of a rage he curses the thought of. He wants nothing more than to be rid of this power, but in a time where many must struggle to live, he resolves to channel it to protect those who cannot protect themselves. Perhaps one day he will discover the source of his rage, but until then he will keep fighting for the smiles that await him at home.


### Sobek's Parents

Sobek is the child of Garthrax the Blue Dragon Queen of the Triumvirate Scales, and a great sea storm sprint known as **Kehtoh**, The Great Unending Thing
