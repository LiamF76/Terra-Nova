## Backstory

Donna grew up an orphan in the misty Valley, taken in and trained by a local guild that posted hunting jobs to keep the wilds in balance. From a young age, she learned to track, hunt, and gather every usable part of her kills—meat, hide, and rare ingredients prized for their subtle magical qualities. During one job, she saved a wounded dire wolf pup from a pack of monsters and raised it as her own, naming it Coco; the two have been inseparable ever since, working in perfect sync. Lately, though, the animals she once understood have begun acting strangely—some even twisted by an unnatural corruption—so Donna left the Valley to travel with a party, determined to uncover the source and protect the balance she was raised to defend.
