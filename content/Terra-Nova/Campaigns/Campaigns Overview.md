
This document outlines what the folder Campaigns contains. This folder is mainly for my own horrible memory and to keep myself organized. I intend to create a game of each length and play through them to test my skill, find what I enjoy most, and see what my players enjoy. See below for more info.


---
### Campaign Organization

Campaigns will be organized by their session lengths. This was an idea from the Youtube channel [Mystic Arts](https://www.youtube.com/@Dadi-MysticArts). 


---
### Campaign lengths:

- One Shots, single day games
- Two Shots, one shots but a second session, just if people enjoyed it so much we wanna play again.
- 4-8 Session Games
- 8-12 Session Games
- 12+ Session Games
- Play by Post
