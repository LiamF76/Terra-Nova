Hello and welcome to another exciting campaign in the world of Terra-Nova! I am Liam your humble Dungeon Master, and you will be by intrepid heroes on this amazing journey!

## Gameplay and System

- Dungeons & Dragons 5e 2024
- Starting level 5
- If you are not continuing with a character from the introductory campaign (The Valley of Broken Oaths), please discuss with Liam on creating a character with a tie / connection to this one. Most importantly, make someone who wants to be here.

---

## Tone and Themes

- **The Dying World**: Catastrophe, magical collapse, climate and world change will be prevalent challenges and issues your character will contend with. How do you cope with the fade of wonder and life, and what will you do in response?
- **A Bittersweet Farewell**: Someone important to us has died, and the age of great heroes seems over. Grief and fond memory sit alongside high-stakes battle and desperate struggle.
- **Time & Memory as Battlefields**: This is literal and thematic. You'll fight through echoes, pasts, and possibilities, not just physical space.
- **High Fantasy with Eldritch and Cosmic Horror**: Magical landscapes and monsters alongside beasts and spells beyond mortal comprehension. You stand against the aspects of Nothing, and will be sharpened, or broken by them.
- **Corruption vs. reclamation**: Twisted lands and fallen kingdoms, expect exploration of ruin and the question of whether it can be redeemed. Will you save those being pushed down by their rulers, or demand they fight for their right to exist?

In keeping with our general campaign themes, roleplay between character including heartfelt connections and difficult arguments is encouraged. We will explore your past alongside that of the world and characters, so please be ready to look inwards as well as ahead! You are the main characters, be legendary!

# The War of the Timekeeper's Soul

The legendary hero: 
#### Ivys Arthur, 
*White Wizard, Purple Wizard, Lesser Demon, First and Last Timekeeper, Son of Horus Windsong, Lord of the Orichalcum Throne, Slayer of Kairos, Bane of Arkonil, Beloved of the Fairy Moon, and Master of Chronurgy*

Has passed on from this world. After a more than 400 year lifespan, his Abyssal-fortified body has finally fallen, and he lies entombed in a coffin of calcified temporal dead-zone.

Many of you know Ivys Arthur as players, and in keeping with his insane legacy and achievements, he has devised a cunning plan for his passing and afterlife.

He has planned to excise his mortal soul (gained through years of life as a Lesser Demon), from the demon soul within him. With the help of Al Graui and Melantha Celestia-Shield, his soul will be split, allowing his mortal soul to pass on to the hands of his beloved Nysria to a peaceful afterlife, and his demon soul (its identity unknown) to pass on the the Abyss and live again. His friends have questioned this plan, knowing with his soul intact he could prolong his life, and easily destroy the demon, but Ivys stood firm that both things must be allowed to move on as the world intends.

Many groups desire control of a soul like Ivys' and as a result both Melantha's companions, and representatives of the Abyss have been invited to the Purple Tower for the funeral, where they have agreed to act in peace and in the interest of the shared goal: protecting Ivys' soul.

## Our Quest

With Ivys, nothing ever goes to plan. Chaos ensues, and while Melantha and Ryfon endeavor to find solutions to the seeming end of the world, you will search for signs of Ivys, and make war against the followers of evil as he once did.

In an effort to rediscover the triumphant past, and stop the instrument of evil, Aldros, from possessing Ivys' soul, you will quest and work against him.

You will scour ruins and forgotten lands for pieces of lore, legendary artifacts, and to help those in need who are being crushed beneath the heel of the forces that rule. Powerful Spirits may grant you boons, and Echoes of darkness will seek to curse and hinder your quest against their leader Aldros.

## Our Setting

This campaign will cover multiple settings and major locations across it's length. In the interest of not spoiling great reveals here are the major ones you will know of.

- *The Triumvirate Scales*: A land once peaceful, green, and good. This Kingdom of Dragons is now alien and corrupted. The landscape is twisted by dark magics, filled with undead who serve the Gravebinder, bloodthirsty warlords who follow Garthrax, and goblin cults who worship Vesponous.

- *Ivys' Chronovault*: An endless structure that spans space and time itself, Ivys' Chronovault contains multitudes of his history, artifacts, and enemies. You will face great foes of the past to unlock Ivys' secrets, and gather information and power to protect his soul

- *The Throne of Aldros*: Through the secrets of the deep, Aldros has manifested one of the most terrible powers in history. Last employed by the legendary evil Thladrax, the Hand of Night, Aldros has constructed his own Throne World within the Ascendant Plane (similar to a God's plane within the Celestial Plane). Adventure through Aldros' memories, and discover his powers and weakness through battle with his many Aspects.
