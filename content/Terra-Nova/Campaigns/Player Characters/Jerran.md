# Jerran Helt — Backstory

## Extended Backstory Narrative

*Start with the event previously shared in Discord character building notes.*

**Three years later**, Gavin Ferwig and Jerran Helt are on an excursion to cleanse the shrine of a nearby elk spirit and make an offering. The elk spirit is aloof, and the ritual work takes far longer than expected.

When they return to **Starnlow**, the village is in chaos.

Several villagers lie bruised and unconscious in the streets. Doors to homes are left ajar or ripped from their hinges. Seeing the state of the village, Gavin orders Jerran to begin tending to the wounded and getting them somewhere safe, while he investigates further.

As Jerran helps the injured, Gavin heads toward the elder’s home. Moments later, a loud crash echoes from inside the house. Gavin rushes ahead with a significant head start. Jerran follows but arrives just as the confrontation is ending.

Jerran only catches a **brief glimpse of the intruder** before they escape. 
The village elder lies dead on the floor, and the home has been utterly ransacked.

Gavin refuses to explain what he knows or what he suspects. Instead, he insists that they gather the villagers and ensure no one else is hurt. Shortly after, Gavin tells Jerran that he must pursue the intruder **alone**, claiming Jerran would be in over his head and that Starnlow needs someone else to protect it.

Gavin promises he will return.

He has not been seen in **four years**.

In Gavin’s absence, Jerran remains in Starnlow, training students to better defend the village. After preparing three capable protectors to take his place, Jerran finally leaves Starnlow to protect the people of [[Sovrentia]]—and to search for Gavin.

---

## Primer

- **Jerran Helt** is an orphan, raised collectively by the people of Starnlow.
- Jerran’s father died in the war in which [[Garthrax]] led troops into Lightbear, before Jerran was born.
- Jerran’s mother died when he was 6 due to an illness that was improperly treated.
- **Gavin Ferwig** is a wandering knight who frequently stopped in Starnlow to aid its people.
- Over time, Gavin’s visits became longer and more frequent, and he became a central role model for Jerran.

---

## Timeline

- **Age 15** 
  Gavin is temporarily incapacitated by an injury. During a monster attack on Starnlow, Jerran dons Gavin’s sword and armor, inspiring the townsfolk to take a stand. The village survives. 
  Gavin formally takes Jerran on as his apprentice.
- **Age 18** 
  Jerran and Gavin return from an excursion to find Starnlow attacked. An intruder has injured villagers, killed the village elder, and ransacked their home. The intruder escapes. 
  Gavin decides to pursue them alone, leaving Jerran to protect the village.
- **Age 18–22** 
  Jerran trains three students to become capable protectors of Starnlow. 
  During this time, he meets **Selphie Faquinal**, a druid, and assists her with ecosystem-focused missions:
  Addressing overbreeding Ochre Jellies 
  Culling a diseased deer population
- **Age 22–23** 
  Jerran sets out into Lightbear to:
  Help those in need 
  Investigate Gavin’s disappearance 
  He encounters Selphie again and helps negotiate with an elder tree that has spontaneously awakened and developed demands of its own.
- **Age 23** 
  Jerran meets **Thalia Celestia-Shield**.
