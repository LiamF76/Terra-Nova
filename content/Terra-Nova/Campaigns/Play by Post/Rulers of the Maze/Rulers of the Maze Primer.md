
Iramo. The Land of Mazes. Once it was a different land, but those days ended many tens of thousands of years ago. Now there is only the maze.

![[Pasted image 20260223105812.png]]

Built by the people known as 'The Ancients', the entire continent of Iramo is consumed by a massive maze. Some sections covered, some are open to the sky, but everything is the Maze. The Maze holds endless secrets, powerful and evil magics, legendary monsters, and items the Ancients hid away from the world. Iramo holds impossible technology left behind by the Ancients, superstructures and devices beyond understanding. 

Society of Iramo is centered around the Maze, and the Rulers. The Rulers of Iramo are powerful supernatural entities who host nations of slave subjects. The Rulers hold only tow goals, shared between all of them. Scour the maze for its lost power, and force their slaves to worship and build monuments to their greatness. 

![[Pasted image 20260223113457.png]]

The people of Iramo live in abject poverty. The only luxury goods worth noting are powerful drugs, opiates and psychedelics, to numb the pain of existence. Those who find secrets or achieve great deeds in the name of their Ruler are given special permissions. These boons often include nicer housing, servants of their own, pleasure slaves, sometimes they rise high enough to even be given gifts of power from the Ruler.

The Rulers each have an inner circle of their most elite slaves and worshippers, known as the Cabal. These individuals gain some amount of trust from their owner, and are the greatest leaders in the Rulers war for the power of the Maze.

Within the Maze cities, monster nests, vaults, and dungeons of all types line the walls and halls. Endless adventure and wealth awaits, but those who seek it are beholden to the cruel and depraved Rulers.

Our story highlights the life of a newly purchased slave being sent to Iramo. Most people outside the island continent do not even know Iramo exists, those that do know it as a dark and cursed land.

You are one such slave, who has been purchased by a Ruler of your choice, and is being sent to work and scour for your new owner. Your land of origin, backstory, and reason why you are a slave at all is entirely up to you.

Our story will cover life as a slave citizen of Iramo, scouring the vast Maze, and interacting with the people and Rulers.
