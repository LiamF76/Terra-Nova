---

## Overview

The Black Sun Sets. Aldred Blacksun has broken, shattered in two. it is not the first time his mind has suffered since achieving Godhood, but divinity lies heavy on his head, and his neck is bending too deeply. The divine in him demands punishment for all creation, as is his domain, but the mortal holds the singular pang of guilt, and fear.
That fear echoes, and for a moment he broke free, begged Emrys to free him from this, and to break the divinity for good. His paramour obliged, and now they must secret themselves from the realm of Pennance, somewhere safe, somewhere secret, somewhere to kill a God.
This Play by Post campaign will cover guilt, fear, internal struggle, battle with divine and alien forces, and freedom from a mantle never truly desired.

## Ideas

- Emrys quest to free Aldred from Godhood
- Piece of mortal Aldred breaks through the veil and begs Emrys to free him
- Emrys is called to Thalun by an unknown source, rumored to be there Illuman shattered a God in the past
	- This source will be revealed as [[Ir Kaska]], dragon disciple of [[Iridescen]], they will be a villain in the campaign, but first an ally
- [[Ir Kaska]] wants to help Emrys free Aldred, hoping to use Aldred's divinity to free Iridescen from his prison on Thalun. 
	- Ir Kaska puts forward a fake motive that his teacher has been captured and imprisoned by a Wizard, a Time Bender who froze his teacher in a moment within a temple. Freeing his teacher can be done by absorbing Aldred's divinity, it can help them both. 
- Kyr Asaar, the Guardian of the Moment. Kyr protects the imprisoned God, the trapped King of Mantles, Iridescen, who he believes to be Ozymandias.
