Emrys Blacksun, Angel of the Willing Repentant, and Aldred Blacksun, God of Penance and Punishment, have lived in harmony for nearly 200 years since Emrys died, and was reborn as Aldred's Chief Angel.

Having lived in such divine domestic bliss for years has bonded the pair deeply, their mortal love having blossomed into something truly divine.

In the material plane, change is stirring. Aldred's Church, the Black Grail, has grown into a religion of small but respectable size. Often incorporated as a minor but important section of major pantheons, and even a following all its own. Many still regard the Black Sun with negativity, due to its dubious origins.

During the last 20 years, Aldred has undergone minor change, as Gods do when their follower change as well. It has been slow and labored, but it has indeed been noticed by Emrys.

This change culminated in a painful and defining moment. A powerful follower of Aldred, formerly of the Black Grail, now leading a group called the Obsidian Fountain, has begun a different kind of teaching. His faith is harsh and cold, unforgiving, and the only fair penalty for almost any crime, is death. They dispose of this often, and indiscriminately.

Aldred was overcome with violent intent, and in a brief moment of clarity, he cast Emrys out of his realm, sending him to Terra-Nova in a desperate gambit to halt his corruption. He declared his love, and that he was sending Emrys towards someone who could help. That is where our story begins.
