## Raid Boss Ideas

- **Storm Elemental**: Blue: storm elemental, fast and chain damage, does low damage but hits multiple targets
	- lightning sweep, large moving line of lightning that forces players to take cover
	- 4 conductive pillars, elemental sweep attack deals an additional d4 damage per active pillar, pillars can be deactivated by attacking (grounding) them but deasl 1d6 lightning damage
	- storm node adds, little lightning balls, 1 shot kill but fly and move fast, ranged attack
	- conductive overload at half health, metal weapons deal an extra d6 damage, metal armor has disadvantage on DEX saves
- **Mutant Goblin**: Green: mutant goblin, big and strong
	- end of each round wart explodes and makes 2 goblins, ground underneath boss becomes 1d6 poison damage and difficult terrain
	- at half health drinks a potion of dragons blood and his hand transforms into a poison spitting dragon head
	- any mini goblins hit by the poison from the dragon head can attack when hit
- **Deathsinger Witch**: Black: Undead witch
	- 10 turn countdown Deathsong spell, big damage / instant kill?
	- end turn standing on resonance plates to take 1d6 psychic damage, reduces the count by 1 per player on the plate
	- 2 undead choir, at the end of each of their turns adds 1 to the Deathsong
