
This is a brainstorm document for my Kingmaker campaign idea. This is a potential campaign to run in Terra-Nova during the Age of Divines.

## Brief Overview

The party are servants / loyal to one potential next Sovereign of [[Sovrentia]]. I will compile a list of potential candidates and let my players pick which ruler they wish to follow. 



## Potential Sovereigns

- **Prince Tyran Celestia III**: A descendant of Maple Celestia who has inherited his position as a leader of a state in [[Sovrentia]]. He is kind hearted and young, a genuine good person. He is not concerned enough with great matters of war and safety as he is with people's happiness and wellbeing. He is also very attached to his creature comforts and does not like accompanying the party on quests that would legitimize his case for Sovereign. (See [[Celestia Family Tree]] for more info) He was named for his ancestor Tyran the Obliterator, hiss parents hoped he would restore their land to greatness through the strength the ancient Obliterator once possessed.
