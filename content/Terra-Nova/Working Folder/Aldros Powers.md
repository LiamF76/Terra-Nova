This document outlines the abilities of [[Aldros]] Wyrm-Chosen.

## Fractal Nothing

Aldros possess a fractal of [[Khal Daraan]]'s Nothing, developed from what he was taught by Vesponous, which she learned from [[The Iridescent Tablets]].

Nothing is not magic, it is truth, and will. It cannot be blocked or countered, only overpowered, or proven false. Aldros possess limited power over Nothing, but what he does possess makes him a threat to even the greatest heroes.

#### The Crux

The Crux is a power of Position, Aldros has the power to bind a being to a specific moment in time, freezing them totally, they do not age, they cannot move, and they do not live or die. They are absolutely removed from the linear flow of time, and return the moment Aldros releases the Crux.

#### The Undertow

The Undertow is the slow and ever present pull of entropy. Aldros can accelerate chaos, decay, death, and even aging. The most potent manifestation of the Undertow is his ability to enforce hopelessness, making a moment feel both unending and battles unwinnable. It is ambient despair. Those who succumb to the Undertow can escape only through their own willpower, of which there are few that have enough.

#### Duplicity

The strongest manifestation of Aldros' Nothing, is Aspects. He can create Nothing clones of himself that are able to act as he controls them, allowing him to be in many places at once. They are not as strong as he is, but they are still formidable foes to all but the strongest warriors in history.

## Aspects

Able to create manifestations of himself called Aspects. Aspects possess a fraction of his power, and are controlled by Aldros. The Aspects are spectral skeletal manifestations of Aldros that fight and enact his will.
