### What kind of lore to write:

- **EVENT** – something that happens (rare, cyclical, or singular)
- **SYSTEM** – something that operates, drains, breaks, or persists
- **LOCATION** – a place that shapes behavior or belief
- **STATE** – what the world feels like now
- **CONSEQUENCE** – what changed because of something else
- **INSTITUTION** – who controls, manages, or reacts to the world
- **ARTIFACT / TEXT** – something left behind
- **THRESHOLD** – something that almost happened

---

- **What is happening?** → Event
- **How does this work (or fail)?** → System
- **Where does this matter most?** → Location
- **What does living here feel like now?** → State
- **What did we lose or gain?** → Consequence
- **Who benefits or enforces this?** → Institution
- **What proof remains?** → Artifact / Text
- **What nearly changed everything?** → Threshold
