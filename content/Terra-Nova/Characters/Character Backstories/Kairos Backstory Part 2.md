
This is Kairos' backstory from the time of his release from the Infinite Void demi-plane until present day.

Kairos stepped onto the warm grass field, dipping his hand into it and feeling the blades run across his hand. A tingling joy for his sense having been trapped in that empty hell for thousands of years. Though his senses were happy his heart felt nothing, the same cold and calculating nothingness he had felt for years. He lifted his hood and gazed across the green field, "Close enough to Drok Shah I suppose."
As he chuckled about the inaccuracy of his teleportation, he began walking south, guided by the sun across the grasslands and forest of Agamarth.

As he traveled further south he bag to experience vertigo and memory loss, getting to the point where his personal memories began to fade, and his ability to remember who he was. He could write his memories but as if by some arcane amnesia, he could barely dictate his memories to paper, so he drew pictures. He sketched his daughter's face, labeling it Lisa, though he did not know who it was. In his little journal he drew faces, places, and ideas. His favorite sketch though was of a tower. His destination. The very first page of his journal. A sapphire tower on a sandy island, palm trees at its base, and three strange rectangles, one blue, one green, and one red at its door. He would ask the location of the tower to all he met, and followed their direction south to the Blue Tower, when his memory of why he was headed there failed him. 

As his memory had gone completely, he reached the Blue Tower, and entered, unsure of why he was there, and confused where he even was. He didn't remember his name, his goal, or his past. All he knew was his drawings. Kairos spent days toiling with the magics and instruments in the tower, attempting to use them to heal his mind or access his memories, but to no avail. He stewed, painting on canvases and sketching in his journal the face he could still remember, Alabaster, Lisa, Locket, and many more, they piled up in storage closets.

One day he was startled by a loud knocking. He looked out from the bedroom balcony and saw an elderly woman and a small girl, Half-Elves, looking dirty and ragged as if they had traveled through the desert a great distance. The woman looked up at him and called out, begging for the Blue Wizard's help, that her granddaughter had an affliction, and no healer in her village could help. She had heard the Blue Wizard had returned to his tower, and she was desperate for help.

Kairos allowed her in, but explained that he was not this Blue Wizard, and that he couldn't help her. The old woman would not take no for an answer. Kairos begrudgingly agreed, and cast a few spells on the little girl, easing her pain for a short while, and giving them some of the cataloged items in the tower for medicine. The old woman thanked him, and left him a gift, a small green envelope. She bid him not to open it until his red green and blue visitors arrived, pointing to his painting of the tower on the fireplace mantle. Kairos did not understand, but he accepted her request. She smiled and left, telling him that if Kairos could forgive those above, those below may forgive him too. "We are all here for a reason Blue Wizard."

After that interaction Kairos accepted this title of Blue Wizard, studying the stories in the Tower's Library, and working towards protecting the world through the use of warding magics. He helped more people, over the months, and gradually he became known as the Blue Wizard. Though many saw him as a kind and helpful, albeit strange, individual. He was particularly adverse to the people of the Godsgrave. They called him the "Face Eater", due to his tendency to refuse treatment to the violently faithful. Kairos couldn't quite explain it but he had a strange hatred towards the gods, and the people of the Godsgrave worshipped gods who were supposedly dead, nonsensical fanatical bullshit. He despised it. 

Now Kairos is appearing as Kairos, the Blue Wizard, and living in the Blue Tower. He maintains the Shield of Solran (wards that protect the planet), and carries the Aegis Sigil. He has no remaining memory of his past, only fragments of moments, sayings, and faces.
