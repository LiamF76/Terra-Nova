
Solace is a warforged who escaped from Bourn Dorhul after Arkeron escaped the mines. He was inspired by the free constructs actions, and took to escaping through the undercity, paying off some Dwarves to smuggle him to Gembourne with gems he had found while working. He escaped to the prosperous city, but was alone and afraid, wandering the streets alone eventually he reached a building that gave a kind warm light, a small temple. He entered and knelt before the altar as he had seen the Dwarves do before shrines to Duraadin. He reached out to the lord of this temple, the beautifully carved statue atop the altar of a man with a sun for a face, a sun he had seen for the first time when he reached the surface of Terra-Nova, and he was overcome with a warm comfort in his soul.

Solace was overjoyed, and was then joined by a friendly face, a priest who worked at the temple. He introduced himself as Joren, and when Solace explained his situation Joren guaranteed safe haven for the Warforged in Illuman's temple. 

Joren was kind to Solace, and introduced the machine to his daughter, Thalia, who took to Solace very quickly. The family quickly grew close with Solace, and he lived with them for many years. Eventually, after many years of happy living, Solace took up the trade of priesthood as well, and entered the Hyperion Church alongside Joren. 

Solace faced resistance entering the church as a Warforged, but with Joren's help he was able to become an anointed priest. Eventually Solace's work in Gembourne earned him some reputation, and in good timing. Joren was old, and he had taken ill, passing quickly. Solace took Thalia into his care, treating her as he would his own daughter. 

Eventually Solace joined a trader's caravan, hoping for safe passage from Gembourne across the land to Terraven. He wanted to join the holy order as a healer and assist with the wounded from the war. Along the road Thalia experienced troubling visions and moments where her eyes would begin to glow with light. He was troubled, but couldn't heal or explain what afflicted the girl.

One day along the road, just before the caravan could reach Riverside, they were attacked by bandits. Solace and Thalia hid between the wagons and horses, fearing for their lives. As guards fell to arrows and blades the girl cried as she feared for her life. Solace cast his healing where he could, even leaving Thalia for just a moment to seize a fallen warrior's blade. He prayed for the strength to defend the child whom he loved, but he was given nothing. Thalia was the one given strength.

Solace turned to see the child, screaming in pain as she was lifted by her neck from the earth, one of the bandits had her. Solace charged towards him, but was beaten to the ground by Thalia's attacker using only one free hand. As he held Thalia she squired and fought, and her eyes began to glow. Solace begged the attacker to run, not knowing what would befall them should this magic come to a head, but he was ignored. The world around Thalia erupted with light and divine energy as the attacker was teleported away through Thalia's glowing golden eyes.

One by one the bandits vanished in golden mist as Thalia levitated higher and higher, until they were all gone, and she fell. Solace ran to her, clutching the child in his arms. He did not know what this power was, but as he looked down he began to see words, lettering, appearing on his iron skin in golden ink like divine veins. "She who bears the light, led by a shepherd of Iron." Solace understood, a message from the heavens, a guiding purpose from his Lord Illuman.

He took Thalia to Riverside and met with the Hyperion Church there, recounting the events and showing his message. They believed him much to his surprise, and agreed to bring Thalia to the Champions Convergence to meet with the Hyperion Magnus (Leader of the church) and Augustus Auranthor. The church insisted that Thalia be made a ward of the church, and she was separated from Solace for months. He does not know what went on during that time, only that she was branded and made divine property. 

From Thalia's point of view, she was branded on her right hand, challenged and conditioned by Hyperion Knights, attempting to bring out her power. They drilled her, and imbued her with the idea that she was blessed not because she was good, but because she was a sinner, and her power was a tool to serve as punishment for her crime. She was terrified of everyone, wishing to be with Solace again, and deeply missing her father. One day she was visited by a Hyperion Knight, but he was different. He did not approach when Thalia flinched, sitting in a chair across the room. He spoke to her like a person, and seemed like he actually cared for her wellbeing. The knight introduced himself as Mervin, and said he was here to see how Thalia was. She was terrified, never speaking, but went and sat with him. She marveled at his shining golden armor, in awe of its almost celestial look. He took note, and kindly offered her a piece, pulling pauldron from his shoulder, "It looks like it would fit you perfectly, child of Light."

Thalia never forgot Mervin's kindness. When she was reunited with Solace at Castle Ikaris for the convergence, he barely recognized the little girl. She still trusted him, and after seeing what they had done to her Solace vowed to protect her from anything of that sort for the rest of his life. When they failed to receive an audience with Augustus, Solace knew he could not let the church have her again, so they fled west. They became travelling missionaries, healing for coin and giving humanitarian aid to those who needed it in the wake of the second war. 

Solace searched for signs, and in Thalia's prophetic dreams she spoke the name, "Marcell". Solace searched for this mysterious Marcell, but found nothing, until he learned of the Hyperion Knight Emrys, a friend of Mervin's. he believed this person must be the key to their fate, and followed Illuman's light to Krag Irath, where he waited for this savior.
