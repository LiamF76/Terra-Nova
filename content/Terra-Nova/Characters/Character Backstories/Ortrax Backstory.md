
Once known as Ortrax Stonecrown, now referred to only as "The Hollow General", Ortrax is a being of legendary power and determination. Ortrax was once the proud **High Marshal of Traxgate**, a legendary dwarf whose iron discipline, strategic genius, and indomitable will led the city to its greatest military triumphs. Revered by many, feared by all, he believed that freedom was weakness and that peace bred decay. His vision: a perfect society ruled by military hierarchy and forged in endless preparation for war. If the society was indominable, he could set Traxgate to rule the land for thousands of years and establish a legacy as the greatest warrior in history.

When the Traxian Council tried to limit his power, he rebelled—seizing control of the city and declaring himself the First <mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">Warlord Eternal</mark>. He amassed his army and conquered an astounding portion of Escarth under his tyrannical rule.

In a moment of unity within mortals, the Wizards Council, Liora the Silver Dragon, and warriors from across the continent united to slay Ortrax, and defeated him. He was banished, not slain, by a powerful ritual that cast his soul into the Shadowfell, cursed to eternally march with his spectral armies beyond the veil.

In the decades—or centuries—since, Ortrax has become something more and less than a god. Now, he is:
- **A colossal suit of twisted black armor**, hollow inside, animated only by malice, will, and shadow magic.
- **Crowned with a shattered war helm**, his voice echoes with dozens of overlapping commands, like spectral officers still barking orders from beyond.
- Wielding **The Woehammer**, a runed war maul forged from Traxian steel and bound with soul-iron, said to toll like a funeral bell when swung.
- Surrounded by **endless ghost legions** known as the *Black Muster*, made up of the souls of warriors who failed or defied him.
