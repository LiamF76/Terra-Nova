The Lightbearer, a demi-god grandson of Calina and Lazarus, the most legendary mortal in all of history. To say the bar was set high is a great understatement. Augustus was raised in Akash, one of the continents beyond Konduell. He inherited the throne from his late father Titus, who had ruled for approximately 60 years. His family tree is... compact. Calina married Lazarus, and had Titus, who married Titus, her own son, and gave birth to Augustus. People throughout the empire refer to her as the "God Empress", or "Eternal Mother". 

Titus was not regarded in the same light as his father and son. He was an addict, he played cards, drank, and did all manner of other debaucheries. At the age of 14 Augustus found his father dead on the steps of the castle, many believe he did it, the official story is that Titus passed from an overdose of Sundrop. Augustus ascended to the Emperor's throne within the week, and was uncontested, a surprise to all. Even Calina, his mother who had the utmost faith in her son to receive the throne, believed Augustus' half brother Cyrus would challenge for the crown. He did not.

Augustus was raised between his mothers palaces and his uncles tents. He lived in both the royal imperialist structure of Calina, and the nomadic freedom of Akash. Augustus learned the way of the horse and sword, becoming a master at arms before reaching his teenage years. He harbored extraordinary strength, and lightning quick reflexes. 

He learned quickly that the fellowship he felt in Akash would never be reflected in the high courts of Lazarus. After an incident involving his uncle, the lords of the nomads were stripped of their governmental power, and subsequently removed from all governmental positions. This began his waves of conquest.

Augustus, under his mother's watchful eye and stern teaching, led his armies across Akash, retaking any rebellious territories, and reclaiming the great power the Empire of Lazarus once had. He quelled rebellion in Akash, swept through the rebel kings in Talayal, and humbled the masters of the Iramo. He became known as Augustus, the Second Coming. The greatest legacy of his grandfather, retaking what had been lost by his father, and fulfilling his destiny.

From the day Augustus was born his mother had battered him with his story, telling of his beauty, greatness, and duty. Above swordplay, tactics, and compassion, he was taught that one day the world would be ending, and he was the only one who could stop it. He was tortured by visions, brought on by his powers. The royal physicians reported that on one occasion he did not sleep for an entire month, complaining of the same issue, blindingly bright light each time he closes his eyes. His mother radiated delight at learning of her sons affliction, a sign of destiny she called it, a cure he carried to the joy of his mother.

At the age of 25 he had completed his quest, conquered the world, everything from the north to the south of the poles, and all things beyond the Forbidden Lands bowed to Augustus. He had felled great wyrms with his bare hands, added some to his army, he had bested the greatest warriors of every culture and practice, and he had earned the respect of the most wise inventors and mages in the world. In honor of his great triumph, his mother led him to the base of Mount Humzuras, the peak where Lazarus had lain down his sword and forsaken violence. 
Augustus climbed the mountain alone, his final test, and at his peak he faced his greatest challenge, a battle no mortal man has faced and won without sacrifice. He descended the cliffs carrying his grandfather's sword, the Championship of Shao Daraan, and told his mother not to worry about the end of the world, for he had crossed the river.

From that day onward Augustus had changed, still tortured be painful visions of disasters yet to come, but focused and matured, as if his meeting with Shao Daraan had made him wise beyond his years. He told his generals to amass their forces. He bid his brother to summon the Red Horde, and his mages to call upon the great dragons of Lazarus, he would lead them into the Forbidden Lands, and save the world.
