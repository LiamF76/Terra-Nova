Name: The One Who Casts, Towc (T-ow-k)

Voice: inquisitive, almost like a child, slightly higher pitched, "What was that? A spell you cast? Can you do it again? Why? Why? Why?"

Description: His face has white skin with large moth like red eyes, a pair of moth antenna and a heavy red fuzzy cloak with strange drawings of eyeballs. He has clawed hands and feel, and his antennae are constantly twitching. He is always hunched forward and sticks to the edges of the room or dark corners.

Important Info: Brother/Sibling/Connected to [[Karegor]].
