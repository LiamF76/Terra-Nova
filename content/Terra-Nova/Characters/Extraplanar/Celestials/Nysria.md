Description:
- <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">Spirit Form:</mark> She has beautiful translucent orange blue purple and pink hair that flows and waves upwards like a river. Tiny dots of starts are scattered through her divine strands. Her skin is dark like a Half-Elf and her ears pointed. She has two black dots beneath her pale yellow eyes ,and a golden diamond between them. her hair wraps around her figure, engulfing her in a dress of her own making, and a shining yellow moon pulses just above her breasts. It is difficult to tell if she wears anything besides her massive flowing hair. 
- <mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">Mortal Form:</mark> her hair is pure white, braided into thick cords along the top and flowing down on the sides in curls. Along the edges of her braiding are small yellow flower petals. Her skin is that of a Half-Elf, and she wears a white dress with a drown corset and golden gilding. Across her face is a splatter of flecked gold freckles. When in her <mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">Mortal Form</mark>, she prefers to go by <mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">Ria</mark>.

Important Info: Spirit of the moon Nysria. She is known as the Veiled One for her mysterious nature, rarely interacting with mortals. 
She has taken a special interest in Ivys, as he is a powerful mage who has traveled across many planes. She has no life on her moon, and would like Ivys to build a planar menagerie in his own Chronovault on her Moon. She really dislikes salty foods, and enjoys playing with the ocean, pulling it back and forth as she likes whenever her siblings move past the shores.
Her favorite spell is Crown of Stars. She likes any spells that are pretty, but usually quaint ones like mending.
