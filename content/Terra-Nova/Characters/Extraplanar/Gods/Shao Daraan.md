# Shao Daraan

## AKA: The Old Man

![[image-2.webp|500x750]]

### Name: 
**Shao Daraan**, The Elder God, The Creator, The Old Man, Father of Heaven and Earth

---

### Voice: 
does not speak with his own voice, uses the voices of those you know in your head telepathically
- "I suppose it was cruel, but it made for a powerful story."
- "I do try to keep to watching, but sometimes the world needs a small... nudge, in my desired direction."

---

### Description: 
Shao Daraan takes many forms. As a God he manifests in black skin, a tail longer than the world. 4 clawed and vile arms adorn his body, and his hair is shadowed tentacles. He looks more beast than God. As a mortal Shao often takes the form of an old Man, often referred to as 'The Old Man', by those who worship the old gods.
