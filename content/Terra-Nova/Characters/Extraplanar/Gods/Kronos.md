Name: Kronos the Forgotten, God of Time, Father Time!

Voice: old man, slow speaking, low voice, “And so you think I am nothing? Ask yourself what kind of monster they would trap in a place like this.”, “They killed my memory a long time ago, now my children worship Vaior, the pretender.”

Description:

His eyes are solid gold and when he speaks his voice ripples like water through your mind, every word echoing within your own consciousness. 
- Time Form: 60 ft tall glowing red monster, he has dragon-like wings and his whole body crackles with golden lightning. His head is shaped like a flat topped spartan helm and golden light leaks from the faceplate.
