Name: **Illuman**, The Dawnfather, Firstborn of Daraan, The Shine

Voice: Illuman speaks in the voice of every mortal champion he has held, layered one atop another. This creates a voice of powerful authority that radiates balance, mercy, and power.
- "I forgive those who fall from the path."
- "My children fear no dark night or shadowed corner of the world. The light shall find its way to the deepest darkest places, and make them bright in my image."

Description: Illuman always wears golden armor, often made of celestial gold plates. His face is never shown, always manifesting as a small sun, shining brightly and warmly. He is tallest amongst the Gods, only bows to Shao. He often manifests as a mortal, taking the form of knights or kings, and bringing his guidance to the lost.
