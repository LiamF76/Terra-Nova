# Illuman

![[image-12.webp|500x650]]

---

## Name
 **Illuman**, The Dawnfather, Firstborn of Daraan, The Shine

---

## Voice
Illuman speaks in the voice of every mortal champion he has held, layered one atop another. This creates a voice of powerful authority that radiates balance, mercy, and power.
His speech 

> [!quote]
> "I forgive those who fall from the path. I embrace them in my shine, but I do not forget their transgressions. They are tested, and they return stronger."

> [!quote]
> "My children fear no dark night or shadowed corner of the world. The light shall find its way to the deepest darkest places, and make them bright in my image."

---

## Description
Illuman always wears golden armor made of celestial gold plates. His face is never shown, always manifesting as a small sun, shining brightly and warmly. He is tallest amongst the Gods, only bows to Shao. 
When he does show his face, it is a falsehood, created to serve a purpose. He often manifests as a mortal, taking the form of knights or kings, and bringing his guidance to the lost.

---

## Important Info
- God of Light
- Embodiment of the Sun, Nova Magnus, and Keeper of Elysium, the city of the afterlife built on Nova Magnus's surface.
