Name: **Khal Daraan**, the Doombringer, The Aspect of Apocalypse

Voice: Deep and rumbling, his voice sounds like the great waves of the ocean that sink ships flowing over the burning explosions of terrible volcanoes. His speech is older than the world, and his voice holds the age of that at its front, rolling over those who hear it like the power of a billion years.
- "Mortals cannot comprehend reality, what is real is beyond you. I am real, and you do not exist."
- "I am the antithesis of all. If it exists, I will unmake it, and if it does not exist... I shall give it form, and bind it to my will. In my will all things find *END*."
- "Mortals fear the dark... my child the Night Serpent. [[Balthazaar]] is but a small and fleeting adder. Before there was darkness, there was I."

Description: Khal has no known appearance. There is only one depiction of Khal Daraan, protected by the Ryfiri Druids. It was carved into the first root of the Oakenheart, and taken from that place. It lies sealed in Druidica, but legends say it shows a great eye, surrounded by a thousand black feathered wings. It is ever bleeding and red, and never closes.

Important Info: 
- God of Destruction
- Khal Daraan is the destroyer, its cosmic purpose and reason for being to to destroy all creation
