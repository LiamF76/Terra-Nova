Name: **Horus Hixs**, Teya'ka, The Phoenix Emperor, The Paragon

Voice: Horus speaks the the accumulated voices of his hosts, which numbers over 50. He carries a divine authority, and often drones on with grand statements beyond the scope of a normal conversation.
- "I do not speak for New Estorias. I am new Estorias. My blood is fire, and that fire is what has carried my holy empire through the darkest days. If I am the torch that must guide us through the night, then I shall shine brightly, and violently."

Description: Horus has a striking face, with sharp features and hair of pure wisping and flowing flame. He stands at 7 feet tall and always carries his blade, Torch, with him wherever he goes. He usually wears ceremonial religious robes of red and orange, but can often be found in his black and silver war armor as well. His eyes are intense, and his gaze can be mistaken for an attempt at intimidation, but in reality he just has a very serious look.
