
Name: Cerkil the Clown

Voice: my voice, little puffs of fire from the nostrils every so often, “I think I have just the thing for you!”, “Diego? Well he’s my second cousin, of course I know him!”

Description: Red dragonborn, 5'8, little guy with deep red scales and a softer pink underbelly. He wears fine silk robes with dragon and petal patterns and always has his smoke pipe. 

Important Info: Owner of the Fire Drake’s Emporium, he bought Bekarys’ shop after he passed and now runs them himself.
