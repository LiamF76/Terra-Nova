Name: Mervin Morningstar, The Sword of the Morning

Voice: cowboy, McCree, "The sun is high in the sky, its high noon."

Description: human 6foot, fair skin and brown hair, soft features, short trimmed goatee, he wears golden armor bearing the insignias of suns and swords, his sword is black and gold, and his shield is silver and gold and glows faintly

Important Info: Champion of [[Illuman]], head knight of the Hyperion Knights, devout follower of Illuman and member of [[The Sunlight Road]], Mervin is dedicated to bringing goodness and light into the world, and although many consider him scary and dangerous, he is 18, he is young and wants to do the right thing by the innocent people. Mervin was a commoner who was adopted as a monk by the Sunlight Road, and rose through the ranks to become a Knight.
