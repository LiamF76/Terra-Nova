Name: Thalia Brightheart (Marcell), the Daughter of Radiance

Voice: shy young girl, rarely speaks, short quick sentences, "Yes, no, thank you."

Description: Young human girl, she has lots of freckles and short blonde hair in a bob. Her eyes are a pale gold, and she has a warm but timid aura about her. She wears nuns clothes with an ornate shoulder cape bearing religious symbols of [[Illuman]]. Her right hand bears a brand in the shape of the sun (a marking for those anointed or "owned" by the church). She wears a single golden armored pauldron on her right shoulder (given as a gift by [[Mervin]]).

Important Info: Protected by [[Solace]]. Experienced a holy miracle from Illuman's angel [[Galara]], who marked her as a chosen vessel of light, and gave her the power to working healing miracles. It was stated that "she would be the Light that shines through the blade".
