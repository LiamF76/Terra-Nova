Name: **Lanxaad**, Son of the Storm Queen, The Blue Prince

Voice: Lanxaad has a voice laced with spit and drool, like he can barely contain his lust for death and fighting. He is almost a wild beast.
- "Kill, maim, burn. Everything will die and I will kill it."

Description: 7ft tall Blue Dragonborn male. Lanxaad has massive teeth and an imposing mouth. He is covered in muscles and scars and always wears some form of heavy metal armor, ready for a fight at any moment. His head bears 4 deep blue horns like his mother's. His cape is tattered and ripped from years of battle, but he never removes it as it was the cloth he was wrapped in at birth, now a symbol of his greatness and strength.
