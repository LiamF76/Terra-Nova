Important Info: Hannibal was once a follower of Lazarus, then a General of Augustus, now a General of Ivar. When Lazarus died, he left Hannibal his journal, where he described the beauty of the river and its power. Hannibal dedicated his life to crossing the river, and he has. 
He hates Kairos and the replicants more than anything, as Kairos was spoken of in Lazarus' journal. They were close, and Lazarus says without Kairos' help, he never would have defeated Arkonil at all. Hannibal hates Kairos for having been necessary for that victory, seeing it as a mockery of the greatest man who ever lived, and oner of his most important goals is to kill Kairos and all his replicants. He holds Emandyr and Augustus as prisoners, and has been given Kairos as a slave by Vulcan.
