Name: Evander Descoteaux

Voice: British accent, better than you Kairos type beat, a little flowy in speech pattern. "Ah yes, Ivys Arthur, the rogue replicant, you are well documented in my databases. Every breath you draw is an insult to us. Worry not, I shall cure you of your impurity post haste."

Description: 
- Human: 
- Reforged: Red and steel reforged robot. His body is red with armor plating steel armor pieces, a head made of carved black angular glass with a single glowing gold circle that acts as his eye. His lower body is comprised of a series of mechanical flowing tentacles that have plugs on the end used to interface with his computers. In his chest and shoulder pads are golden power cores that fuel his magic and body.

Important Info: Replicant of [[Kairos]] (pride), in and out of operating, notably the one who gave the Druidforged designs to the Dwarves and invented the Reforged process. He also worked with [[Geisor]], and killed him, stealing his soul for his own reforging and took his heart to create [[Oztar, Heart of the Demi-God]]. Built [[Chronovault Mythos]]. Extremely vain and thinks he is better than anyone and everyone.
