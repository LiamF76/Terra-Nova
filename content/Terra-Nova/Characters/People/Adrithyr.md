Name: Adrithyr Realmcrest, the Red King

Voice: royal voice, deeper with a sense of importance, "I am King of Estor, the most powerful nation on the continent, you are nothing.", "The War God's Champion will not be held at the door while I am King."

Description: 5'11 human, pale skin and brown hair with slight graying, wears a red crystal crown, royal garments of gold black red and green. Thick beard with lots of gray and very light wrinkles on his face. His attitude is pompous, clear that he thinks as king he is better than others, but not that others are nothing. He cares about maintaining his power because he believe he is the rightful king. He is heavily invested in making his son Lukas king, and does not claim his son Kaladin.

Important Info: King of Estor. Father of [[Ivar]], Kaladin, and Lukas, Husband of Annabelle, Brother of Calix. Advised by Damion, formerly advised in magic matters by [[Clovis]],
