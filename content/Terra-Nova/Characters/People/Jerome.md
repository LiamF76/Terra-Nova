Name: Jerome Darksteel, Fury of the Free Cities

Voice: master chief, short simple phrases

Description: Tall dark and handsome. 6'6 black dragonborn with black and red eyes that glow faintly in the dark. He has pronounced and jagged scales and almost demonic horns that curve forward. Along his back are raised spines with a fin and his snout is abnormally large with 2 rows of teeth. He looks like a mutated, almost startlingly large super dragonborn.

Important Info: Twin Brother of Agamemnon Darksteel, super soldier from The Ritual of Abyssal Rebirth, former servant of Ivar, host of the 3 Eyed Devil. Not technically allied to but fighting for the Freehold Confederation, he fights to protect the area from demons and evildoers. Jerome is a living weapon, largely emotionless but he cares deeply about his brother, and would neutralize him before killing him, maybe even turning against his allies to save his brother.
