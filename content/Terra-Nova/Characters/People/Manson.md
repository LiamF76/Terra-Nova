Name: Hyperion Magnus Manson Bloodhorn, The Red Sun

Voice: sly and evil undertone, snake energy, "Those who cannot see the sunrise, must be made to watch the sunset."

Description: 6 foot tall male red dragonborn, wears white robes of Illuman with red stitched suns and swords across them. He has beady black eyes, and wears a small crown of thorns (a religious and historical symbol of the Hyperion Magnus title). 

Important Info: Leader of the [[The Sunlight Road]]/ Hyperion Magnus. Brother of Bloodhorn, cousin of Diego, he betrayed his family and fled with his daughter (the girl Diego was looking for), and offered her as a sacrifice to Illuman, who allowed him to enter the church as payment. Manson is A FANATICAL follower of Illuman, he believe above all things in the Sunlight Road, and will die for it, or kill for it, or worse.
