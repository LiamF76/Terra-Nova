I try to have an open mind about new places, but Redwater looks like a bad place to end up. The whole town is built barely a foot off the ground on raised wooden platforms. They're shoddy, creaky, and each beam you walk on feels like it's just about to snap.

The sky has a strange hue here, yellow and greed, the air is the same. Putrid, almost sweet like rotting flesh.

We tie off Coffee and make our way into a tavern. Buckets. First place we could find for me to wipe the mud from my boots. I had walked the two miles to the town, Lukas warned me that this wasn't the place to show off my shiny new magic horse. I took that advice, and on my walk I used the time to wrap my stone hand. 

Covering it almost makes me sad, I have grown quite proud of the look, but better to be safe than pretty.

As I finish wiping off my well worn boots, loud thunk draws my attention. A knife has been stabbed into a table. Lukas and someone quite tall, and quite green, are facing off on either side of the skewered table just a few feet away. Their eyes are aflame, and somehow this has escalated to the very brink of a scuffle.

I drop my bag and rush over, getting myself just enough between Lukas and the burly Orc. "Woah now we're all friends here!" I place a hand on Lukas' shoulder and lean in. "What in the Road's name happened? I was wiping my boots for ten seconds."

Lukas doesn't answer me, but I notice his hand slip from his waist, and onto the handle of his axe behind his head. The Orc grunts, and turns, either he realized what kind of weapon that is, or decided the old man wasn't worth the trouble.

"He wanted your boots Ina." Lukas says as the small crowd that formed around us disperses. "Said they looked too good for a boy like you, those were a man's boots. Look how he runs away in his man's boots!" He shouts the last part, and though the Orc doesn't flinch, he must have heard it.

I haven't seen Old Petlia take such insult to a comment like that in years. "Well thank you, but don't go getting us in trouble here. If I'm the one who needs to stay calm and rational between the two of us, we're both dead."

Lukas waves to the barkeep, asking for a round of drinks to the table. He plops down in a chair and pulls the knife left behind right out of the wood, twirling it in his hand. 

His eyes scan the room with a focus I don't recognize. "You won't believe me, but I'll keep saying it Inayet. You are a greatly talented swordsman. Truly, had you competed in tournaments or fought in the Scales War I have no doubt you would be a great hero."

I sit beside him, and a pair of heavy wooden cups are placed beside us by a metal hand. The barkeep is a Warforged, a sentient construct. Lukas thanks him and takes a deep swig of the drink before continuing. "But you trained, caravanned, and worked in Kheagon. This is the frontier, the Drowning Dream Swamp... it's a hell all of its own, and you will need your strength to survive it. Be aware, if you let the town before the swamp disrespect you, the swamp will take your soul before you can say, 'Petlia save me!'"

He laughs heartily at his own joke, and finishes his drink before I have even touched mine, which is promptly refilled by our metal friend the barkeep. Lukas hands him a gold coin with a nonchalance that hides the fact its our last one.

"My friend, it's been quite a long time since I crossed the Dream." He downs the class in one motion, "Does Rethus still run the crossing? Me and my companion here are hoping to get across quickly. We are hoping to be on our way up the river before the week is out."

As if summoned by the prospect of work, a man appears. He has a sharp smile, because his teeth are literally carved into points. His skin is a deep hue of red, like blood, and his head is adorned with a pair of thick and heavy horns. A Tiefling, and with a deep scratchy voice like a whetstone against my blade, he speaks. "That couldn't be Lukas Petlia... not Petlia! Come back from the dead to repay the debt he holds so dearly, as a man of honor of course."

Lukas takes a smaller, more meager sip of his drink. I lean in to whisper to him again, "Don't tell me you forgot that you owe him money..."

Rethus does not wait for Lukas to answer. "I overheard!" He say jubilantly, "That you were looking to cross the Dream! That, yes that, I can certainly help with. And not to worry Lukas, I know you're too honorable and just for this wretched world! I would never *dream* of letting you use my services without repaying your *substantial* debt to me first."

His pinkish palm flashes before me, snatching my cup from the table. I hadn't even gotten a chance to drink from it.

"But you simply must introduce me to this fine young man here!" He sips my drink, his amber eyes locked onto mine over the rim of the cup. They're like little Scars, burning and red, so alive it looks like a tiny devil may pop right out and bit me. "He looks strong. Fast too. That'll be helpful. Oh and those little streaks of grey in his hair? I could've made a fortune off-"

Lukas cuts him off before whatever that was going to be happens. "His name is Inayet. He's my companion, and of course Rethus I will gladly... repay you. It would be quite wrong not to. So long as you can help us get through the mist, and safely on our way up the river."

---

Lukas and I convene in the stable with Coffee. He looks stressed, and embarassed.

"Lukas I don't know what you owe him for, and I am not one to judge you, but I do not trust him." I grab the map from my bag and lay it on Coffee's side. "Thank you girl. Look here, if we backtrack barely half a day, we can go through Highstride. That will keep us out of this swamp, out of that cursed province Ark, and we will barely lose a month of time."

He shakes his head no the entire time I am speaking. "No. Too long, we don't have that kind of time."

"Then let me bring you South, to Druidica! They can certainly delay it, probably even heal it. By the Abyss I would swear myself to the Green Knights for another month with you. Come one, let's just not go through here. This place is bad new. I can't explain it..."

I want to tell him about the pit in my stomach that this swamp is giving me. There's somethign dark and enchanting about it, gnawing at me while I sleep, making my hair stand up, even Pollux agreed. He would barely lay a hoof in the mud. I want to say that I keep seeing mud and dirty water in my dreams, but that won't convince him.

"It's the only way forward." He says, gaze fixed on the floor.

"Why? Why can't we try something else? Why is it that every time I try to get you even another hour of life you shove it away?"

"Because I am ready to go. I am ready to die Inayet, I have made my peace with the Green Mother. I need to get you... I need to get you as close as I can before I am gone. I do not want another day, or another year. I..." He pauses, sniffling slightly, "I have loved you like a son but I am dying. You are dying. If there is anywhere with the magic to heal you, it will be in the forest of my fathers. I believe that. I need to get you there. I know you will keep the promise I hated asking you to make, but I need to get you as far as I can before I can no longer help you!"

He is whispering and shouting all in the same breath. "You are strong and suddenly chosen by not one but two magics! That is no mere coincidence! Something is happening for you, something wonderful, but a man can be great in many ways. You could become heroic, or notorious. I would like to do as much as I can to make you heroic. If that means pushing you further north, so you are sooner free of my burial and burden, the yes I will fight you on it."

I grab him tightly around the waist. I am not waiting for him to ask for the hug. I hold him and remind him he is not dead year with my body. "Feel that Old Man? The thunder in your chest, the warmth of my cheek on yours? You are not dead y-yet."

I fumble the words at the end, because my cheek is warm, hot from my drink and emotions. I feel his face, and it is cold like a stone in the night. He is not warm anymore.

"I will follow you Lukas Petlia. I will follow you, but this job for Rethus... this is a bad job. It is on its home ground, it's nest. It knows the land better, we are not of this place. You are the one that warned me about it today. Let's find another way?" I plead, but I see a soulless look in his eyes.

It is not Lukas that is driven to do this mission. There is something else on the surface now, and his hand holds that axe again, just barely running his fingertips along the gold. "What's the matter boy, scared?" He asks, a faint smile on his face. "I relish the challenge, your stone hand and its silver veins, its strength ancient and honored, alongside my family's divine axe... we must test ourselves someday. Today is that day. Besides, it won't be the first Hydra I have slain."
