# Novellas

### 7 Point Story Structure

- **Opening**: early pages, what is the world like for the character, often includes the inciting incident
1. **Hook**: something interesting, but also the opposite of the end state / resolution
2. **Plot Turn 1**: moves story from beginning toward the midpoint, the character's world changes
3. **Pinch 1**: forces characters into action
4. **Midpoint**: character moves from reaction to action
5. **Pinch 2**: applies even more pressure. This is the jaws of defeat clamping down
6. **Plot Turn 2**: character obtains the last piece they need to 'make it happen'
7. **Resolution**: the climax of the tale, something is completed or finished
- **Denouncement**: wraps up the tale in a satisfying way
