## Overview

Devilstar Daughter is the story of Astarielle, the youngest daughter of Nysria the Veiled One and Ivys Arthur. Consult the [[Nysria Ivys Family Tree]] for more info.

Astarielle, nicknamed Astar, is the MC of this story, she is a hasty, shy, often overcorrecting mage who wants to learn more about human magics and follow int he footsteps of her father, whom she never got to meet.
