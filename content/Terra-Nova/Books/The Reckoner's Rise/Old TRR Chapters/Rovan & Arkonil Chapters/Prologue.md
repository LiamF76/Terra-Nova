
Rovan stormed through the golden halls, the shining marble floors beneath his feet crackling with energy as his anger radiated out of his body.
A soothing female voice called out to him through his clouding frustration, "You must calm yourself, you'll cause another collapse. Iros was angry enough for tearing down his astral telescope before."
Rovan grunted angrily, rounding a corner, lifting his foot to barely miss crushing a group of Aelarian souls beneath his sandal, "I will not be placated again Kallisa. He would have lost us a second war had my father not intervened. He may be our leader but he has failed us for a second time. I will give him no more chances. He has killed more of us with his foolishness than any enemy."
As Rovan rounded a final corner, the massive hundred foot tall doorway to the Celestian Hall faced him. Rovan looked down at himself, his tunic still covered in his brother Phobis' blood. Rovan saw his terrified face in his mind, his little brother begging for the pain to stop, to protect his worshippers after he was gone, all because of the Dawnfather's pride and arrogance. Rovan clenched his fists, his starlight fingers tightening into a painful grip as he punished himself for failing his brother, all his siblings, every god that had fallen in the battle against Balthazaar, until now against the Titans. 
All those divines, those celestials who had been brought to this world by Shao Daraan as guardians and leaders for the mortals, they had died because Illuman was too proud to admit that someday they might be challenged, they could lose.
Kallisa watched her beloved, seeing the stars in his form glow brighter, the golds and blues replaced with dark reds and purples, it always happened when he was angry, but this was different. Stars went out, their light gone, replaced with dark black voids consuming all around them, devouring the light that once shone within Rovan, the Guiding Star, God of Valor and Bravery. "Rovan, I need you to calm down, let me work my magics on you, we only just returned, you're covered in blood. Rest, then return to speak to Illuman."
She placed a kind hand on his shoulder, her healing spread through him, Kallisa was the Mistress of Healing and Empathy, even divine wounds were trivial to her power, but Rovan gently removed her hand, holding hers and pulling her close. "Are you with me darling?" He said, their eyes locked together as other figures approach, the doorway opened to the great hall as the gods took their seats.
"No matter what love, he must be held responsible for his failings, I just don't want you to get hurt, I can't lose you." She said, the soft droplets of tears forming on her freckled face. Rovan wiped them, running his hand through her dark curled hair.
Rovan smiled kindly, taking her hand and leading the pair into the hall, facing the countless other divines around them without fear, "You will never lose me. Now come, there must be a reckoning."
