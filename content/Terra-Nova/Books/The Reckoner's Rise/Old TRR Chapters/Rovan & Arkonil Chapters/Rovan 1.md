
The council sat in their thrones, everyone entering after the brutal conflict. Iros breathed heavily, no longer able to carry his mighty hammer and tongs he dropped them beside his massive iron seat. Oras slid gracefully into their cushioned throne, wide enough to lay upon like a bed. At the head of the room stood the two largest seats, one black and scaled, meant for the Night Serpent, sitting empty since the dawn of time as a reminder to all who dared even think about rising against Illuman's will. Rovan cared not for warnings, he had lost patience for serving the God of Light on that battlefield. Beside the black throne was Illuman. His sickly white skin and throne made of pure sunlight radiated through the hall, piercing the other gods eyes, drawing their eyes to his seat and their minds to his demands, his decrees, of which there were always many.
Illuman sat slumped in his gold chair, his gold toga and gold crown and golden everything reeked of the same arrogance Rovan had snapped about to Kallisa. As the last of the gods took their seat the doorway shut, pulled closed by the winged multi armed Illumanic Angels that protected the gods and served in the great palace. The gods were left alone in total silence, save the comforting whispers of Kallisa as she spread healing magi through the air, slowly mending the elemental wounds all of the divine had obtained in the final battle against the titans.
Rovan stepped forward from the center of the room, about to address the gathering when Illuman interrupted him, the Dawnfather could never allow someone to speak first in his heavenly city of Elysium. "Welcome brothers, sisters, children, and all our divine allies." He took up his chalice, drunkenly spilling his wine as he attempted to toast his family.
Kimutan, even with his affliction, blinded at birth as a cruel curse by the cruel fey Oberyn, tilted his head slightly, not annoyed, but overwhelmingly tired of Illuman's habit. His face turned and nodded at Rovan, as if to say he understood the God of the North Star's anger, Kimutan was with him.
"A great battle it was indeed. Seven of us dead, millions of innocent Aelarian and other mortals lost, but we won! We beat back the titans that assailed us at YOUR challenge! All without your help. I have seen it become a common occurrence that great things happen while we go out and fight to survive upon your command. All the while Illuman, THE DAWNFATHER, sits in his throne getting drunk on mortal spirits! So tell me, oh great God of Light, first chosen by Shao Daraan, will you hold yourself responsible for the deaths of these seven? MY BROTHER DIED FOR YOU! WILL YOU BE ACCOUNTABLE FOR THE MORTALS NOW LOST WITHOUR A GOD TO WORSHIP, TO PROTECT AND GUIDE THEM? Or are the fallen just MORE SOULS TO FILL YOUR PRECIOUS CITY OF THE DEAD!?"
Rovan found himself just before Illuman's throne, as if he had moved unknowingly. From the start of his speech to the end he had steadily moved closer and closer, beyond the tiled floors, one foot resting on the steps up to Illuman's seat, a grave offense. Just as quickly as he moved forward though, Kallisa had come to rescue him, pulling him by the bicep down from the golden steps onto the center dais again. Illuman looked down at Rovan, barely registering his presence, his vicious gold and red eyes seemingly unaware of what was happening, until Rovan mentioned Elysium.
As if his fury was awakened by criticism, a challenge like Balthazaar, like the titans, there was nothin Illuman loved more than those foolish enough to question why he was the God King, The God of Light, The Dawnfather, the Father all celestials looked to.
His eyes focused in an instant as he leapt from the throne, golden wings erupting from his back and his clawed hand firing out from his side, gripping Rovan by the throat and lifting him up from the tiles. Illuman floated fifty feet from the ground, raising Rovan as high as he could, squeezing his neck, his claws ripping the starlight flesh between them.
He spoke like a whisper, rasping and dry, yet it echoed with the force of an explosion through the ears of all in the room, "Say that again little star. Say to me that I am no longer fit to rule, and I will show you my fury. You were taught as all were, all you born gods, you children of the first generation. Walk always in the light of Illuman, lest you be ripped into its glow, and burned for your heresy. How far do you think the greatest god could have fallen, that a tiny speck in Daraan's universe could rival me?"
Rovan looked down at Illuman, the stars shining as brightly as they could, a wall of red and purple light fighting against a wall of gold from his assailant, "I do not believe you are now unfit, I believe you always were. You have led us into ruin, AND I WILL NOT STAND FOR IT ANY MORE!"
As Rovan screamed his battle cry, a blade of pure starlight blinked soundlessly into his hand, he pulled it backwards and drove forward into Illuman's chest. The God of Light gasped, dropping Rovan as he fell to one knee, he had only ever been struck once, by the ancient and terrible Night Serpent, and he was appalled with a divine fury none had seen since the birth of Terra-Nova. 
Rovan realized in his moment of anger what he had done, looking around the room in a panic as the other gods stared, amazed and horrified at what had occurred. No matter Illuman's transgressions, gods did not fight one another, they knew since the battle with Balthazaar that it led only to ruin, and Rovan had begun something he could not stop. As Rovan contemplated for just a moment, he decided that his action, albeit wrong, was the means to a better end, and he spoke with the confidence of the God of Bravery, "My divine family, look at how your leader kneels. He has brought us NOTHING but squalor. Today I put myself before you as a chance for change. I am the God of Bravery, Heroism, it is more a part of me than any other. He is unfit, I was born to lead you. Make me your new God King, and I will not lead us to senseless wars any longer. I WILL LEAD US INTO A NEW GOLDEN AGE OF PEACE!"
Kallisa stood beside him, her hand on his shoulder. He looked down to smile at her but she was focused, her gaze hardened and scanning the other gods. Kimutan stepped down from his throne, reappearing beside Rovan, nodding to him kindly. Rovan was happy to have the support of the God of Mediation and Martial Skill. As Rovan turned smiling to see who would join him next, he was horrified. A few minor gods stood for him, joining behind him, but no great lord, no god of the first generation even considered it. They readied their weapons for a fight.
In horror Rovan raised his hands as a sign of non-aggression, "I do not wish to fight my family, but he will destroy us all one by one! Please! End his madness, lest you be the next to die for his wars!" He begged his celestial brethren, but his words fell upon deaf ears.
Illuman stood, pulling the sword from his gut and crushing the blade to nothing. He pointed to Vulcan, who smiled. The four armed war good gladly took up his blades and approached with violent intent, "I did not get to fight the Night Serpent, a foe of his caliber deserves a one on one duel as first generation gods, but for you little Rovan... I could kill a nephew with my eyes closed tighter than Kimutan. Come, I will bathe in divine blood like your father did against Balthazaar! SEE THE GOD OF WAR AND DESPAIR!"
Vulcan charged with his four swords, and before Rovan could reach the room was in turmoil. The gods took to the skies above Nova-Magnus, a war of celestials on the surface of the sun. Thousands will die Rovan thought, no... billions, but there was no stopping it now. He took up his starlight blades and entered the fray, if there was to be a reckoning, then he would be the reckoner. "Death to Illuman!" He shouted, he would save the gods from ruin, or die fighting against it.
