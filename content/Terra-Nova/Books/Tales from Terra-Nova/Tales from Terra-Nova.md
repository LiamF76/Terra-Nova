
This is a compilation of short stories from throughout Terra-Nova. Most of these stories will pertain to the current Fourth Age, the Age of Divines. This helps me to world build and help Terra-Nova feel alive. Some of these stories will take place during pivotal moments in history, or other time periods at my discretion.

I want to write this from the perspective of an in world book. It is written by a Lich Historian, his name is Sorvael of Atlas. He is an Aelarian.

## Tales from Terra-Nova
##### Compiled and written by Sorvael of Atlas

Title and subtitle above.

### Story Ideas

- Chosen One - A story of a sentient magical sword destined to save the world, but it chooses the wrong person. Someone cowardly and self serving instead of a brave hero.
- Bazelon Story - Story about Bazelon Duskfire, a wandering Tiefling warrior. He meets a strange traveler visiting their town, who tells him of a great monster a hero must slay. If Bazelon does his father will surely be proud of him. The monster and traveler are both a god in disguise.
- The Queen's Dancer - Story of an assassin dancer in the strange maze continent of Iramo
- Tong Story - Story about Tong, a Dragonborn 
- Saurian Cowboy - A lone frontier sheriff transports a captured warlord across the land of Sauria. 
- Max in a Million - A goblin falls in love with a powerful demi-goddess daughter of Tena, he goes on a quest to find her favorite flower trying to earn her favor.
