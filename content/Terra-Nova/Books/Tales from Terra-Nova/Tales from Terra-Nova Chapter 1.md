## Ivory and Stone

His hands were rougher than the stone, which was good, that meant when cuts happened, they were small abrasions on the rock slabs, and not tears on his leathery skin. He hurled the stone onto his right shoulder, the chains around his ankles and wrists clanging with a metallic reminder of his station.

Another slave beside him gasped as he watched the boy lift an entire slate by himself, which should reasonably require 4 Lox together to lift. It was a struggle for him, but it made the work go faster, and the more the others could rest the more likely it was that they survived the day.

The Loxodon were a hearty people, literally. They had massive hearts, both for love and for pumping blood through their imposing forms. Hannibal shuddered as his feet stomped forward, his shoulders hunched so far forward. He felt the strength in his arms falter for a moment, and stumbled, before he caught himself and paused to steady. The eyes burned holes in his back, the masters and the other Lox, he couldn't allow either to see him weaken, and he pushed forward. 

They were the people of memory, their traditions spanned generations since before men, before elves, before law and magic. Loxodon were Beastfolk, children of Wahellia, the Wild Continent. Beastfolk got a bad enough reputation for their origins, common folk said they were born from twisted evil magics, the churches said they were cursed by the gods to bear animal flesh, but Hannibal knew the truth.

There was a reason the Lox worked in the mines carrying the heavy stones, the Leonin fought on the front lines with their manes flowing in courage, and Owlin studied more deeply and innately into the magics of this world. They were not cursed, they were blessed. The Beastfolk bore a connection to nature that men could never understand. He flexed his trunk and ran it over the stone as he threw it down onto the pile of rock to be cut for the new temple. He could feel the heartbeat of everything around him, he could feel their souls, emotions, they flowed like water, and all things must flow or drown.

As he imagined the water connecting him to his fellow slaves, he felt a tug around his hands, and was reminded that they were connected by iron, not water. A master carrying a heavy iron studded whip tugged on Hannibal's chains, dropping him to his knees as his trunk muzzle was shoved down.

Jeck, one of the Lupin, the dog folk. He was spotted tan and black, more akin to a Hyaena than a wolf like King Crax, but still he held station over others, and of course over the Loxodon. He laughed as Hannibal collapsed, he could resist, he was stronger than Jeck, much stronger, but that would only lead to more pain. To resist simply on principal was pointless. Higher purpose, memory, legacy, those were reasons to fight.

Hannibal felt a last strike his back, then another. The warm blood began to trickle down his back, but he offered no response save his ragged breathing as a the beating began. Higher purpose, just as father said, something worth fighting for, legacy.

"Not so strong when I come round eh you dirty fucking Lox?" Jeck cackled as he reveled in his power over the massive figure, who had become content to simply accept his punishment. Hannibal coughed and shuddered as a spatter of dust and small stones hit the ground 15ft away, and blew with a breeze into his eyes. It made him tear up, and that opening was all Jeck needed.

He leapt onto Hannibal's back, digging his claws into the skin as he stood, making a decree to all the slaves as they were forced to watch his display. "You are not Beastfolk Lox! You are just beasts, you carry stone, you fight, and you die for us! AND HOW LUCK YOU ARE! But do not think yourselves more than tools, you do as your masters say, " Hannibal felt a sting of cold silver on his neck, Jeck had placed his Khopesh on the slave's neck, "This took started thinking for itself! It carried a stone all alone, but how can a tool think without its master? It cannot, and it will never again, or I will dispose of my broken slave."

Hannibal barely noticed the change in pressure on his back as Jeck hopped off, he was so little, fragile...

He gave Hannibal one last kick in the head, which sent his tusks scraping against the carved stone floor, the vibration was terrible. It caused his brain to shudder and overwhelmed his senses. The holes along his ivory extensions made this nearly unbearable, but he endured. He grunted painfully, watching Jeck return to the cart he was leaning against prior, filled with sweet fruits and water jugs. Fruit for the masters, water for the slaves. Jeck took an apple from the cart and began to cut it with his knife, before he made his way up to a higher level of the quarry.

There was no point to his outburst, other than to show that he could, and that Hannibal, despite his strength and wit, was just another slave he could abuse and bully until the day a falling rock finally broke open his skull. A kinder way to go than some, crueler than others, but this is death, and death has no emotion. Erebos comes for us all, everyone dies, and the God of Death feels nothing but a sense of when, and how. Hannibal daydreamed for a fleeting moment, imaging how free one would be, with no dear of death, but the pleasant thought was snuffed out quickly when he opened his eyes and remembered where he was.

His hands pushed against the stone, lifting his battered body from the earth. This pain was sick, he thought to himself, his people were once the gentle giants, the shepherds that guided pilgrims through Wahellia, now they were tools. As he forced himself upright again another spatter for dust, larger chunks of rock came down. Someone shouted to watch their heads for small debris, they were cutting a large block one level above. Hannibal knew the Lox who worked above, they were not careful, and it would be small debris for long.

His young eyes scanned the Quarry above, searching for the source of the massive shakes he began to feel in the stone, and he found it. The block was big, clean cut, at least 10ft on each side. It would weigh many tons, and it was starting to tip. Hannibal dusted his shoulders before someone called out again, but they were calling to him, "Hannibal!" It was Jeck.

He shot his gaze to the voice, and saw a sight with greater purpose. His father and brother, their chains taught against the wall, held by Jeck and a few more slavers, trapped beneath the tipping block. His Loxodon heart beat in triple time. There was no force in their minds greater than memory, and memory was love. His father was his guide, his teacher, his family, and his brother was barely 26, a child by all Loxodon standards. He did not belong here, and neither of them deserved to be threatened by that stain of a creature Jeck.

Bados' eyes met with his son's, their fear was palpable, but contained. Hannibal knew the message, if he fought back they would die, but if he didn't they would die. There were no winning options, was Jeck pushing him again, before stopping the block? Was he baiting Hannibal into another humiliation and beating, or was he serious? There was nothing to stop him, two dead Lox in a quarry accident was like losing a single grape off the vine, inconsequential.

He would make it consequential. This was it, finally. Every fiber of his being wanted to fight back, every day he suffered their insults, their abuses, their chains. He tore his arms upward and slammed them into the ground, cracking the stone around him, ripping the rusty anchors that held him in line out from the earth. Jeck's eyes widened as Hannibal began to charge forward, closing the 40ft between himself and his family, but rock falls faster than Lox can run.

The slab toppled over the edge, sending shards and debris over that shattered on his fathers back as he shielded young Ojon. His legs thundered as he tore off the iron muzzle, stretching his trunk as far as he could, reaching out to Ojon. His father tossed the boy as far as he could, which landed him in Hannibal's trunk. He rolled his terrified baby brother off to the side as he reached the stone, the impact was like every bone in his back breaking.

Hannibal pushed upwards with every muscle he could muster, and he caught it, barely. He felt a violent and echoing snap, his left tusk was hit by the stone as he caught it, and the better half of it fell from his face to the ground. It was worth the loss. Hannibal could feel his fathers gaze up at him, the coiled fearful from of the man that raised him. He could feel his body shaking, cowering, he feared death.

He pulled himself upwards, and placed a hand on his sons cheek as Hannibal struggled to keep the stone up on his back, the pain radiating across his spine and down his broken tusk. This was worth fighting for. Bados whispered quietly into his sons flopping ears, "Thank you my boy, but I fear you have doomed me to Jeck's blade all the same. Thank you for saving Ojon."

It was a solemn decree, he had resisted a master, and someone would die for it, likely his father. Jeck would not allow Hannibal to take the punishment himself, he would use it as another mental torture. Hannibal felt the rage building inside him again, he would not allow it. He stayed silent, never retorted, never disobeyed, but this was too far. He was not property, he was a Loxodon, he was strong, and he was not going to lay down while this Lupin executed his father.

Jeck obliged Hannibal's anger, by leaping from above and landing before him, where he proceeded to draw his Khopesh, and advanced toward the father and son. "You dirty shit eating Lox! How dare you! I'm going to kill your dirty father right here, and you will watch. Then you'll hold that block until it crushes you. You both die today."

Hannibal did not answer. He held the stone, burning searing pain as he shifted slightly, and took a step forward. Jeck's eyes widened at Hannibal's show of resistance. His father looked on in horror, knowing that this would only lead to worse punishment, but Hannibal no longer cared. He had worked the quarry for three years, he was barely 30, still two years away from Lox adulthood, he would not suffer it for another day. He could feel the others looking at him, he heard the rustles of chains, the other slaves were restless, watching. Their eyes were his fuel, his fire would light theirs, freedom was coming, and it was coming today.

Jeck held out his blade, pointed directly at Hannibal, but he did not stop. He took another step forward, struggling through the insurmountable weight on his back. The blade pierced his chest, he embraced the pain, grunted, and his eyes bored into Jeck. 

"Hannibal you stop now!" Jeck whelped as he started to pull his blade back, Hannibal did not allow it. He shot his trunk around Jeck's arm, gripping him, pulling him closer. His trunk flexed as Jeck screamed in protest, he could feel the tiny muscle fibers and bones fighting against him strength, they were futile. He snapped them one by one as he continued, the other slavers watching on in horror. 

Someone else was shouting, a slaver moved towards Ojon, huddled on the ground and covering his eyes. Another big Loxodon charged, ripping off his chains, and shoving the slaver down. More fighting followed, the trumpeting sounds of Loxodon in combat echoed in the quarry.

Hannibal reached his goal, tossing the weak body of Jeck on the pile of cut stones, ready for transport to the Horned Wolf's palace to be. Jack screamed and shouted, begging for his life. Hannibal gave the same courtesy that Jeck had given to all those who died under his whip. He ignored it completely.

Hannibal hoisted the stone, the effort almost broke him, the burning in his arms as he lifted it high above his head, arms outstretched carrying it on his palms. He let loose a terrific trumpet, an announcement to the Loxodon around him, who halted their assaults on the slavers and watched. Hannibal kept his words close to his chest, only speak when you have something worth saying, he had something to say.

He stepped back, and dropped the massive stone on Jeck, the sound of crunching bones, snapping tendons, and screams came and passed quickly. A pool of blood leaked out from beneath the stone, the splatter of guts painting Hannibal's face in red.

He turned and ripped the Khopesh from his chest, holding the silver blade above his head, and he shouted with his rarely used but booming voice, "NO MORE!"
