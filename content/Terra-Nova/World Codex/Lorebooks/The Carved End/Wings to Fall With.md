## Wings to Fall With

His scythe carved the hills, bodies torn in many pieces scattered the ruins of the once great city, and [[Aldros]] stood unrelenting against his God's undead horde.

The bones shuddered and began to rise again. [[Aldros]] killed the bones and made them dust, never again to rise.

[[Gravebinder]] was pleased with absolution.

He speaks with the voices of all who have died and been risen with his necromancy. The Choir of suffering and song of salvation. "[[Garthrax]] has trained you well, and those secrets, sweet succulent secrets that you have learned from [[Vesponous]]... tell me Wyrm-Chosen, how deep must you dive to learn such truths?"

[[Aldros]] held his weapon aloft, and allowed it to float while he ran his hands through the dust. "Deeper than anything dies, deeper than anything has lived. I would not have made it so far without your gifts my Lord."

[[Gravebinder]] was pleased with absolution.

"Perhaps my champion, there is another gift I can give you, something the Deep, would not offer, There is nothing Khal hates more than the Sky, so filled with hope. I can give you the sky, twisted, taken, *earned*. Just as you demand."

[[Aldros]] agreed, his voice laced with joy, not at the gift, but at the understanding, new knowledge to feed [[Vesponous]]. All truths are gifts. "I demand nothing my Lord, it is the logic of the old way, the truth of the world. I nothing but a true believer."

[[Gravebinder]] led [[Aldros]] to a holy temple, and there they desecrated, killed, and made violence upon the followers of the faith. The temple was to the Punisher God, to Aldred, the namesake of [[Aldros]].

Where Aldred was the punisher, [[Aldros]] was punished, and [[Aldros]] relished in the chance to push the Old Way forward, and destroy Aldred's domain.

In the grave, the Black Dragon showed [[Aldros]] his new taken power, wings.

Beautiful and white feathered, shimmering with golden light. 'A gift of true love'. The priests died protecting them, futile sentimentality.

[[Aldros]] donned the wings, and destroyed all that was of them, skeletal, vile, and dark were his new avian limbs. He took to the sky as a terror, and raised cities in the name of his Lord [[Gravebinder]].

[[Gravebinder]] was pleased with absolution.
