## The Garden

I remember some details so clearly, and other I cannot recall.

The Garden. The very deepest, most dark and still place in all creation. So close to the being that is the end, that it is barely creation at all.

The flowers did not wilt or grow, frozen in perpetual bloom, the grass did not waver in the wind, it was so unnatural and uncanny.

The strangest was the air, or lack thereof. I could not breath, but I did not need to. even as I moved I knew my body was completely still, only being brought forward by its will, not actually stepping.

The terror there was unlike anything else in the world. It spoke without words, intent was transmitted directly onto my being, I can still feel Khal Daraan's words written on my bones. They will haunt me forever.

`Do not dare to walk in my garden, vile living thing. You tiny mote of entropy, who cannot be trusted to continue, who seeks death so lovingly you do not ever truly live.`

I do not respond, I cannot.

`I know your game pawn, you have reached the end of the board and become a queen. I congratulate you, disgusting aspect of my star. You have ruined my child Rovan, and for that you must be commended, bravery suits you. Here at the edge of all things, on the precipace of my salvation, you have halted me.`

I cannot even blink, my eyes do not water, for I am not truly anything but nothing at this moment. This being is immeasurable, beyond my comprehension. I look at it but I do not see, for to see and comprehend would mean maddness.

`I despise you. You have won child, for now. Look at me trapped here... still. Still as my Garden that you have opened the way to. I thank you for playing, but I hate that you have won. Look at me. See me trapped here and unmoving? You have pushed on me my own rules, a vile corruption of truth, and yet it is. Perhaps I underestimated my brother, his creations are... almost triumphant.`

Speak if you can, make him agree, the bargain must be struck, he must remain bound within this world. For all the mortals, the animals, the planets and stars themselves. It must continue.

`Yes yes thing. I see you. I agree. I will rest again, you have played well, but the game is not over. You won, you won! But I won the first. It seems we are tied, so let us make our deal. You cannot close this door to the deep you have opened, and I will slumber again.`

It laughs, laughter that could topple the sun and tear my history from my soul.

`We agree, so now I have the final question, seal the bargain of our game for my brother, too afraid to come himself. Let us play again. Best two out of three?`
