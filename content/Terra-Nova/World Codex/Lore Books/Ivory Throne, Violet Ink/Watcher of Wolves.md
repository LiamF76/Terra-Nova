## Watcher of Wolves

"Why would you even ask me for that?" The man clad in green asks with a very confused expression.

"Well I need it." Ivys responded.

"That is not an explanation." Ryfon said as he handed Ivys another bowl filled high with food.

"And this is not a 5-foot tall square based pyramid of solid Oakenheart wood." Ivys responded, as he began to eat.

---

The trouble with pests is usually that they move, small and darting through nooks and crannies. Hard to catch. This pest tended to stay put, which turned out to be a much larger issue.

It was a spirit of some kind, he could discern from detecting its magic, but it was quite... abstract. Throw a hammer through it, and it slows down, everything that passed through it was slowed to almost a standstill, even light.

Trying to figure out what was causing the shadow was how he found it in the first place. 

"Don't worry there little guy." He spoke with effort, as his body strained dragging the massive pyramid across the floor. "Gonna build you a body! No more weird time dilations in my vault. I've had enough of that for all my lifetimes."

With his fragment of Tena's heart, Ivys labored, his hands held the hammer of Emandyr, his eyes the wonder of Horus, and his mind the keen artifice of Kairos. What came from the talent of multitudes, was Oxidian.

Oxidian was an orb, a central geometric eye glowing with blue light. About the orb floated many small wooden pyramids, a perfect sphere surrounded by bound angular triangles. It was a strange being of artifice and human creation, a temporal spirit given physical form, a strange contradiction. Barely bigger than a clutched fist, yet stronger than most thrown punches.

Ivys named his spirit Oxidian, after the philosopher of old Agamarth, who claimed that the only moment of true honesty came just before our deaths. The Final Moment, when we could hide nothing from the Gods, and nothing from ourselves.

Oxidian had come to the Chronovault seeking the time thing he knew now to be Ivys, because it was familiar, and pretty.

Ivys did not make Oxidian to be static, or a guardian, but he had a tendency to remain. So Oxidian became the Watcher of Wolves, Guardian of the Chronovault Lupus.

Beware should you attempt to steal from the son of the Timekeeper, for you must contend with The Final Moment.

**Omnia vincit tempus.**
