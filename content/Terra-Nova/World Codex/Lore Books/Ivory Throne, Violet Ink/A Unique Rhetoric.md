## A Unique Rhetoric

The Replicants taught me more the less I listened to them. Their voices faded quickly after I got a new soul, and I guess I am a sucker for company.

So I made a replicant.

Ok and I know that sounds bad, but hear me out. He isn't really a full replicant (Just like you Ivys!) yeah yeah. Funny. He isn't though, he's kind of... broken? And that's better.

In my research I discovered quite a bit about the original replicancy projects both Kairos and Horus conducted. They were essentially the same, except for one thing: biological material. Horus used different pieces of himself to create the replicants, and that biological material determined the shape of the replicant!

It's truly fascinating. He gave his entire right arm to make Emandyr, and he was the strongest out of all the ones he made. So that's what I did! I tried it without bio, and no results, but with bio...

Kairos. My demon soul's first replicant, using my left horn, was Kairos. What does this mean? Well it's obvious, replicating from a demon soul creates the most evil thing in the universe. Kairos, but not just any Kairos. It manifests so cruelly, so evil to its core, it is the pure embodiment of Kairos' implied superior intelligence. This fragment of a replicant is un able to hold a conversation, but entirely able to recall the history of Agamarth, but good luck getting him to answer you!

You don't want to know what he called me when I asked, 'What's 2+2?'

**Tempus omnia revelat.**
