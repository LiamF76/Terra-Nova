## Hunger of the Ether

My first memory is clearer than the sky above Gravebinder's castle. Strange a slimy black scaled thing like him has such a fondness for the sun, perhaps it is the only thing that can truly make him feel warm.

I remember the letters on the page, Aelarian writing, a spell tome. My siblings had already devour all the spell pages, the useful and powerful magic. Mother made them leave me the index, the list of spells, the weakest of the magics. It still lies in my hoard.

I could feel the pit in my mind, like hunter from my growling belly, but different. An ethereal desperation to control it, to own this page of the book, to feast on its magic. This is the Dragon's curse, and blessing. To hoard is in our nature, but most die before they gain an understanding of our bargain with the world.

We are defined by what we own, what we can control, our ability to take life is directly correlated to the size of our hoard. Magic. Control riches, magic, and people, and you become stronger.

Lose control, become weak, have nothing, and we die. For tens of thousands of years, this has been no issue, until the actions of some meddling mortals. Humans, filthy fucking humans.

They ripped a hole in the material, and brought the Abyss, its hunger is angry, and ever growing. It ate so much of the magic right out of the air, we can barely breathe it. Their Scars, littered across Escarth, have literally begun to starve us. There is not enough magic, and the babies die young, if they ever find enough magic to make it out of the egg.

I hold well to my hoard, my influence, and my magics. They satiate my form, but none of my brood for the last 80 years have come to birthing. Their tiny claws cannot pierce the egg, and when I open them myself... they are far more deformed than myself. Not worth raising.

Garthrax mates with mortals and monsters in that vile male form. As for Gravebinder... well if he ever had a baby he would kill it and raise it as a mindless thrall. We Dragon Gods three can survive, but the rest... well it is grim.

I keep spies across the world, and I believe my count on dragons is accurate to the current day, though some will always hide in the deep or the sky past where I can see.

In Escarth we number just 8 true dragons, not counting those bred and broken by the Winter Riders of the North. In Ikon they live on decently, 15 true dragons, and a handful of half-breeds. Though in Akash, Drok' Shah, and beyond, I have none that I can say confidently still life. Their status is in question, and in my estimation, unlikely to have lived.

Perhaps I should take a trip to their hoards, take what remains. After all, they died, it should all be mine.

This cycle cannot be allowed, we cannot undo the actions of the mortals, so we must find new magics to satiate ourselves. As we grow, so too will out hunger, and I do not wish to see the world where dragons cannot be satisfied, that is a ruin even I would not enjoy.
