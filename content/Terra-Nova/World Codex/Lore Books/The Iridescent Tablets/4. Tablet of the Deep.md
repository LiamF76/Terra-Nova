## 4. The Tablet of the Deep

A door does not open on its own. It is forced. One exerts its Will upon them, and their Nothing is remade.

A mouth, however, will widen gladly, when a morsel happens to wander through the maw.

When you find a path torn open before you, do not study the threshold. 

Count the teeth instead. For teeth cannot lie.

They are blades and truth all the same, and their bearing is their honesty.

A door yields to power, a mouth yields to hunger.

Look inwards at your Will, run your fingers along your teeth, and your hand along the door.

Are you powerful, or hungry?

I will see, when you are Nothing, and I gaze upon your rotting carcass, if you truly had teeth at all.
