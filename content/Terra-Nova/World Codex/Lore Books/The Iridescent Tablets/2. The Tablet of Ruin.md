## 2. The Tablet of Ruin

I do not hate the broken. I only refuse to carry them.

When the world cracked, it confessed which parts were pretending.

Those that fall have earned their death. Those that live have labored towards that existence.

I refuse to pretend, and in that honesty I see clearly.

My eyes are open, and I see Ruin. 

What will you see, when you are sharp enough to look?
