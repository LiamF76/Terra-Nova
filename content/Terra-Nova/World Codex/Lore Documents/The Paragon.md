Horus Hixs was born to the name Teya'ka, given to him by the Ryfiri Druids of Escarth. Horus was born a spirit of fire, a Phoenix and Demi-God son of Agni. Horus was Agni's first child since being revived by Melantha Celestia-Shield.

While living as a phoenix spirit Teya'ka acted as a guide through darkness to mortal travelers. On the continent of Escarth during the slow dying of the Kingdom of Estor, he protected wanderers from monsters and bandits during the night, acting as a protective flame.

Eventually word spread of Teya'ka the helpful Phoenix, and he was approached by a merchant asking for help. This merchant sought to cross a dangerous wasteland once inhabited by demons known as a "Scar". Teya'ka obliged, but when they crossed the boarder into the Scar, the merchant captured the Phoenix in a magic bottle, revealing himself to be the demon [[Malphas]]. Teya'ka was sold in that magic bottle to a powerful lord of the frozen north, where he was forced to warm and fight for a tyrant ruler.

Eventually however, Teya'ka was freed from his capture. In a particularly bloody battle, the lord realized he was in far outside his depth. He hat attempted to raid south, towards an island that once housed a powerful wizard and their tower of purple stones. A wizard that had been thought o have moved on long ago, this was incorrect.

A Tiefling of purple skin and extreme magical ability walked out the front door and sundered the lord's longships with little effort. He sent the raiders back to the north in shame, but collected the magical bottle, and freed the Phoenix. This wizard, the legendary Ivys Arthur, formerly the White Wizard and member of the Young Wolves, helped Teya'ka to escape the bottle and instead transferred the Demi-God spirits soul into an amulet.

This amulet, when worn by a willing subject, would allow Teya'ka to take mortal form once again. Teya'ka was so grateful to the Wizard that he adopted a mortal name to accompany his new state, choosing one of great honor to Ivys, calling himself Horus Hixs.

---

Horus was given a temporary host in the form of a Warforged body by Ivys, but this would not last forever. When Horus took human form his divine flame was too powerful to be contained by a body, and the host would eventually burn up.

Horus lived in his first body for many years, but as it degraded his abilities and strength waned. He amassed a following, and eventually became the ruler of a small land in the heart of the continent. As he lay dying, one of his loyal followers offered themselves as a host. Horus was initially hesitant, but the kingdom came under attack as he delayed his revival. A nearby warlord had sought to capitalize on the death of the Phoenix, but he failed. Horus accepted his follower's sacrifice, and was reborn. He obliterated the invaders, and took up the mantle of Phoenix Emperor.

Horus and his followers expanded his domain over 200 years, and formed the Empire of New Estorias, with Horus at the throne. The most loyal of his people consider him to be their God Emperor, and Horus is the figure of the state religion in New Estorias. He is a living god emperor, and is revered through his lands.

Now Horus rules as a military mastermind with the strength and wisdom of a Demi-God. Those who follow his church call him 'The Paragon', a holy title derived from the ancient myth of the Lightbearer, a term of revelry referring to Horus as the living savior. They believe Horus must be made Emperor of all the world, and he agrees, his divine mandate was to guide mortals, and he will do so as Phoenix Emperor.

---

The Holy Phoenix Emperor Horus Hixs. A name and title to rival any throughout history, with the charisma in politics and the mind for strategy to match. Ask any scholar and they'll tell you Horus has a mind to match any throughout history, from the ancient Aelarians to figures like the Auranthor of Old Sovrentia.

It isn't hard to find petty kings calling a single mountain their grand domain, or charlatans claiming to be the messiah, sent to save the world from Scar devils or monsters. People are always trying to found their own church, uplift themselves as the center or leading figure of their own grand legend. Horus did no such thing.

Rarer than men who seek to become martyrs, are men who avoid becoming Gods, and yet Horus became one despite that effort. Having led quite the life prior to becoming sealed in an amulet, he had already amassed a decent group of followers. When he became manifest though, that sacred ritual of burning away the soul of his host, reborn in fire... they lost all semblance of worship for the divine. It was said that those who were with him that day fell to their knees, witnessing the phoenix emperor born, and renounced their gods that very moment. He has had that effect on many people now.

---

The first, and most brutal tradition of the Paragon, was born from the likeness of Horus himself. Followers who must repent, those who have sinned or fought against New Estorias, must be cleansed. This cleansing comes naturally, through fire.

The aspirant is covered in holy ointments, and their entire body is wrapped in bandages, bandages usually used for burial. In this state, the aspirant must enter a great flame built by a priest of Horus, and enter its flames. It is said the ointments are blessed, and protect those who can be redeemed, those who cannot...

Those who survive the fire are still scarred. It is different for everyone, but everyone burns somewhere. In some it is a small mark, for small sins, but others have their entire face melted and twisted.

Horus does not delight in this tradition, he has often commented on its barbarity, but his followers are adamant, it is the truest test of the soul.

The only truer test, when asking his greatest priests, is to bear the amulet of the Phoenix Emperor himself, and become one with the holy flame.
