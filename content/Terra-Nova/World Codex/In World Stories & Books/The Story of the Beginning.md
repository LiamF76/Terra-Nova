Welcome to Terra-Nova, home of the Young Wolves, the crew of Fulgrim's arrow, and our stories. 
We start at the very beginning, eons before recorded history, when there was no world to name, Shao Daraan began creation. The creatures origins are unknown, but his power is absolute to write his story upon the world, and with this power it created a world, chaotic and rumbling with the power of its birth.

This creation and world acted as a beacon to the divine souls of the cosmos, formless celestial entities that Shao Daraan offered a place in his Pantheon. In exchange for form, power, and presence, Shao Daraan only asked that the gods allow their followers to be protected by them and they would be bound by worship.

Daraan made a divine rule, that any who lost all of their believers, would be unmade, forgotten, unwritten from existence. Those that agreed were given names, forms, and chose domains. Illuman was first, the eldest, and he chose the Sun, Nova-Magnus, as his power. Then came [[Balthazaar]], who chose the dark, the antithesis to his now brother Illuman. After that came many many more, too many to name.

When the gods had become, Daraan birthed the children they must protect and be worshipped by, the firstborn mortals, the Aelarian elves. Then came dwarves, the children of the earth.

Then came other races, born from the ancient primordial and eldritch beings that were birthed by the creation of the world itself. Konduell, the Weaver, and Jahaad, they gave birth to many races as well, goblins, beasts and orcs. 

In addition Shao Daraan created 12 beings of immense power, some aligned with good, others not. They became known as the 12 prime creations, pledges to defend the mortals. The gods sought to protect their children, and gave them the power to use magic, to defend themselves from the chaos of their short violent lives.

The gods were at peace, for many years the world lives in radiant prosperity, until [[Balthazaar]] rose against his brother. The Night Serpent south to show his dominance over the Sun, and attempted to devour it, to plunge creation into darkness. The gods united, and together were able to defeat [[Balthazaar]], chaining him to the lowest point of creation, the Abyss. 

This battle devasted the mortal populations, and caused a great change in the landscape of the world. Many gods fell, their names forgotten, but the one remembered is Tena, who gave her life to keep [[Balthazaar]] imprisoned.

This devastation was overcome, the mortals thrived, and with their magic they rose to heights unprecedented to the gods. The Aelarians built a great floating city on the sea, known as Atlas, and they created their own elementals, giants of untold arcane power known as Titans. They built divine gates, allowing them to explore through all the planes of existence. 

The titans were a great power, a power that could rival the gods, and they knew it. The titans rose up against the gods, and another great war began. The gods were victorious, binding the titans back into the land, but the mortals were scattered. The city of Atlas was lost. The surviving Aelarians went east and became the elves we know today, making their cities in the mystic land of Agamarth.

In the aftermath of this second divine war, one of the gods who followed the Dawnfather Illuman, Arkonil, believed he could lead them better than the firstborn. He made his case in the heavenly city of Elysium, before his brothers and sisters, but he was struck down. He sought to take power by force if he could not through words, and lost (although many gods fell in the conflict). His followers and himself became known as the Fallen Gods, and they were banished to the Abyss forever. 

Arkonil's Aelarian faithful were punished by Aton, the revenge god, for Arkonil slaying his closest brother Oteus, the God of Justice. The elves that worshipped Arkonil had their skin darkened, marking their betrayal in their very existence. 

In response to tis cataclysm, a new race came from the heart of Shao Daraan itself, humans. In the wake of all the destruction, and the birth of the new people, the inheritor pact was forged. Elves, Dwarves, and Men united to protect one another. 

Now with the power of divine and arcane, martial and magical, the peoples of Terra-Nova live in their vast and beautiful world. These calamities shook the very foundations of the world, but in their wake new strongholds were born.
On the continent to the west, dwarves made kingdoms deep beneath the mountains, and were accompanied by their long standing allies, humans. They built together the kingdom of Praetoria, a great bastion of arts and wealth. Led by the first divine champion Lazarus, whom felled the demon Arkonil when his like once again threatened this world, mortals prospered. Many wars were waged across this land, an when Praetoria fell, a new kingdom rose. Through hundreds of years of strife, a new king known as Escarth Realmcrest, united the land beneath the Blade Peaks, and the continent was named Escarth in his honor. His bloodline still reigns over the kingdom of Estor today. 

In the west of Escarth lies the Freehold, an alliance of free cities ruled by a democratic council of merchants and traders. And there is the rich and bountiful kingdom of Calixos, whom possess the greatest silver and mithril mines in all of Terra-Nova. 

Further east is the beautiful and mystic continent of Ikon, said to be where the very first Aelarians awakened. The elven kingdom of Agamarth rules over the vast green forests and enchanted plains. To the south of the Red Mountains, the vast and dangerous desert of Drok Shah lies. Ruled by the ancient tradition of Khans, and the laws of the desert known only to the mysterious Dark Elves. 

This land is volatile, dangerous, and fragile. Every moment there are forces of darkness and strife seeking to bring chaos into the world, but while there are heroes willing to rise to the challenge, Terra-Nova will never be lost.
