I can tell you of the great darkness. There is [[Balthazaar]], the Night serpent, the God who fought his brothers and sisters and lost, imprisoned in the Abyss, but his power was not gone from this world. The greatest evil in history rose from the serpent's power, The Hand of Eternal Night. 

Thaldrax the dark mage called out to the darkness, not the evil of [[Balthazaar]], or the hatred of Arkonil, but to the deep. The pit beneath hell itself, and when Thaldrax called, it answered. The shadows and chaos called him down, down to the depths of creation, so he went.

Thaldrax build a great and terrible tower, but this tower did not stretch high into the sky and touch the clouds, he dug. His army of monsters and beasts built a tower so deep into the earth it stretches from the earth itself down to the pit. 
Thaldrax reached his master, and was given an evil gift, the Hand of Eternal Night. A gauntlet able to create destruction and darkness like no other. 

Thaldrax rose from the tower and caused suffering across the land for many years.
