While there have not been many throughout Terra-Nova's history, those that have been, have been quite loud, powerful, and memorable. Namely, throughout the known history of our world, there have only been 3 instances of true dragons becoming vampires, thus the turn "Blood Dragon's."
This term though leaves quite a bit of ambiguity. Each dragon is affected by the vampirism disease very differently, which will be discussed in detail here. 

Firstly, there was Samgaust, the third keeper of the Flame, son of Scaldor, Son of Thrydrax. Samgaust given the gift of vampirism by the dark Goddess and Mother of vampires, Drathara. Samgaust was known for his small size, and immensely powerful burning blood breath. While Samgaust was strong, he was the only of the 3 Blood Dragons to have ever been defeated. When he faced against the Wyrm-Wardens who defeated the first Tiamat, he was imprisoned in an ancient magical artifact known as the Prism.

Next is Tyroskar the Crimson Wyrm, the second of the 3 vampire dragons. Tyroskar was known as the strongest of the three, and face off against many legendary heroes, notably doing battle with Aralin Swiftstar and her legendary adversary, Agamar the first Witch King. Tyroskar was so powerful he fought Agamar to a standstill, but after long battle was able to be banished from this plane, cursed to dwell in the Shadowlands.

The final of the wyrms is called Bloodwing, bound to the master who turned it, Valen Drakovar, member of the Court of House Rex in Arkonia. Bloodwing is the most well documented thanks to the tenacity and diligence of his leader, Catherine Rex. Bloodwing was turned as a fully grown adult fire dragon, brought on a ship from the mysterious land of Lazarus as a weapon for Augustus of the Freehold's army. The dragon however, was less then willing, and escaped. When found and attacked by Valen, Bloodwing was wrestled into a stalemate by Valen, and they came to an agreement, where Valen turned Bloodwing and they became bound, to live and die together.

After this brief analysis there are some factors that must be presented again to give you a full understanding of the nature and power of Blood Dragons. In all of history there have only been 3, only 1 has ever been outright defeated. Notably from our research we have discovered a unique property of Blood Dragons, they, by all means known to us, cannot be defeated outright by killing them. Banishment to another plane or sealing is the only known way.
