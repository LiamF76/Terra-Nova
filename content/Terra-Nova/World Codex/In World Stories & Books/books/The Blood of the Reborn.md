By: **Sylvar Laethire**, First Mage of Atlas and Keeper of the Forbidden Tomes, Chief Demonologist of the Academy of Luminescent Words.

When researching demons and their nature one must understand the difference between the born and reborn, that is to say, the difference between those creatures native to the Abyss, and those who have been molded to it. Voracia, once known as Iselene the Maiden of Healing, has heralded a process by which many have gone on to become demons, when their original state was not such.

**ACADEMY MANDATED WARNING**
This ritual is demonic, Abyssal, and heretical in nature. While I have documented it for study here my research shows that it can unlock forces beyond the imagination of the caster, or the subject. These effects include: Making yourself a glowing beacon for attack by Abyssal creatures, alerting Voracia to your presence, killing the subject violently, and accidentally giving the demon used in the rebirth a new body with complete autonomy.

Through methods I cannot disclose in this text, I was able to view the process and materials required for this ritual, and will list the materials hereafter:
**Common**: 
- Holy water blessed by a priest of the divine
- Brimstone/Ashes
- Two Runestones carved with the Abyssal symbol for barrier (no larger than 1cm)
- Shard of Obsidian or Shadowglass useable for cutting
- Wood from an abyssal tree
- Potion of fire resistance
**Rare/Expensive**:
- Blood from a celestial or fiend
- Dragon's bone dust (must be a true dragon, Wyverns and Drakes blood will not suffice)
- Liquid Mithril
- Functional phylactery
- Abyssal Demon/Devil soul for binding and transference

As per my estimate, obtaining these materials via mercantile methods would take the Academy over three years, and a vast amount of gold, so this ritual is expensive to say the least. I must state again before detailing the procedure, this procedure has a 1% survival rate, and involves obtaining highly volatile and dangerous materials, not to mention involving the need to somehow seal a demon soul and use that for the rebirth. This is unadvisable, but the results... are astoundingly potent. For purely academic purposes, it may be worth just one sanctioned attempt.

After outlining the process, here I will go into detail about the experiments I have witnessed. Bear in mind, these were not my experiments, and many outside factors were allowed to meddle in this beautifully violent ritual that would have been prevented had I been in charge.

**Procedure**: 
- Before the procedure can begin you must choose a location. This location must have potent ambient magic, the presence of magical creatures be they celestial or divine may help (although demonic will be dangerous). It is integral to the ritual and will not work otherwise. I advise an area with ambient and revered spirits, the better the disposition they have towards you the stronger your protection against Abyssal forces will be.
- To begin you must prepare the subject and the demon. The subject must ingest a potion of fire resistance, and then drink a quantity of 1.5 ounces of Liquid Mithril. The fire resistance should prevent death, but the Mithril will be painful. (10d6 fire damage).
- After coating the inside of the body with the Mithril, the subject should be able to withstand the remaining portion of the procedure physically, but mentally you must be strong to survive.
- Now you must construct the ritual circle, you must make a perfect unbroken circle of a mixture between 3 parts brimstone and 1 parts dragon bone dust. this must surround the subject, which should be at the center, lying down and facing their head east.
- After creating the ritual circle, place yourself inside it, along with the subject in the proper orientation, and any guards you wish to protect you from potential Abyssal incursions (which are likely). While the subject lays down place a single drop of the demons blood in both eyes, this will be very painful and the subject will be temporarily blinded (blinded condition lasting until the end of the ritual + 2d6 necrotic damage).
- Now that the subject is prepared for the ritual, you must begin the invocation which must be read in Abyssal, the chant will be cited below as a purely academic resource. 
- While the chant is being read, the subject must be bathed in the holy water to protect the devil from infecting the outer layer and avoiding complete fusion with the subject.
- When the chant is finished, devils are likely to appear, and attempt to kill the one applying the ritual. DO NOT allow the applicant to die or fall unconscious. Dying during this section of the ritual will let loose the sealed, and the newly summoned Abyss warriors. In addition, the devils are bound within the ritual circle, do not allow the line of brimstone to be broken, or far worse will be enabled to escape.
- Take the shard of obsidian, pour a drop of demons blood on the tip, and fix it to the abyssal tree wood. Using this new weapon, make an incision large enough for the phylactery above the subjects heart (2d8 slashing damage). Then cut into the heart, and insert the phylactery inside (4d12 necrotic damage). The subject will likely fall unconscious or die from this part of the procedure, do not awaken or revive them, as it will interrupt the rebirth. (DC 20 CON save or fall unconscious)
- Now that the phylactery is inserted, close the heart wound (DC 18 medicine check), and place the Runestone in front of the heart, do not worry about closing the chest wound, as it will heal itself should the ritual be successfully completed.
- Take the sealed demon soul and guide it from its holding place into the body of the subject using the other Runestone (DC 20 Arcana), should the check fail the demon will gain some freedom, and may need to be defeated to be resealed, but that will be much easier than the initial sealing.
- When the demon enters the body, the ritual casters job is finished. Now you just simply wait to see if the ritual was done correctly, and if it is, a terribly beautiful thing is born. 

**ABYSSAL CHANT**: 
*Into the depths of chaos we descend, scars etched into the soul, bring forth the soul of the Abyss, into this mortal body. Bring it your strength, your hate, your heart, your fury, take it and bind it, and be born again. Become fused, become one, and the indelible mark of the Abyss be branded on their body for all of time until the end. Be forged in the crucible of the Blood Queen, the Maiden of healing, the blood weaver, the violent mage, she who works in our veins, make them one, make them vile, make them beautiful. You are not mortal, you are not abyssal, you are new, you are twisted, bloody and heretical perfection. Live now, in un-life, and show the heavens the power of the Abyss.*

**Ritual 1**: 
- Subject: Aelarian Male, age 17
- Administered By: Voracia
- Bound Soul: Sword devil, born of Korvus
- Result: Failure
- Effects: The host was not strong enough to house the devil, it escaped and devoured his body tearing it to bits, brutal mutilation beyond recognition. Had it succeeded, especially on a younger host, it would have vastly more powerful augmentations.

**Ritual 2**: 
- Subject: Aelarian Female, age 57
- Administered By: Voracia
- Bound Soul: Blood eater devil
- Result: *Technical* Success
- Effects: The host was able to merge with the devil, successfully being reborn. The subject was willing in this case, a rarity, which assisted in the success. After the fact however, she could not accept nor conceptualize what had happened to her, and she ripped herself apart from madness over the course of days in lab isolation. Body saved for analysis of effects. She gained vast magical capabilities in both control of her own blood as a weapon, and of others, able to manipulate the libs of lab assistants, then devouring them.

**A Final Word**.
To say this ritual is worth attempting is reasonable from an academic sense. The warriors and power you could create with it, simply fascinating. However, you cannot underestimate the risks, statistically I believe willing subjects are more likely, but you will not find many. I believe the most valuable course would be to offer as many unwilling subjects as possible, for the eventuality that one would survive, and you would have an even more valuable testing subject... note to study this path further.
