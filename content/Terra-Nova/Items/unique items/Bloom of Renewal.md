**Plant Name:** Bloom of Renewal  
**Type:** Abyssal Plant  
**Appearance:** A rare, luminescent flower that grows on blackened vines in the Abyss. The petals shimmer with iridescent hues of green and gold, contrasting against the dark, gnarled terrain of its native environment. The center of the bloom emits a faint, golden glow, pulsing gently like a heartbeat.

The Bloom of Renewal is a miracle amidst the desolation of the Abyss, often regarded as a gift from Tena. It is a symbol of defiance against the corrupting, death-sealing properties of Abyssal wood and its weapons. The plant is exceedingly rare, guarded by twisted Abyssal creatures and hostile terrain.

The bloom draws its power from the faint remnants of life and light within the Abyss, absorbing and purifying the dark energy around it. Legends say that its existence persists because it balances the essence of death with rebirth, making it anathema to the destructive properties of Abyssal wood.

When a creature is killed by a weapon made from Abyssal wood, their soul is bound to the Abyss, prevented from returning to the cycle of life. This can be counter by the Bloom:
**Petal Elixir:**
    - Crushing the petals into a fine paste and mixing them with holy water or other purifying agents creates a potent elixir.
    - Applying the elixir to the corpse of a creature slain by Abyssal wood releases the bound soul, allowing the creature to be revived using resurrection magic.
    - The elixir must be applied within one week of the creature's death, as the soul’s bond to the Abyss strengthens over time.
