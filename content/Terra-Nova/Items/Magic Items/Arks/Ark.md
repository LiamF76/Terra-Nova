## Arks, The Weapons of Nothing

Arks are evil weapons forged from powerful souls. Arks can be crafted from the divine souls of [[Gods of Terra-Nova|Gods]] and Angels, or from the souls of powerful mortals. Demon souls cannot be used to create an Ark.

Once a soul is taken, it must be imbued into the weapon by defeating the ture essence of the soul with the weapon you wish to fuse it into. This essence can manifest as a specter that attacks you, or some other form of self-understanding the person possessed in their life. It must be destoryed, which in turn completely obliterates the soul, using the power of Khal Daraan's Nothing.

The soul is then infused into the weapon, and it becomes an Ark. The Arks all possess the property of soul destruction, but each Ark has unique powers based on the soul used to create it.
