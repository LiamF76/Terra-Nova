This is a list of common sayings and phrases in Terra-Nova and their meanings/ origins. Those that are common across the world will be in the general list, and from there they will be organized by the areas they are common in.


## General

- *"Carry a shield, meet the sword. Carry a sword, meet the shield."* Whatever you prepare for invites the opposite. This comes from the ancient enemies Arkonil the Betrayer God of the sword and Melantha Celestia-Shield.
- *"Keep the Road's Honor."* Respect fellow travelers and adventurers. This comes from the Rising Sun Church of Illuman
